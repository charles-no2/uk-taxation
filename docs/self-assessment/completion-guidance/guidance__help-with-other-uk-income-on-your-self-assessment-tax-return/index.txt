Find information on completing the other UK income section of your Self Assessment tax return.

You may need to declare other UK income on your self assessment tax return, including:

- interest from securities

- life insurance gains

- share schemes

- employment lump sums

If you own shares in a company and have received a dividend payment, you can check how to report them on your Self Assessment tax return (https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-dividends/how-to-report-tax-on-dividends).

If your employer has offered you company shares as a reward for working for them, you can find out if you’ll need to pay tax on Employee Share Schemes (https://www.gov.uk/tax-employee-share-schemes).

Helpsheets for filling in your tax return

Helpsheets provide information that can help you complete different sections of your Self Assessment tax return.

Employment-related shares and securities

Find out how to work out the taxable amount on your employment-related shares and securities (HS305) (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/employee-shares-and-securities-further-guidance-hs305-self-assessment-helpsheet).

Gains on UK life insurance policies

If you have gains on UK life insurance policies (HS320) (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gains-on-uk-life-insurance-policies-hs320-self-assessment-helpsheet), you can find out:

- who should report the gain on the UK life insurance policy

- how to report the gain

- if you are entitled to any reliefs

Accrued Income Scheme

Use the helpsheet on the Accrued Income Scheme (HS343) (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/accrued-income-scheme-hs343-self-assessment-helpsheet) to:

- decide if the Accrued Income Scheme applies to you

- work out your accrued income profits or losses if the scheme does apply

Other taxable income for Self Assessment

If you have other taxable income (HS325) (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/other-taxable-income-hs325-self-assessment-helpsheet) you can work out any tax you need to pay on income such as:

- casual earnings

- commission

- freelance income
