Find information on completing the charitable giving section of your Self Assessment tax return.

Individuals may be able to claim tax relief on donations made to charity or to community amateur sports clubs (CASCs). Find out more about tax relief when you donate to a charity.

Before filling in your tax return, you can work out how much tax relief you can claim if you donate or sell land, property or shares to a charity.

Helpsheets for filling in your tax return

Helpsheets provide information which can help you complete your Self Assessment tax return.

Use Charitable giving tax relief (HS342) if you donate to charity through:

  • Gift Aid
  • Payroll Giving
  • gifts of shares and securities
  • gifts of land or buildings
