Find information on claiming tax reliefs and allowances on your Self Assessment tax return.

Tax reliefs you can claim on your Self Assessment tax return include:

- Seafarers’ Earnings Deduction (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seafarers-earnings-deduction-tax-relief-if-you-work-on-a-ship)

- Community Investment Tax Relief

- subscriptions for shares under the Enterprise Investment Scheme

Find out about tax relief on your private pension contributions (https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-your-private-pension/pension-tax-relief) and whether you need claim it in your Self Assessment tax return.
