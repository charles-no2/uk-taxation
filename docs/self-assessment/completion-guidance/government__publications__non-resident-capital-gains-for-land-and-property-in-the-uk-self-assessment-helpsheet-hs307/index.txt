Find out about the treatment of non-resident Capital Gains on direct and indirect disposals of interest land and property in the UK

This helpsheet explains the treatment of certain capital gains. It is for persons chargeable to capital gains tax such as individuals and trustees who need to report their non-resident capital gains or losses in their Self Assessment return.

This helpsheet will help you to fill in the Capital Gains Tax summary pages of your tax return.
