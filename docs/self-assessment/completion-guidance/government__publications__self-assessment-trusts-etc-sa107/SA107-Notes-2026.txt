Box 25.1 Unused residential finance costs
brought forward
The amount of unused residential property finance
costs brought forward from earlier years is subject
to limits.  
For each property business:
• if the total of unused residential finance costs
   brought forward plus residential finance costs
   (incurred in year) does not exceed the amount of
   the property profits, include the full amount of
   unused residential finance costs brought forward
   in box 25.1
• if the total of unused residential finance costs
   brought forward plus residential finance
   costs (incurred in year) exceeds the amount
   of the property profits, you can only include
   in box 25.1 the amount of unused residential
   finance costs which, when added together with
   residential finance costs incurred in year,
   is equal to the amount of property profits
Any balance of residential finance costs which is
still unrelieved, may be carried forward to future
years of the same property business.
