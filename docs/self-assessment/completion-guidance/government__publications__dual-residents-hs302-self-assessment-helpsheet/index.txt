Use the Self Assessment helpsheet to claim relief on dual residency, when there's a double taxation agreement between the UK and another country you live in.

This helpsheet gives information on how to decide if you’re a dual resident and how to apply for relief from a double taxation agreement between the UK and the other country you’re living in.
