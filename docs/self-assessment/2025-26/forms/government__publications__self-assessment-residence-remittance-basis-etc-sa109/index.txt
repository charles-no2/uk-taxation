Use supplementary pages SA109 to record your residence and domicile status on your SA100 Tax Return.

Use the SA109 supplementary pages when filing your SA100 Tax Return (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-complete-your-self-assessment-tax-return-for-last-tax-year) to record your residence and domicile status, and claim personal allowances as a non-UK resident.
