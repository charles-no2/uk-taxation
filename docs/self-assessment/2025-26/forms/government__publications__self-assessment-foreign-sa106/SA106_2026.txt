Foreign Tax Credit Relief
If foreign tax was taken off your foreign income you may be able to claim Foreign Tax Credit Relief. Please read the
‘Foreign notes’ to see if you can claim the relief and how you should make the claim.
If you’re calculating your tax bill you may also want to calculate your Foreign Tax Credit Relief. If you do, use the
Working Sheet provided with Helpsheet 263, ‘Relief for Foreign Tax paid’ and fill in box 2.
