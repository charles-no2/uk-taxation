Use supplementary pages SA104S to record a short version of partnership income on your SA100 Tax Return.

Use this short version of the SA104 supplementary pages when filing your SA100 Tax Return (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-complete-your-self-assessment-tax-return-for-last-tax-year) if you’re only declaring partnership trading income, and interest or alternative finance receipts received after tax was deducted from banks or building societies.
