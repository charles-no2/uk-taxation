If you have to pay for certain fees or annual
subscriptions to professional bodies to carry

Box 20 Other expenses and capital allowances
You can claim the costs of buying small items of
equipment that you need to do your job (which
your employer did not supply), such as electric
drills and protective clothing.
You can also claim additional household costs
if you have to work from home on a regular
basis. If you previously claimed tax relief when
you worked from home due to coronavirus
(COVID-19) you might no longer be eligible.

 A	For more information on working from home
    expenses, go to www.gov.uk/tax-relief-for-employees/
    working-at-home


You may be able to claim capital allowances for
larger items, including plant and machinery and
computers (but not cars or vans). Put the total
amount in box 20.
