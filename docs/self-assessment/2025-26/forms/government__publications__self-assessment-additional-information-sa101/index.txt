Use supplementary pages SA101 to record less common types of income, deductions and tax reliefs on your SA100 Tax Return.

Use the SA101 supplementary pages when filing your SA100 Tax Return (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-complete-your-self-assessment-tax-return-for-last-tax-year) to record less common types of income, deductions and tax reliefs, and provide information about Income Tax losses, pension charges and tax avoidance schemes.
