Use this helpsheet to fill in your Self Assessment tax return and the trading pages of a Partnership Tax Return.

This guide explains how to fill in the self-employment pages and the trading pages of the Partnership Tax Return.

It covers Standard Accounts Information, what to do if you have no accounts, tax calculations, sending additional information, business expenses and wrong entries on the tax return.
