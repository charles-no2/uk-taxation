Use supplementary pages SA102MP to record employment income on your SA100 Tax Return if you're a Member of Parliament (MP) or a minister.

Use the SA102MP supplementary pages when filing your SA100 Tax Return (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-parliament-sa102mp) to record your employment details.
