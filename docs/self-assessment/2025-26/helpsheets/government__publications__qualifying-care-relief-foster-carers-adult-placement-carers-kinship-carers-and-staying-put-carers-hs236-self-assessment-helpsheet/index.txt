Find out how to get certain payments tax-free for foster carers, adult placement carers, kinship carers and staying put carers.

If you’re a foster carer or an adult placement carer, this guide will help you fill in your tax return. It also covers parent and child arrangements, supported lodgings schemes, claiming capital allowances and qualifying care in partnerships.
