Use this helpsheet to help you fill in your Self Assessment tax return when you get payments in kind from your employer.

Use this guide to fill in box 13 of the employment pages in your Self Assessment tax return. It will help you work out:

- the value of an asset provided by your employer for your private use

- when to use a figure which is higher than the second hand value
