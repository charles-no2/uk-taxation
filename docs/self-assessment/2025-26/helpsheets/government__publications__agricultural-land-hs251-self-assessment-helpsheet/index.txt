Find out when you can claim additional loss relief, known as ‘agricultural relief’, if you have agricultural land included in your rental business.

This guide gives information to help you fill in the UK property pages in your Self Assessment tax return. It covers additional loss relief, which is available when your rental business includes land used for agricultural purposes.
