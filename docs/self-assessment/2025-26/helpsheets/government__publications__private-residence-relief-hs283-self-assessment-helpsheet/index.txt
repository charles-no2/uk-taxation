Find out about tax relief available when you sell or dispose of your main home.

This guide explains the tax relief that may be available when you sell or dispose of your main home (https://www.gov.uk/tax-sell-home). It can apply to a:

- house

- flat

- houseboat or fixed caravan which is your home

- part of a house which is your home

- part of the garden attached to your home
