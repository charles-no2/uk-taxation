Use supplementary pages SA104F to record full partnership income on your SA100 tax return.

Use the SA104F supplementary pages if you do not qualify to complete the short version and are both:

- a member of a partnership

- filing an SA100 Tax Return (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-tax-return-sa100)

This full version covers all the possible types of partnership income you might receive.
