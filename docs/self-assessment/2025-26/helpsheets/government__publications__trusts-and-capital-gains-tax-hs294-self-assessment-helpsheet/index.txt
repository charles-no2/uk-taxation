Find out how UK resident trusts are treated for Capital Gains Tax and what to include in your tax return.

To help you fill in your tax return this helpsheet explains how UK resident trusts are treated for Capital Gains Tax and includes:

- settlements

- bare trusts

- annual exemptions

- disposals by trustees
