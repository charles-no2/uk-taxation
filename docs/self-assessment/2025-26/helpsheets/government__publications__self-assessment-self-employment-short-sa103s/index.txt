Self Assessment: self-employment (short) (SA103S)
Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-self-employment-short-sa103s
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:40.862055+00:00
Updated: 2026-04-06T00:15:20+01:00

Use supplementary pages SA103S to record self-employment income on your SA100 tax return if your annual business turnover was below the VAT threshold for the tax year.

If you’re self employed, have relatively simple tax affairs and your annual business turnover was below the VAT threshold for the tax year, use the SA103S short version of the self-employment supplementary pages when filing your SA100 tax return (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-complete-your-self-assessment-tax-return-for-last-tax-year).
