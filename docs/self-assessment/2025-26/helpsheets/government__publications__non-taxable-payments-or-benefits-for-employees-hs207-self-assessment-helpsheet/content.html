Find information to help you fill in the Employment pages of your Self Assessment tax return.

Find out about benefits and expenses that you do not need to put on the Employment pages of your tax return. This is because they’re covered by concessions or exemptions.

This may include:

  • accommodation
  • meals
  • meal vouchers
  • pension
  • medical treatment abroad
  • health screening
  • nurseries
  • childcare
  • travel expenses
  • retraining
  • long service awards
  • suggestion schemes
  • parking
  • an annual party
  • sports facilities
