Completing Partnership Tax Returns for partners non-residence in, or domicile outside, the UK (Self Assessment helpsheet HS380)

Find out how to fill in the Partnership Tax Return for partnerships that operate partly overseas or where one or more partners are not resident in the UK.

This guide will help you fill in the Partnership Tax Return if:

- the partnership operates partly overseas

- the partnership includes one or more partners who are not resident in the UK

- a partner becomes or ceases to be a UK resident

It also includes information on:

- how non-residence in the UK or domicile outside the UK can affect the partnership and its members

- partnerships made up of UK resident partners only

- partnership made up of non-resident partners only

- partnerships made up of a mix of UK resident and non-resident partnership
