Find out how to complete the employment pages of your Self Assessment tax return if your employer provides living accommodation for you or your relations.

If your employer provides you with living accommodation because of your employment, you must include it in your tax return. This guide will help you work out the amount to put on the employment page.

Living accommodation includes:

- a house

- apartment

- flat

- houseboat

- holiday home
