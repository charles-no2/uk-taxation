Use this helpsheet to understand tax equalisation and prepare tax returns for tax equalised individuals.

This guide is used by professional advisers and employers who are preparing tax returns for tax equalised people. It covers arrangements between an employer and a foreign national working in the UK. This covers pay, tips, expenses, benefits and foreign earnings.
