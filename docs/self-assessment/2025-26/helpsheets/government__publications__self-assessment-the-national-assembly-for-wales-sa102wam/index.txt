Use supplementary pages SA102MS to record employment income on your SA100 Tax Return if you're a member of the Senedd Cymru — Welsh Parliament.

If you’re a member of the Senedd Cymru ― Welsh Parliament, use the SA102MS supplementary pages when filing your SA100 Tax Return (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-complete-your-self-assessment-tax-return-for-last-tax-year) to record your employment details.
