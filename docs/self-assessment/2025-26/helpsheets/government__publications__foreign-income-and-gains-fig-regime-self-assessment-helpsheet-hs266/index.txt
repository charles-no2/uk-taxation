Find out who can claim under the foreign income and gains (FIG) regime, what income and gains qualify, how to claim in Self Assessment, and the effect on your allowances.

This helpsheet is for individuals who wish to claim relief under the foreign income and gains (FIG) regime. It gives information to help you complete the relevant supplementary pages of your Self Assessment tax return and must be read together with the relevant notes accompanying each of these pages.
