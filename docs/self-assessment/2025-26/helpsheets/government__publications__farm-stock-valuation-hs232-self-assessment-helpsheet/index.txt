Find out how to value your farm stock for Self Assessment purposes.

This guide explains the methods of farm stock valuation that HMRC can accept in your Self Assessment tax return.

It covers:

- livestock

- growing crops

- harvested crops

- cooperatives

- the effects of grants and subsidies on stock valuations

- deemed cost valuation
