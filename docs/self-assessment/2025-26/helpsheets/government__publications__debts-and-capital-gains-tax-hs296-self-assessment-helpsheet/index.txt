Find out about the treatment of debts, loans and securities for Capital Gains Tax.

This guide covers the basic rules on the treatment of debts for Capital Gains Tax purposes. It includes:

- loans

- securities

- qualifying corporate bonds

- bankruptcy

- receivership

- liquidation
