Use this helpsheet to work out Foreign Tax Credit Relief for Capital Gains and how to complete the Trust and Estate tax return.

This guide is to help calculate how much Foreign Tax Credit Relief you can claim against Capital Gains for a Trust or Estate of a deceased person.

From 6 April 2019, the Self Assessment helpsheet HS390 has been merged with Relief for Foreign Tax Paid: HS263 Self Assessment helpsheet. (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/calculating-foreign-tax-credit-relief-on-income-hs263-self-assessment-helpsheet).
