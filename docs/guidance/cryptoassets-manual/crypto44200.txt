Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) is paid on the acquisition of land and buildings over a certain price in England and Northern Ireland. More information on SDLT is available in the Stamp Duty Land Tax Manual (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/stamp-duty-land-tax-manual).

SDLT does not apply if the land or buildings acquired is/are located in Scotland or Wales:

-

You pay Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (https://www.gov.scot/policies/taxes/land-and-buildings-transaction-tax/) if you purchase land in Scotland on or after 1 April 2015.

-

You pay Land Transaction Tax (https://gov.wales/land-transaction-tax) if you purchase land in Wales on or after 1 April 2018.
