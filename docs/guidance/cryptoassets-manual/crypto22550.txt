Cryptoassets for individuals: Capital Gains Tax: currency
Identifier: CRYPTO22550
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/cryptoassets-manual/crypto22550
Published: 2021-03-30T09:03:16+01:00
Updated: 2021-03-26T10:54:34+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:18:35.186108+00:00

HMRC does not consider cryptoassets to be currency or money. This reflects the position previously set out by the Cryptoasset Taskforce report (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cryptoassets-taskforce) (CATF).

This means that sections 252 and 269 Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 do not apply.
