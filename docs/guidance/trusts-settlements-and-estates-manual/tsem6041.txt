Legal background to trusts & estates: gift of land in a will
Identifier: TSEM6041
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6041
Published: 2016-05-02T16:44:37+01:00

A gift of land in a will is properly described as a ‘devise’. The recipient is a ‘devisee’. The testator ‘devises’ land, rather than bequeathing it. Using the incorrect word will not affect the correct result in law. This is based on the facts and their legal interpretation.
