Deceased persons: charges and interest paid
Identifier: TSEM7270
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7270
Published: 2016-05-02T16:51:16+01:00
Updated: 2018-04-05T10:48:06+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:29.127916+00:00

Charges and payments of allowable interest can be treated as paid by the deceased only if they fell due for payment on or before the date of death.
