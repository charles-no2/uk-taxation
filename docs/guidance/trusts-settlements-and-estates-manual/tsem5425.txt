Trusts for particular purposes: employment-related trusts - Share Incentive Plans (SIPs) previously all employee share ownership plans (AESOPS)
Identifier: TSEM5425
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5425
Published: 2016-05-02T16:41:37+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:32:59+01:00

Companies may set up a share plan via a trust to acquire shares for the benefit of employees. From 28 July 2000, the company may apply for approval for the share plan under Paragraph 81, Schedule 2 ITEPA 2003, as a ‘share incentive plan‘. Such approval entitles the company and the trust to various tax advantages.
