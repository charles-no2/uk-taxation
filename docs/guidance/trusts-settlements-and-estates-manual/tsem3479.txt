The details are the same as in the example in TSEM3477 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3477) but the beneficiary reaches the age of 18 on 5 January 2025 and so no longer qualifies as a vulnerable beneficiary.

The election is therefore only in force for nine months of the tax year.

All the income arises evenly throughout the year but the trustees pay allowable management expenses of £2,700 in the period to 5 January and the remaining £1,800 in the last three months of the tax year.
