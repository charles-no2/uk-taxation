The main cases on trust management expenses are Carver v Duncan [1985] STC 356 (‘Carver v Duncan’) and H M Revenue & Customs v Trustees of the Peter Clay Discretionary Trust [2009] STC 469 (‘HMRC v Peter Clay’).

Sections TSEM8110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8110) - 8115 give details of Carver v Duncan.

Sections TSEM8120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8120) - 8150 give details of HMRC v Peter Clay.
