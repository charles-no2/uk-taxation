There is an exception to the requirement that a trust must be in writing as set out in S53(1). Section 53(2) of the Law of Property Act 1925 says:

‘This section does not affect the creation or operation of resulting, implied or constructive trusts.’

See TSEM9600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9600) and TSEM9700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9700) for guidance on resulting and constructive trusts
