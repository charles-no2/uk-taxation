Trust income: trustees are not resident in the UK
Identifier: TSEM3175
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3175
Published: 2016-05-02T16:32:11+01:00
Updated: 2021-06-10T13:15:57+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:35.861577+00:00

If trustees are not resident in the United Kingdom, they may not be chargeable on all the trust income. BT & C Trusts deal with all non-resident trusts.

TSEM1450 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1450) has a list of the type of trusts that BT&C Trusts deals with.

TSEM1455 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1455) shows how to decide trustees’ residence status for income tax purposes for periods to 5 April 2007.

TSEM1460 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1460) shows how to decide trustees’ residence status for capital gains tax purposes for periods to 5 April 2007.

TSEM10020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10020) shows how to decide trustees’ residence status for both income tax and capital gains tax purposes for periods from 6 April 2007.
