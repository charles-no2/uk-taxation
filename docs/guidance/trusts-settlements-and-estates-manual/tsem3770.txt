Trust income and gains: beneficiaries: beneficiary entitled to trust income - trust that trades or has rental income - general
Identifier: TSEM3770
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3770
Published: 2016-05-02T16:37:27+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:53.293581+00:00

Where a trust has a trade or a rental business, it may incur expenses, or claim Capital Allowances or losses. These items may affect the taxation of the IIP beneficiary.

TSEM3771 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3771) gives guidance on business expenses. TSEM3772 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3772) gives guidance on Capital Allowances. TSEM3773  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3773)gives guidance on losses.
