Trust income and gains: the charge on trustees: the tax pool - credit to beneficiaries

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3022
Published: 2016-05-02T16:31:04+01:00

When a discretionary income payment is made to a beneficiary of a non-settlor - interested trust, it carries a credit at the trust rate (see TSEM3041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3041) for current rate). Such credits reduce the amount available in the ‘tax pool’ - see TSEM3020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3020).
