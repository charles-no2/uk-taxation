The Thalidomide Trust is a charity that was set up in 1973 to support the victims ofThalidomide.

TSEM5954 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5954) explains the tax treatment of payments tobeneficiaries up to 4 August 2004.

TSEM5956 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5956) explains the tax treatment of payments tobeneficiaries from 5 August 2004.
