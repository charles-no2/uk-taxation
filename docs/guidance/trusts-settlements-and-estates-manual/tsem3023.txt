Trust income and gains: the charge on trustees: the tax pool - trustees pay excess tax
Identifier: TSEM3023
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3023
Published: 2016-05-02T16:31:06+01:00
Updated: 2025-11-06T00:00:00+00:00

If the tax in the tax pool is not enough to cover the tax credit needed for the payments to beneficiaries, the trustees must pay the difference in their Trust & Estate Self Assessment Tax Return for the year. The relevant legislation is at ITA/S496.

For example, in 2024-2025 the trustees make discretionary payments from income to beneficiaries totaling £5,000.

This is treated as a net amount from which tax at the trust rate of 45% has been deducted.

The tax credit is £4,090.91 (£5,000 x100/55=£9,090.91).

If the amount in the tax pool is £2,500, the trustees have to pay a further £1,590.91 (£4,090.91 -£2,500=£1,590.91).

See TSEM3024  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3024)about how trustees can calculate the maximum discretionary payment to cover the additional tax under S496.
