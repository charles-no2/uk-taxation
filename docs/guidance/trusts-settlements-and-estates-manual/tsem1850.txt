Introduction to trusts: supplementary deeds: deed of surrender or release

A person may accept entitlement under a trust then give it up. This enables a subsequent trust to take effect. If that person seeks to impose new trusts, the deed is not a surrender or release. It is an assignment (TSEM1845 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1845)).

A deed of surrender or release cannot apply retrospectively. It is effective only from the date the deed is executed.

The person surrendering or releasing is a ‘settlor’ within Section 620(1) ITTOIA (TSEM4120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4120)).

The following provisions may also apply

- Section 624 ITTOIA (TSEM4200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4200))

- Section 629 ITTOIA (TSEM4300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4300)).
