TSEM8210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8210)+ explains what trust management expenses are allowable for accumulation/discretionary trusts in the light of tax law and the relevant case law. It also explains the method of taking TMEs into account when computing the income tax liability of accumulation/discretionary trustees.
