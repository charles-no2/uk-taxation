Ownership and income tax: Specific types of property: shares: example 7: joint names - 50/50 rule applies
Identifier: TSEM9977
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9977
Published: 2016-05-02T17:04:58+01:00

A and B, civil partners living together, hold shares in non-close company X in their joint names. A provided 30% of the purchase price, and B 70%.

They do not make a form 17 declaration (TSEM9842 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9842)). The 50/50 rule (TSEM9814 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9814)) applies to tax them equally on the dividends.

If they had made a valid declaration on form 17 in respect of the shares in company X and provided evidence, they would be taxable 30/70 on the dividends.
