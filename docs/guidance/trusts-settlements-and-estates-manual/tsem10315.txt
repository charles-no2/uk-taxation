Non-resident trusts: settlor’s chargeability: income tax - minor unmarried child
Identifier: TSEM10315
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10315
Published: 2016-05-02T16:26:20+01:00
Updated: 2024-06-20T11:15:46+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:19.727379+00:00

Where trustees make an income or capital payment to a (relevant) child of the settlor, the payment can be treated as that of the settlor for all tax purposes (TSEM4300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4300)).

Where the payment is made from income, because the provisions of ITA07/S493 do not apply to non-resident trusts, there is no grossing up and no tax credit. The payment is regarded as untaxed foreign income (TSEM10255 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10255)) and should be taken into account by the settlor when considering his or her liability to Income Tax for the year - but see TSEM10320 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10320) regarding the operation of ESC A93.
