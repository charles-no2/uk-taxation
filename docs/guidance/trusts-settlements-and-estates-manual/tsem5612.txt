Record details of all payments made to trustees under ESC A68. Record the following information

- name of trust,

- file reference,

- year(s) of assessment,

- amount paid to the trustees and

- date of payment.

If a payment to trustees covers tax on payments made in more than one year of assessment, record separately the amounts relating to each year of assessment
