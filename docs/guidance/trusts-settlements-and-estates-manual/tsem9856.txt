Property held jointly by married couples or civil partners: Form 17 rule - further change in beneficial interests
Identifier: TSEM9856
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9856
Published: 2016-05-02T17:02:41+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:35:28+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:04.005480+00:00

A couple might declare that their interest in property is split 60/40. Later their interests change so that they hold it 80/20. If they wish they may make a fresh declaration to reflect the new split. But it must reflect the actual position.
