Non-resident trusts: new non-resident trust: obtaining information
Identifier: TSEM10115
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10115

When BT & C Trusts become aware of a non-resident trust, they need to ensure that they request sufficient information so that tax returns are not issued where the trustees have no income or gains chargeable to tax (TSEM10200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10200#)+).

If the trust has been non-resident throughout a tax year, then if the trustees’ only income is ‘foreign’ income, they will not be chargeable to UK Income Tax for that year (TSEM10205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10205)).

If the trust has been non-resident throughout a tax year, then the trustees are not chargeable to Capital Gains Tax on trust gains for that year (TSEM10265 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10265)).

BT & C Trusts ask trustees to confirm in writing where the trustees believe that no liability to tax arises.
