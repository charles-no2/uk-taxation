Taxation of income is based on beneficial ownership (TSEM9130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9130)), not legal ownership (TSEM9120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9120)).

For income tax purposes, you need to know who is the beneficial owner of the income, that is, who is ‘entitled to’ the income.
