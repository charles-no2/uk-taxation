Legal Background to Trusts and Estates: All beneficial interests cease
Identifier: TSEM6352
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6352
Published: 2016-05-02T16:48:29+01:00
Updated: 2016-09-22T14:58:03+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:27.694203+00:00

A trust ceases when the only remaining interests are absolute and vested in possession.They are no longer deferred.

Normally this results from the wording of the trust documents. Sometimes it results from the operation of law.
