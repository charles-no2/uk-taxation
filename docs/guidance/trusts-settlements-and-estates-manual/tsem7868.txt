The rights of the surviving spouse or civil partner and the rules of inheritance are explained at TSEM7864 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7864).

If the intestate is not married or in a civil partnership, the rules of inheritance apply. Unmarried partners and/or friends will not inherit. The rules set out the priority order in which the estate would be equally shared.

-  Children

-  Parents

-  Brothers or sisters

- Nieces and nephews

- Grandparents

- Uncles and aunts

- Cousins

No surviving kin

Where a person dies intestate and without leaving kin entitled to his/her estate, the estate passes to the Crown.
