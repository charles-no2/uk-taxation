A trust can be non-resident for tax purposes - see TSEM1455 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1455).

As a separate matter, a trust may be governed by a foreign law - see TSEM1460 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1460).
