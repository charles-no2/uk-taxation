Ownership and income tax: Specific types of property: land and buildings: example 9: joint names of husband and wife - 50/50 rule applies
Identifier: TSEM9929
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9929
Published: 2016-05-02T17:03:50+01:00
Updated: 2021-02-24T15:07:05+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:08.286062+00:00

A house is purchased in the joint names of A and B, a married couple who live together. The house is rented out. A is a higher rate taxpayer, B does not work and has no income. A does not return any rents - B returns them all and they are covered by personal allowances, etc.

A and B have not submitted form 17 (TSEM9842 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9842)).

As the house is in the joint names of spouses living together, ITA/S836 applies - the income is taxable 50/50 (TSEM9814 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9814)).
