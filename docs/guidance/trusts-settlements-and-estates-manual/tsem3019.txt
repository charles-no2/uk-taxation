Trust income and gains: the charge on trustees - charge on accumulation or discretionary trusts
Identifier: TSEM3019
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3019
Published: 2016-05-02T16:30:57+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-10T14:05:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:33.452337+00:00

A trust is within ITA/S479 if:

- the trustees have the power to accumulate income or make discretionary payments, and the exemption for settlor -interested settlements to the special trust rates no longer applies.

Tax paid at the special trust rates is taken into account in the trustees’ tax pool -TSEM3021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3021) explains how.

Certain discretionary trusts are exempt from the charge at the special trust rates (TSEM3011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3011)). TSEM3025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3025) has details.
