This manual does not cover the tax treatment of, or what is meant by, trusts established as unit trust schemes. Advice on unit trust schemes is at:

- IFM02000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/investment-funds/ifm02000) onwards for authorised unit trust schemes

- IFM10000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/investment-funds/ifm10000) onwards for unauthorised unit trusts

- IFM12000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/investment-funds/ifm12000) onwards for foreign unit trusts.

Business Tax (Unit & Investment Trusts) can supply additional advice on

- the taxation of unit trust schemes

- any question about whether a trust is a unit trust scheme.

The taxation of participants depends on the character and nature of the scheme in which the interest is held. This applies to

- income from unit trusts

- gains on disposal of interests in unit trusts.

Information about this is in the above guidance and also at CG57680 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg57680p)
