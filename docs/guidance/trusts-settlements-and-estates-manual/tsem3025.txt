Trust income and gains: the charge on trustees: trusts exempt from the special trust rates

Some discretionary trusts are exempt from the special trust rates. The exemption applies to:

- income arising to a trust established for charitable purposes only

- income arising to the trustees of flat management company trusts from 2006-07 - see TSEM5750 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5750)

- some dividends arising to the trustees of approved profit sharing schemes - see TSEM5410 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5410)

- for years to 2005-06 only, income from investments, deposits or other property, held:

-

- for the purposes of a fund or scheme approved under Chapter I Part XIV ICTA 1988 and established for the sole purpose of providing relevant benefits within the meaning of Section 612

- for the purposes of a FURBS established for the sole purpose of providing relevant benefits within the meaning of Section 612

(see TSEM5200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5200) onwards); or

-

- for the purposes of a personal pension scheme approved under Chapter IV ICTA 1988.
