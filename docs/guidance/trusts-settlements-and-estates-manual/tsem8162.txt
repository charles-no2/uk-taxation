Trust management expenses : ‘properly chargeable to income’: apportionment and tax: recording and evidence: expense separately recorded
Identifier: TSEM8162
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8162
Published: 2016-05-02T16:56:28+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:45:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:43.342857+00:00

The invoice etc. should clearly show that it refers exclusively to income.

Example 1

Invoice states ‘Accountancy services - reconciling income receipts’.

Example 2

Invoice states ‘Data inputting - income transactions’.

Example 3

Bank statement shows a separate charge that the trustees can identify as relating to transfer of income to beneficiaries

Example 4

An annual statement shows an insurance premium for property occupied by a beneficiary that falls within TSEM8729.

Example 5

A quarterly or annual statement shows rent paid by the trustees for a property occupied by a beneficiary that falls within TSEM8755 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8755).
