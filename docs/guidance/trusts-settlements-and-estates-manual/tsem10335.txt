Non-resident trusts: settlor’s chargeability: Income Tax - capital sums paid to settlor
Identifier: TSEM10335
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10335
Published: 2016-05-02T16:26:30+01:00
Updated: 2024-06-20T11:01:10+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:20.400901+00:00

If capital sums are paid to the settlor or to his or her spouse or civil partner by the trustees of a non-resident trust, you need to consider:

- the application of the settlements legislation ITTOIA05/S633 and S641 (TSEM4400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4400) to TSEM4405 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4405)) where the settlor may be charged to tax on income of the trust that has not already been treated as his or her income under ITTOIA05/S624 and S629;

- the application of the Transfer of Assets Abroad legislation ITA07/S727 (INTM600660 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/international-manual/intm600660)) where income payable to a person abroad (which includes trustees of a non-resident trust and any non-resident company underlying a trust) may be chargeable to tax on the settlor;

- the application of the Capital Gains Tax anti-avoidance legislation TCGA92/S87 (CG38570c (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg38570c) ) where capital gains of the non-resident trust have not been charged to Capital Gains Tax on the trustees (TSEM10265 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10265)), and the same gains have not already been attributed to the settlor under TCGA92/S86, then they may be attributed to the settlor as a result of the payment of the capital sum. In this case the settlor is also considered to be a ‘beneficiary’ of the trust.

A non-resident trust may give details of such capital sums on, for example, completed Tax Returns or form 50(FS) (TSEM10130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10130)).
