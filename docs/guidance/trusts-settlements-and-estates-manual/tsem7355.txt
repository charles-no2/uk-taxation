Deceased persons: legal authority of personal representatives
Identifier: TSEM7355

Before a personal representative can settle the deceased person’s estate they normally need to get legal authority to do so. This comes in the form of a document issued by the Court.

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland it is called probate where it is issued to executors.

It is called letters of administration where it is issued to administrators.

In Scotland the document is called confirmation.
