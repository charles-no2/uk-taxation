Trust income and gains: vulnerable beneficiaries - definition of a vulnerable person
Identifier: TSEM3420
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3420
Published: 2016-05-02T16:34:15+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:42.805087+00:00

A vulnerable person is either:

- a disabled person (TSEM3421 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3421)) or

- a relevant minor (TSEM3423 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3423)).

A vulnerable person does not have to be resident in the UK but see TSEM3425 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3425).
