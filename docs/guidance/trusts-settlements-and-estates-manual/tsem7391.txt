Deceased persons: tax reliefs for personal representatives - how to allow relief
Identifier: TSEM7391
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7391
Published: 2016-05-02T16:52:09+01:00

Where a relief mentioned at TSEM7390 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7390) is due, the relief should first of all be set against any untaxed income, such as business profits or rents from property. In this way the relief can be given in any informal calculation of the personal representatives’ tax liability, provided the conditions at TSEM7410 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7410) are met.

It is often the case that personal representatives will have insufficient untaxed income to utilise all of the relief. When this happens, the relief will give rise to a repayment of tax. The repayment can only be claimed, by the personal representative completing a Trust & Estates Self Assessment return for the year(s) in question.
