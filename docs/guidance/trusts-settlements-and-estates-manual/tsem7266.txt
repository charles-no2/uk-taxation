Deceased persons: taxed income and gains
Identifier: TSEM7266
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7266
Published: 2016-05-02T16:51:11+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:28.816274+00:00

Taxed income and gains in the categories referred to at SAIM9010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim9010) and IPTM3110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/insurance-policyholder-taxation-manual/iptm3110) can be treated as income of the deceased only if paid or credited on or before the date of death.
