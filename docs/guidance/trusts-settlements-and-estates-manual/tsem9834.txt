Property held jointly by married couples or civil partners: The 50/50 rule: year of death
Identifier: TSEM9834
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9834
Published: 2016-05-02T17:02:13+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:35:26+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:02.032252+00:00

The 50/50 rule ceases to apply on the date that one spouse or civil partner dies. Income arising after that date is wholly attributable to the surviving spouse or civil partner.
