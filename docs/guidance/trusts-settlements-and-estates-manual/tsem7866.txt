The fixed sums referred to at TSEM7864 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7864) are

- If there is a child/children: £250,000 where the death occurred on or after 1 January 2008.

- if there is no child/children but a surviving parent, brother or sister £450,000 where the death occurred on or after 1 January 2008
