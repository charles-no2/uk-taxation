A constructive trust is imposed by the rules of equity. It must satisfy the demands of justice and good conscience. For further details see TSEM9705+.

An important set of circumstances where there may be a constructive trust over residential property is explained in CG65420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65400p) onwards.
