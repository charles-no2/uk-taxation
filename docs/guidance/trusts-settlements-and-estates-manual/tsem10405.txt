Non-resident trusts: beneficiary’s chargeability: introduction

This section considers the United Kingdom tax liabilities arising to UK-resident beneficiaries of non-resident trusts. In particular it looks at:

- Beneficiaries of bare trusts - see TSEM10410 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10410)

- Beneficiaries with an entitlement to trust income - interest in possession trusts (IIP) trusts - see TSEM10415 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10415) to TSEM10430 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10430)

- Beneficiaries who may receive trust income at the trustees’ discretion - discretionary trusts - see TSEM10435 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10435) to TSEM10455 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10455)

- Capital distributions made to beneficiaries - see TSEM10460 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10460)
