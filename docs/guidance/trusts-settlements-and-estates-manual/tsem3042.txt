Trust income and gains: the charge on trustees - annuity as charge on trust
Identifier: TSEM3042
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3042
Published: 2016-05-02T16:31:27+01:00
Updated: 2018-08-22T13:36:09+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:35.240848+00:00

An annuity is normally regarded as a charge on the trust income generally. See Trust & Estate return boxes 10.2A - 10.4A.

Sometimes an annuity is legally secured on the income of a particular part of the trust fund. If so, it is a charge on that income only.
