Trusts for particular purposes: employment-related trusts - not within TSEM5010 onwards
Identifier: TSEM5575
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5575
Published: 2016-05-02T16:41:52+01:00
Updated: 2021-05-13T09:54:30+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:05.276678+00:00

You may come across a new employment-related scheme administered by trustees that does not come within any of the categories in TSEM5010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5010) onwards. If you do,

- do not ask to see the trust deed

- do not submit to Trusts Technical

- do not issue a guidance summary to other offices

Instead, set up on SA as a normal trust.
