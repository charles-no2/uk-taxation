Deceased persons: intestacy - no letters of administration
Identifier: TSEM7806
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7806
Published: 2016-05-02T16:54:53+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:45:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:38.386128+00:00

Where letters of administration are not taken out, HMRC must take extra care in finalising the tax liabilities up to the date of death.

If a repayment arises, follow the guidance in TSEM7280 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7280).

Where additional liability arises, refer to TSEM7254 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7254).
