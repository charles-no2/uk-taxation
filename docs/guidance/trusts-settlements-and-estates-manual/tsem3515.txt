Trust income and gains: Sub-fund elections - time limits
Identifier: TSEM3515
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3515
Published: 2016-05-02T16:36:05+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:30:07+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:48.876207+00:00

The time limit for making an election is the first anniversary of 31 January following the tax year in which the specified date falls. So, for an election to be treated as having taken effect on a date falling in the year 2023-24 it must be made no later than 31 January 2026.
