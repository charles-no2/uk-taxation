Trusts for particular purposes: employment-related trusts: retirement benefits schemes - lump sum death benefit
Identifier: TSEM5200
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5200
Published: 2016-05-02T16:40:45+01:00
Updated: 2021-06-10T14:04:08+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:01.390420+00:00

If a scheme member dies in service, a lump sum benefit may become payable. The scheme rules may allow the trustees to put this benefit into trust for the relatives/dependants of the member. BT & C Trusts deal with these trusts. (TSEM1420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1420)).

Trustees should notify HMRC of the trusts using the Trusts Registration Service TSEM1402 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1402).
