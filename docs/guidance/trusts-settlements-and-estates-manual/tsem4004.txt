Settlements legislation: Customer request for clearance, approval or advice

Published: 2017-07-25T09:06:38+01:00

HMRC may receive a request to give clearance, approval or advice in respect of the settlements legislation.

HMRC do not provide any form of clearance (including non-statutory clearance) or advice in these circumstances.

The official clearance and approvals guidance (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seeking-clearance-or-approval-for-a-transaction) says, “HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) won’t give clearance or advice in respect of the application of the ‘settlements legislation’ in Chapter 5 Part 5 Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2002 or the consequences of executing a non-charitable trust deeds or settlements”

If internal users receive a request for clearance they should send it to  HO Technical, Trusts(BT&C) (mailto:4953547@internal.hmrc.gov.uk) and they will reject it.

External customers can find this guidance at seeking clearance or approval (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/seeking-clearance-or-approval-for-a-transaction).
