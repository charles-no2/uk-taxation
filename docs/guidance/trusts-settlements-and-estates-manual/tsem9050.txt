If you are dealing with a claim featuring property that is

- held in joint names

- by two individuals who are a married couple or civil partners living together

you should consult TSEM9800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9800) first because there is specific tax legislation on this subject. You may also want to look at the relevant examples in TSEM9900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9900).

In all other cases you should consult the guidance on general principles in TSEM9000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9000) - 9700. You may also want to look at the relevant examples in TSEM9900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9900).
