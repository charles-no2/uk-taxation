Use this table if you need to convert the legislation in ICTA 1988/Part XV into its Tax Law Rewrite equivalent in ITTOIA.

For income arising under a settlement in tax years up to 5 April 2005, ICTA 1988 applies.

For income arising under a settlement in tax years from 6 April 2005, ITTOIA applies.
