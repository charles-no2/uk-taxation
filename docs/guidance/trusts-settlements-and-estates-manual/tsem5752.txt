Trusts for particular purposes: flat management companies not within S42 LTA 1987 - definition of ‘relevant housing body’
Identifier: TSEM5752
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5752
Published: 2016-05-02T16:42:53+01:00
Updated: 2018-01-17T09:09:36+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:09.113253+00:00

The term ‘relevant housing body’ means

- a local authority,

- a registered social landlord,

- a Northern Ireland housing association,

- a charitable housing association,

- a charitable housing trust,

- a housing action trust established under Part 3 of the Housing Act 1988,

- the Housing Corporation, or

- the Northern Ireland Housing Executive.
