Trust management expenses: ‘properly chargeable to income’ in general trust law: apportionment and tax

For tax purposes, the onus is on the trustees

- to show that expenses were incurred exclusively for the benefit of the income beneficiary, and

- to identify the relevant expenses, considering apportionment if necessary.
