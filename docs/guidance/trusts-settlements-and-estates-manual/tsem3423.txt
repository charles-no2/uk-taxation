Trust income and gains: vulnerable beneficiaries - definition of a relevant minor
Identifier: TSEM3423
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3423
Published: 2016-05-02T16:34:22+01:00
Updated: 2018-01-18T10:19:26+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:43.269328+00:00

A relevant minor is a child under the age of 18 at least one of whose parents has died.
