Trusts for particular purposes: employment-related trusts - new general employee benefit trusts

‘Employee benefit trust’ here means a general employee benefit trust (TSEM5025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5025)).

That is a trust, which is not

- a retirement benefits scheme

- an approved share scheme.

Trustees should notify HMRC of new Employment Benefit Trusts using the Trusts Registration Service TSEM1402 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1402).

Trustees are required to check the information held in the register is accurate and up to date.
