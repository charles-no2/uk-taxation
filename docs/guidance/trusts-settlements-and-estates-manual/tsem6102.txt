The laws of intestacy determine who will inherit the possessions, and how much each will get. The rules are set out at TSEM7800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7800) onwards.

There are rules that determine which persons have the right to apply for appointment as administrators of the estate and in what order. They obtain a grant of letters of administration. These establish their authority and standing to administer the estate. If everyone declines it is possible for a creditor of the estate to make an application. That could be HMRC.
