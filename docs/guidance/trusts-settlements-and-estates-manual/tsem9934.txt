Ownership and income tax: Specific types of property: bank and building society accounts - taxation of interest

If the owners are married couples or civil partners, first refer to the guidance in TSEM9800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9800). You should also bear in mind the Settlements legislation (TSEM4000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4000)). Otherwise, the following applies.

Under ITTOIA/S369, income tax is charged on interest. Under ITTOIA/S371, the person liable for tax charged under S369 is the person receiving or entitled to the interest.
