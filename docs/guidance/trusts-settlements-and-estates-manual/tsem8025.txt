Trust management expenses: introduction: tax rules

There are two sets of tax rules governing whether trust management expenses are allowable in the two main types of trust:

- one for accumulation/discretionary trusts - where what is ‘properly chargeable to income’ in general trust law has priority - see TSEM8205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8205)+.

- one for interest in possession trusts - where what is ‘properly chargeable to income’ in the trust deed has priority - see TSEM8305 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8305)+.

There are rules derived from case law about what is meant by ‘properly chargeable to income’ in general trust law - see TSEM8105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8105)+.

There are special rules for bare trusts (see TSEM8405 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8405)+), settlor-interested trusts (see TSEM8505 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8505)+) and mixed trusts (see TSEM8605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8605)+).
