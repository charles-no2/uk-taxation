The legislation in ICTA 1988/Part XV was rewritten as part of the Tax Law Rewrite programme. The legislation is now in ITTOIA.

TSEM4002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4002) gives a table of destinations, showing where the specific parts of the ICTA 1988/Part XV legislation are now in ITTOIA.

TSEM4003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4003) gives a table of origins, showing where the specific parts of the legislation in ITTOIA derived from in ICTA 1988.
