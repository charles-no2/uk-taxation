Introduction to trusts: new trust: administration period not followed by a trust

The period of administration starts at the date of death. It finishes when the personal representatives have discharged all their duties (TSEM6071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6071)).

If there is no trust, the administration period is usually dealt with by HMRC PAYE & SA. If the total liability income tax and capital gains tax for the whole administration period is expected to be in excess of £10,000 or the case is regarded as ‘complex’ (TSEM7376 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7376)), HMRC Administration of Estates Cardiff will assume responsibility. There are details at TSEM7204 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7204). There is also further guidance at CG30780 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg30780).
