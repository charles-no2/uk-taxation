BT&C Trusts (mailto:4953547@internal.hmrc.gov.uk) deal with claims under ESC B18 (https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5b714ba7ed915d6d05698ac3/Extra_Statutory_Concessions.pdf) and are responsible for confirming that the conditions have been met and for calculating the amount of relief due.

BT&C Trusts will confirm to the beneficiary’s tax office the amount of relief due.
