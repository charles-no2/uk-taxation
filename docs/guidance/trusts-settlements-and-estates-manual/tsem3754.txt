Trust income and gains: beneficiaries: beneficiary receives discretionary income payment from a resident trust
Identifier: TSEM3754

Published: 2016-05-02T16:36:50+01:00

Trustees of a discretionary trust have the power to decide how to apply the trust funds. They may pay from trust income or capital.

Where the trustees make a discretionary payment from income, they usually give the beneficiary a formR185 (Trust Income). They have to do so if the beneficiary requests it.

TSEM3755  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3755)explains the position for discretionary income payments to company beneficiaries.

Sections TSEM3756 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3756)-TSEM3759 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3759) explain the position for discretionary income payments to individual beneficiaries.

TSEM3756 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3756) explains the position for discretionary income payments from trusts that are not settlor-interested.

TSEM3757  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3757)explains the position for discretionary income payments from settlor-interested trusts.

TSEM3759 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3759) explains when a discretionary payment is considered to be made.

TSEM3781 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3781)+ explains the position for capital payments from trusts.
