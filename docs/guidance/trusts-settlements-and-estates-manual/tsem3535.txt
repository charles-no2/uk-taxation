Trust income and gains: Sub-fund elections - interaction with a vulnerable beneficiary election
Identifier: TSEM3535
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3535
Published: 2016-05-02T16:36:16+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:49.367683+00:00

If a sub-fund election is made for a settlement to which a vulnerable person election already applies, the trustees of the sub-fund settlement are treated as though they had made the vulnerable person election even if they did not actually do so. The power to require information under FA05/S40 (TSEM3480 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3480)) still applies in respect of the trustees of the principal statement but only in relation to matters arising before the sub-fund election takes effect.
