Trusts for particular purposes: ESC A68 - the amount of the compensation - years to 2009-2010

The effect of this concession is to relieve partially the double charge described at TSEM5600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5600#), by paying to the trustees a sum in compensation. For any year, the amount payable to trustees is the lesser of

- tax at the trust rate on the total payments which are treated as employment income, and

- tax in the Section 497 tax pool.

To see how to calculate the relief, refer to

- TSEM5615 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5615#) for example with savings income

- TSEM5620 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5620#) for example with dividend income.
