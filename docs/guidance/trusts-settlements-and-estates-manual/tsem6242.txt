ITTOIA, Part 5, chapter 5

It might be possible to get tax advantages by making a trust. The Taxes Acts include provisions to stop this. Details are at TSEM4000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4000) onwards.

The anti-avoidance provisions apply to all settlements, other than those set up by a will. The word settlement has a very wide meaning. For tax purposes it includes

- dispositions

- trusts

- deeds of covenant

- agreements

- arrangements

- transfers of assets.
