Trust management expenses: IIP trusts: IIP beneficiaries: tax law: order of set-off

ITA/S503(2) provides the order of set-off for TMEs to reduce the income of an IIP beneficiary. The order of set-off of TMEs in an IIP beneficiary's tax calculation is the same as for accumulation/discretionary trustees (TSEM8250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8250)). But there is no grossing up of expenses as there is for accumulation/discretionary trustees.
