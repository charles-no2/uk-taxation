Ownership of property comprises

- legal ownership (TSEM9120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9120))

- beneficial ownership (TSEM9130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9130)).

The same person may be both legal owner and beneficial owner of the property, but there can be a separation of the two.
