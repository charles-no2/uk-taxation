Property held jointly by married couples or civil partners: Overview: two main rules

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9812
Published: 2016-05-02T17:01:47+01:00

There are two rules about property held jointly by married couples and civil partners:

- the ‘50/50 rule’ (ITA/S836) whereby most income from jointly held property is treated as split equally between the two spouses or civil partners for income tax purposes; the 50/50 rule applies unless there is a valid declaration on form 17; sections TSEM9814 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9814)-9840 contain the details;

- the ‘form 17 rule’, whereby, if the true income split is different from 50/50, the couple can opt to be taxed on that basis for income tax purposes (ITA/S837); sections TSEM9842 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9842)-9878 contain the details.
