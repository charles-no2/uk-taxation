Use this table if you need to convert the legislation in Part XVI ICTA 1988 into its Tax Law Rewrite equivalent in ITTOIA.

For all payments made before 6 April 2005, the relevant provisions are in Part XVI ICTA 1988.

For payments made after 6 April 2005, the provisions of Chapter 6 Part 5 ITTOIA apply. But where a company is the beneficiary of a deceased estate - see TSEM7474 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7474#).
