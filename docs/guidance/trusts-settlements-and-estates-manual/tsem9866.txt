Property held jointly by married couples or civil partners - Form 17 rule: death, separation and divorce or dissolution
Identifier: TSEM9866
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9866
Published: 2016-05-02T17:02:53+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:45:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:04.823730+00:00

After death, permanent separation or divorce or dissolution (1 to 3 at TSEM9864 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9864)) the income is split in the normal way; that is, the person who is beneficially entitled to the income is taxable on it.
