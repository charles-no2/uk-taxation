Settlements legislation: settlor - reciprocal arrangement: enquiry
Identifier: TSEM4130

Published: 2016-05-02T16:38:49+01:00
Updated: 2024-02-07T08:55:54+00:00

When HMRC open an enquiry into a trust or settlement, they need to consider whether there are any reciprocal arrangements - see TSEM4125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4125#).

HMRC will consider the original named settlor, any additional settlor and any other person with whom there may be a reciprocal arrangement. Internal users should send any case of doubt or difficulty to Trusts Technical for advice. see TSEM11100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem11100#)
