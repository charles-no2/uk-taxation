Non-resident trusts: beneficiary’s chargeability - trust income - interest in possession trust - UK law

A non-resident trust can be governed by a law of the UK.

If the trust is governed by the law of England and Wales, the tax position is as set out at TSEM3760 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3760)+.

If the trust is governed by the law of Scotland or of Northern. Ireland, advice should be sought from Trusts Technical - see TSEM11100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem11100)
