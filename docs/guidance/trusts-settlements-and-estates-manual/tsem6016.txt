There are two groups of chattels personal (TSEM6015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6015)) - choses-in possession and choses in action.

Choses in possession

Choses in possession are tangible property, transferred by assignment, or by manual delivery.
