Trustees are liable to capital gains tax. There are specific rules governing their capital gains tax liability. Full instructions are in the Capital Gains Manual. Help Sheet HS294 (http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/helpsheets/hs294.pdf) contains an introductory guide to the way in which UK resident trustees are dealt with for capital gains tax purposes.

Guidance about trustees

CG33000SUBC (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg33000subc)+ onwards provides a general outline of the tax position of trustees, beneficiaries and settlors.

CG33100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg33100)+ onwards has a summary of CGT provisions that relate to trusts.

Submissions about CGT

Internal users only, send CGT submissions to Capital Gains Technical Group unless the instructions indicate otherwise.
