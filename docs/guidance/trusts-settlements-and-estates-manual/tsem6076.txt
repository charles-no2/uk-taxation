Legal background to trusts and estates: personal representatives pay assets to trustees
Identifier: TSEM6076
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6076
Published: 2016-05-02T16:45:29+01:00
Updated: 2023-07-04T14:05:42+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:18.052071+00:00

Personal representatives may assent to assets going to trustees. Trustees and personal representatives may be the same individuals but acting in a different capacity. For capital gains tax purposes, they receive the assets as legatees (CG31140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31140)).

They cannot hold an asset simultaneously in both capacities. But they may hold some assets as personal representatives, at the same time as they hold other assets as trustees.
