Property held jointly by married couples or civil partners: The 50/50 rule: income in relation to which a declaration by the individuals under section 837 has effect
Identifier: TSEM9826
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9826
Published: 2016-05-02T17:02:04+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:45:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:01.388198+00:00

The 50/50 rule does not apply where the couple make a declaration of unequal beneficial interests under ITA/S837, using form 17.

For full details of the ‘form 17 rule’, see TSEM9842 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9842)-9878.
