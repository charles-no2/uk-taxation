Trust income and gains: the charge on trustees: the tax pool - general

When trustees of a discretionary trust pay income to beneficiaries they have to ensure that they have paid enough tax to cover the tax credit at the trust rate (see TSEM3756 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3756)). Trustees therefore need to keep a record of tax payments, known as the ‘tax pool’.

The ‘tax pool’ in an accumulation/discretionary trust at any point is

- the total of tax that has entered the tax pool - see TSEM3021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3021)

less

- the total amounts that have left the tax pool to cover credits on discretionary income payments to beneficiaries - see TSEM3022 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3022)

If the total of credits to beneficiaries in a tax year exceeds the amount of the tax pool available for the tax year, the trustees have to pay the excess tax - see TSEM3023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3023).
