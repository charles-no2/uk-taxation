Non-resident trusts: trustees’ chargeability: Income Tax - interest in possession trusts
Identifier: TSEM10260
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10260
Published: 2016-05-02T16:25:58+01:00
Updated: 2023-12-13T11:08:24+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:18.503221+00:00

The trustees of a non-resident IIP trust are chargeable at the basic rate on the trust’s UK-source income, without any deduction for trust management expenses.

The trustees are also taxable at the special trust rates on certain receipts that are deemed to be income under ITA07/Ss481-482 - see TSEM3201 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3201)
