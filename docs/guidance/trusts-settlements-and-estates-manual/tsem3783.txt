Trust income and gains: beneficiaries: payment from trust capital - exceptions to normal rule
Identifier: TSEM3783
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3783
Published: 2016-05-02T16:37:41+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:54.327644+00:00

There are certain circumstances in which payments from trust capital are treated as income of the beneficiary. They are:

- express gift of an annuity (TSEM3784 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3784))

- payments to supplement or augment an income interest (TSEM3785 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3785))

- compensation for loss of income (where beneficiary entitled to compensation) (TSEM3786 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3786))
