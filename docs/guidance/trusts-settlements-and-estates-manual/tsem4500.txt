Settlements legislation: settlor's right of recovery of tax
Identifier: TSEM4500
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4500
Published: 2016-05-02T16:39:35+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:11.970248+00:00

Sometimes the settlor can recover the additional tax that has been suffered under ITTOIA/S624 and 629. The settlor claims this from

- any trustees, or

- any other person to whom the income is payable.

The settlor may require a certificate from HMRC to support the claim. There are details at TSEM4505 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4505) onwards.

There is no right of recovery of tax under the provisions of ITTOIA/S633 and 641.
