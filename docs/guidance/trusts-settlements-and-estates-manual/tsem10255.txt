Non-resident trusts: trustees’ chargeability: Income Tax - tax pool

TSEM3020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3020) describes the ‘tax pool’ for UK resident trusts.

Tax paid by trustees while a trust is non-resident does not enter the tax pool. Tax goes into the pool only if it is tax paid at a time when the trustees are UK resident.

See TSEM3790 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3790) if income distributions are made to beneficiaries of non-resident trusts.
