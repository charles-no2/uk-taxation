Legal background to trusts and estates: Scottish law: restriction on liferent
Identifier: TSEM6541
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6541
Published: 2016-05-02T16:49:43+01:00
Updated: 2023-11-02T14:34:46+00:00

Scottish law does not allow a liferent for a person unborn at the date the trust is setup.

For example, a truster may leave his estate in liferent for his grandchildren, and then for his great grandchildren. This would become potentially invalid if a beneficiary who had not been born when the trust was set up became entitled to a liferent.

Internal users should refer any cases with an invalid liferent to Trusts Technical, see TSEM11100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem11100).
