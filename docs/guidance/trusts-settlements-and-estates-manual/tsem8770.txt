Trust management expenses: allowable expenses: specific items: travel and subsistence costs

Published: 2016-05-02T16:59:11+01:00

The incidence of travel and subsistence costs properly incurred by trustees depends on their purpose. The matter turns on whether those expenses were incurred exclusively to confer benefit on the income beneficiaries of the trust.
