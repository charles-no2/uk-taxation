Sections TSEM9921 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9921)- TSEM9929 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9929) give examples illustrating the principles about ownership and joint ownership of property described in TSEM9000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9000) to TSEM9800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9800) with respect to land and buildings.

These examples will not fit all scenarios so reference should be made to the general principles.
