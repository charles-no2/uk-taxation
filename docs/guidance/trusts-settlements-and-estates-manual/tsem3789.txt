If the trustees use capital to pay personal expenses of a beneficiary, the position depends on the circumstances. For details, see the examples at TSEM3781 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3781) and TSEM3785 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3785).
