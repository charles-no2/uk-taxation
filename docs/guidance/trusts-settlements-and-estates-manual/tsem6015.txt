Personal property consists of chattels real and chattels personal

Chattels real

Leasehold is an example of chattels real. Chattels real are transferred by assignment.

Chattels personal

There are two groups of chattels personal

- choses in possession

- choses in action.

TSEM6016 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6016) has details of choses in possession and choses in action.
