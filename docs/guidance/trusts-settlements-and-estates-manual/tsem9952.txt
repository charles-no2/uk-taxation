Ownership and income tax: Specific types of property: bank and building society accounts: example 11: joint names of married couple - 50/50 rule applies
Identifier: TSEM9952
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9952
Published: 2016-05-02T17:04:27+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:45:23+00:00

A and B, a married couple living together, open a bank account in their joint names. A claims that the interest should be taxed on B only, as B alone provided the funds for the bank account.

A resulting trust (TSEM9600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9600)) would otherwise apply, but ITA/S836 specifically operates to tax the interest 50/50 on A and B (TSEM9814 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9814)). Unless A and B submit a valid form 17 (TSEM9842 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9842)) in respect of the bank account, that is the position.
