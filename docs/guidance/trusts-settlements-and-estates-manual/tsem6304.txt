Legal background to trusts and estates: tenant for life

A tenant for life has a life interest (TSEM6203 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6203)) in the settled land. The vesting deed (TSEM6302 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6302)) shows any restrictions on the title.

The tenant for life of a trust between the living (inter-vivos) has various powers because he has legal title. For CGT purposes he may rank as a trustee. Details are at CG70500P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg70500p) onwards.
