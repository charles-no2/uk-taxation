The guidance in TSEM7602 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7602)-TSEM7656 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7656) explains the basic principles involved where a beneficiary has an absolute, limited or discretionary interest in an estate. This part of the guidance describes some of the practical aspects involved.
