Legal background to trusts & estates: personal  representatives act on verbal instructions
Identifier: TSEM6037
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6037
Published: 2016-05-02T16:44:28+01:00
Updated: 2023-07-13T06:47:33+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:14.244547+00:00

The personal representatives should follow what is actually written in the will. If there is no valid will, they should obey the rules of intestacy (TSEM6036). They should not normally act on verbal instructions they received before the death. Internal users should refer any cases to Trusts Technical where personal representatives intend acting on verbal instructions. See TSEM11100
