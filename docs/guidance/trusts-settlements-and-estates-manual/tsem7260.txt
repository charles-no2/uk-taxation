Deceased persons: income chargeable as property income or as savings and investment income
Identifier: TSEM7260
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7260
Published: 2016-05-02T16:51:06+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:34:24+01:00

Tax is chargeable on the property income or savings and investment income after making all adjustments resulting from death.
