These notes do not cover the transfer of assets abroad.

The appropriate legislation is in ITA/Part 13, Chapter 2.

The appropriate instructions are at INTM600000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/international-manual/intm600000) onwards.

If the transfer of assets legislation might apply to a case, follow the guidance set out at INTM604300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/international-manual/intm604300).
