Settlements legislation: more than one settlor
Identifier: TSEM4555
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem4555
Published: 2016-05-02T16:40:00+01:00
Updated: 2023-09-28T10:26:06+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:13.712493+00:00

There may be more than one settlor. Apply the provisions of the settlements legislation to the income derived from each settlor’s share, as if they were the only person who had made the settlement.
