Legal background to trusts and estates: express trust
Identifier: TSEM6262
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6262
Published: 2016-05-02T16:47:32+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:42:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:24.564097+00:00

An express trust has a clear, expressed intention to create a trust. The trust may be

- a will trust, or

- between the living (inter-vivos), or

- statutory.

Express trusts are distinguishable from

- implied or presumptive trusts (TSEM6263 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6263)), and

- constructive trusts (TSEM6271 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6271))
