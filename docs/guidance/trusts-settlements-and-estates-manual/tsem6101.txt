Legal background to trusts & estates: intestacy
Identifier: TSEM6101
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6101

If

- a deceased does not leave a will, or

- the will was not in a valid format (TSEM6034 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6034)).

the deceased died intestate, and there is an intestacy.

The deceased’s assets vest in the Public Trustee.

The laws of intestacy determine who will inherit the possessions, and how much each will get. The rules are set out at TSEM7800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem7800) onwards.
