Non-resident trusts: beneficiary’s chargeability - bare trust
Identifier: TSEM10410
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10410
Published: 2016-05-02T16:26:37+01:00

The position is the same as for UK resident bare trusts. The beneficiary is chargeable on trust income and gains as they arise. See TSEM1563 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1563) for details.
