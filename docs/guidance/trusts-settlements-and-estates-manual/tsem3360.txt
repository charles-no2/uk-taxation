TSEM3020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3020) explains which trusts are within ITA/S479. The tax that these trusts suffer on accrued income profits goes into the trust tax pool. It is available to frank income payments to beneficiaries.
