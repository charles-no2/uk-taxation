The period of administration starts at the date of death. It finishes when the personal representatives have discharged all their duties (TSEM6071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem6071)) and CG30700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg30700).

If there is a trust, BT & C Trusts will deal with the trust. (TSEM1420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1420))
