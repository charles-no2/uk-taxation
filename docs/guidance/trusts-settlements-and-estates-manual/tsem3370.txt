Trust income and gains: Accrued Income Scheme: trust receives UK interest gross
Identifier: TSEM3370
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3370
Published: 2016-05-02T16:33:58+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:29:57+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:42.054153+00:00

If the trustees are resident in the United Kingdom, they remain chargeable on accrued income charges.

As the interest is not chargeable to UK tax, accrued income allowances are not relevant to either the trustees or the beneficiaries.
