The wording of the certificate is:

Section 646(2) ITTOIA

Settlement dated [date] by [settlor’s name] [This will only be relevant where the settlement is a trust]

I certify that, for the year ended 5 April [year],

£[amount of income] was treated as the income of [settlor’s name].

It was charged on them by virtue of Section [660A or 660B] ICTA 1988 Section [624 or 629] ITTOIA.

They paid £[amount of tax] income tax in the year ended 5 April [year] in respect of this income.

HM Inspector of Taxes or Officer of Revenue and Customs
