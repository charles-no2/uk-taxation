Non-resident trusts: trustees’ chargeability: Income Tax - accumulation and discretionary trusts
Identifier: TSEM10235
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10235
Published: 2016-05-02T16:25:46+01:00
Updated: 2024-07-03T12:02:15+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:17.630448+00:00

If the trustees of a non-resident trust have power to:

- accumulate income or

- pay income at their discretion or

- accumulate income and pay income at their discretion

then the amount of UK-source income on which the trustees are chargeable to tax is charged at special rates under ITA07/S479.

The trustees are also taxable at the special trust rates on certain receipts that are deemed to be income under ITA07/Ss481-482 - see TSEM3201 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3201).

See TSEM3041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3041) for trust tax rates
