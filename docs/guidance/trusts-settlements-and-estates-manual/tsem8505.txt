Trust management expenses: settlor-interested trusts: general
Identifier: TSEM8505
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8505
Published: 2016-05-02T16:58:02+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:45:23+00:00

Income arising under a settlement where the settlor or the settlor's spouse or civil partner retains an interest is deemed to be the settlor's (ITTOIA/S624). This applies to both accumulation/discretionary trusts and IIP trusts.

Sections TSEM8510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8510) - TSEM8515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8515) explain the TMEs position in settlor-interested accumulation/discretionary trusts.

Section TSEM8520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8520) explains the TMEs position in settlor-interested IIP trusts.

See also TSEM8615 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem8615) about partly settlor-interested trusts.
