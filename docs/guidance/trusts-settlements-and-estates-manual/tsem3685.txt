Trust income and gains: relief for overseas tax: mixed trust
Identifier: TSEM3685
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3685
Published: 2016-05-02T16:36:44+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:30:09+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:50.765389+00:00

A mixed trust has both a discretionary interest and an interest in possession.

The trustees may claim double taxation relief in respect of overseas tax that qualifies for relief. The instructions at TSEM3655 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3655) apply to income that the beneficiary is entitled to receive. The instructions at TSEM3670 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3670) apply to the balance of the income.
