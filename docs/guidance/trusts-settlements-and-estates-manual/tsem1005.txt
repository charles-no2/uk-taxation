Ways of creating a trust include:

- inter-vivos (literally ‘between the living’)

- by Will or codicil - created following the death of the testator

- by court order - typically this is after a divorce, inheritance on death or compensation claim

- by operation of law - this could result from intestacy (someone dying without leaving a valid Will).

Trusts are usually created in writing, but they may also be created orally. See TSEM9510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem9510)+.
