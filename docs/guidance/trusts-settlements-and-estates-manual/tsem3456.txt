The election form requires the ‘effective date’ to be specified, that is the date from which the election is to have effect. See TSEM3451 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3451) for the time limits in which elections may be made.
