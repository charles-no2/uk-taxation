Family Law Reform Act 1969

The age of majority for a beneficiary under intestacy is 18, provided the intestate died on, or after, 1 January 1970. If, exceptionally, the intestate died before 1 January 1970, the age of majority is 21.

The rules are contained in the Family Law Reform Act 1969
