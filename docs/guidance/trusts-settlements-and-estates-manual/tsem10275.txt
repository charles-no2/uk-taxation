Non-resident trusts: trustees’ chargeability: Capital Gains Tax - trustees fail to pay exit charge
Identifier: TSEM10275
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem10275
Published: 2016-05-02T16:26:06+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-29T15:29:08+01:00

If the trustees do not pay the ‘exit charge’ within 6 months from the time it became payable, a former trustee may be required to pay the unpaid tax and interest (TCGA92/S82).
