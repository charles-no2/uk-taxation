BT & C Trusts deals with:

- all UK resident trusts

- non resident trusts TSEM1455 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1455) and foreign law trusts TSEM1460 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem1460)

- pension schemes that have lost HMRC approval.
