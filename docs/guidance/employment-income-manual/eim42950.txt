Action on receipt of a completed form P85 or P85(S)

Take the action referred to in the Residence and FIG Regime Manual (see RFIG30120  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig30120)onwards) according to the information supplied by the taxpayer on form P85 or form P85(S) (withdrawn from 30 November 2010).
