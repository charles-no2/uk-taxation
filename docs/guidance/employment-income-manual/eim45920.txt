Employment income provided through third parties: anti-forestalling rules: early step within section 554C(1)(a) or (d): exclusion for transactions under employee benefit packages

Schedule 2 paragraph 55 FA 2011

If a relevant third person (P) takes an early step within section 554C(1)(a) or (d) (see EIM45910 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45910) and EIM45915 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45915) respectively), it may be that one of the statutory exclusions will prevent the anti-forestalling rules from applying.

Section 554G (exclusions: employee benefit packages) applies with 2 modifications. The modifications are designed to ensure that the effect of the anti-forestalling provisions published on 9 December 2010 was not altered in a way that would disadvantage taxpayers.

This guidance discusses section 554G at EIM45215 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45215) onwards.

These are the 2 modifications.

|  Early step within section 554C(1)(a) or (d) |  Relevant step taken on or after 6 April 2011 |

Pension schemes |  A pension scheme can count as an employee benefit package. See EIM45215 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45215). |  A pension scheme cannot count as an employee benefit package. See EIM45215 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45215). |

Definition of ‘group of companies’ |  ‘Group of companies’ means acompany and any other companies of which it has control (as defined in section 995 ITA 2007). See EIM45220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45220). |  ‘Group of companies’ is defined as for corporation tax on chargeable gains except that in Section 554G the test is a 51% test rather than a 75% test. See EIM45220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45220). |
