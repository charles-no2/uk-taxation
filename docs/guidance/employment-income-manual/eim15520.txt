Value Added Tax: inter-relation with earnings and  expenses: VAT and pecuniary liability payments
Identifier: EIM15520
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15520
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-03T06:12:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:11:22.533701+00:00

If an employer meets the pecuniary liability of an employee (see EIM00580 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00580)) and that liability includes an element of VAT, the amount taxable as employment income should include the VAT whether or not the employer may later recover all or part of it by set-off or repayment from HMRC.
