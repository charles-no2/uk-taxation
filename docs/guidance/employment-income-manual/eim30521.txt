Employers who take out qualifying contracts of insurance (see EIM30513 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30513)) should be encouraged to apply for dispensations in respect of them.

See EIM30051  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30051)onwards for more detail on dispensations.
