Tax treatment of National Health Service employees: expenses deductions: laundering uniforms

Sections 336 and 367 ITEPA 2003

A flat rate expense deduction has been agreed for healthcare workers working in the NHS, private hospitals or nursing homes who:

- are required to wear a uniform as part of their duties, see EIM32475 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32475) and

- must meet the cost of laundry themselves.

The circumstances in which a deduction can be given are the same as those for the similar deduction for nurses, see EIM67220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim67220).

For the amount of the deduction that can be permitted, see EIM66795 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66795).
