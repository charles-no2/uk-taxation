Deductions from earnings: capital allowances: particular items of plant or machinery: pocket calculators
Identifier: EIM36800
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim36800
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2025-10-23T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:14:13.599121+00:00

Pocket calculators should normally be dealt with under EIM36510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim36510) as minor items for which a deduction may be due under Section 336 ITEPA 2003, if the expenditure meets the conditions of that Section.

As regards computers, see EIM36730 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim36730).
