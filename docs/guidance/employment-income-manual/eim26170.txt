Section 176(5) ITEPA 2003

There is an exemption for:

- loans made by a person in the ordinary course of a business that includes the lending of money and

- that are varied onto terms that are the same as the terms of loans held by members of the public (see EIM26171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26171)).
