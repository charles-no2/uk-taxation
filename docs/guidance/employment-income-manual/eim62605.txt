“1. Introduction

Paragraphs 2 to 5 below set out the basis on which FEDC [the Federation of Engineering Design Companies] advises its members to pay travelling and subsistence allowances. Paragraphs 6 to 9 contain guidelines agreed with the Inland Revenue on how these allowances should be treated for tax purposes.”
