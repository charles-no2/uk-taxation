Section 174(5)(a) ITEPA 2003

The exception explained in EIM26114  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26114)also applies to loans made by a person within the third bullet of EIM26113 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26113).

So it will apply where:

- a loan is made by an individual who has a material interest in a close company or in another company or a partnership which controls such a company and

- the loan is made to an employee whose employer is that close company or controls it or is controlled by it and

- the loan was made in the normal course of the lender’s domestic, family or personal relationships.
