Tax treatment of insurance agents: expenses: full- time agents
Identifier: EIM64640
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64640
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00

Subject to paragraph 33 of Statement of Practice SP4/97 (see EIM64605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64605)), you should consider the expenses of an employed insurance agent, in so far as they are not borne or reimbursed by the employer, in accordance with EIM31620 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31620) onwards (see also EIM64645 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64645)). But note that no deduction is admissible for bad debts incurred through keeping policies alive. (Agents are forbidden to advance premiums for this purpose beyond a stipulated period during which they are reimbursed by the company or society.)
