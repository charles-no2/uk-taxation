Airline pilots: industry wide FRE for 2013 to 2014 onwards: summary
Identifier: EIM50051
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50051
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-16T15:45:37+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:15:32.456330+00:00

Under the terms of the agreement between HMRC and BALPA, uniformed pilots, co-pilots and other uniformed flight deck crew may obtain the following expenses deductions for 2013 to 2014 onwards (for details of the FRE applicable from 2006 to 2007 - 2012 to 2013 please see EIM50050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50050)):

A FRE deduction of £1,022 per year (reduced, if necessary, for employer contributions - see EIM50059 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50059)) or their actual, vouched expenses in respect of allowable items in that year (see EIM50052 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50052))

plus

If the employer requires pilots to wear a uniform, but does not provide one, the cost of replacing items of uniform (see EIM50053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50053) and EIM32475 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32475))

plus

If the employer does not provide a noise-cancelling headset, capital allowances for the cost of a noise-cancelling headset (see EIM50054 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50054))

plus

A further expenses deduction (EXP) of £110 per year for travel to certain specified events or the actual, allowable expenses of travel to those events (see EIM50060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50060))

plus

The actual, allowable expenses of travelling to other temporary workplaces in the year (see EIM50060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50060))

See also EIM50045 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50045) (BALPA subscriptions), EIM50055 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50055) (luggage) and EIM50056 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50056) (computers).
