Section 150 ITEPA 2003

The car fuel benefit charge is incurred as detailed at EIM25510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25510).

The cash equivalent of the charge is reduced to nil for a particular tax year if either of the conditions in EIM25555 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25555) is met.

Otherwise, the cash equivalent:

- is calculated for a full tax year as shown at EIM25560 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25560)

- is then reduced proportionately if the car is unavailable for part of the year, see EIM25565 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25565) and

- can also be reduced if free fuel is withdrawn in a tax year and not reinstated, see EIM25570 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25570) and

- can also be correspondingly reduced if a reduction is made to the cash equivalent of the benefit of a car to an employee because the car is shared, see EIM25575 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25575).

Years prior to 2003/04

The fuel benefit charge had a different basis in these years.
