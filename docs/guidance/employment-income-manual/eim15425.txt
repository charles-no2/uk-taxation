Statement of Practice 13/1991

A lump sum otherwise chargeable under Section 394 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM15401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15401)) is not chargeable where the conditions in Statement of Practice 13/91 are satisfied (see EIM15429 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15429)). The Statement was withdrawn with effect from 6 April 2006 so applies only to lump sums paid before that date.
