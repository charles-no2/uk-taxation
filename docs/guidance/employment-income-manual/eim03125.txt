Section 284 ITEPA 2003

An employee starts a new job on 1 February 2004. TheIr employer makes them an interest-free bridging loan of £100,000 on 1 May 2004. The loan is repaid on 1 March 2005. TheIr other qualifying removal expenses and benefits are £7,500. The time limit (see EIM03104 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03104)) expires on 5 April 2005. The official rate on 1 May 2004 is 5%.

So in the formula in step 3 at EIM03124 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03124):

A = £500 (£8,000 - £7,500)

B = £100,000

C = 5%

(£500 × 365) ÷ (£100,000 × 5%) = 36.5

The result is rounded up to 37 and the loan is treated as having been made on 8 June 2004. The charge under Part 3 Chapter 7 ITEPA 2003 is then calculated taking into account all the normal rules (see EIM26101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26101) onwards).

If the loan is repaid before the end of the number of days calculated by using the formula there is no charge to tax under Part 3 Chapter 7.
