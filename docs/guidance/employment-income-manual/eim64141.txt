Tax treatment of Association footballers: travel expenses: travel in the performance of the duties
Identifier: EIM64141
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64141
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-06-17T11:38:35+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:10.277661+00:00

Relief under S337 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM32350 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32350) onwards) will not be due because, like most employees, a footballer who travels from home to a football ground or to a training venue is simply travelling to work. He is not travelling “in the performance of the duties”. Necessary travel between the club’s home ground and a training venue would qualify for relief under the “two places of work” principle (see EIM32360 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32360)).
