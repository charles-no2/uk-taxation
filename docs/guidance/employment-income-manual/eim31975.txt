Travel expenses: general: overseas conferences,  seminars and study tours: trips provided by third parties

Sections 337 to 339 ITEPA 2003

If the trip was provided by a third party it will not qualify for the third party entertainment exemption from tax if it was an incentive award, see EIM21837 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21837).

Where a trip provided by a third party is not an incentive award, no deduction should be given from the chargeable benefit unless the conditions set out in EIM31950  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31950)are met. The fact that a trip is paid for by a third party may in itself suggest the employee’s attendance was optional. See, however, EIM21835  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21835)onwards regarding third party entertainment.
