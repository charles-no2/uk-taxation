The benefits code: expenses payments of  employees with earnings taxable on remittance
Identifier: EIM20605
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20605
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-12-30T09:49:37+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:19:59.161777+00:00

Sections 22 and 26 ITEPA 2003

Where the earnings of a director or employee (except for 2015/16 and earlier one in an excluded employment (EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))), are chargeable on remittance, include expenses payments in the United Kingdom as chargeable earnings as well as those remitted to the UnitedKingdom.
