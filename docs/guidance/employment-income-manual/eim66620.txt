Tax treatment of mineworkers: children's allowances
Identifier: EIM66620
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66620
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:32.799957+00:00

An allowance (pension) payable by the Mineworkers Pension Scheme in respect of a child of a deceased contributor should be regarded as income of the child in his or her own right.
