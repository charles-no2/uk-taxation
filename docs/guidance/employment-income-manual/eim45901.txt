EIM45905 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45905) introduces the anti-forestalling rules.

EIM45910 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45910) and EIM45915 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45915) explain the anti-forestalling rules which apply if a step was taken within (what are now) Section 554C(1)(a) and (d) respectively.

Some of the statutory exclusions apply with modifications to steps taken during the anti-forestalling period. EIM45920 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45920) onwards explain these modifications.

EIM45935 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45935) onwards explain the ‘settlement credit’ rules in Schedule 2 paragraph 59 FA 2011.
