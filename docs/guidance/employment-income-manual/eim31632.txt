The general rule for employees expenses: relief is limited to expenses actually incurred: example
Identifier: EIM31632
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31632
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-30T10:14:57+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:13:06.186066+00:00

An employee receives from their employer a payment to meet the cost of hotel accommodation when they have to stay away overnight on business. However, instead of staying in a hotel, they sleeps in their car. They are taxable on the amount they receive from their employer and are not entitled to a deduction for overnight accommodation because they have not incurred any expense.
