Section 296 ITEPA 2003

The exemption for leave travel facilities provided for members of the Armed Forces applies to service personnel, except for 2015/16 and earlier those in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)).
