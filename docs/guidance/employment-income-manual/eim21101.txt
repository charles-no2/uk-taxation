Section 203(1) ITEPA 2003

The value of a benefit to be charged to tax is called its “cash equivalent” (section 203(1) ITEPA 2003).

The general rule for determining the amount of a cash equivalent is described at EIM21102 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21102). There are special rules in the case of certain benefits. They are listed at EIM21230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21230).
