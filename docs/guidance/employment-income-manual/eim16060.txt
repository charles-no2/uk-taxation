Section 84(3) ITEPA 2003

For a description of the points to consider in a case involving a voucher or a credit-token see EIM16030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16030).

A transport voucher does not have to be capable of exchange for goods or services. It is different from other non-cash vouchers in this respect. It is defined as any kind of ticket, pass, document or token intended to enable a person to obtain passenger transport services.

A separate definition for a transport voucher was required mainly to ensure that all season tickets provided for employees are within the scope of the special rules.

A transport voucher is concerned with travel alone. Where for example, a voucher enables an employee to obtain a holiday including both travel and accommodation, it will fall to be dealt with as an ordinary non-cash voucher and not as a transport voucher (see EIM16040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16040)).

Part 3 Chapter 4 ITEPA 2003 does not apply to transport vouchers provided for employees of certain passenger transport undertakings, see EIM16070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16070).

For the amount of earnings see EIM16140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16140).

As regards leave travel facilities for members of the Armed Forces see EIM50100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50100).
