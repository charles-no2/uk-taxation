Section 218(4) ITEPA 2003

This section only applies for 2015 to 2016 and earlier.

EIM20101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20101) explains how to calculate whether or not an employment has earnings of less than £8,500 a year.

Step 3 provides for the subtraction in the calculation of “authorised deductions” (section 218(4) ITEPA 2003). The deductions concerned are (references to ITEPA 2003, unless otherwise stated):

- capital allowances treated as deductions (section 262 CAA 2001), EIM36890 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim36890)

- employee liabilities and indemnity insurance (section 346), EIM30500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30500)

- expenses of entertainers (section 352), EIM62800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim62800)

- payments by non-domiciled employees (section 355)

- fixed sum deductions from earnings out of the public purse (section 368), EIM60720 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim60720)

- various deductions for foreign travel expenses (sections 370 to 376), EIM34000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim34000)

- personal security assets and services (section 377), EIM21810 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21810)

- contributions to exempt approved superannuation schemes or exempt statutory schemes (sections 592(7) and 594 ICTA 1988) and

- payroll giving to charities (section 713).

Deductions not included in the calculation

For the purpose of the calculation in section 218 no deduction can be given for:

- necessary expenses (sections 336), EIM31600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31600)

- expenses of travelling in the performance of the duties or to a temporary workplace (sections 337 and 338), EIM31810 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31810)

- professional membership fees (section 343), EIM32880 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32880)

- annual subscriptions (section 344), EIM32880  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32880)and

- clergymens’ expenses (section 351), EIM60040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim60040)
