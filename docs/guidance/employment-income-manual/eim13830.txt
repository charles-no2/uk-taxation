The following table shows the types of payment most frequently made under site agreements (see EIM13825 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13825)) and the appropriate tax treatment. See EIM13832  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13832)for the report to be made in certain cases.

Type of payment |  Tax treatment |

Completion bonus or time-saved payment: this is a sum paid for completing a contract before a specified date. \nPayment for completing a project on time. \nRetention payment: this is based on the number of hours worked. |  Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00515)). \nThe sum is a reward for services in the form of a contractually guaranteed terminal payment (see EIM13765 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13765)). |

Severance payment (excluding a statutory redundancy payment). \nUsually related to length of service and paid where an employment lasts less than 2 years. |  May satisfy the definition of payments for redundancy (see EIM13800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13800)) and so fall within section 401 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13775 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13775)). \nOtherwise, section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00515)). |

Payment in lieu of notice (PILON) |  See EIM12975 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12975) and EIM13874 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13874) |

Statutory redundancy payment made under Employment Rights Act 1996 or Northern Ireland equivalent (see EIM13760 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13760)) |  Section 401 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13760 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13760)) |
