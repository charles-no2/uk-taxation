Travel expenses: travel for necessary attendance:  fixed term appointments and agency workers: fixed term  appointments: example

A junior hospital doctor undergoes a five year training programme on a rotational basis. During this time he works at five different hospitals in succession, each for 12 months. As each hospital is managed by a separate NHS trust he has separate employment contracts with each hospital.

Each hospital is a permanent workplace because the doctor will work at that hospital for the whole of the period for which he holds that employment, see EIM32125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32125). It does not make any difference that the five employments are part of a continuing training programme.
