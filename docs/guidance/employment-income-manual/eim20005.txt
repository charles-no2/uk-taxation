Section 7(5) ITEPA 2003

Income which an individual receives from an office or employment is charged to tax as “employment income” (EIM00510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00510)). Employment income consists of:

- general earnings (EIM00511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00511)) and

- specific employment income (EIM00512 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00512)).

“General earnings” are usually the main part of a person’s employment income for tax purposes and these are:

- earnings within Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (EIM00515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00515)) and

- any other amount treated as earnings (Section 7(5) ITEPA2003).

Amounts treated as earnings include

- “the benefits code” (Section 63 ITEPA 2003) and

- other provisions (see EIM00513 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00513)).

The guidance that follows is concerned with the benefits code (EIM20006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20006)).
