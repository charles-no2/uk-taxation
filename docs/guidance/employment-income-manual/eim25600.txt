Section 152 ITEPA 2003

This example illustrates the principles at EIM25560 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25560).

Example

A car has an appropriate percentage of 20% and is made available to the same employee for the whole of 2003/04.

The fixed amount for 2003/04 is £14,400 (see EIM25580 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25580)).

The fuel benefit charge for 2003/04 is £2,880 (£14,400 x 20%).

There is a more complex example at EIM25615 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25615).

Years prior to 2003/04

The fuel benefit charge had a different basis in these years.
