Section 62 ITEPA 2003

Even if a lump sum payment is not made under a legal obligation, it is taxable as earnings within Section 62 ITEPA 2003 if it is customary within a particular employment for such payments to be made to a particular class of employee.

The case of Corbett v Duff (23TC763)(see example EIM00645 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00645)) illustrates this principle.

This principle will not usually apply where a director of a family company retires and receives a lump sum. Such payments need to be considered under EIM02100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim02100) and EIM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13000) onwards, see also EIM15000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15000) onwards.
