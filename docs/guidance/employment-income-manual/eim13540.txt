Section 404(2) to (3) ITEPA 2003

This guidance explains which employers are “associated”. EIM13530 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13530) explains that payments and benefits from associated employers must be aggregated before applying the £30,000 threshold.

Employers are associated if, on a “termination or change date” (as defined inthe last bullet below):

- Test A: one employer is under the control of the other – see example 1 at EIM13965 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13965), or

- Test B: one employer is under the control of a third person who controls, or is controlled by, the other employer – see example 2 at EIM13965 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13965), or

- Test C: on a different “termination or change date” one employer is under the control of a third person who controls, or is controlled by, the other employer – see example 3 at EIM13965

For this purpose:

- “control” means the ability to exercise control over the affairs of a company, etc whether by possessing the greater part of the issued share capital or voting power, or otherwise (see EIM20213 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20213))

- “person” (singular) is to be read as including “persons” (plural) – s6 Interpretation Act 1978 (so that, for example, Test B above is satisfied if the employers are under the control of the same persons)

- employer and person controlling or controlled by an employer include their successors

- “termination or change date” means the date of the termination or change giving rise to the payment or benefit being considered. For example, if you are looking at a redundancy payment made on 1 March 2004 in respect of an employment that ended on 1 January 2004, the “termination or change date” is 1 January 2004
