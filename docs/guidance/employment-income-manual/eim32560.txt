Other expenses: employment agency fees
Identifier: EIM32560
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32560
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T13:19:14+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:22:25.557623+00:00

Fees paid by an employee to an employment agency for obtaining a job are not deductible under Section 336 ITEPA 2003.

The leading case is Shortt v McIlgorm (26TC262). A deduction was refused for a payment made to an employment agency. The payment was not made in the performance of the duties of the employment, see EIM31650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31650).
