Section 281 ITEPA 2003

The guidance on travelling and subsistence within Section 281 covers the following items:

- travel and subsistence of the employee (see EIM03113 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03113))

- travel and subsistence of the employee’s family and household (see EIM03114 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03114))

- continuity of education for a child (see EIM03115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03115))

- international moves (see EIM03116 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03116))

- temporary living accommodation (see EIM03117 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03117))

- meaning of subsistence (see EIM03118 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03118))

- provided cars or vans (see EIM03119 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03119)).
