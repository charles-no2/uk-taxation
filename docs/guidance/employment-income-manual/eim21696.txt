Particular benefits: club membership fees
Identifier: EIM21696
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21696
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-12-30T12:50:57+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:24:27.674290+00:00

An employer may pay a club membership fee for directors or employees in circumstances where it is not chargeable by virtue of Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM01060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01060)).

The expense incurred will be a benefit chargeable on directors and employees, except for 2015/16 and earlier those in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)), under Part 3 Chapter 10 ITEPA 2003.

As regards allowable deductions for club subscriptions see EIM32500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32500).
