Sections 99 and 100 ITEPA 2003

There are four situations where Part 3 Chapter 5 ITEPA 2003 does not apply to provided living accommodation:

- see EIM11336 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11336) onwards for an explanation of particular jobs treated as having representative occupier status at 5 April 1977

- see EIM11341 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11341) onwards for an explanation of the necessary for the proper performance exemption

- see EIM11346 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11346) onwards for an explanation of the customary and better performance exemption

- see EIM11361 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11361) onwards for an explanation of the special security exemption.

Most directors do not qualify for the second and third of these exemptions (see EIM11366 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11366)).

If the living accommodation is not chargeable other exemptions and reliefs may be due (see EIM11332 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11332)).

These exemptions only apply where the accommodation is living accommodation. The exemptions do not apply to board and lodgings (see EIM11321 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11321)).
