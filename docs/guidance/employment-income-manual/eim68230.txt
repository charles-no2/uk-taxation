Tax treatment of Post Office employees: sub- postmasters: reduced remuneration in first year
Identifier: EIM68230
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68230
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:48.792548+00:00

Where a sub-postmaster is newly appointed the remuneration payable for the first year may be at a reduced level. The amount taxable as earnings is the amount actually received, that amount being the contractual earnings for the year.
