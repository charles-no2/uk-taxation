Sections 121 to 124 ITEPA 2003

Before reading the guidance that follows this paragraph, ensure that you are familiar with:

- the method statement in section 121(1) ITEPA 2003, see EIM24015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24015) (this page concerns step 1)

- the general introduction at EIM24050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24050)

Steps 1 to 4

The steps are those in the section 121(1) ITEPA 2003 method statement, see EIM24015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24015).

Step |  Stage |  Accessories |  Car with a list price |  Car with no list price |

Step 1 |  Stage 1 |  None |  Find the list price of the car, see EIM24105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24105) onwards |  Find the notional price of the car under section 124, see EIM24150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24150) |

Step 2 |  Stage 1 |  Initial extra accessories (type 1) |  Add these, whether or not they are available with the car in the tax year in question, see EIM24235 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24235) |  Nothing to add (these accessories are included in the notional price) |

Step 2 continued. Note: the price at this stage is normally fixed while the same employee has the car |  Stage 2 |  Initial extra accessories (type 2) |  Add these if they remain available with the car in the tax year in question, see EIM24240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24240) |  Nothing to add (these are also included in the notional price) |

Step 2 continued |  Stage 3 |  Initial extra accessories (type 3) |  Add these if they remain available with the car in the tax year in question, see EIM24245 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24245) |  Nothing to add (these are also included in the notional price) |

Note: the following steps apply to all cars, whether with a list price or a notional price

Step |  Stage |  Accessories |  All cars |

Step 2 continued |  Stage 4 |  Later accessories |  Add those available in the tax year in question that were not available with the car before 1 August 1993 and have a price of at least £100, Section 126(3) ITEPA 2003, see EIM24250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24250) |

Step 2 continued |  Stage 5 |  Replacement accessories |  Adjust as required at EIM24255 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24255) onwards |

Step 3 |  Stage 1 |  None |  Deduct allowable capital contributions made in the year in question or earlier, see EIM24350 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24350) onwards |

Step 4 |  Stage 1 |  None |  For years to 2010 to 2011 only, the figure carried forward from step 4 is capped at £80,000, see EIM24440 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24440) |

Types of initial extra accessory:

- type one: with a list price published by the manufacturer, distributor or importer of the car

- type two: with a list price published by the manufacturer, distributor or importer of the accessory

- type three: for which a notional price must be used (for example, when there is no list price)

Other points:

- see EIM24175 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24175) about cars manufactured to run on road fuel gas

- see EIM24180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24180) regarding the price of an automatic car for use by a disabled person from 2009 to 2010 onwards

- see EIM24275 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24275) regarding equipment for disabled persons

- see EIM24280 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24280) on the cost of converting a car to run on road fuel gas

- see EIM24400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24400) onwards for the calculation in respect of classic cars
