Section 318 ITEPA 2003 (amended by paragraph 1 Schedule 13 FA 2004)

The legislation at Section 318 ITEPA 2003 was replaced by a new Section 318 with effect from 6 April 2005. This renewed the unlimited exemption but included some significant amendments. The guidance at EIM21905 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21905) - EIM21950 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21950) explains the position before 6 April 2005.

- The guidance at EIM22001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22001) - EIM22009 covers the position from 6 April 2005.
