Travel expenses: travel for necessary attendance: safeguards against abuse: journeys treated as private travel: example
Identifier: EIM32322
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32322
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T13:19:14+01:00

An employee works in a dry cleaners in Carlisle. Her employer sends her to the Darlington branch to repair a machine that has been damaging clothes. While there she visits her ex- husband to discuss maintenance arrangements. She spends a longer time dealing with her private affairs than in fixing the machine.

Her main purpose in going to Darlington was to fix the machine. The discussion with her ex- husband was time consuming but was merely incidental to her business travel. So this is not substantially private travel, see EIM32320 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32320). She can deduct the cost of travel between her home and Darlington.
