Tax treatment of mineworkers: payments towards  cost of travel between home and permanent workplace
Identifier: EIM66610
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66610
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:32.433947+00:00

Mineworkers’ employers may make payments to employees towards the cost of travel between their homes and permanent workplaces under various agreements.

These sums are taxable as earnings from the employment and should be shown as remuneration on deductions working sheets when PAYE is operated.
