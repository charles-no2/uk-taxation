Particular benefits: Christmas presents in kind

The instruction relating to small Christmas presents in kind at EIM21715 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21715) should be regarded as applying to directors and employees, except for 2015/16 and earlier those in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)).
