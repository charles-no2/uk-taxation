Tax treatment of lorry drivers: subsistence allowances paid to lorry drivers: payments in excess of the approved amount
Identifier: EIM66115
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66115
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2017-03-03T10:07:55+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:25.551810+00:00

Sections 289A and 289B ITEPA 2003

An employer wishing to make subsistence payments in excess of either the benchmark scale rates or the agreed sleeper cab rate (see EIM66130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66130)) for nights away, without deducting tax may apply for an approval notice for use of an individual bespoke scale rate EIM30250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30250).

Expenses not paid or reimbursed using the benchmark scale rates or covered by an approval notice should be subjected to tax and NICs as earnings from the employment.
