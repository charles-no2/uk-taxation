Section 182 ITEPA 2003

If the appropriate official rate changes during the period when the loan is outstanding, the average official rate is calculated by reference to both:

- the number of days in the period

- the number of days for which each rate was in force

See the example at EIM26221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26221).
