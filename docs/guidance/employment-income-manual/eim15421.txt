Sections 398(2)-(6) and 399 ITEPA 2003

In addition to cash benefits in the form of a lump sum or pension, schemes may also provide non-cash benefits on or during retirement.

Non-cash benefits may take many forms. For example, the use of an asset may be provided, or a person may be given a loan at less than a commercial rate of interest.

The value of the benefit is the greater of:

- its money’s worth (see EIM00515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00515)), and

- its ‘cash equivalent’ under the benefits code (see EIM21006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21006))

in both cases making the assumption that the benefit is received for performance of the duties of the employment. This is the same process as for termination benefits (see EIM13270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13270) and EIM13310 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13310)).

Note that when applying the benefits code for this purpose:

- any reference to employer is to be taken as including a former employer

- the same difference applies when calculating the cash equivalent of certain living accommodation as applies for the purposes of Section 401 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13330 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13330)).

The cash equivalent of a beneficial loan is treated as a payment of interest (Section 399(2) ITEPA 2003). This means that it can be claimed as a relief. The rules are the same as in EIM26101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26101) onwards (except that Section 399 ITEPA 2003 applies to all employees). When the figure for notional interest has been calculated, the same rules as in EIM26270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26270) apply to give relief for it.

Where a non-approved scheme provides only for non-cash benefits, Section 394 ITEPA 2003 will not apply (see EIM15403 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15403)). This is because the Section can only apply to benefits that come from a scheme that includes relevant benefits (which meant cash only benefits before 6 April 2006).
