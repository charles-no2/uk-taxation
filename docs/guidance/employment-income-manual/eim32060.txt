Section 337 and 338 ITEPA 2003

An employee cannot turn what is really an ordinary commuting journey into a business journey simply by arranging a business appointment along the way. To get a deduction the employee must be able to show that the attendance at the particular place on that occasion was necessary, in the sense used in EIM31645 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31645), for the performance of the duties of that employment and was not just a matter of personal convenience.

Similarly, an employer cannot turn an ordinary commuting journey into a business journey by requiring the employee to stop off on the way to carry out business tasks such as making telephone calls, see EIM32270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32270).
