Section 63(1) ITEPA 2003

A benefit is not chargeable under the benefits code (EIM20006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20006)) if it is already fully chargeable to Income Tax under some other provision. For example:

- benefits capable of being turned into money charged (the “money’s worth” principle) under section 62 ITEPA 2003, see EIM00540 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00540)

- benefits obtained by discharge of an employee’s debt (the “pecuniary liability” principle) under section 62, see EIM00580 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00580).
