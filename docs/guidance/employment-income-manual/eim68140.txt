Tax treatment of members of the police service: awards for meritorious conduct
Identifier: EIM68140
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68140
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:44.326434+00:00

Police officers may receive monetary awards for meritorious conduct involving acts of gallantry over and above the normal call of duty. These awards are not regarded as chargeable to tax and PAYE should not be applied.
