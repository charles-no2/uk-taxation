Particular benefits: incentive scheme awards
Identifier: EIM21730
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21730
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-12-30T13:53:57+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:24:37.541020+00:00

Non-cash awards under incentive award schemes are chargeable on directors and employees (except for 2015/16 and earlier those in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))), on the full cost to the provider, or, if the amount is greater, their second-hand value if they can be converted into money (see EIM21005 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21005)).
