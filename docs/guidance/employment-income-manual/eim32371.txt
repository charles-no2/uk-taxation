Section 337 ITEPA 2003

The Courts have considered employees’ home to work travelling expenses on a number of occasions. Recent examples include Miners v Atkinson (68TC629), which concerned a director of a one-man service company, see EIM32380 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32380), and Kirkwood v Evans (74TC481), which concerned an employee who agreed to be based at home under a homeworking scheme, see EIM32374 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32374).

In two cases the House of Lords has accepted that in certain exceptional circumstances an employee’s home to work travelling expenses qualified for relief under Section 337. The cases are:

- Taylor v Provan (49TC579), see EIM32372 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32372) and

- Pook v Owen (45TC571), see EIM32373 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32373).
