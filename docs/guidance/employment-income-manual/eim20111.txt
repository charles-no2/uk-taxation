The benefits code: employee with employment for only part of a year
Identifier: EIM20111
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20111
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:24:43+00:00

This section only applies to 2015/16 and earlier.

When working out whether an employment has earnings of less than £8,500 a year (EIM20101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20101)):

- if an employment, other than as a director, begins or ends during the year, convert the earnings (including any amount treated as earnings under the benefits code (EIM20006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20006))) for the part year to an annual rate in order to decide whether or not it is an excluded employment (EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))

- for example, if an employee commenced work on 6 January and earned £2,500 by 5 April, the earnings at an annual rate are £10,000 (4 x £2,500). Hence this is not an excluded employment and the benefits code applies to the employment in full (EIM20006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20006)).
