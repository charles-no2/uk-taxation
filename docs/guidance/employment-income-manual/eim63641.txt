Tax treatment of fire officers: motor mileage allowances
Identifier: EIM63641
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim63641
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:04.081919+00:00

Motor mileage allowances are paid to fire officers who use their own cars for business journeys.

There are no special arrangements for these payments after 2001/02. They are dealt within the normal way under the approved mileage allowance payments (AMAPs) scheme, see EIM31200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31200) onwards.
