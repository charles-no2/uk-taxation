Car and van benefit: pooled cars and vans: inadequate parking facilities
Identifier: EIM23485
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23485
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2017-08-23T14:56:48+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:20:27.730874+00:00

You will sometimes see it argued that a car or van that would otherwise qualify as a pooled car or van is only taken home at night by an employee because:

- there are inadequate car parking facilities at the employer’s premises, or

- if the car or van were left overnight at the employer’s premises it might well be damaged by vandals, etc.

Apart from the exception at EIM23480 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23480) for chauffeurs, do not admit such a claim or offer any concession. A vehicle must satisfy all five of the tests set out in EIM23450 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23450) to qualify as a pooled car or van.
