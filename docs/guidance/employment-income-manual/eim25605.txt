Section 152 ITEPA 2003

This example illustrates the principles at EIM25565 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25565).

Example

The car in EIM25600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25600) is not made available to the employee until 1 July 2003. It is therefore not available from 6 April to 30 June 2003 inclusive (86 days out of 366; note that tax year 2003 to 2004 is a leap year).

Using the formula at EIM25565 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25565), the reduced benefit is (£2,880 × (366 - 86)) ÷ 366 = £2,203.

Tax years prior to 2003 to 2004
