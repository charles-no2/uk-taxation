Employer-financed retirement benefits schemes: receipts excluded from charge: general
Identifier: EIM15121
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15121
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T11:31:27+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:11:00.347915+00:00

A receipt from an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme which is otherwise chargeable under Section 394 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM15015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15015) and EIM15120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15120)) may nevertheless be excluded from charge.

For further information, see the general comments at EIM15015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15015), the guidance for prior employer contributions at EIM15125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15125) and EIM15128 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15128): and for prior employee contributions at EIM15126 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15126)

For examples see EIM15329 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15329).
