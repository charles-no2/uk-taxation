Employment income: earnings from employment: sums paid to  an employee for not resigning, or for continuing to serve in the employment

Section 62 ITEPA 2003

A sum paid by an employer, or by a third party, to encourage an employee to continue in the employment is taxable as earnings within Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00510)). Such payments may be made where, for example, a director wishes to retire and is paid a lump sum for continuing in office, perhaps with reduced hours or duties.

Cases illustrating this point are:

- Prendergast v Cameron (23TC122) (see example EIM00655 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00655))

- Wales v Tilley (25TC136)

- Wilson v Daniels (25TC473)
