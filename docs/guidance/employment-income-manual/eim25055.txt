This page illustrates steps 5 and 6 of the method statement in Section 121(1) ITEPA 2003, see EIM24015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24015) and the example shows how to do the calculations explained at EIM24855 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24855).

Example 1: Diesel car with a low approved CO2 emissions figure

The price of the car and accessories (the figure at step 3) is £15,000. The approved figure of CO2 emissions is 119g/km.

First, check whether the car is either:

- for 2011 to 2012 only, a QUALEC (EIM24605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24605))

- for 2012 to 2013 and 2014 to 2015, a car with CO2 emissions between 1-75 g/km (EIM24610 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24610))

If it is not, round the CO2 emissions figure down to 115 g/km, then look up the appropriate percentage for a type A car at in the ready reckoner at EIM24705 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24705).

Finally, make any required adjustments for this type of car (see EIM24730 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24730)).

2011 to 2012

The car is a QUALEC: the unrounded CO2 emissions figure of 119 exceeds 75 but does not exceed 120, EIM24605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24605).

The appropriate percentage (the figure at step 5) is 10% (EIM24605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24605)). Add the 3% diesel supplement (EIM24730 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24730)). The basic car benefit charge (the figure at step 6) is £15,000 × 13% = £1,950.

Example 2: Diesel car with a high approved CO2 emissions figure

The price of the car and accessories (the figure at step 3) is £20,000. The approved figure of CO2 emissions is 217g/km.

First, check whether the car is either:

- for 2011 to 2012 only, a QUALEC (EIM24605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24605))

- for 2012 to 2013 to 2014 to 2015, a car with CO2 emissions between 1-75 g/km (EIM24610 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24610))

If it is not, round the CO2 emissions figure down to 215g/km, then look up the appropriate percentage for a type A car in the ready reckoner at EIM24705 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24705).

Finally, make any required adjustments for this type of car (see EIM24730 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24730)).

2011 to 2012

The car is not a QUALEC, so the appropriate percentage from the ready reckoner is 33%. Add 3% diesel supplement, subject to a maximum at step 5 of 35%. The appropriate percentage (the figure at step 5) is therefore 33% + 2% (restricted) = 35%. The basic car benefit charge (the figure at step 6) is £20,000 × 35% = £7,000.
