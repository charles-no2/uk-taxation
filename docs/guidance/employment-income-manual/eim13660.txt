Termination payments and benefits: Section 401 ITEPA 2003:  exceptions: lump sums from certain pension schemes
Identifier: EIM13660
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13660
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-10-17T13:44:16+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:10:17.931408+00:00

Section 407 ITEPA 2003 excepts, for the purposes of Section 401 ITEPA 2003, a lump sum or other benefit from a tax-exempt pension scheme if:

- the lump sum or other benefit is paid in compensation for loss of employment or loss or diminution of earnings and the loss or diminution is due to ill-health, or

- the lump sum or other benefit is properly regarded as earned by past service (essentially it is part of the individual’s retirement benefits)

Note

- This guidance applies only for tax exempt pension schemes (see below) A lump sum or other benefit from any other pension scheme should be considered under Section 394 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM15010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15010) and subsequent guidance).

- This guidance only applies to lump sums or other (non-cash) benefits. A pension is taxable as pension (see EIM75010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim75010))

Definition of tax-exempt pension scheme (Section 407(2) ITEPA 2003)

For this purpose tax exempt pension scheme means a retirement benefits scheme (see EIM15020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15020)) which is either a registered pension scheme (or before 6 April 2006 an approved scheme or a relevant statutory scheme, that is a scheme for which the particulars are set out in statute or regulations or which has been approved by a Minister or Government Department - examples are the Civil Service and Local Authority Schemes), or a scheme set up by a non-UK government primarily for its employees, or
a scheme described in Section 221 or (2) ICTA 1970 (these are now uncommon).
