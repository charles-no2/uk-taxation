Section 205 ITEPA 2003

There are special rules for calculating the chargeable benefit where an asset other than a motor car or living accommodation is hired or rented. The instructions are at EIM21630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21630) to EIM21632.

Note that the rent or hire charge is only used in the calculation under EIM21630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21630) if it is greater than the “annual value of the use of the asset” (see EIM21632 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21632)).

As regards:

- motor cars, see EIM24000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim24000) onwards

- living accommodation, see EIM11301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11301) onwards.
