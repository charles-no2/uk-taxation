Employment income: transfer of real property to employees: alternative charges to tax
Identifier: EIM08005
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim08005
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-04-06T12:12:45+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:19:36.774410+00:00

Where on a transfer of property at undervalue liability is not established under Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM08001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim08001)), it may arise under other legislation:

- where the taxpayer is a member of the company providing the asset – as Savings and Investment Income (see CTM15250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/company-taxation-manual/ctm15250)onwards)

- where the employment concerned is not an excluded employment - under Part 3 Chapter 10 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM21640  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21640)onwards)

- where the transfer is part of an unapproved retirement benefits scheme - under Part 6 Chapter 1 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM15010  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15010)onwards)

- where the transfer is otherwise in connection with the termination of an office or employment - under Part 6 Chapter 3 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13000  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13000)onwards)

- where, exceptionally, a voucher or credit token is involved - under Part 3 Chapter 4 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM16010  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16010)onwards)
