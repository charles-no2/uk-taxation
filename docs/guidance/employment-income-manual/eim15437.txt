Section 386 ITEPA 2003

Note: Section 386 ITEPA 2003 is repealed with effect from 6 April 2006 (see first bullet point of the final paragraph at EIM15400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15400))

Example 1

On 1 January 2006, a company pays £100,000 into a trust set up to provide retirement benefits for Mrs A, an employee. The scheme is not approved (see EIM15407 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15407)).

The PAYE Regulations do not apply (see EIM15433 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15433)) and the trustees receive £100,000. The payment is entered on form P11D (see EIM15431 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15431)).

£100,000 is included in Mrs A’s self assessment computation for 2005/06 (see EIM15411 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15411)) as employment income.

Example 2

On 1 January 2006, a company pays £100,000 into a trust set up to provide retirement benefits for all its 500 employees. The scheme is not approved (see EIM15407 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15407)).

Each employee includes £200 in his or her self assessment computation for 2005/06 (see EIM15411 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15411)) as employment income.

See EIM15412 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15412) for background.
