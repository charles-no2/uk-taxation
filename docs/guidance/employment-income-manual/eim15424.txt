Non-approved schemes: receipts excluded from charge: prior employee contributions
Identifier: EIM15424
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15424
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T11:31:27+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:11:15.766334+00:00

A lump sum otherwise chargeable under Section 394 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM15401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15401)) is not chargeable, or only partly chargeable, if it is attributable wholly, or partly, to the employee’s own contributions to the scheme (see EIM15414 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15414) and example EIM15438 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15438)).
