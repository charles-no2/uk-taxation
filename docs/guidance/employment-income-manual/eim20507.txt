The benefits code: expenses and benefits from non- resident employers
Identifier: EIM20507
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20507
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T11:31:27+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:19:56.543073+00:00

The residence of the employer has no effect on whether benefits and expenses within the rules in the benefits code are chargeable.

The residence of any other person providing benefits or expenses payments within those rules similarly has no effect.
