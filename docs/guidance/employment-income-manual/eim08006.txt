Employment income: transfer of real property to  employees: capital gains tax liability and Trading Income  deduction for the employer
Identifier: EIM08006
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim08006
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2021-01-19T14:06:01+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:19:37.136977+00:00

If an employment income liability is established on the transfer of an asset (see EIM08001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim08001)), the capital gains tax liability of the employer should be dealt with in accordance with CG16270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg16270) onwards. A claim for a Trading Income deduction should be dealt with in accordance with BIM47110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47110).
