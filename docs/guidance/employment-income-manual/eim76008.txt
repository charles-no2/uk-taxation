Social security benefits: benefit paid on behalf of EU countries
Identifier: EIM76008

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2019-04-08T13:49:44+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:17:39.082812+00:00

The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) can pay social security benefits on behalf of other EU countries.

These benefits may be chargeable to tax as a foreign benefit (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76009).
