Tax treatment of Parish and Community Council Clerks in England and Wales: payments on termination of office

A Clerk giving up office may receive a terminal gratuity. When deciding how the payment should be taxed, the advice at EIM65870 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim65870) should be noted.
