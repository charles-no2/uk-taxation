Particular benefits: assets transferred to a director or employee: assets transferred before they have been used or depreciated

Sections 203(2) ITEPA 2003

If a person transfers an asset to a director or employee or a member of his family or household by reason of his employment before the asset has depreciated or been used the amount chargeable is:

- the greater of

- the expense incurred by that person in connection with the provision of the asset less any amount made good (see EIM21120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21120)) or

- the second-hand value of the asset in the hands of the employee if it is chargeable under Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00540 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00540)) less the amount the employee has to pay for it

This rule also applies to the transfer of any unused trading stock of the employer.

The examples at EIM21647 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21647) and EIM21648 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21648) illustrate how the rules work.
