The general rule for employees expenses: in the performance of the duties: example
Identifier: EIM31653
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31653
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-09T11:00:38+01:00

An employee is a salesman for a company with a major export trade to France. He knows that he will not be able to perform his duties properly unless he improves his command of the French language. He enrols in a night school course for one evening each week. He asks for a deduction for the cost of the course.

No deduction is due. The employee’s duties as a salesman do not extend to learning French at night school, see EIM31650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31650).
