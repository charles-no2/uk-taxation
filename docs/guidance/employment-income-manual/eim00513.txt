Section 7(5) ITEPA 2003

The following tables list the things that are treated as earnings, and which therefore count as general earnings for tax purposes (see EIM00511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00511)). There are two tables.

Table 1 - The benefits code - note that for the tax year 2015 to2016 and earlier some of the provisions listed in the benefits code do not apply to people in lower paid employment. Those provisions are identified in the table. EIM20101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20101) tells you how to work out if a person is in lower paid employment.

Table 2 - Other provisions - apart from section 223 ITEPA 2003, which is a director-only provision, these provisions all apply to all employees and office holders, whatever their rate of pay.
