Tax treatment of mineworkers: compensation on retirement
Identifier: EIM66600
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66600
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-10-17T13:53:26+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:31.691845+00:00

Mineworkers are subject to a normal retirement age of 62. Such a worker may opt to join the Mineworkers’ Pension Scheme.

Benefits under this scheme are a lump sum payment and a weekly pension.

The lump sum payment should not be regarded as chargeable to tax. However the pension is taxable (see EIM75010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim75010)).

This scheme (and the British Coal Staff Superannuation Scheme) stopped accepting new members from December 1994.
