Section 115(1) ITEPA 2003

A car is a mechanically propelled road vehicle that is not:

- a goods vehicle (a vehicle of a construction primarily suited for the conveyance of goods or burden of any description):

- this will include most commercial vehicles such as lorries, pick-up trucks or vans. For vehicles that have been permanently and substantially modified to change their construction to one primarily suited for the conveyance of goods or burden (see EIM23115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23115) onwards). There is guidance on multi-purpose vehicles at EIM23145 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23145) and double cab pickups at EIM23150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23150).

- note that the test is of construction, not use.

- a vehicle that escapes from being a car for car benefit purposes by this route will be treated as a van (see EIM22700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22700), unless it is large enough to be a heavy goods vehicle (see EIM22300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22300) onwards).

- a vehicle of a type not commonly used as a private vehicle and unsuitable to be so used:

- this will obviously apply to a specialised vehicle, such as a bus. But it may also include outwardly conventional vehicles that have been modified in some way. Further guidance on this point is at EIM23125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23125) onwards.

- the benefit of the use of a vehicle that escapes from being a car for car benefit purposes by this route will be taxed under the general benefit rules on use of an asset (see EIM21615 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21615) onwards):

- a motor cycle as defined in Section 185(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988

- an invalid carriage as defined in Section 185(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988.

EIM23110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23110) is designed to be issued to taxpayers wanting guidance on the difference between cars and vans for tax purposes.
