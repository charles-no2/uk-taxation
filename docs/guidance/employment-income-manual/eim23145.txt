Car benefit: off road and multi-purpose vehicles

Section 115(1) ITEPA 2003

Luxury off road vehicles, four wheel drive recreational vehicles and multi-purpose vehicles will usually count as cars. This is because in most cases:

- they are clearly not designed or constructed primarily for carrying goods or burden, so do not come within the exception outlined at EIM23115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23115) onwards and

- there is nothing about them that renders them unsuitable for private use; indeed, they are very commonly used as private vehicles, in urban as well as in rural locations. So they do not come within the exception outlined at EIM23125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23125) onwards.

Some, however, are produced as commercial variants and, depending on the particular specifications, may come within the exception outlined at EIM23115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23115) onwards.

For guidance on double cab pickups please see EIM23150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23150).

In case of difficulty see EIM23160 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23160) and the summary of whether a vehicle is a car or a van at EIM23110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23110).
