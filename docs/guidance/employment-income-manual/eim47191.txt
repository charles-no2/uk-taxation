Finance (No 2) Act 2017: part 3A: 2019 loan charge reporting requirement: para 35C: requirement for employee or personal representative to supply loan charge information to HMRC
Identifier: EIM47191
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim47191
Published: 2018-10-05T14:23:34+01:00
Updated: 2022-04-05T09:51:39+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:25:36.300131+00:00

The employee or their personal representatives must supply HMRC with the loan charge information, where applicable, after 5 April 2019 but before 1 October 2020. The loan charge information must be supplied in such form or manner as may be specified by or on behalf of the Commissioners for HMRC.
