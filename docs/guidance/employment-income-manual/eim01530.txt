Section 62 ITEPA 2003

A cash allowance for meals taken at the normal workplace is taxable as “earnings” within section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00510) onwards, and Sanderson v Durbridge (36TC239)).

Where the cost of a meal is part of the expenses incurred in travelling, see EIM31815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31815).
