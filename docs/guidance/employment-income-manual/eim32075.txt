Section 339(3) ITEPA 2003

A workplace is a temporary workplace if an employee goes there only to perform a task of limited duration or for a temporary purpose. So even where an employee attends a workplace regularly, it will be a temporary workplace and so not a permanent workplace, if the employee attends for the purpose of performing a task of limited duration or other temporary purpose.

Limited duration is explained at EIM32080 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32080).

Temporary purpose is explained at EIM32150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32150).

If a workplace is capable of being a temporary workplace by reference to this rule, you must consider the following additional rules:

- the 24 month rule, see EIM32080 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32080)

- the fixed term appointment rule, see EIM32125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32125)

- the depots and bases rule, see EIM32160 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32160)

- the area rule, see EIM32190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32190)

These rules can only apply if the workplace is capable of being a temporary workplace by reference to S339(3).
