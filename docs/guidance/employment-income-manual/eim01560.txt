Section 62 ITEPA 2003

Where an employer contributes to the purchase of National Savings Certificates for employees, the amount or value of the contribution is taxable as earnings within section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see generally EIM00520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00520) onwards).
