Section 281 ITEPA 2003

The travelling and subsistence benefits and expenses of the employee’s family or household (see EIM20504 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20504)) that are within Section 281 are for:

- preliminary visits to the new location

- travelling from the old home to the new home when the move takes place.
