Holiday pay

A holiday pay scheme is in operation in the electrical contracting industry.

Travelling allowances

Under the national working rules of the Joint Industrial Board for the Electrical Contracting Industry, employees are entitled to payments in respect of daily travel and lodging, etc., expenses.
