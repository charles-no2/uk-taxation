Sections 336 and 351 ITEPA 2003

For an overview of the taxation of ministers of religion see EIM60001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim60001).

This page gives an overview of employment expenses that ministers can deduct.

Ministers have their own special legislation dealing with deductions for expenses in section 351 ITEPA 2003. Part of that is very similar in wording to section 336 ITEPA 2003. So the guidance on what is allowable under section 336 should be used in deciding if a deduction is allowable, see EIM31620  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31620)onwards.

Section 351 allows some additional deductions beyond section 336. For details of what that means in practice see EIM60044  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim60044)onwards.

Section 360B ITEPA 2003

The removal of the deduction for non-reimbursed household expenses from 6 April 2026 also applies to Ministers of religion. This means that a deduction for additional household expenses will not be allowable under section 351 ITEPA 2003 or section 336 ITEPA 2003, see EIM32759 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32759).
