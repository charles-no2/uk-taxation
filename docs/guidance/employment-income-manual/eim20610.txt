Section 72(2) and (3) ITEPA 2003

A director or employee, (except for 2015 to 2016 and earlier one in an excluded employment (EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))), can obtain a deduction in respect of any expenses payments received which satisfy the conditions of the following (all references to ITEPA 2003):

- necessary expenses in section 336 (EIM31620  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31620)onwards)

- various travel expenses in sections 337 to 342 (EIM31800  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31800)onwards)

- professional fees and annual subscriptions in sections 343 and 344 (EIM32880 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32880))

- employee liabilities and indemnity insurance in section 346 (EIM30500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30500))

- the expenses of ministers of religion in section 351 (EIM60040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim60040)) and

- earnings charged on remittance in section 353 (EIM40301  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim40301)onwards)

See example EIM20607 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20607).
