Car and car fuel benefits: class 1A National Insurance contributions payable by employers
Identifier: EIM23055
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23055
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2021-01-19T14:20:11+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:20:13.985680+00:00

Employers are liable to pay Class 1A National Insurance contributions on most taxable benefits. This includes car and fuel benefits.

Full details are in the National Insurance Manual, starting with an index to Class 1A NICs at NIM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13000co).
