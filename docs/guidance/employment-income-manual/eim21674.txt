Section 89 ITEPA 2003

The exemption in Section 89 ITEPA 2003 covering luncheon vouchers (see EIM16210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16210)) applies to all employees whether or not they are directors, or for 2015/16 and earlier within an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)).
