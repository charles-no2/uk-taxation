Section 393A ITEPA 2003

A retirement benefits scheme will be employer-financed (see EIM15020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15020)) unless it is registered by Pension Schemes Services (PSS) or it is a section 615(3) scheme i.e. a superannuation scheme to which Section 615(3) Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 applies.
