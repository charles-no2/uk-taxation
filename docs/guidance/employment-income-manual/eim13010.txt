Section 401(1) ITEPA 2003

For a payment or other benefit to be chargeable under section 401 ITEPA 2003 it must be received (as defined in EIM13110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13110)) in connection with (as defined in EIM13012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13012)):

- the termination of an office or employment, or

- a change in the duties or earnings from an office or employment (see EIM00680 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00680))

and

- not be chargeable under any other section (see EIM12810 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12810))

The scope includes a payment made for giving up rights in a redundancy scheme even though no termination occurs when it is paid (as confirmed by the Upper Tax Tribunal in the case of HMRC v Colquhoun).

A commutation is within the scope (see EIM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13000)).

The section includes payments and benefits made in many circumstances, see EIM13020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13020) for details.

See EIM13030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13030) for details of benefits chargeable under section 401 ITEPA 2003.

The most common section 401 ITEPA 2003 payments and benefits are set out in the table at EIM13005 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13005).

With effect from 6 April 2018, an element of all payments made on termination of an office, or employment are chargeable as general earnings rather than under section 401 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13874 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13874)).
