Part 3 Chapters 1, 5 and 10 ITEPA 2003

In any case where accommodation is provided by reason of employment, you need to answer the following questions to get the correct category of general earnings:

- does it count as general earnings (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00511) within section 62 ITEPA 2003?

- is the accommodation (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11321) living accommodation or is it other accommodation, such as board and lodging?

- if the accommodation is other accommodation (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11322) what is the earnings category?

- if the accommodation is living accommodation is it excluded from earnings (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11331)?

- if the accommodation is not excluded living accommodation (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11401) what is the cash equivalent of the benefit?

- have any accommodation services (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11521) been provided, such as light and heat?

We look at each of these questions in more detail in subsequent pages.
