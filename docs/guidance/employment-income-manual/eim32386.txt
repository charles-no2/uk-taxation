Section 337 ITEPA 2003

No deduction is due for the cost of travel between an employee’s home and his or her permanent workplace even where the employee is on stand-by, see EIM32385 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32385). In the same way, no deduction is due for the cost of travelling on an emergency call-out between an employee’s home and his or her permanent workplace.

There is one exception. The cost of emergency call-out travel between an employee’s home and his or her permanent workplace is deductible where the employee’s home is a place of work, see EIM32760 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32760) and all of the following conditions are met:

- the employee must give advice on handling the emergency before starting the journey and

- responsibility for those aspects appropriate to the employee’s duties must be accepted from that time and

- the employee must have a continuing responsibility for the emergency while travelling to the workplace.
