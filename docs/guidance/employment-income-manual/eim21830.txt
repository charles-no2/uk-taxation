For the system that employers who wish to make “free of tax” payments to their employees can adopt see EIM07700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim07700).

For guidance where PAYE tax is not deducted from certain directors’ earnings see EIM21790 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21790) onwards.

See EIM11800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11800) onwards where the failure to deduct tax is in respect of a readily convertible asset.
