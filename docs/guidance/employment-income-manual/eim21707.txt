Section 250 ITEPA 2003

The exemption for further education and training costs provided by Section 250 ITEPA 2003 can apply to benefits provided to directors and employees (see EIM20100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20100)), except for 2015/16 and earlier those in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)).
