Section 80(2) CAA 2001

An employee cannot claim capital allowances for a bicycle.

The only way an employee can get tax relief for using their own bicycle for work is by a deduction for mileage allowance relief (see EIM31330  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31330)onwards).
