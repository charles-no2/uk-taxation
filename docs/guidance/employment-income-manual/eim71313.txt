Tax treatment of working rule agreements: site-based staff employees: qualifying conditions
Identifier: EIM71313
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71313
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:17:18.519745+00:00

The application of the working rule agreement taxation procedures for site-based staff employees is subject to certain conditions in the following main areas:

- operation of a working rule agreement on the same site (see EIM71314 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71314))

- working conditions (see EIM71315 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71315))

- payments of daily travel allowances (see EIM71316 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71316))

- payments of lodging allowances (see EIM71317 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71317)).
