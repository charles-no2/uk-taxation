EIM23815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23815) explains why the arrangements described on this page exist and EIM23825 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23825) explains how the guidance on them is laid out.

This page follows from Step 4, described at EIM23850 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23850).

STEP 5: Calculate the car benefit charge for the notional car in each group

For the notional car in each group, multiply the average price (as determined at Step 3, EIM23845 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23845)) by the appropriate percentage (as determined at Step 4, EIM23850 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23850)).

Example

There is an example of Steps 1 to 5 at EIM23870 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23870).

Step 6 follows at EIM23860 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23860).

2008/09 and earlier years

This guidance applies only for 2009/10 and later years. Local arrangements applied for years to 2008/09 as described at EIM23750 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23750) onwards.
