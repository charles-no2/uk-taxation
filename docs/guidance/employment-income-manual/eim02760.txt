Employment income: incidental overnight expenses: example
Identifier: EIM02760
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim02760
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T11:31:27+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:18:43.353057+00:00

An employee stays in a hotel for one night as part of a business trip. His employer pays him an allowance of £6 to cover incidental expenses.

The employee is taxable on the whole of the £6 unless £1 is reimbursed within a reasonable time. The £6 cannot be paid tax free under the incidental overnight expenses legislation because it exceeds the permitted amount, see EIM02730 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim02730).
