Section 187 ITEPA 2003

An election (see EIM26192 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26192)) can be made for certain loans made by a close company to one of its directors to be aggregated. This overrides the usual rule (see EIM26180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26180)) that loans are not aggregated for those years.

These aggregable loans are:

- made between the same lender and borrower and

- beneficial loans (that is, at no time in the year is a rate of interest applied that is equal to, or greater than, the official rate) and

- not qualifying loans (qualifying loans (see EIM26137 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26137)) cannot be aggregated) and

- in the same currency.
