Organisation

The Royal Naval Auxiliary Service (RNAS) is a uniformed, general civilian volunteer organisation trained and administered by the Royal Navy.

The full-time members of the RNAS are dealt with at Public Departments.

Payments to part-time members

The following allowances paid to part-time members of the RNAS are not taxable:

- training expenses allowance

- course and exercise allowance

- uniform allowance

- travelling and subsistence allowances.
