Section 145 ITEPA 2003

Before reading this example, ensure that you are familiar with:

- the guidance on step 7 at EIM25100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25100) onwards and

- in particular, the meaning of arrangements, see EIM25135 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25135).

An employee has as his or her normal company car a small hatchback with a list price of £9,000.

That car develops a fault that is expected to take a week to repair and the garage provides a courtesy car as a replacement. The only courtesy car left in the garage that particular week is a larger family saloon car with a list price of £15,000.

Although the replacement car has a higher price than the normal car it has not been made available under an arrangement whose main purpose is to provide the employee with the benefit of a materially better car. Accordingly, the car is treated as a replacement car, see EIM25125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25125).
