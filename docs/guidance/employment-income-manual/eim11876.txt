Section 700 ITEPA 2003

Certain shares are excluded from being assets for the purposes of Part 11 Chapter 4 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM11931 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11931)). These include shares acquired by an employee:

- as a result of the exercise of an option obtained under a scheme approved under Schedule 3 ITEPA 2003 or Schedule 9 ICTA 1988;

- as a result of the exercise of an option under a scheme approved under Schedule 4 ITEPA 2003 but only in circumstances in which Condition A or B as set out in section 524(2) or (2A) ITEPA is met for shares acquired after 8 April 2003; and

- own company shares acquired by an employee on exercise of an option granted before 27 November 1996.

Own company shares

Shares are own company shares if they are shares in a company that:

- is the employer (the employer company)

- has control of the employer company, or

- either is, or has control of, a company that is a member of a consortium owning either the employer company or a company having control of the employer company.
