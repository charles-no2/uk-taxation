Exemption for amounts which would otherwise be deductible: other benefits
Identifier: EIM30285

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-23T18:11:09+00:00

S289D ITEPA 2003

Where a benefit is provided that would previously have been included in a dispensation, because a fully matching deduction is available, the exemption applies in the same way as to paid or reimbursed expenses. Benefits provided under a ‘relevant salary sacrifice arrangement’ are excluded from the exemption (see EIM30230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30230)).
