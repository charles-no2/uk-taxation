Sections 343 and 344 ITEPA 2003

The general rules dealing with deductions allowed from earnings set out at Part 5 Chapter 1 (see EIM31620 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31620)) apply to fees and subscriptions paid to professional societies.

A deduction cannot be allowed under Section 336 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM31650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31650)) because the expenditure is not incurred in the performance of the duties (see Simpson v Tate (9TC314) and Wales v Graham (24TC75)).

But a deduction can be given if the conditions of either Section 343 or 344 ITEPA 2003 are satisfied.

There are two types of expenditure that qualify for deduction:

- Section 343 - certain professional membership fees (see EIM32890)

- Section 344 - annual subscriptions paid to bodies of persons approved by HMRC (see EIM32900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32900)).

As regards subscriptions to trade unions see EIM32885 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32885).
