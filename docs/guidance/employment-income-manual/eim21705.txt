Particular benefits: examination awards and grants
Identifier: EIM21705
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21705
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-12-30T12:54:41+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:24:31.049501+00:00

Examination awards paid by employers to directors or employees, except for 2015/16 and earlier those in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)), are chargeable as benefits under Section 201 ITEPA 2003 and should be reported on forms P11D (see EIM01100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01100)).
