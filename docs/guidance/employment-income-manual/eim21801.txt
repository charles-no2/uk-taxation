Particular benefits: pension provisions: directors and employees of groups of companies
Identifier: EIM21801

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T12:24:47+01:00

The exemption of the provision of death and retirement benefits in EIM21800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21800) applies to those provided by employers in respect of their own employees. You should also permit exemptions where:

- the employer is a member of a group of companies and

- the expense is incurred by another company in the same group and

- there is no evidence that matters are being arranged so as to give an overall tax advantage to the group and its employees.
