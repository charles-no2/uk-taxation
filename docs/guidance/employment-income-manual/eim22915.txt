Section 161 ITEPA 2003

The van fuel charge is not incurred:

- for years up to tax year 2009 to 2010, if the van is powered solely by electricity

- for years from tax year 2010 to 2011 onwards, if the van cannot in any circumstances emit CO2 by being driven (a zero emission van)

Cash equivalent where the charge is incurred

The cash equivalent of the van fuel benefit charge is as follows:

Possible adjustments to the charge

The above charge is subject to adjustment as follows:

- see EIM22920 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22920) for conditions under which it is reduced to nil

- see EIM22925 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22925) if the van is unavailable for part of the year

- see EIM22930 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22930) if free fuel is withdrawn during the year and not reinstated

- see EIM22935 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22935) if the van is shared

- see EIM22940 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22940) for the effect of replacement vans.
