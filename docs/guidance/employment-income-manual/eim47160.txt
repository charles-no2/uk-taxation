Schedule 11 F(No 2)A 2017

Para 33 provides an exclusion from the 2019 loan charge for car loans under an employee car ownership scheme where the provisions of section 554O apply to the car loan – see EIM45230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45230).
