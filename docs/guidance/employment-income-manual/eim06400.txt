Section 62 and Section 221 ITEPA 2003

Sick pay, other than Statutory Sick Pay (see EIM76350 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76350) onwards), may be funded by:

- the employer, or

- the employees, or

- both employer and employees.
