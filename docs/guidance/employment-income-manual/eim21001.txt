Part 3 Chapter 10 ITEPA 2003

The broad effect of the special rules in Part 3 Chapter 10 ITEPA 2003 (EIM20010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20010)) is to tax a director, or an employee (except for 2015/16 and earlier one in an excluded employment (EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))), on benefits provided by reason of the employment for himself or for members of his household or family, except for those which are:

- already chargeable to income tax by virtue of other legislation (EIM21005 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21005)) or

- specifically exempt from charge (EIM21240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21240)) or

- covered by a dispensation issued by an Inspector (EIM30051  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30051)onwards).
