Section 176(7)(b) ITEPA 2003

For the purposes of the exemptions for loans varied onto commercial terms (see EIM26171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26171)) the relevant loans are:

- the employee loan in question

- any existing loans that were varied at or about the time (see EIM26162 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26162)) the employee loan was varied, so as to be on the same terms (see EIM26176 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26176)) as that loan after it was varied

- any new loans made by the lender, at or about the time the employee loan was varied, on the same terms as the employee loan after it was varied
