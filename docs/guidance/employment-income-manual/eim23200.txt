Car benefit: meaning of made available

Section 114(1) ITEPA 2003

Car benefit can only apply when the car is “made available”. This requires a decision by someone (normally, but not necessarily, the employer) having control of the car to do so.

It also requires that the decision is conveyed to the employee - a decision not conveyed to the employee is worthless because, so far as they are aware, they cannot use the car.

For the purposes of the car benefit charge, the way the employer obtains the car is not relevant. A car benefit charge may arise (subject to the rules at EIM23020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23020)) when a car is made available to an employee, regardless of whether the employer buys the car, leases it, or hires it for a short period from a car hire business.

See EIM23300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23300) for the meaning of available.

See EIM23205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23205) for the related phrase, “without any transfer of the property in it”.
