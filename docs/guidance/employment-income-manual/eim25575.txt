Section 153 ITEPA 2003

If the cash equivalent of the benefit of a shared car is reduced under Section 148 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM25200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25200)) a corresponding reduction is to be made to the car fuel benefit.

Example

See EIM25205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25205) for the facts. The cash equivalent of the car fuel benefit would be allocated in exactly the same way.

Possible further reductions in the charge

- See EIM25565 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25565) if the car is unavailable for part of the tax year.

- See EIM25570 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25570) if free fuel is withdrawn in a tax year and not reinstated.

Years before 2003/04

These arrangements were formerly part of Extra-Statutory Concession A71. The fuel benefit charge had a different basis in these years.
