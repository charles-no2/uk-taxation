Employment income: strike pay from trade unions
Identifier: EIM06500
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim06500
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T11:31:27+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:19:29.369252+00:00

Some trade unions make payments to members who are on strike. Such payments are not earnings from the employment and are not taxable (see also AP1168).
