Particular benefits: third party entertainment: interaction with £8,500 calculation
Identifier: EIM21839
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21839
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-12-30T14:32:45+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:25:02.300511+00:00

An employee or office holder is exempt from tax on employment income on certain goodwill entertainment, provided by third parties, for him or her or for a member of their family or household (see EIM21837 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21837)).

Entertainment that qualifies for exemption should be left out of any calculations in respect of:

- the £8,500 test for employees for 2015/16 and earlier years only (see EIM20100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20100) onwards)

- the calculation of net earnings for the charge on expenses connected with living accommodation (see EIM21722 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21722) onwards).
