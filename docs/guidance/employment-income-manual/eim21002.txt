Section 201 ITEPA 2003

Section 201(1) ITEPA 2003 concerns “employment-related benefits”.

A benefit is defined in section 201(2) as “a benefit or facility of any kind”. When a benefit is provided to an employee (or to any member of his or her family or household (section 721(5) ITEPA 2003) by reason of the employment (EIM20501 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20501)), it is an “employment-related benefit” (section 201(2)).

The definition of what is a benefit is thus very wide and includes everything that confers a special bounty of any description on the recipient.

However, something (other than a loan where special provisions apply, see EIM26101  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26101)and EIM26111 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26111)) which is a “fair bargain” (EIM21004 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21004)) between the employer and the employee is not a “benefit”.

For benefits specifically exempted from charge see EIM21240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21240).
