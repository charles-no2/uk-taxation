Tax treatment of lorry drivers: subsistence allowances paid to lorry drivers: approved amount

Where the conditions in EIM66105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66105) are met, the following amount should be accepted as doing no more than reimburse the expenses of accommodation and subsistence in the United Kingdom.

Date of payment |  Payment per night |

1 January 2013 onwards |  £34.90 |

Where greater sums are paid, see EIM66115. (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66115)

Where the lorry has a sleeper cab, see EIM66130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim66130) .
