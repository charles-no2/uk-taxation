Tax treatment of seafarers: non-resident seafarer coming to the United Kingdom
Identifier: EIM70235
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim70235
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2020-06-22T12:38:52+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:58.814375+00:00

Where a seafarer who has not been resident in the United Kingdom comes to this country, see EIM42890 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42890) onwards. If you are unable to decide the individual’s residence status, you should refer to the Residence, Domicile and Remittance Basis Manual (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis). If you are still unable to make a residence decision, you should submit the case to CS&TD BAI Personal Tax International.
