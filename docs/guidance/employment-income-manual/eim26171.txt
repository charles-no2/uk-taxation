Section 176(6) ITEPA 2003

The exemption for loans varied onto commercial terms (see EIM26170 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26170)) applies to loans varied if the following conditions are met:

- at the time of the variation of the loan in question, members of the public who had loans (for the same or similar purposes, see EIM26159 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26159)) from the lender had a right to vary their loans on the same terms and conditions as the variation of the employee loan and

- a substantial proportion (see EIM26160 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26160)) of the relevant loans (see EIM26175 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26175)) were made to members of the public at large (see EIM26164 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26164)) and

- after the variation, the loan in question and relevant loans made by the lender to members of the public at or about the time of variation (see EIM26162 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26162)) are held on the same terms (see EIM26176 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26176)) and

- if the terms differ from those applying immediately after the variation, they were imposed in the ordinary course of the lender’s business.
