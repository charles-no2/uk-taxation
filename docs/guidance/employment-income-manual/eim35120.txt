Travelling expenses: Non-resident and qualifying new resident employees working in the United Kingdom: operation of PAYE
Identifier: EIM35120
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim35120
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-06-07T15:19:11+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:14:06.295571+00:00

Sections 373 to 375 ITEPA 2003

An employer need not operate PAYE in respect of reimbursements of expenses falling within EIM35030  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim35030)and EIM35050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim35050). Such reimbursements should be treated as falling within the circumstances described in section 5.7 of the Employer's Further Guide to PAYE and NICs under the heading 'Travel and Subsistence Payments'.
