Tax treatment of seafarers: general: taxation of allowances
Identifier: EIM70215

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-09-27T10:15:36+01:00

The instructions in EIM70200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim70200) to EIM70205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim70205) concern the taxation treatment of various allowances payable to seafarers whose pay is chargeable to United Kingdom Income Tax.

As regards the liability to tax of seafarers in general, including questions of residence, see EIM70225 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim70225) onwards.

The instructions at EP8145 and EP8312 to EP8313 deal with procedural matters.
