Sections 336 to 338 ITEPA 2003

A Clerk may deduct from the amounts he or she receives from the council any amount that is deductible under sections 336 to 338 ITEPA 2003. These consist of:

- travel in the performance of the duties and travel to a temporary workplace, see EIM31810  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31810)and

- other expenses incurred wholly, exclusively and necessarily in the performance of his or her duties, see EIM31630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31630).

The expenses that a Clerk would typically incur are summarised at EIM67315 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim67315).
