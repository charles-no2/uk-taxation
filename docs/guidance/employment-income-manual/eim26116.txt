The benefits code: beneficial loans: loans released or written off

Sections 62 and 188(1) ITEPA 2003

The release or writing off of any loan to an employee may be chargeable to tax (see EIM01490 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01490) and EIM21740  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21740)to EIM21747 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21747)). It does not matter:

- for what purpose the loan was applied or

- whether it was a cheap or interest-free loan

Consult the guidance at EIM01490  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01490)and EIM21740  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21740)to EIM21747 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21747) in relation to all loans written off.
