Tax treatment of Post Office employees: introductory  payments
Identifier: EIM68235
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68235
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:49.142414+00:00

Some new sub-postmasters may be appointed on terms that require the sub-postmaster to make an introductory payment but which do not cause the remuneration payable for the first year to be reduced. In such cases the full remuneration for the first year is assessable (see EIM68230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68230)).
