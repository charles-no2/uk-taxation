Tax treatment of waiters: tips, etc.
Identifier: EIM71200
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71200
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:17:13.522797+00:00

Tips paid to waiters and others working in hotels, restaurants, clubs, etc. are earnings that are chargeable to tax in addition to normal wages (see EIM07900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim07900)).

As regards coding, see guidance in the PAYE Online Manual (POM) - search for “Tips, Gratuities, Service Charges And Troncs”.
