Value Added Tax: inter-relation with earnings and expenses: VAT and amounts made good
Identifier: EIM15535
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15535
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-14T16:33:57+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:11:23.627321+00:00

Where the cost to an employer of providing a benefit for a director etc is reduced by a contribution from the director concerned (see EIM21120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21120)) the employer may be chargeable to VAT in respect of that contribution.

Any VAT so chargeable will represent expenditure incurred by the employer in connection with the provision of the benefit and is included in calculating the amount of the taxable benefit.
