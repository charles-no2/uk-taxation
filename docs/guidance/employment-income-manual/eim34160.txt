Foreign travel: approach to be taken on residence status
Identifier: EIM34160
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim34160
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-07T16:32:30+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:14:00.121379+00:00

The foreign travel rules at EIM34010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim34010) onwards apply only to employees who are resident in the United Kingdom (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42802). Some deductions are prohibited if the employment is with a foreign employer (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim40031) and the employee is non-resident or a qualifying new resident in the United Kingdom. This requires consideration of:

- the residence status of the individual

- the state in which the employer is resident.

Unless the employee has completed the non-residents pages which accompany the Self Assessment tax return, HMRC may accept that an employee is resident in the United Kingdom for the purposes of the foreign travel rules.
