Section 87(4) ITEPA 2003

All employees provided with season tickets, for ordinary commuting journeys, receive a benefit that is treated as earnings under Section 87 ITEPA 2003, see EIM16140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16140).

As regards leave travel facilities for the armed forces see EIM50100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50100).
