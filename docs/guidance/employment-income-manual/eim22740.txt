Sections 118 and 155 ITEPA 2003

‘Private use’ means any use other than for the employee’s business travel (EIM22735 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22735)), section 118.

This is important because one of the basic conditions for a van benefit charge to apply (EIM22701 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22701)) is that the van is available for private use, though remember that

- there must be actual private use before the charge applies, and

- a van for which private use is insignificant (EIM22745 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22745)) remains outside the charge.

Two kinds of private use

For the purpose of the vans charge from 2005/06 onwards only, private use is split into two kinds:

- where the restricted private use condition (EIM22795 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22795)) is met in theory and in practice

- where the restricted private use condition is not met.

Note that these two kinds of private use apply only to van benefit from 2005/06. They have no relevance to van benefit before 2005/06, nor to car benefit.

The cash equivalent: calculating the benefit charge

See EIM22790 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22790) for guidance on calculating the benefit charge.

Examples
