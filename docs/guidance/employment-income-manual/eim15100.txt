Section 393B(2)(a) ITEPA 2003

Most benefits from employer-financed retirement benefits schemes are paid as a lump sum. However, they may also take the form of a pension, an annuity or an annual payment, or be in non-cash form. These various payments are dealt with as follows:

- where the benefit is paid as a pension, or is any other kind of income within Part 9 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM75000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim75000) and subsequent guidance), it is taxed only under that Part - section 394 ITEPA 2003 does not tax any income that falls within Part 9, nor does Part 7A (see EIM45610 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45610))

- where the benefit is paid as a limited annual payment that is not within Part 9 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM75000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim75000) and subsequent guidance) it counts as employment income under section 394 ITEPA 2003 unless first charged under Part 7A (see EIM45000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45000))

- where the benefit is paid as a lump sum ‘relevant benefit’ (see EIM15020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15020)) it counts as employment income under section 394 ITEPA 2003 (but see EIM15121 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15121) for exceptions), unless first charged under Part 7A (see EIM45000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45000))

- where the benefit is in non-cash form, see EIM15120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15120).

See EIM15055 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15055) for who is assessed.
