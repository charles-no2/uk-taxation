Section 339(7) ITEPA 2003

The test for a change of workplace has two conditions, see EIM32280 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32280). So far we have focussed on a change in the cost of the journey.

However, where the journey to the new workplace is significantly different to the journey to the old workplace we can recognise a change of workplace even where the cost of the two journeys is similar. This is illustrated by example EIM32286 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32286).
