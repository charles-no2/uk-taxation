Part 3 Chapter 4 ITEPA 2003

If you have a case involving vouchers or credit-tokens and you are unsure where to start go first to EIM16030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16030). This has a description of points to consider and action to take in any case involving vouchers or credit tokens.
