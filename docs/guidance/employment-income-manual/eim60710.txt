The bodies treated as Public Departments (see EIM60700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim60700), third bullet) include:

- The Bank of England

- The Church Commissioners

- The College of Arms

- The Ecclesiastical Courts

- The Elder Brethren of Trinity House

- The Metropolitan Police

- The Post Office

- The University of London.
