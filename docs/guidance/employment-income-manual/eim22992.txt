Sections 238, 239 and 269 ITEPA 2003

Where the availability of a heavy goods vehicle is not chargeable because of Section 238 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM22990 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22990)) no charge arises in connection with the vehicle except where, exceptionally, expense is incurred by or on behalf of the employer in providing a driver for the vehicle while it is being used privately.

The more detailed rules at EIM22710 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22710) apply equally to heavy goods vehicles as they do to vans.

Years to which the guidance on heavy goods vehicles applies

This guidance applies to all years from 2003/04 onwards and was formerly at EIM22300-3. No changes were made by the new van regime which began in 2005/06.
