Tax treatment of members of the police service: car allowances
Identifier: EIM68170
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68170
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:45.393264+00:00

Some police officers receive allowances, identical to those paid to local authority employees (see EIM65815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim65815)), in respect of the use of their own cars on police business. Where such allowances are paid, apply the guidance in EIM31200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31200) onwards.

Some other officers, particularly Chief Officers, may receive different rates of allowance.
