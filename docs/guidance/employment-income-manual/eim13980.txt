Section 414(2) ITEPA 2003

An employee receives a termination payment within section 401 ITEPA 2003 of £100,000 which fails to qualify for full foreign service exception under EIM13690 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13690).

The employee is non-UK resident in the tax year the employment is terminated (2018 to 2019) and there is some foreign service during the employment, so the reduction for foreign service in EIM13700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13700) is considered.

To calculate the foreign service reduction, the first step is find the amount of ‘post-employment notice pay’ and the ‘amount charged to tax’ as follows.

Termination payments and benefits within section 401 ITEPA 2003 |  £100,000 |

Post-employment notice pay (see EIM13874 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13874)) |  £10,000 |

Termination awards subject to section 403 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13874 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13874)) |  £90,000 |

Less the £30,000 threshold (see EIM13505 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13505)) |  £30,000 |

The ‘amount charged to tax’ * |  £60,000 |

* More accurately, the amount that would otherwise count as employment income. See EIM13700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13700).

The £10,000 and £60,000 amounts may be eligible for reduction, as described at EIM13700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13700) and shown in the examples at EIM13985 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13985).
