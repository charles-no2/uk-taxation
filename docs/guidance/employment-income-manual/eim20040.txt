Sections 70 and 201 ITEPA 2003

The benefits code charges an expense payment (Section 70(1) ITEPA 2003) or a benefit provided (Section 201(1) ITEPA 2003) to a director or employee (except, for 2015/16 and earlier, one in excluded employment (EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))), who holds an employment during a year. It follows that no charge can arise under either section in a year when there is no employment unless, exceptionally, the benefit or expense was arranged before the employment ceased but not provided or paid until after the employment ceased in a deliberate attempt to exploit the wording of the statute.

Consequently expenses payments made and benefits provided after a director or employee has left the employment are not chargeable under the benefits code (EIM20006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20006)), except that two benefits are chargeable. They are:

- the waiving or writing off of a loan (EIM21740 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21740) onwards) and

- a failure to deduct PAYE tax from the remuneration of certain directors (EIM21790 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21790) onwards).

However, benefits provided for a director or employee on or after termination of employment may be chargeable under other legislation. See EIM15120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15120) where the benefit is provided on or after retirement and EIM13270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13270) where the benefit is otherwise connected with the termination of the employment.
