Car fuel benefit: when cash equivalent is nil

Section 151 ITEPA 2003

The car fuel benefit charge is incurred as detailed at EIM25510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25510).

The car fuel benefit charge is reduced to nil if it can be shown that either condition A or condition B applies.

Condition A

This is met if, in the tax year in question:

- the employee (this includes a director) is required to make good (see EIM25650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25650)) to the person providing the fuel the whole of the expense incurred by that person in connection with the provision of fuel for the employee’s private motoring and

- the employee does make good that expense.

Condition B

This is met if in the tax year in question the fuel is made available only for business travel (see EIM23305 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23305)).

Years prior to 2003/04

The fuel benefit charge had a different basis in these years.
