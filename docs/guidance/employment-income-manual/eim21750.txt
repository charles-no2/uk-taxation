Section 323 ITEPA 2003

Section 323 ITEPA 2003 exempts from a benefits charge long service awards in the form of tangible articles given to directors or employees, within the benefits code (see EIM01500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01500)).
