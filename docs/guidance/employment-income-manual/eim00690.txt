Section 62 ITEPA 2003

An employee’s conditions of service may alter through their losing rights as an employee. That happened in Hamblett v Godfrey (59TC694), where the taxpayer lost her rights under the employment protection legislation, principally the right to join a trade union. She received a payment of £1,000 in consequence. It was held to be taxable as earnings within Section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00515)). Neill L J said:

“The rights had been enjoyed within the employer/employee relationship. The removal of the rights involved changes in the conditions of service. The payment was in recognition of the changes in service … It was referable to the employment and to nothing else.” (page 727)

This case demonstrates very clearly that a payment does not have to be a reward for services to be taxable as earnings within Section 62.
