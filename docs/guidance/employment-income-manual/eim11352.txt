Section 99(2) ITEPA 2003

You can accept that the following classes of employee satisfy the customary test in Section 99(2) ITEPA 2003 (see EIM11346 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11346)). If the employee also satisfies the better performance test then Part 3 Chapter 5 ITEPA 2003 does not apply to the living accommodation provided for them (see EIM11349 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11349)):
- veterinary surgeons assisting in veterinary practices - managers of camping and caravan sites living on or adjacent to the site.

For a list of classes of employees that we accept meet both the customary and better performance tests see EIM11351 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11351).
