Benefits: scholarships provided for members of the  family or household of a director or employee: returns by  trustees of payments of educational trusts
Identifier: EIM30007
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30007
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-11T12:04:16+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:12:30.053799+00:00

After 5 April each year a nominated Band D officer should:

- review the establishment file headed ‘Educational and Scholarship Schemes’

- send letters, adapted where necessary, to the trustees of each trust on the lines of the draft letter in EIM30008 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim30008). The name and address of the trustee will be shown on the ruling from IR Trusts Bootle or Financial Intermediaries and Claims Office in the employer’s file

- enclose a small supply of forms P11D (https://www.gov.uk/employer-reporting-expenses-benefits/reporting-and-paying) with the letter

- send a copy of the trustees’ return to any other office which deals with an employer shown
