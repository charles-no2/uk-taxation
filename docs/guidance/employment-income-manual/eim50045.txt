Section 344 ITEPA 2003

Subscriptions to the British Airline Pilots’ Association (BALPA) qualify for relief under Section 344 ITEPA 2003 for 2003/04 onwards. Relief is due for two-thirds of the member’s annual subscription.

Relief for a pilot’s BALPA subscriptions is due in addition to the nationally agreed Flat Rate Expenses (FRE) allowance which applies for 2006/07 onwards (see EIM50050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50050) onwards).
