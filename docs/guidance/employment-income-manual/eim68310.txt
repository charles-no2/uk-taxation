Tax treatment of people employed in the prison service: living accommodation provided by the prison authority
Identifier: EIM68310
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68310
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:49.886660+00:00

Prison governors, officers and chaplains, who, by reason of their employment, occupy living accommodation provided by the prison authority are within the exemption from charge provided by Section 99 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM11346 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11346) onwards).

Such accommodation is maintained and decorated by the prison authority to a basic standard only and the provision of ancillary maintenance services should not be treated as giving rise to a taxable benefit (see EIM20001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20001) onwards).
