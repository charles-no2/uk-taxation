Tax treatment of working rule agreements: site-based staff employees: extension of arrangements
Identifier: EIM71311
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71311
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:24:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:17:17.811976+00:00

Working rule agreements entitle site-based operatives to payments of travel and lodging, etc, allowances, and the procedures described at EIM71300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71300) to EIM71310 refer to taxation of such allowances in the hands of operatives. These special taxation procedures also cover site-based staff employees subject to various conditions. See:

- EIM71312 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71312) for a definition of site-based staff employees

- EIM71313 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71313) to EIM71317 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim71317) for the qualifying conditions
