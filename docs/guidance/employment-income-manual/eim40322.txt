Section 20(2) and (3) CAA 2001

No deduction for capital allowances can be given against general earnings taxable on remittance under Section 22 or Section 26 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM36880 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim36880)).
