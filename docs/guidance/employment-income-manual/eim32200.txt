Travel expenses: travel for necessary attendance: duties defined by reference to a particular area: deductions for  travel

Where the conditions in EIM32190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32190) are met the whole of the geographical area is treated as a permanent workplace. If the employee lives outside that area any journey between home (or any other place that is not a workplace) and the edge of that area is ordinary commuting, see EIM32055 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32055). No deduction is due for the cost of that travel. This is illustrated by examples EIM32205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32205) and EIM32206 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32206).

A deduction is due for:

- the full cost of business travel within the geographical area and

- the full cost of business travel between the area and other workplaces outside the area. (see example EIM32207 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32207)).
