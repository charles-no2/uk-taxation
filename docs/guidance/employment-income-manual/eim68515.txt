Tax treatment of racehorse training or stud farm  establishment employees: stable lads: subsistence allowances

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00

The allowances paid to stable lads when they have to work away from their normal place of work are:

- overtime, see EIM68520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68520)

- Sunday racing payment, see EIM68520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68520)

- racing abroad allowance, see EIM68520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68520)

- overnight allowance, see EIM68525 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68525)

- daily allowance, see EIM68525 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim68525).
