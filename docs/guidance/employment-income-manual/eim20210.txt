Section 216(3)(b) ITEPA 2003

Non-profit making company

For the purposes of deciding whether or not a director’s employment is an excluded employment (for 2015/16 and earlier) (EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)), a non-profit making company (EIM20201 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20201)) is one:

- which does not carry on a trade and

- whose functions do not consist wholly or mainly in the holding of investments or other property.

The mere fact that the company does not make a profit does not mean that it is non-profit making for the purposes of this test.
