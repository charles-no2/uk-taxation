Schedule 11 F(No 2)A 2017

Para 27 provides an exclusion from the 2019 loan charge where P would be treated as making a quasi-loan but the provisions of section 554OA(1)(a) to (e) apply in relation to the transfer of employment related loans – see EIM46115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim46115).

The exclusion does not apply if there is a connection between the acquisition of the right by the new employer and a tax avoidance arrangement.
