Section 62 ITEPA 2003

The most common termination payments and benefits within EIM12810 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12810) which are taxable in full under section 62 ITEPA 2003 are shown below.

Type of payment | Notes | Guidance |

A payment received under the terms and conditions of service - such terms may be in the main contract document or elsewhere, for example in a staff handbook or letter of appointment - see example EIM13920 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13920). | Most such payments are taxable under section 62 ITEPA 2003 but exceptions are redundancy payments (see EIM13750 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13750)) and lump sums from pension schemes. | EIM00610 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00610) EIM13920 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13920) |

A non-contractual payment that the employer is accustomed to make and/or that the employee could reasonably have expected to receive. | - | EIM00640 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00640) EIM12977 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12977) |

A payment made to induce an employee take up an employment. | The payment is earnings (see EIM00515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00515)) from the new employment. | EIM00700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00700) |

See EIM12950 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12950) for information concerning payments made under the Employment Rights Act 1996.
