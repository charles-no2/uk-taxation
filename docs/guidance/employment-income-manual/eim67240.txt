Tax treatment of nurses: expenses deductions: laundering uniforms: amount to be deducted

Sections 336 and 367 ITEPA 2003

A flat rate expense deduction (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32700) has been agreed for nurses who must bear the cost of laundering their uniforms (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim67210). The amount of the flat rate expense deduction is £125 per year from the tax year 2014 to 2015 onwards (£100 for the tax years 2007 to 2008 to 2013 to 2014).

A deduction of this amount should be permitted only for years in which the nurse has not been permitted a deduction of an amount agreed locally.

If a nurse requests a deduction greater than the agreed flat rate expense, see EIM67250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim67250).

Flat rate expense deductions for other healthcare staff vary and are listed at EIM32712 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32712).
