Sections 321(6) and 322(1) ITEPA 2003

An encouragement award is an award (other than a financial benefit award (see EIM06630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim06630))) made for a suggestion with intrinsic merit or showing special effort.

The permitted maximum for an encouragement award, for the purpose of exemption underSection 321, is £25.
