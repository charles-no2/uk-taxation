Note

The earlier examples cover the simpler types of case. The later ones cover more complex situations. The heading at the top of each example indicates the points of principle itaims to cover, as shown in the table above.

All the examples show how to calculate the chargeable benefit using both:

- the normal averaging method (see EIM26210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26210)) and

- the alternative precise method (see EIM26230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26230)).
