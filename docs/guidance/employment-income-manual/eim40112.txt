Part 5 Chapter 6 ITEPA 2003

Seafarers may be eligible for a deduction from earnings taxable under Section 15 ITEPA 2003 (or under Section 21 if before 6 April 2008) where they satisfy the conditions in Part 5 Chapter 6. See EIM33000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim33000) and subsequent for further information.
