Car benefit: scope of the charge
Identifier: EIM23025
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23025
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2017-08-22T10:16:44+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:20:11.882100+00:00

The car benefit charge is the only charge on the benefit of a car available for private use. But there are also other charges where:

- fuel is provided for private motoring in the provided car (see EIM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25500) onwards), or

- expense is incurred by an employer (or another person acting on the employer’s behalf or otherwise by reason of the employment) in providing a chauffeur for the car (see EIM23045 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23045))

- fines or penalties are incurred in connection with congestion (EIM21680 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21680)) or parking charges (EIM21686 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21686)) and it is possible for the employee to become liable to pay them.
