Tax treatment of fire officers: motor mileage allowances paid for private journeys
Identifier: EIM63645
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim63645
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:24:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:04.458629+00:00

Mileage allowances are paid to fire officers for some journeys that are private for tax purposes (see EIM63610 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim63610) and EIM63620 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim63620)). If a particular fire brigade makes such payments then:

- payments for business travel should be dealt with under the approved mileage allowance payments (AMAPs) scheme in the normal way; see EIM31200  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31200)onwards

- any payments for private miles should be subject to tax under PAYE
