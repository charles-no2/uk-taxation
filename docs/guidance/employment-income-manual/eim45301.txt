Employment income provided through third parties: exclusions: Summary of structure of guidance on: share schemes etc

The next sequence of pages tells you about the exclusions which are specific to employee share and share option schemes.

EIM45305 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45305) gives you an overview of these exclusions and sketches the structure of this part of the guidance.

EIM45310 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45310) explains what happens if earmarked shares are exchanged for shares in a different company.

EIM45315 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45315) onwards are about the exclusions for tax-advantaged share and share option schemes in Section 554E.

EIM45325 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45325) onwards are about the exclusions for various cases involving employment-related securities in Section 554N.

EIM45350 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45350) introduces the guidance on the exclusions for earmarking for employee share and share option schemes in Sections 554J to 554M.
