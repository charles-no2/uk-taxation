Section 386(1)-(2) and section 394(1)-(3) ITEPA 2003

Income within Section 386 ITEPA 2003 is income of the tax year in which the employer’s contribution is paid (see example EIM15437 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15437)).

Income within Section 394 ITEPA 2003 is income of the tax year in which the benefit is received (see EIM15420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15420) and example EIM15435 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15435)).
