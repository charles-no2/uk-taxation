Requests for the inclusion of employment income in Trading Income accounts where the conditions set out at EIM03002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03002) are met should be dealt with by the Inspector dealing with the individual’s or the partnership’s business accounts.

(This content has been withheld because of exemptions in the Freedom of Information Act 2000)

As regards compensation received in connection with the termination of an office or employment where the employment income has been included within trading income accounts see BIM40135 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim40135) onwards.
