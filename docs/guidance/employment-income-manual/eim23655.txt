Section 246 ITEPA 2003

If a vehicle which is not a car for car benefit purposes (see EIM23100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23100) onwards) is used to provide transport for a disabled person, Section 246 ITEPA 2003 prevents a liability to income tax as long as the transport is only provided for the purpose of ordinary commuting or travel between any two places that is for practical purposes substantially ordinary commuting.

If it is also provided for other purposes, the benefit is calculated in the way set out in EIM21630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21630) onwards.

If the vehicle is a car that would normally be chargeable under Section 120 ITEPA 2003, see EIM23660 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23660).

Years before 2003/04

These arrangements were formerly part of Extra-Statutory Concession A59.
