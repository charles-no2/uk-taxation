Social security benefits: how to deal with late objections
Identifier: EIM76308

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-24T11:28:04+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:17:51.353488+00:00

A Benefit Office or Jobcentre will accept late objections if the claimant has a reasonable excuse.

If a Benefit Office or Jobcentre is not prepared to accept a late objection it will make a written report to the relevant HMRC Area office.
