Section 180(1)(b) ITEPA 2003

Where:

- the exemption under Section 180(1)(a) (see EIM26140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26140)) is not due because the total balance outstanding on all loans exceeds £10,000 at some point in the year (£5,000 for 2013/14 and earlier tax years) but

- the maximum total balance of non-qualifying loans (see EIM26137 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26137)) does not exceed £10,000 at any time in the year (£5,000 for 2013/14 and earlier tax years)

the non-qualifying loans are exempt and only the qualifying loans are taken into account for the purposes of Section 175 ITEPA 2003.

See example at EIM26146 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26146).

Note the second and third sub-paragraphs of EIM26140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26140) regarding respectively the meaning of the maximum balance outstanding and the treatment of interest accrued.
