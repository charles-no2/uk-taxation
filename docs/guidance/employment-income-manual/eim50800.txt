Census of population: tax treatment of remuneration
Identifier: EIM50800
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim50800
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T13:19:14+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:15:42.489779+00:00

A census of population of the United Kingdom is carried out periodically. The remuneration of all officers employed in connection with the census is taxable as employment income. Special instructions regarding the application of PAYE to census remuneration will be issued on the occasion of each census.
