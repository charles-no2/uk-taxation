Car benefit: exceptions

Sections 118 and 167 ITEPA 2003

There are various situations where no car benefit charge will arise in respect of a car that is made available to and actually used by an employee or director who, for 2015/16 and earlier, is not in an excluded employment. These are where the car in question:

- is one that the employee is specifically prohibited from using for private purposes and which is not so used (see EIM23400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23400)), or

- satisfies all the conditions to be treated as a pooled car for tax purposes (see EIM23450 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23450) onwards)

- is used by a disabled employee and the conditions in EIM23655 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23655) or EIM23660 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23660) are met

- is an emergency vehicle and all the conditions at EIM23600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23600) (including those at EIM23605 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim23605)) are met.
