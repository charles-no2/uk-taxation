Section 248 ITEPA 2003

Section 248 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM10210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim10210)) applies to a maximum of 60 journeys in a year. If, exceptionally, the number of journeys exceeds 60 then the excess journeys have to be taken into account for tax purposes. So if, for instance, in a tax year there are 63 journeys potentially within the exemption, 60 will be exempt and 3 taxable.

If the terms of the exemption are not met for any reason the cost of the journeys not falling within the exemption may be taxed as follows.

All employees |  Charging section |

Cash payments or reimbursements by the employer (see EIM00580 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00580) onwards) |  Section 62 ITEPA 2003 |

Payments made by the employer that meet the employee’s pecuniary liability (see EIM00580 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00580) onwards) |  Section 62 ITEPA 2003 |

A voucher provided by the employer is used (see EIM16140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16140)) |  Section 82 ITEPA 2003 |

Directors and employees not in an excluded employment |  Charging section |

The costs are borne by the employer in some way (see EIM21001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21001) onwards) |  Section 203 ITEPA 2003 |
