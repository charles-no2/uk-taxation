Heavy goods vehicles: treatment of vehicle which is  not exempt

Where availability of a heavy goods vehicle is not exempted by Section 238 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM22990 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22990)) it will be charged in the same way as availability of any other asset which is not a car or van (see EIM21615 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21615) onwards).

Years to which the guidance on heavy goods vehicles applies

This guidance applies to all years from 2003/04 onwards and was formerly at EIM22300-3. No changes were made by the new van regime which began in 2005/06.
