Non-approved schemes: receipts excluded from charge: general 
Identifier: EIM15422

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T11:31:27+01:00

A receipt from a non-approved retirement benefits scheme which is otherwise chargeable under Section 394 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM15401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15401) and EIM15420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15420)) may nevertheless be excluded from charge.

See the following guidance:

Receipt from non-approved scheme |  EIM15400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15400) |

Prior employer contributions |  EIM15423 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15423) |

Prior employee contributions |  EIM15424 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15424) |

Small lump sums where no other pension scheme |  EIM15425 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15425) |

For examples see EIM15438 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15438).
