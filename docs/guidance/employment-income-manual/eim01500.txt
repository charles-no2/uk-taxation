Section 323 ITEPA 2003

Payments to employees by employers to mark long service are taxable as earnings within section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM00520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00520) onwards and Weston v Hearn (25TC425)), or as benefits within section 201 (see EIM21001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21001) onwards).

For tax year 2002 to 2003 and earlier tax years, some long service awards were exempt from tax under Extra-Statutory Concession A22 (see SE01500).

For tax year 2003 to 2004 onwards, the concession has been replaced by a statutory exemption in section 323 ITEPA 2003. The conditions for exemption are as follows:

- the award must be made to mark a period of not less than 20 years service with the same employer (see EIM01504 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01504))

- it must be in a form that satisfies the condition in section 323(3) (broadly, it must be something other than money) (see EIM01502 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01502))

- the taxable value of the award (before applying the exemption) must not be more than £50 for each year of service (see EIM01501 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01501)) (£20 per year of service if the award was made on or before 12 June 2003)

As regards multiple awards, see EIM01503 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01503).

If a non-cash award fails to satisfy the conditions for the exemption, or exceeds the £50 per year of service limit, the employer may be prepared to pay the tax due under a PAYE Settlement Agreement. Cash awards do not qualify for exemption at all, and the employer should operate PAYE.
