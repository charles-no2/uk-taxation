The benefits code: beneficial loans: exemptions from charge: general
Identifier: EIM26132
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26132
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-04T16:19:05+00:00

No charge is made on beneficial loans obtained by reason of a person’s employment in the circumstances summarised in the following table. The relevant instructions should always be consulted.

Circumstances |  Relevant instructions |

The loan is a qualifying loan (see EIM26136 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26136)) on which the whole of any interest qualifies for relief |  EIM26135 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26135) |

The aggregate balance outstanding on all beneficial loans does not exceed £10,000 at any time in the year (£5,000 for 2013 to 2014 and earlier tax years) |  EIM26140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26140) |

The aggregate balance on all non-qualifying loans (see EIM26137 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26137)) does not exceed £10,000 at any time in the year (£5,000 for 2013 to 2014 and earlier tax years) |  EIM26145 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26145) |

The employee shows that he or she derived no benefit from a loan made to his or her relative |  EIM26150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26150) |

The loan is for fixed period at a fixed rate of interest and the loan was first made ‘before 6 April 1978 at a genuine commercial rate’, or ‘on or after 6 April 1978 at an interest rate equal to, or more than, the appropriate official rate(s) for the year the loan was made’ |  EIM26152 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26152) |

Advances for necessary expenses made by an employer to an employee (subject to certain limits) |  EIM26155 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26155) |

A loan or credit on terms that are available to and substantially taken up by the public at large, made by a person whose business includes the lending of money or the giving of credit |  EIM26158 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26158) |
