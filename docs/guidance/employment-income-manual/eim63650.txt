Tax treatment of fire officers: expenses deductions: laundering uniforms
Identifier: EIM63650

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:04.850106+00:00

A flat rate expense deduction has been agreed for fire officers who:

- are required to wear a uniform as part of their duties (see EIM32475 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32475)) and

- must meet the cost of laundry themselves.

The circumstances in which a deduction can be given are described in EIM32710 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32710).

The amount of the deduction is £80

In some circumstances an employee can request a deduction for the actual expense they have incurred instead of the flat rate expense, see EIM32715 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32715).
