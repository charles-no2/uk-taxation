Residence: taxpayer coming to the United Kingdom: action on receipt of completed P86: year of arrival

The form P86 has now been withdrawn.

New arrivals to the UK are to be integrated into HMRC processes by existing means:

- form P46/P46 (Expat) for new employees

- CWF1 for the newly self-employed

- SA1 registration process for customers who are not self-employed but who need to complete a tax return

If, however, the department received the form P86 before 1 September 2010, please see the following guidance.

For the year of arrival, take the action referred to in the Residence, Domicile and Remittance Basis Manual (RDRM) (see RDRM10220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm10220) onwards to RDRM10235 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm10235) (Chart A), RDRM10240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm10240) onwards to RDRM10265 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm10265) (Chart B), and RDRM10270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm10270)) according to the information supplied by the taxpayer on the completed form P86.
