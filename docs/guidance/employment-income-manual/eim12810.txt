Sections 62, 225, 394 and 401 ITEPA 2003

Having found what each element of a termination package is for (see EIM12805 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12805)), each element should then be looked at separately following the structure set out below.

- First, consider section 62 ITEPA 2003: is the payment or benefit earnings from the employment? See EIM12850 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12850) for the more common termination payments that are within section 62. Material on earnings generally is at EIM00600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00600) and subsequent guidance, particularly EIM00530 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim00530) regarding non-money receipts. If section 62 does not apply,

- second, consider section 225 ITEPA 2003: is the payment or benefit for a restrictive covenant? See EIM03600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03600) onwards. If section 225 does not apply

- thirdly, consider whether the payment is made in connection with an employee’s (or ex-employee’s) retirement or death. Is the payment a relevant benefit from an employer financed retirement benefits scheme (efrbs) under section 394 ITEPA 2003? An efrbs does not require the existence of a formal pension scheme; it can be a quite informal agreement or arrangement, such as a decision at an employer’s meeting. Guidance is at EIM13600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13600) (death) and from EIM15010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15010). If section 394 does not apply,

- last, and only if no other Income Tax charge applies (see the last bullet below), consider section 401 ITEPA 2003: is the payment or benefit made in connection with termination? See EIM12850 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12850) onwards. Note: it’s common for benefits (such as use of a car or medical insurance) to continue through the termination of the employment. See EIM12815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12815) for details of how these are taxed.

There is a typical example of this process at EIM13900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13900).

Note that:

- redundancy payments (as defined in EIM13800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13800)) will always be within section 401 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13000) onwards)

- a lump sum from a tax exempt pension scheme is usually exempt from being taxed within section 401 - see EIM13660 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13660).

- section 401 ITEPA 2003 applies only if there is no other charge to Income Tax. Apart from sections 62, 225 and 394 ITEPA 2003 mentioned above, other sections may apply before section 401 in particular situations. For example, compensation for loss of a share option on termination (see ERSM110025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-related-securities/ersm110025)) and annual payments (see SAIM8020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim8020))

- if payment is not made by the employer, but by a third party, see from EIM45000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45000)
