Section 97 ITEPA 2003

All employees are chargeable, (including, for 2015/16 and earlier, those in lower-paid employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007))), apart from those in certain job-related accommodation (see EIM11331 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11331)).
