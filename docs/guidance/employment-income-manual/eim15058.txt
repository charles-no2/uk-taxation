Section 394(1)-(3) ITEPA 2003

Income within Section 394 ITEPA 2003 is income of the tax year in which the benefit is received (see EIM15100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15100) and example EIM15310 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15310)).
