Section 180(1) ITEPA 2003

This example shows how the exemption for small loans (where the total balance on all loans does not exceed £10,000 (£5,000 for 2013 to 2014 and earlier tax years), see EIM26140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26140)) applies. An employee had three interest free loans from his employer as follows:

Purpose |  Maximum balance outstanding |

To pay for home improvements |  £4,000 |

To pay for a holiday |  £4,000 |

To buy his season ticket |  £2,000 |

Total |  £10,000 |

Since the maximum total balance in the year is not more than £10,000 no tax is chargeable under section 175(1) ITEPA 2003.

Note that if at some time in the year the balances were:

Purpose |  Maximum balance outstanding |

Home improvements loan |  £4,100 |

Holiday loan |  £3,950 |

Season ticket loan |  £1,975 |

Total |  £10,025 |

exemption under section 180(1) ITEPA 2003 would not be due because the total balance has exceeded £10,000. See EIM26145 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26145) where the total balance exceeds £10,000, but the total of non-qualifying loans (see EIM26137 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26137)) does not.
