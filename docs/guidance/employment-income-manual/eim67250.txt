Tax treatment of nurses: expenses deductions: laundering uniforms: amount to be deducted: requests for more than the flat rate

Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00

Sections 336 and 367 ITEPA 2003

A nurse may request a deduction for laundry expenses that exceeds the agreed flat rate expense deduction in EIM67240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim67240). In these cases a deduction should only be given if evidence is provided by way of receipts for the full amount of the deduction.
