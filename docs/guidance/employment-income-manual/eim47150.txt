Schedule 11 F(No 2)A 2017

Para 29 provides an exclusion from the 2019 loan charge where P would be treated as making a payment of a sum of money by way of a loan but the provisions of section 554G apply in terms of transactions under employee benefit packages – see EIM45220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45220).

The exclusion does not apply if there is a connection between the relevant step and a tax avoidance arrangement.
