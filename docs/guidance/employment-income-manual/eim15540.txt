Value Added Tax: inter-relation with earnings and  expenses: advice to employers about VAT

Updated: 2015-10-28T13:45:20+00:00

Areas should not attempt to advise on the incidence or administration of VAT and should refer such enquiries to the local office of Customs and Excise. However, you should give every assistance to employers seeking advice about the effects of VAT on the operation of PAYE (see EIM15510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15510)).
