Residence or employment in the United Kingdom: Irish Republic employments
Identifier: EIM43015
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim43015
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:24:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:22:59.934757+00:00

Before 6 April 2008, general earnings from an Irish Republic employment received by an individual who was resident in the United Kingdom were not “chargeable overseas earnings (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim40102)” because an employer who was resident in the Irish Republic was not a “foreign employer (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim40031)” for these purposes.

From 6 April 2008, the rules changed to include an Irish Republic employment as a foreign employer. Consequently, earnings from this employment can be chargeable overseas earnings.

The ordinary rules relating to the deduction from seafarers’ earnings (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim33000) apply.
