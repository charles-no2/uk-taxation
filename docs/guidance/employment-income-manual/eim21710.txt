Particular benefits: furniture
Identifier: EIM21710
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21710
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:24:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:24:32.572970+00:00

If furniture is provided for the use of a director or employee, except for 2015 to 2016 tax year and earlier one in an excluded employment (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007), by reason of the employment, the chargeable benefit should be calculated as in EIM21630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21630) to EIM21632. But no charge will arise if the furniture is on the employer’s premises (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21610) and is used by the employee solely in performing his duties.

If furniture is provided in job-related living accommodation (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21720) the chargeable benefit may be capped to 10% of the employee’s net earnings.
