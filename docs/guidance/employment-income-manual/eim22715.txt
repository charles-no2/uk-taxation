Van and van fuel benefit from 2005 to 2006: Class 1A National Insurance contributions payable by employers
Identifier: EIM22715
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22715
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:24:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:11:42.748978+00:00

Employers are liable to pay Class 1A National Insurance contributions on most taxable benefits. This includes van and van fuel benefits.

Full details are in the National Insurance Manual; see the index to Class 1A NICs at NIM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13000).
