Tax treatment of visiting teachers

Where a claim for exemption or part exemption of remuneration from teaching arises under a double taxation agreement, follow the instructions in DT226 and DT1935 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt1935) to DT1945. As regards procedure, see also EP8105.
