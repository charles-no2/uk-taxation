Tax treatment of foreign students employed in the United Kingdom during their holidays

Action

Procedural instructions covering vacation jobs taken by foreign students in the United Kingdom on visits sponsored by several organisations are at PAYE81585 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-manual/paye81585). The guidance also tells you what to do in other circumstances.

Double taxation agreement

If, in a particular case, the above instructions do not apply, consider whether exemption under a double taxation agreement is due (see DT2140pp (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt2140pp) and DT1930 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt1930) to DT1931 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt1931)).

Payments to students without deduction of tax

For the arrangements under which payments may be made without deduction of tax to students engaged for vacation work, including those from abroad, see PAYE81530 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-manual/paye81530).
