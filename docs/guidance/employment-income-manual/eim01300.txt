Section 250 ITEPA 2003

The exemption in Section 250 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM01210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01210)) applies to training provided by a third party as well as to training provided by the employee’s employer. For example a washing machine manufacturer might provide training to the employees of its principal retail outlets. Section 250 ensures that there is no benefit chargeable on the employees.
