Non-approved schemes: tax charges

Sections 386, 393 and 394(1) ITEPA 2003

A tax charge arises where there is a lump sum receipt from a non-approved scheme (see definition in EIM15402 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15402)) before 6 April 2006 and where an employer makes a contribution to a non-approved scheme before 6 April 2006 (see EIM15412 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15412)).

The above guidance is subject to the following qualifications:

- pension income is always charged under Part 9 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM75000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim75000) and subsequent guidance)

- some receipts are excluded from charge in whole or in part (see EIM15422 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15422))

Contributions by the employee may need to be taken into account (see EIM15422 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15422)).
