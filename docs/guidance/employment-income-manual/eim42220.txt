Employment income: basis of assessment for general earnings: earnings received before an employment starts or after it ends
Identifier: EIM42220
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42220
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-05-23T12:18:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:14:38.861556+00:00

Sections 15(3), 22(3), 26(3) and 27(3) ITEPA 2003

General earnings are taxable in the year they are received (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42260) (or, in some special cases, received in the United Kingdom (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42370)) whether or not the office or employment is held at the time of receipt.

So if earnings are received before a job starts or after it ends you look at the year to which the emolument relates to see if it is taxable (see generally EIM40001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim40001) onwards). If it is, it’s taxable in the year of receipt even if the job had not started when it was received or had ended before it was received.

See:

- an example of a case where earnings are received before an employment starts (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42221)

- an example of a case where earnings are received after an employment ends (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42222).

If it’s claimed that earnings cannot be attributed to any period during which the job was held, see EIM40005 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim40005).

For earnings paid after the death of the employee, see EIM42380 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42380) to EIM42400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42400).
