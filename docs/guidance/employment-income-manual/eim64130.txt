Tax treatment of Association footballers: payments  for representative appearances
Identifier: EIM64130
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64130
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T15:00:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:09.587902+00:00

An Association footballer who represents England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland may be entitled to a match fee. Payment is made to the player’s club, which subjects the fee to PAYE in the normal way.
