The general rule for employees: expenses: how to find the advice you need

To check whether a deduction can be made under the general rule for employers’ expenses, you should follow the steps below in order:

- Is the employment included in the list in EIM31623 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31623)? If it is then look at the advice on that employment to find what deductions can be made

- Is the expense listed at EIM31624 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31624)? If it is then look at the advice on that expense to find out whether a deduction can be made

- Otherwise, look at the general advice on the operation of the rule beginning at EIM31630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31630)
