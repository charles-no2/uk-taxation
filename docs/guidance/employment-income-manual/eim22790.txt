Section 155 ITEPA 2003 and section 58(12) Finance Act 2010

There are 2 levels of van benefit charge from the tax year 2005 to 2006 onwards, depending on whether or not the restricted private use condition (EIM22795 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22795)) is met.

Vans for which the restricted private use condition is met - section 155(2)(a)

2005 to 2006 onwards | Nil |

Zero emissions vans

A zero emission van is one which cannot in any circumstances emit CO2 by being driven.

For tax years 2010 to 2011 to 2014 to 2015 inclusive, the cash equivalent for such vans is always nil.

For tax years 2021 to 2022 onwards, the cash equivalent for such vans is also nil.

Adjustments to the charge for the whole year

The van benefit charge for the whole year is subject to reduction where:

- the van is unavailable for part of the year (EIM22820 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22820))

- the van is shared for some or all of the period for which it is available (EIM22830 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22830))

- the employee makes payments for private use of the van (EIM22840 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22840))

Examples

There are basic examples at EIM22870 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22870) and more complex ones on later pages.

Additional charges

There are additional charges where:

- fuel is provided for a van for which the restricted private use condition is not met, EIM22900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22900) onwards

- exceptionally, expenses are incurred by or on behalf of the employer in providing a driver for the van, section 239(5); see the residual benefit rules from EIM21001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21001)
