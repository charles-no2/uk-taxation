Section 401 ITEPA 2003

A settlement agreement frequently includes items that are not payments of money. Where they are taxable only under section 401 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM12810 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim12810) and EIM13030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13030)), where the benefit is received on or after 6 April 1998, see EIM13270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13270)

EIM13110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim13110) explains what received means.
