Sections 272 and 284 ITEPA 2003

To qualify for the exemption expenses and benefits have to fall within Section 272(1)(c):

- disposal or intended disposal of old residence (see EIM03109 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03109))

- acquisition or intended acquisition of new residence (see EIM03110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03110))

- transporting belongings (see EIM03111 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03111))

- travelling and subsistence (see EIM03112 to EIM03119)

- domestic goods for the new residence (see EIM03120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03120)).

Bridging loan expenses may also be exempt if they are within Section 284 (see EIM03121 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03121) to EIM03125).
