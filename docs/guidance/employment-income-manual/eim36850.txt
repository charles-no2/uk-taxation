Section 262 CAA2001 and Section 7(5) ITEPA 2003

Capital allowances

Capital allowances are given as a deduction from earnings, in the same way as expenses under section 336 ITEPA 2003. If the allowances due exceed the emoluments, see EIM36890 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim36890).

Balancing charges

Balancing charges relating to employees and office holders are chargeable to tax as earnings from the employment or office.
