Tax treatment of Association footballers: travelling expenses
Identifier: EIM64140
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64140
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-28T15:08:09+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:16:09.942872+00:00

There is a tax charge when a club meets the expense incurred by a contracted Association footballer or a professional Rugby footballer in travelling between his home and the home ground. Cash payments are taxable as earnings within section 62 ITEPA 2003 (see EIM10020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim10020)). Travel vouchers are chargeable under Section 87 (see EIM16060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16060)).

As regards expenses payments to non-contract and amateur players, see EIM64145 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim64145).
