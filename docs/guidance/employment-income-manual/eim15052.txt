Employer-financed retirement benefits schemes: periods when a scheme is not registered
Identifier: EIM15052
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15052
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-01-20T12:37:47+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:10:55.214790+00:00

From 6 April 2006, a registered pension scheme may be de-registered by Pension Schemes Services. From the date of de-registration it will be an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme (see EIM15010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim15010)).
