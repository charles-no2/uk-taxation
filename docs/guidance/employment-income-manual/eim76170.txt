Deaths before 6 April 2017

From 9 April 2001 until 5 April 2017, a new system of bereavement benefits (BB) for widows and widowers replaced the previous widow’s benefits (WB). For details of WB before 9 April 2001 see EIM76176 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76176).

BB replaced the WB previously available only to widows of working age, but the changes did not affect those receiving WB before 9 April 2001. Widows receiving widowed mother’s allowance (see EIM76177 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76177)) or widow’s pension (see EIM76178 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76178)) before that date, who meet the previous entitlement conditions, continued to receive WB.

Bereavement benefits (BB)

These benefits were available to people who were legally married or in a civil partnership but were not available to:

- persons divorced from their late husband or wife or whose civil partnership has been dissolved

- persons who remarry following the death of a husband or wife or forms a new civil partnership

- persons living with someone else as husband or wife or a civil partners

- persons in prison or in legal custody.

Types of BB

There were three types of BB:

- bereavement payment, see EIM76171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76171)

- widowed parent’s allowance, see EIM76172 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76172)

- bereavement allowance, see EIM76173 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76173).

Information

If claimants ask for advice and/or help in claiming BB they should be directed to the nearest Benefit Agency Office.

Deaths on or after 6 April 2017

Bereavement Support Payment (BSP) has replaced Bereavement Allowance, Bereavement Payment, and Widowed Parent’s Allowance for deaths on or after 6 April 2017.

See EIM76171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76171) for details of BSP.
