Particular benefits: transfer of used or depreciated assets: living accommodation
Identifier: EIM21656
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21656
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T12:24:47+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:24:21.981316+00:00

The special rule at EIM21650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21650) relating to the transfer of an asset which has previously been available for the private use of a director or employee does not normally apply to living accommodation. It does apply to non-residential accommodation such as a garage or workshop.

The transfer of living accommodation will normally represent earnings from the employment chargeable under Section 62 ITEPA 2003. The special rule at EIM21655 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21655) relating to an asset which has depreciated or been used should only be invoked if liability under Section 62 cannot be established (see EIM08001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim08001) onwards). The guidance at EIM11478 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11478) and EIM11479 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11479) should be followed with any necessary modifications as regards:

- the information the District Valuer will need if you ask for a “not negotiated” valuation and

- what you should do if the employee disputes the District Valuer’s opinion on appeal against a charge to tax on employment income.
