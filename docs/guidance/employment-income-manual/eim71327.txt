Travel allowance

The Building Engineering Services Association (BESA) pays 2 elements of travel allowance:

- Daily Travelling Allowance payments

- Reimbursement of Fares

Daily Travelling Allowance payments

These relate to daily travelling time and are fully taxable.

Reimbursement of Fares

This can take the following forms:

- public transport fare for employee’s travel from centre to site - payable free of tax, or

- mileage rate of 25p (20p until 2018) per mile, all miles paid free of tax
