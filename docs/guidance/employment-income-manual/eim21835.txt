Sections 265, 266(1) and 267(1) ITEPA 2003

An employee or office holder is exempt from tax on employment income on certain goodwill entertainment, provided by third parties, for him or her or for a member of their family or household. Details are at EIM21837 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21837).

The exemption applies to all employees whether or not for 2015/16 and earlier, they are directors or employees in an excluded employment (see EIM20007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20007)).

The exemption covers liability that could otherwise arise under the following provisions:

- Part 3 Chapter 4 ITEPA 2003 (non-cash vouchers, see EIM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16000) onwards)

- Part 3 Chapter 4 ITEPA 2003 (credit-tokens, see EIM16090 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16090) onwards)

- Part 3 Chapter 6 ITEPA 2003 (benefits of directors and employees, except those in an excluded employment).

For the meaning of “family or household” see EIM20504 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim20504).
