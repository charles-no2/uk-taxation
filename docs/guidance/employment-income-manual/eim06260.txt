Section 776 IT(TOI)A 2005

Scholarships or studentships awarded by a university or other educational establishment from its own funds or from endowments under its control should be considered for tax purposes in accordance with the instructions at EIM06235 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim06235) and EIM06250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim06250).
