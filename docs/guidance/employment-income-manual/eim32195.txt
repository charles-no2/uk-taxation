Travel expenses: travel for necessary attendance: duties defined by reference to a particular area: some duties  outside the area
Identifier: EIM32195
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32195
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2015-10-02T13:19:14+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:21:51.827969+00:00

Where the conditions in EIM32190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32190) are met the whole of the geographical area is treated as a permanent workplace. The occasional performance of duties outside the area does not prevent that area from being a permanent workplace.

This is illustrated by example EIM32204 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32204).
