Social security benefits: dependency additions
Identifier: EIM76102

Sometimes a claimant can get extra benefit if he or she has a dependent.

Child dependency additions are not taxable, even if the basic benefit itself is taxable.

Where an adult dependency addition is payable the following rules apply:

- if the benefit is listed at EIM76100  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76100)the adult dependency addition is not taxable

- if the benefit is listed at EIM76101  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim76101)the adult dependency addition is taxable.
