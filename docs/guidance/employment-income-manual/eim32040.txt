Section 337 to 339 ITEPA 2003

Two or more employers may act together on a particular project and may form a joint enterprise for this purpose. An employee of either of them whose duties require him or her to act on behalf of the joint enterprise will be entitled to a deduction for the cost of business travel.

The practical application of this rule is illustrated by example EIM32041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32041).
