Section 230 ITEPA 2003

This page illustrates the calculation of chargeable mileage allowance payments and mileage allowance relief (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31235) where no mileage allowance payments are made for business travel in an employee’s own vehicle (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31260).

Example for tax year 2025 to 2026

Employee A uses their own car for occasional business travel and covers 860 miles in the tax year 2025 to 2026; they receive nothing in recognition of this.

The calculation (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31235) is:

Step 1: find the amount of mileage allowance payments (MAPs) received

Maps received: nil

Step 2: deduct the approved (exempt) amount (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31230)

Approved amount: 860 miles × 45 pence = £387

Step 3: is the answer positive or negative

The answer is negative:

- mileage allowance payments received: nil

- mileage allowance relief available (EIM31330 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31330)): £387
