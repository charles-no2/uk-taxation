The benefits code: beneficial loans: calculation of  the cash equivalent: the normal averaging method

Section 182 ITEPA 2003

The normal averaging method of calculation is based on:
- the average amount of the loan (or aggregate loan, see EIM26180  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26180)onwards) calculated by reference to its maximum opening and closing balances (see EIM26212 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26212)) at the beginning and end of the tax year. If the loan was not in existence throughout the whole year, the average is based on the maximum balances on the dates the loan was made or discharged and - the average appropriate official rate of interest (see EIM26104 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26104)) for the tax year, or for such shorter period as the loan was in existence.

See EIM26215  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26215)for how to perform the calculation step-by-step.

See EIM26300  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26300)for a list of examples showing the averaging method of working out the cash equivalent of a beneficial loan.
