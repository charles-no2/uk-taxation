Finance (No 2) Act 2017: part 3A: 2019 loan charge reporting requirement: loan charge information: para 35F: penalties 
Identifier: EIM47196
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim47196
Published: 2018-10-05T14:39:42+01:00
Updated: 2018-10-05T14:38:55+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:25:44.398296+00:00

A person who fails to provide loan charge information is liable to a penalty of £300. If the failure to provide information continues after the date when such a penalty is imposed, the person is liable to further penalties not exceeding £60 for each subsequent day, up to a maximum of 90 days, that the failure continues.
