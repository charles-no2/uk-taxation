The general rule for employees: expenses: relief is limited to actual expenses incurred: example
Identifier: EIM31633
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31633
Published: 2014-05-22T10:00:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-02-08T14:39:00+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:13:06.538066+00:00

An employee has to travel on business. Her employer pays her a travel allowance that is enough to buy a standard class rail ticket but she chooses to travel first class and pays the difference herself. The allowance is taxable but she can deduct the full cost that she incurred from the whole of her earnings from that employment.
