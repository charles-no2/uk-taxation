Travel expenses: general: choice of route: costs  incurred in error

Sections 337 to 339 ITEPA 2003

Where, in the course of a business journey, an employee incurs additional expenditure through genuine error, that expenditure should be treated as a cost of business travel and not as personal expenditure.
