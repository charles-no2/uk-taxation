EIM45355 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45355) onwards are about Section 554J. Section 554J is relevant to employee share schemes with specified vesting dates.

EIM45380 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45380) explains how Sections 554J and 554K are related.

EIM45385 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45385) onwards are about Section 554K. Section 554K is relevant to employee share schemes with specified exit events.

EIM45405 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45405) onwards are about Section 554L. Section 554L is relevant to employee share option schemes with specified vesting dates.

EIM45430 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45430) explains how Sections 554L and 554M are related.

EIM45435 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45435) onwards are about Section 554M. Section 554M is relevant to employee share option schemes with specified exit events.

EIM45455 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45455) onwards explain some key terms used in the Part 7A rules about employee share and share option schemes.
