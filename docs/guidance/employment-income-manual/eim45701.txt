The next sequence of pages tells you about Part 7A income.

EIM45705 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45705) tells you about Part 7A income generally.

The pages that follow tell you how to measure Part 7A income.

EIM45710 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45710) gives you the main rules on the measure of Part 7A income.

EIM45715 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45715) is about the special rule which applies if the step is within Section 554Z19.

In certain circumstances, other valuation rules apply which override the rules in EIM45710 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45710) and EIM45715 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45715). EIM45720 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45720) onwards explain these rules.

For example, if a transaction gives rise both to general earnings and to Part 7A income, the general earnings may (depending on the facts) reduce the Part 7A income to nil. See EIM45735 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim45735).
