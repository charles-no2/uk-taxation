Specific deductions - employee benefit trusts: main uses
Identifier: BIM44510
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44510
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:20:13+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:23:42.987758+00:00

Some EBTs are set up to provide employees and directors with specific kinds of benefits such as:

- share-related benefits under employee share schemes set up to give employees a stake in the company or group by which they are employed - see BIM44515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44515),

- pension and other benefits under retirement benefit schemes - see BIM44520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44520),

- accident benefits - see BIM44525 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44525),

- healthcare benefits - see BIM44530 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44530).

EBTs may also be set up for more general purposes. Guidance on deductions for employers’ contributions to general-purpose EBTs is at BIM44535 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44535) onwards.
