Specific receipts: fees: articled pupils or apprentices
Identifier: BIM40360
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim40360
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:13:24+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:22:19.936740+00:00

Fees or premiums received for articled pupils or apprentices by a person carrying on a profession (for example, as a solicitor, architect, accountant or engineer) should be treated as ordinary professional receipts.
