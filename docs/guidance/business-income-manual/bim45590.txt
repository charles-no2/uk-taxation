Specific deductions: insurance: premiums paid to mutual concerns
Identifier: BIM45590
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim45590
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:20:35+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:23:57.835008+00:00

Insurance premiums otherwise admissible as a deduction should not be regarded as inadmissible on the grounds that they are paid to an insurance association that is a mutual concern (see Thomas v Richard Evans and Co Ltd. [1927] 11 TC 790).
