Specific deductions: subscriptions: trade associations
Identifier: BIM47420
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47420
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2018-11-27T11:41:00+00:00

A member is entitled to deduct part of his subscription to a trade protection or other trade association. The allowable part is that applied by the association towards expenditure which would have been admissible as a deduction if incurred by the member himself (Lochgelly Iron & Coal Co Ltd v Crawford [1913] 6TC267; Thomas Merthyr Colliery Co Ltd v Davis [1932} 17TC519; Joseph L Thompson and Sons Ltd v Chamberlain [1962] 40TC657).

For the allowance of subscriptions where an HMRC arrangement is adopted, see BIM47425 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47425).

For subscriptions to local trade associations and foreign associations, see BIM47430 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47430).

For detailed guidance on the taxation of trade protection associations, see BIM24800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim24800) onwards.
