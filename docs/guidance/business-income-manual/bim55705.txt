Farming: stud farms: open market valuations of thoroughbred horses
Identifier: BIM55705
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim55705
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:29:23+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:24:42.918609+00:00

Specialist advice on open market valuations of thoroughbred horses is available from the Bloodstock Section, Shares and Assets Valuation, Nottingham. Requests for advice should include

- the name(s) of the horse(s) to be valued

- the valuation dates

- any opinion of open market value together with reasoning, if supplied.
