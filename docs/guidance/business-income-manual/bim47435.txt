Specific deductions: subscriptions: collective publicity schemes
Identifier: BIM47435
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47435
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:21:28+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:24:23.217334+00:00

Contributions by a trader to a collective publicity scheme organised by a committee or other body representing the general interests of a town or area should be allowed as a deduction. This is subject to the contributions being out-and-out payments and of a reasonable amount in relation to the benefit likely to accrue to his business.

Help should be obtained from Business Profits on the treatment of contributions to trade publicity funds where substantial contributions are involved. A modified arrangement on the lines of BIM47425 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47425) is usually made in such cases.
