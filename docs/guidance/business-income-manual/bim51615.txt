Builders, property dealers & developers: valuation: contentious appeal
Identifier: BIM51615

Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:28:46+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:03.684171+00:00

Before making arrangements for a contentious appeal hearing before the First-tier Tribunal on a trading income valuation point, a report, accompanied by the file, should be made to Business Profits (Technical).

The report should set out:

- the amount of tax at stake as a result of the disputed valuation, and

- confirm that the principle of using open market value has been accepted by the taxpayer and that it is only the amount of the valuation which is in dispute.
