Customers should be referred to our online guidance ‘Unincorporated organisations and Corporation Tax’ at https://www.gov.uk/corporation-tax (https://www.gov.uk/corporation-tax). Customers include anyone concerned with the organisation of a club, society, residents’ association or other similar body, where it is helpful.
