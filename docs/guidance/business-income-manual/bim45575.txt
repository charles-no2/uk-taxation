Specific deductions: insurance: customers
Identifier: BIM45575
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim45575
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:20:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:23:57.518052+00:00

The premiums paid by a trader on insurance policies taken out wholly and exclusively for the benefit of customers are allowable as a deduction.
