Cash basis: rules not applied in calculating profits
Identifier: BIM72050
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim72050
Published: 2025-04-07T09:53:27+01:00

In calculating the profits of a trade using the cash basis, certain rules do not apply, and some claims and reliefs are not available.

Claims, elections and reliefs not available when using the cash basis:

- Herd basis rules (see BIM55501 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim55501))

- Averaging profits of farmers and creative artists (see BIM84050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84050))

- Compensation for compulsory slaughter of animals (see BIM55180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim55180))

- Relief for unremittable amounts

Special rules not applied in calculating profits on the cash basis:

- Sound recordings (see BIM56200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim56200))

- Certain telecommunication rights (Chapter 9 or Part 2 ITTOIA 2005)

- Long-funding leases (Chapter 10A of Part 2 ITTOIA 2005)

- Changes in trading stock (see BIM33630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim33630))

- Disposal and acquisition of know-how (Chapter 14 of Part 2 ITTOIA 2005)

- Oil activities (Chapter 16A of Part 2 ITTOIA 2005)
