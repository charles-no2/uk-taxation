Meaning of trade: mutual trading and members clubs: members' clubs: commission income
Identifier: BIM24315
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim24315
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:06:35+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:19:11.149261+00:00

The proprietor of vending or fruit machines, or electronic games, will often pay a commission to a members’ club for the right to install machines on the club’s premises. These commissions are not assessable on the club as either trading income or miscellaneous income.
