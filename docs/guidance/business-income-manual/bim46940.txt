Specific deductions: repairs and renewals: what is a repair: required by a lease
Identifier: BIM46940
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim46940
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:20:59+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:24:16.355776+00:00

A taxpayer may lease a property. If, as part of the lease, the tenant agrees to restore the property to a good state of repair, then the repairs are capital expenditure. See BIM35620 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim35620) for more information on the case law background.

The taxpayer may be able to get relief for all or part of the expenditure under the premium legislation. See BIM46250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim46250) onwards.
