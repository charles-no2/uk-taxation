Where to find further specific guidance

Unclaimed balances may be conveniently divided into four broad categories:

- receipts that become taxable receipts by operation of law (see BIM40230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim40230))

- overpayments (see BIM40240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim40240))

- holding of funds that always belong to someone else (see BIM40250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim40250))

- trade debts that are never paid (see BIM40255 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim40255))
