Doctors and dentists: remuneration withheld - breach of terms
Identifier: BIM54030
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim54030
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:28:58+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:10.594874+00:00

Amounts withheld from the remuneration of a medical or dental practitioner by virtue of regulations made under the National Health Service Acts because he or she is held to have committed a breach of his terms of service, are allowable deductions from the gross remuneration in computing their trading profits.
