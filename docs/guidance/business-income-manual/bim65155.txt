Research grants and fellowships - British Academy
Identifier: BIM65155

Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:37:59+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:57.820580+00:00

As a result of discussions with the British Academy, HMRC have agreed that in general, awards and grants it makes are not taxable. Where, however, such awards are received in the course of the beneficiary's profession or vocation, advice should be obtained from BAI (Business Profits). As regards other awards, see EIM06205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim06205) onwards and BIM50710 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim50710).
