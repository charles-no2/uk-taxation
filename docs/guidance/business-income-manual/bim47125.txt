Specific deductions: staffing costs: locums
Identifier: BIM47125
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim47125
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:21:03+01:00

The expense incurred by a doctor or other professional person in respect of a locum (i.e. a substitute or temporary replacement professional) is an allowable deduction.
