Care providers: qualifying care relief: partnerships
Identifier: BIM52770
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim52770
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:28:54+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:07.721693+00:00

People cared for are placed with individuals, not with partnerships. However, it is accepted that a partnership can carry on a business of providing foster care or shared lives care. Where this is the case, a partnership tax return should be completed on the basis that the qualifying amount relates to the partnership rather than to the individuals involved in the partnership.
