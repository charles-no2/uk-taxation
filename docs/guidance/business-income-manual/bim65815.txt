Solicitors: Duty to deduct tax on interest paid to a client
Identifier: BIM65815
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim65815
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:38:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:58.460145+00:00

The solicitor will be required to deduct tax on interest paid to clients only where the interest is both:

- ‘yearly’, and

- paid to a client whose usual place of abode is overseas.

The obligation to deduct tax on yearly interest is discussed further in SAIM9070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim9070) onwards.

Note that as the solicitor is a person by or through whom interest is paid, they are a 'relevant data holder' for the purposes of Sch23 Finance Act 2011. This means they can be required to produce details of these payments under the data-gathering powers (see BIM65820 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim65820)).
