Measuring the profits (particular trades): land: trading transactions: timeshare schemes - builders and developers
Identifier: BIM60145
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim60145
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:37:08+01:00

Where a builder or trading developer constructs and sells timeshares, we consider that the timeshare receipts should be charged as trading receipts. The developer is merely exploiting land in the most advantageous manner.
