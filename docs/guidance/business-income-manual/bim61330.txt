Leasing: avoidance: sale and leaseback of land: liability of recipient not affected
Identifier: BIM61330
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim61330
Published: 2013-11-22T09:00:00+00:00
Updated: 2016-04-01T15:37:23+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:37.908018+00:00

It should be noted that an adjustment under the guidance at BIM61301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim61301) onwards affects only the lessee and does not affect the liability of the recipient of a payment in respect of land.
