To carry out an initial review of an averaging claim, check that:

- The trade, profession or vocation is a qualifying one (see BIM84050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84050)).

- Where the trade is farming, market gardening or intensive rearing of livestock or fish, it is carried on in the UK (see BIM84050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84050)).

- The claimant is not a company (see BIM84050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84050)).

- The claim was made not later than the normal time limit, or, exceptionally, under the extended time limit for further claims (see BIM84150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84150)).

- The claim covers two or five consecutive tax years (see BIM84115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84115)).

- The profits dealt with by the claim are the trade profits after capital allowances and balancing charges (see BIM84120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84120)).

- Of the profit figures, the lower figure is less than 75% of the higher figure (see BIM84130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim84130)).
