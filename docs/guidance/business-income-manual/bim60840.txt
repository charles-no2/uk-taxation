Definitions: Relevant asset
Identifier: BIM60840
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim60840
Published: 2017-01-28T22:15:48+00:00
Updated: 2017-01-28T22:15:43+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:24:49.515223+00:00

A relevant asset is defined for the purposes of Section 356OE(6) and Section 517E(6) as land, or property which derives the whole or part of its value from land.
