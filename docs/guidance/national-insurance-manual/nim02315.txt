Class 1 NICs: Earnings of employees and office holders: Religious orders
Identifier: NIM02315
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02315
Published: 2016-04-11T17:04:26+01:00
Updated: 2021-11-18T13:33:34+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:13:29.905923+00:00

Include in gross pay any payments made to a member of a religious order which are identifiable as earnings regardless of whether they covenant all or part of the earnings to the order. See NIM02010  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02010)for guidance on the meaning of “earnings”.

See NIM02260  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02260)for the treatment of offerings, gifts or fees paid to ministers of religion.
