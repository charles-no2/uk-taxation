Paragraph 18(3) of Part 9 of Schedule 3 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations (SSCR) 2001 (As inserted by The Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 2) Part 1, Regulation 21(3) 2015 / Statutory Instruments 2015 No. 478)

Paragraph 18(3) of Part 9 of Schedule 3 SSCR 2001 provides the calculation by adding together every instalment (see NIM06890 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06890)) that satisfies the conditions in paragraph 18(4) - (6) of Part 9 of Schedule 3 SSCR 2001, see NIM06900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06900), NIM06905 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06905), and NIM06910 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06910).

N.B. This calculation is sometimes referred to or known as “apportionment”.
