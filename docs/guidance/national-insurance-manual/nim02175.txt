Class 1 NICs: Earnings of employees and office holders: Give as You Earn (GAYE)
Identifier: NIM02175
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02175
Published: 2016-04-11T17:03:10+01:00
Updated: 2021-10-07T14:51:20+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:13:17.619930+00:00

These are schemes approved by HMRC to allow tax relief on gifts to charities. The employee authorises deductions from their gross pay but such deductions are not free of NIC liability. NICs will still be due on the full gross pay.
