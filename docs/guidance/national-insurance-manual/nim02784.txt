You must read this page in conjunction with NIM02780 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02780) (first considerations) and NIM02782 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02782) (lump sums).

Payment out of the EFRBS is a serious ill-health lump sum.

Step 1

Before the payment is made, has the “responsible person” (NIM02765) (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02765) received written evidence from a registered medical practitioner that the employee is expected to live for less that one year?

Yes Go to step 2

No Go to step 5

Step 2

Does the lump sum extinguish the individuals entitlement to benefits under the arrangement (NIM02771) (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02771)?

Yes Go to step 3

No Go to step 5

Step 3

Is the lump sum paid before the individual is aged 75?

Yes Go to step 4

No Go to step 5

Step 4

Payment can be disregarded in the calculation of earnings. See NIM (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim14510)14510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim14510) for Class 1A position.

Step 5

Payment cannot be disregarded in the calculation of earnings.
