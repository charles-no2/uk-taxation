There are no specific regulations for ROW countries, in most cases we consider liability under domestic regulations,

The Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992

- Section 1(6) - liability to pay Class 1, Class1A and 2 contributions

- Section 2(1) (a) - definition of employed earner

The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

- Regulation 145 - Conditions as to residence or presence in GB

Further infromation on going to or coming from a ROW country see NIM33500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33500)
