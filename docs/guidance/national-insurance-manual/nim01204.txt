Section 5 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (SSCBA 1992) and Regulations 10 & 11 of the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 ( SI 2001 No 1004)

The Primary Threshold (PT) and Secondary Threshold (ST) represent the points from which payment of NICs arises - as opposed to the point at which entitlement to benefit builds up and which is set by the LEL. The monthly and yearly PT and ST are fixed each year, for years prior to 6 April 2011, see NIM01008 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01008).

From 6 April 2011, the PT and ST are no longer:

- set at the same level for primary and secondary NICs (see NIM01011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01011))

- aligned with the Income Tax personal allowance.

For guidance on how to calculate the PT and the ST for pay intervals of other than one month or one year from 6 April 2011, see NIM01010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01010).

For further guidance on NIC thresholds, see NIM11000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim11000) onwards. If aggregation of earnings is involved see NIM01004 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01004), see also NIM10000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim10000) onwards.
