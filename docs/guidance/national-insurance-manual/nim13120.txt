Class 1A National Insurance contributions: Liability for Class  1A NICs: Employer is self-employed or trading as a partnership
Identifier: NIM13120
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13120
Published: 2016-04-11T17:28:58+01:00
Updated: 2022-05-04T11:35:18+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:15:26.629800+00:00

If a self-employed earner or partnership provides benefits to an employee and the conditions outlined at NIM13021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13021) are fully satisfied, Class 1A NICs are due in the normal way. For example, a partnership may own a company van and allow one of its employees to use the van privately.

However, there are no Class 1A NICs payable on benefits provided to someone who is self-employed or who is trading as a partnership. For example, a partnership may own a van which both partners may use for private purposes, but no Class 1A NICs are due.
