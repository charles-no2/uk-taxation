Paragraph 21A of Schedule 4 and Schedule 4A to Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Where an employer is required to make a return of information (see NIM11520 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim11520)), they must report the data specified in Schedule 4A (but please see special rules for aggregated earnings - NIM11545 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim11545)).

Guidance (http://www.gov.uk/) on the requirements for individual data items is published for employers.

Detailed guidance (https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/real-time-information-online-internet-submissions-support-for-software-developers#internet-submissions) is also provided for software developers.
