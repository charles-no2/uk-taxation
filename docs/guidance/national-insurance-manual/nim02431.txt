Regulation 25 and paragraph 5B(e) of Part 5 of Schedule 3 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

Sections 266(3) and 317 Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003

NIM02416 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02416) shows the types of non-cash voucher (see NIM02413 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02413)) that are specifically disregarded in calculating an employee’s earnings for Class 1 NICs purposes. Since 6th April 1999, a non-cash voucher which is exempt from income tax by virtue of the above tax provisions is so disregarded.

For details of the tax position of such vouchers, see EIM16105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim16105).
