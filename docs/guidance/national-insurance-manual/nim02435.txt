Regulation 25 and paragraph 6(d) of Part 5 of Schedule 3 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

Section 323 Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003

NIM02416 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02416) shows the types of non-cash voucher (see NIM02413 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02413)) that are specifically disregarded in calculating an employee’s earnings for Class 1 NICs purposes. Since 6th April 1999, a non-cash voucher which is exempt from income tax by virtue of the above tax provision is so disregarded.

For details of the tax position of long service awards, see EIM01500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01500) - EIM01510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim01510).
