Section 3 & 6 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (SSCBA) 1992

Earnings includes any remuneration or profit derived from an employment.

Liability for Class 1 NICs arises at the time the earnings (see NIM02000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02000) onwards) are earned. The Class 1 NICs liability is assessed and paid when the earnings are placed unconditionally, at the employee’s disposal.
