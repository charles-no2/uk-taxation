*DCC

New Zealand - The agreement is to apply UK Domestic Legislation.

Switzerland

From 1/6/2002 the EC Social Security Regulations will apply to Switzerland by virtue of a special EC agreement. The agreement between Switzerland and the EC covers Swiss and EEA nationals and includes the UK.

Anyone who is not a national of the EEA / Swiss national, or is not a stateless person or refugee living in the EEA or Switzerland, will continue to be covered by the bilateral convention between the UK and Switzerland.

Isle of Man

The Isle of Man agreement is limited and liability is generally determined under place of residence.

Contributions can only be paid in one country. As a general rule an employee will be insurable in the country where they work. Where a person falls within UK legislation they are treated as being ordinarily resident in the UK and are liable for Class 1 from the outset of employment in the UK.
