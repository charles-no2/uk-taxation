Special Cases: Married Women who are Crown Servants: Certificates of Election
Identifier: NIM30016
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim30016
Published: 2016-04-11T17:45:54+01:00
Updated: 2022-12-09T13:20:38+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:17:01.096110+00:00

Certain employers, e.g. Civil Service, HM Forces, do not have to hold certificates of election for married women employees who, since before 6 April 1975, have:

- had a reduced rate election; and

- been continuously employed.
