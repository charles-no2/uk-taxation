Class 1 Structural Overview: zero-rate of secondary NICs for Freeport and Investment Zone special tax site employees: zero-rate of secondary Class 1 NICs for employees under the age of 21, apprentices under the age of 25 and Armed Forces Veterans
Identifier: NIM01550
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01550
Published: 2022-04-05T20:37:39+01:00
Updated: 2022-04-05T20:37:13+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:18:23.249208+00:00

Where:

- the zero-rate rate of secondary Class 1 NICs for employees under the age of 21 (see NIM01295 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01295)) applies,

- the zero-rate rate of secondary Class 1 NICs for apprentices under the age of 25 (see NIM01305 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01305)) applies, or

- the zero-rate of secondary Class 1 NICs for Veterans (see NIM01400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01400)) applies

then employers should claim that relief instead of the zero-rate of secondary NICs for Freeport or Investment Zone employees.
