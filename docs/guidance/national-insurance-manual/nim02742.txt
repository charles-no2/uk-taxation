Paragraph 11 of Part 6 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security (Contributions)Regulations 2001 (as amended by regulation 8(7) of the Social Security (Contributions)(Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2006, and regulation 4 of the Social Security(Contributions) (Amendment No.5) Regulations 2006)

Section 615(3) ICTA 1988

NIM02741 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02741)

explains the meaning of a “section 615” scheme.

A payment by way of:

- a pension; or

- an annuity

from a section 615 scheme is disregarded in the calculation of earnings for Class 1NICs purposes.

For details of:

- the Class 1A NICs position, see NIM14510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim14510)

- the income tax position on lump sum payments from such schemes, see EIM15064.
