Regulation 65(2) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No 1004)

Higher rates did not apply and contributions were payable at their original rate if the two tests set out in the regulation are both satisfied.

- The first test is that any failure to pay the contributions within the time limits was due to the contributor’s ignorance or error.

- The second test is that ignorance or error was not caused by the contributor failing to exercise due care and diligence.

NIM23005 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim23005) shows how the tests were satisfied. The first test must be satisfied before the second test can be applied. If there is no ignorance or error, there is no need to establish due care and diligence.

See NIM23010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim23010) if a person asserted that the higher rates should not be applied because HMRC did not issue payment requests.
