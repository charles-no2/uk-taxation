Class 1 NICs : Earnings of employees and office  holders : Profit-related pay

Profit-related pay (PRP) is that part of an employee’s pay which varies according to the profits made by the business in which they work. Half of an employee’s PRP was able to be exempt from income tax until recent changes ended the tax relief. There has, however, never been any exclusion from liability for NICs purposes.
