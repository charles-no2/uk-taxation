Special Cases: Married Women: Continuing to pay reduced rate after cancelling an election
Identifier: NIM30012
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim30012
Published: 2016-04-11T17:45:47+01:00
Updated: 2022-12-09T13:14:10+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:16:59.623672+00:00

A woman who cancels her election can change her mind and continue paying reduced rate if she tells the Department in writing before the date on which payment of full rate is due to start. Pass any requests to continue paying reduced rate contributions to PT Ops North East.
