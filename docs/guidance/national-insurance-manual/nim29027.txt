Part 9, Case C of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

For mariners and their employers SSCR 2001 gives effect to the policy objective of successive Governments to protect the benefit entitlement of mariners by extending their ability to pay NICs beyond the shores of the UK in certain circumstances.

For the purposes of this guidance it is assumed that the mariner is not on a vessel where the EC Regulations or a Double Contributions Convention/Reciprocal Agreement applies.

NIM29003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29003) to NIM29006 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29006) deals with vessels registered in such countries.

NIM29008 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29008) - NIM29024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29024) sets out the special rules for Mariners and their employers

NIM29008 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29008) sets out the definition of a Mariner.
