Regulations 89 and 90 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

NIM20300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim20300) explains when Class 2 National Insurance contributions (NICs) were collected by DD. Where

- DD arrangements broke down; and

- it was not possible to resume payment by DD; and

- HMRC were not advised that the liable person was no longer liable to pay;

HMRC issued six monthly payment requests, see NIM20550 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim20550).

Where this happened before 10 April 2011, HMRC issued payment requests quarterly, which was referred to as quarterly billing. See NIM20500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim20500) for information about quarterly billing.
