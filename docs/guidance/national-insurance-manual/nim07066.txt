Class 1 NICs: Payments made under employment legislation: Re-allocating primary NICs paid on a payment made under a reinstatement order, a re-engagement order, an order for the continuation of a contract of employment or a protective award

Regulation 5(b) Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

In order to protect or enhance their benefit/pension position, an employee may request that NICs paid on a payment made under:

- a re-instatement order (see NIM07020  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07020)– NIM07021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07021));

- a re-engagement order (see NIM07030  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07030)– NIM07031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07031));

- an order for the continuation of a contract of employment (see NIM07040  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07040)onwards);

- a protective award (see NIM07050  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07050)onwards)

are treated as paid in an earlier tax year to the one in which the NICs were actually paid. Such a request should be made in writing.

The situations in which NICs may be re-allocated are as follows:

- the period to which the payment relates falls wholly in a tax year other than the one in which the NICs were paid (see NIM07067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07067));

- the period to which the payment relates falls partly in the tax year in which the NICs were paid and partly in one or more other tax years (see NIM07068 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07068));

- the period to which the payment relates falls wholly in two or more years other than the one in which the NICs were paid (see NIM07069 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07069)).

For:

- guidance on the information required in order to apportion the amount of primary NICs paid, see NIM07070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07070)

- an example of how to apportion the amount of primary NICs paid, see NIM07071

- guidance on adjusting the employee’s NI account, see NIM07072 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07072).
