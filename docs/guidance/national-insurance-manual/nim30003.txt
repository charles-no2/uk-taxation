Special Cases: Married Women: Effect of reduced rate on other contribution liabilities
Identifier: NIM30003
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim30003
Published: 2016-04-11T17:45:30+01:00
Updated: 2022-12-09T12:59:18+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:16:56.288861+00:00

Where a woman has a valid reduced rate election this does not affect

- the employer’s liability to pay secondary contributions, or

- the woman’s liability to pay any Class 4 contributions which may be due as a self-employed earner
