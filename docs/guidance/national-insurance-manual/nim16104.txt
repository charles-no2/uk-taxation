Regulation 40(5) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Class 1A NICs position before 6 April 2005

In the context of the exemption from Class 1A liability for childcare benefits described at NIM16102 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16102), the provision by the employer, or a third party, of non-cash vouchers which are exchangeable for child care, are also excluded from liability for Class 1A NICs. Regulation 40(5) of the SSCR 2001 refers.

Class 1A NICs position from 6 April 2005

NIM16102  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16102)explained that the Class 1A NICs exception in regulation 40(5) of the SSCR 2001 was repealed with effect from 6 April 2005. See NIM16110  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16110)for guidance. The income tax position is explained at EIM22010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim22010).

Class 1 NICs position from 6 April 2005

See NIM02439 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02439) for information about the Class 1 NICs treatment of non-cash vouchers exchangeable for childcare.
