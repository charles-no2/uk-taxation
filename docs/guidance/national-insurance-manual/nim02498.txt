Section 6(1) Social Security Contributions & Benefits Act 1992

The amount of a cash voucher (see NIM02497 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02497)) to be included in an employee’s earnings is an amount equal to the amount for which the voucher can be exchanged.

Liability for Class 1 NICs arises at the time the cash voucher is provided to the employee.

Example

A voucher:

- costs an employer £190 to provide

- can be exchanged for £200 cash.

The amount to be included in the employee’s earnings for NICs purposes is £200.

To calculate the amount of earnings where a cash voucher is provided to two or more employees, see NIM02499 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02499).
