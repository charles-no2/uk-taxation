Class 2 National Insurance Contributions: Small Earnings Exception: Special Groups: Members of religious orders
Identifier: NIM21024
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim21024
Published: 2016-04-11T17:38:32+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-23T13:13:21+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:16:05.105469+00:00

Members of religious orders who were self-employed were eligible to claim exception on the ground of small earnings. They normally took vows of poverty and lived as a community and their “earnings” consisted of the value of their maintenance. It was taken in such cases that this was less than the SEE limit.
