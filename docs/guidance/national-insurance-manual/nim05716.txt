Class 1 NICs: Expenses and allowances: Mileage allowances: Rules before 6 April 2002: Rates paid are at or below the authorised rates
Identifier: NIM05716
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05716
Published: 2016-04-11T17:12:43+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-28T17:25:15+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:21:51.603896+00:00

Where an employer’s mileage rates for business travel for periods to 5 April 2002 where paid at, or below, HMRC’s Authorised Mileage Rates (AMRs) (see EIM31240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31240)) for up to 4,000 miles, no Class 1 NICs became due. This applied irrespective of the level of the employee’s business mileage – see NIM05719 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05719) for an example.
