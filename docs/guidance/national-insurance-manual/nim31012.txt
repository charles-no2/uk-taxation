Regulation 128 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

If a qualifying widow remarries after the initial period (see NIM31003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31003)) the election will cease from the date of remarriage.
