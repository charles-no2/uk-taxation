Class 1A National Insurance contributions: Special Class 1A  NICs cases: Child care benefits from 6 April 2005: School fees

Depending upon the particular contractual arrangements, a Class 1 or a Class 1A NICs liability will arise if an employer meets the cost of school fees for the children of his employees, see NIM13180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13180).
