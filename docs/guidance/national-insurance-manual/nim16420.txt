Case F of Part 9 of the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

If a worker coming from or going abroad is subject to UK NICs legislation (see NIM16403 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16403) for more information), the period for which Class 1A NICs are due mirrors that for which Class 1 NICs are due.

Where liability for Class 1 NICs commences, liability for Class 1A NICs commences and where liability for Class 1 NICs ceases, liability for Class 1A NICs also ceases.

In some cases this may be part way through a tax year, requiring the amount of Class 1A NICs due to be calculated on a pro-rata basis, see NIM16500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16500).
