Class 1 NICs: Expenses and allowances: Relocation allowances: Employees who relocate abroad
Identifier: NIM06135
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06135
Published: 2016-04-11T17:15:07+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-15T14:49:01+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:19:17.855361+00:00

If there is a continuing liability for UK NICs in respect of a person who relocates abroad, any relocation allowances paid remain subject to the same conditions as an employee relocating within the UK.

This means that the guidance set out in the following paragraphs apply just as they would within the UK:

NIM06120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06120) |  Employees who start work at the new location on or after 6 April 1998 |

NIM06125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06125) |  Employees who started work at the new location before 6 April 1998 |

NIM06130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06130) |  Special provision for 1998/99 where relocation expenses included in a PAYE Settlement Agreement (PSA) |
