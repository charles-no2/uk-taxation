Regulation 44 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

Exception was not normally granted for a period in respect of which Class 2 contributions had already been paid (But see NIM22000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim22000) covering “SEE Refunds”). However, if exception was granted to someone paying contributions by Direct Debit, it was normally applied from the Sunday after the date of application and a refund was made in respect of contributions paid after this date.
