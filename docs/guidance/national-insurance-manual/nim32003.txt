Schedule 6; Part 1 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

- Any of the regular naval, military or air forces of the Crown

- Royal Naval Reserve

- Royal Marines Reserve

- Royal Fleet Reserve

- Royal Naval Special Reserve, the Special List

- Women’s Royal Naval Reserve and Women’s Royal Naval Supplementary Reserve

- Queen Alexandra’s Royal Naval Nursing Service Reserve

- Voluntary Air Detachment, the Naval Reserve

- Regular Army Reserve of Officers

- Army Reserves including Regular Reserves, Army General Reserve, Long Term Reserve and Army Pensioners

- Territorial and Army Volunteer Reserve

- Royal Air Force Reserve including Royal Air Force Reserves of Officers, Royal Air Force Volunteer Reserve, Women’s Royal Air Force Reserve of Officers and Women’s Royal Air Force Volunteer Reserve

- Royal Auxiliary Air Force including Women’s RAAF

- Princess Mary’s Royal Air Force Nursing Service Reserve

- Royal Irish Regiment to the extent that its members are not members of any force within paragraph 1 above
