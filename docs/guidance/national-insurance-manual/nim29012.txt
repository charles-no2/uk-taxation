Regulation 122 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Liability to pay secondary Class 1 NICs rests with the employer. If the employer of a mariner does not satisfy the residence or place of business conditions NIM29009 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29009) but the person who actually pays the mariner does satisfy the conditions then that person is treated as the secondary contributor.
