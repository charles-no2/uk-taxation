Regulation 149 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

A volunteer development worker is a person whom HMRC has agreed that they may pay the special rate of Class 2 NIC because they are:

- ordinarily resident in Great Britain or Northern Ireland; and

- employed outside of the United Kingdom; and

- working for an organisation approved by the Inland Revenue; and

- working in a country which HMRC recognises as a developing country.

For details of

- the meaning of “ordinarily resident”, see NIM33031, NIM33032 and NIM34010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim34010)

- approved organisations, see NIM34015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim34015)

- recognised developing countries, see NIM34020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim34020)

- the special rate of Class 2 NIC, see NIM34040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim34040).
