Voluntary National Insurance contributions for periods abroad – Provisions in Social Security Agreements affecting the payment of voluntary National Insurance contributions
Identifier: NIM33207
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33207
Published: 2026-05-07T15:12:09+01:00
Updated: 2026-05-07T15:12:09+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:21:09.649626+00:00

If a person is in scope of a Social Security Agreement (SSA) that the UK has with another country, there may be provisions which affect their entitlement to pay voluntary UK NICs.

Detailed below, are the relevant provisions within each SSA which should be considered.

Barbados (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/812)

See Articles 6(7), 6(8) and 9(3) of the agreement for further information.

Canada   (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/263/made)

See Articles 4(4) and 6(3) of the agreement for further information.

Chile   (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/828/made)

See Articles 5(6) and 8(3) of the agreement for further information.

Isle of Man (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/157/schedule)

See Article 9 of the agreement for further information.

Jamaica (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/871)

See Articles 6(7), 8 and 10(3) of the agreement for further information.

Japan  (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3063/schedule/made)

See Article 5(3) of the agreement for further information.

Jersey and Guernsey (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2802/contents)

See Articles 6(7) and 8 of the agreement for further information.

Republic of Korea (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1823/made/data.xht?wrap=true)

See Articles 4(5) and 7(3) of the agreement for further information.
