Until 1 January 1977, elections and cancellations were recorded on the record sheet, form RF1.

The terms used to show elections, cancellations, and notations on form RF1 are:

Notation |  Explanation |

MW/NP (date) |  An election not to pay by a woman whose categorisation (status) could not be determined from her application |

MW1/NP (date) |  an election not to pay flat-rate Class 1 contributions before 6 April 1975 |

MW/P (date) |  cancellation of election not to pay |

MW2/P (date) |  an election to pay flat-rate contributions before 6 April 1975 |

MW2/3 (date) |  an election by a self-employed woman to pay Class 3 contributions before 6 April 1975 |

Treat as MW3/NP (date) |  a notation indicating that the woman was non-employed and that no Class 3 contributions were being paid. This was merely an administrative procedure, the woman never having formally made an election |
