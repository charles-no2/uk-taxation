When reviewing a director’s current or loan account with a company, bear in mind that it is always a question of fact, based on all the evidence available, whether a withdrawal is a:

- loan,

- earnings; or

- a payment on account of earnings.

See NIM12018  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12018)for guidance to help identify the type of payment.

If the withdrawal results in the director owing the company money and the overdrawn amount is a loan, Class 1A NICs may arise if the conditions in NIM13021  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13021)are satisfied. See NIM16677 for further guidance about overdrawn loan accounts.
