Section 12 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (SSCBA) 1992

The late payment higher rate provisions (HRP) will continue to apply in the following circumstances:

- to all Class 2 NICs paid in respect of periods prior to 6 April 2015

- from 6 April 2015, to Class 2 NICs paid voluntarily by self-employed earners who have profits below the small profits threshold

- to self-employed earners who pay Class 2 NICs outside Self Assessment

For more information on HRP see NIM72150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim72150)
