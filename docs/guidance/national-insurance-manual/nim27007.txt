Regulation 111 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulation 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Regulation 111 of the SSCR 2001 defines airman as a person who is, or has been, employed under a contract of service (entered into in the UK with a view to its performance, in whole or in part, while the aircraft is in flight) either as a

- pilot

- commander

- navigator

- other member of the crew of any aircraft

Or is employed in any other capacity on board any aircraft for the purposes of the aircraft or its

- crew

- passengers

- cargo or mails

This definition does not include members of HM Forces - see NIM32000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim32000) onwards.
