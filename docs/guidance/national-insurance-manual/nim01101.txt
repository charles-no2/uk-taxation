National Insurance Contributions Act 2002

Changes were made to the structure of Class 1 NICs from 6 April 2003.

These changes were put into effect by the National Insurance Contributions Act 2002, together with various supporting regulations.

Guidance on these changes can be found at NIM01102 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01102) onwards.

The changes affect the amount of Class 1 NICs due but they do not disturb any of the existing Class 1 rules covering:

- the date upon which liability arises

- the methods used to assess liability or

- the starting point at which liability arises.

Guidance on:

- those areas of Class 1 NICs liability which are not affected by the changes and

- the structure of Class 1 NICs before 6 April 2003

can be found at NIM01000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01000) onwards.

Guidance on changes to the structure of Class 1 NICs from 6 April 2009 can be found at NIM01200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01200) onwards.
