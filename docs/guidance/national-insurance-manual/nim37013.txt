All errors which come to light within the current tax year should be corrected by the employer in accordance with the instructions contained within the CWG2 ‘Employer’s Further Guide to PAYE and NICs’ and NIM11530 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim11530).
