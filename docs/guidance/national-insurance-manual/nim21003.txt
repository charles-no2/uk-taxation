Class 2 National Insurance Contributions: Small Earnings Exception: Evidence of earnings
Identifier: NIM21003
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim21003
Published: 2016-04-11T17:37:51+01:00
Updated: 2022-03-09T13:58:08+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:15:57.263936+00:00

Unless the business is new, an applicant had to supply one of the following in support of their application for Small Earnings Exception

- A Self Assessment (SA) return which was not under enquiry (for further information on filing dates etc see the SA Guides); or

- The Net Profit figure from the certified Profit and Loss account for the last trading year; or

- Any other evidence of earnings such as business receipts or written income and expenditure records;

- The special shortened income and expenses section of the self-employed pages of the SA return for use by businesses with a turnover of less than £15,000.
