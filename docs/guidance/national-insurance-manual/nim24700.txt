Section 16 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (SSCBA) 1992

S16 of SSCBA 1992 provides for the application of the Income Tax Acts to Class 4 NICs. This means that Class 4 NICs are assessed and collected in the same way as Income Tax.
