Regulation 117 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

The normal conditions of residence or presence in GB under Case F (see NIM33000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33000)) are disregarded for mariners and their employers.

For a liability to pay a primary Class 1 contribution to arise the mariner has to be:

- domiciled; or

- resident in GB

For a liability to pay a secondary Class 1 contribution to arise the employer has to be:

- resident; or

- have a place of business in GB

You should operate Residence, Domicile and Remittance Basis Manual guidance in deciding whether a person is domiciled or resident. Any difficulties on residence should be submitted to Marine NICs in Cardiff (see NIM29034 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29034)).

Note : These conditions do not apply where EC Regulations or a Reciprocal Agreement apply
