Class 2 National Insurance Contributions: Small Earnings Exception: Evidence of Earnings: New business

If a person commenced self-employment within the immediately preceding 12 months and consequently had no evidence of earnings, the expected figure of net earnings was acceptable for the purposes of considering entitlement to Small Earnings Exception.
