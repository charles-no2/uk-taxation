Regulation 114 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

Where the EC regulations - see NIM33002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33002) - or Reciprocal Agreement provisions - see NIM33015 - do not apply, liability is determined under domestic legislation.
