Paragraphs 8(b), 10(4)(b) and (5)(b), and (6) of Part 6 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (as inserted by regulation 8(7) of the Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2006)

Sections 164(b) and 166(1) of, paragraph 1 of Schedule 28 and paragraphs 1-3 ofSchedule 29, as modified by paragraph 10 above, to Finance Act 2004

NIM02765 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02765) explains that, subject to the satisfaction of other conditions, a payment out of an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme is disregarded in the calculation of earnings if, had the scheme been a registered pension scheme (NIM02715 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02715)), the payment would have been an authorised member payment of the type shown there. One of those types of payment is a pension commencement lump sum which would have satisfied the following conditions:

- the individual becomes entitled to it in connection with their becoming entitled to a “relevant pension”

- it is paid within the period beginning 6 months before and ending one year after the day on which the member becomes entitled to it

- it is paid when the individual has reached normal minimum pension age unless they satisfy the ill-health condition (paragraph 1 of Schedule 28; RPSM08100070);

- it is paid before the individual has reached age 75

- the amount must not exceed the “permitted lump sum” (“PLS”; NIM02770 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02770)). If the amount of the lump sum exceeds the PLS, then no amount can be disregarded in the calculation of earnings.

Here, “relevant pension” means:

- income withdrawal (NIM02768 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02768))

- a lifetime annuity (NIM02768 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02768))

- a scheme pension (NIM02767 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02767)).

For the meaning of “normal minimum pension age”, see NIM02767 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02767) (pension rule 1).
