Class 1 NICs: Earnings of employees and office holders: Payment for changed method of paying earnings
Identifier: NIM02275
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02275
Published: 2016-04-11T17:04:03+01:00
Updated: 2021-11-11T13:56:28+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:13:25.840174+00:00

Include in gross pay any payment made to an employee as an incentive to change their method of payment. This will apply for instance where a payment is made to encourage an employee to move from weekly cash payment to monthly payment by automated transfer to a bank/building society account.
