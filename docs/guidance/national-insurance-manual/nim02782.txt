You must read this page in conjunction with NIM02780 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02780) (first considerations).

Payment out of the EFRBS is a lump sum.

Step 1

Is the lump sum a:

- pension commencement lump sum (PCLS) NIM02769 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02769); or

- serious ill-health lump sum (SIHLS) NIM02771 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02771)?

Yes Go to step 2

No Go to step 4

Step 2

Is the lump sum a PCLS?

Yes Go to NIM02783 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02783)

No Go to step 3

Step 3

Is the lump sum a SIHLS?

Yes Go to NIM02784 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02784)

Step 4

Payment cannot be disregarded in the calculation of earnings.
