Special Cases: Married Women: Automatic cancelling of election following failure of "2 Year Test"
Identifier: NIM30005
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim30005
Published: 2016-04-11T17:45:33+01:00
Updated: 2022-12-09T13:02:28+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:16:57.051816+00:00

National Insurance Recording System 2 (NIRS2) cancels a reduced rate election for the following reasons:

- LAPSED BY POSTING is where contributions are received two complete tax years or more after the last reduced rate NICs were recorded

- LAPSED BY LO 1717 where the cancellation of the election has been manually input

- LAPSED FOR RP is where a Retirement Pension calculation is requested and the woman has failed the two year test (see NIM30004)

- LAPSED BY CREDITS if credits are posted at least two complete tax years after the last reduced rate contributions
