Regulation 128 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

If a widow remarries during the initial period of reduced liability (see NIM31003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31003)), she keeps her entitlement to reduced liability.
