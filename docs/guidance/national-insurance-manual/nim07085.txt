Regulation 7 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

Any payment made by the Secretary of State for Trade and Industry(“SofSforTI”) – see NIM07082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07082) and NIM07084  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07084)- should be regarded as a regular payment paid otherwise than at a regular interval. NIM08050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim08050) gives further details on the earnings period to be used in such circumstances.

Although the guidance at NIM08050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim08050) refers to payments made by the employer, it should also be taken as referring to payments made by the SofSforTI.
