Case F of Part 9 of the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

When UK NICs legislation either starts or ceases to apply to workers coming from or going abroad part way through a tax year, liability for Class 1 NICs and Class 1A NICs, starts or ceases at that time. In such cases calculate the amount of Class 1A NICs due by apportioning the benefit between the period for which liability exists and the period for which no liability exists.

Example

A posted worker from South Africa arrives in the UK on 28 September 2020. The UK subsidiary employer supplies the worker with a company car that is chargeable to UK tax. By virtue of regulation 145(2) of SSCR 2001, there is no UK NICs liability for the first 52 contribution weeks. Liability for Class 1 NICs commences on the 3 October 2021. The amount of Class 1A NICs payable in respect of the car is calculated on a proportion of the cash equivalent of the car based on the number of days in the tax year that the employee is liable to UK NICs legislation.

For the 2020 to 2021 tax year - no Class 1A NICs are due

For the 2021 to 2022 tax year, Class 1A NICs are due for the period 3 October 2021 to 5 April 2022 – 185 days.

- Cash equivalent of the benefit £7,000

- Pro-rata cash equivalent £7,000 x 185/365 = £3,547.95
