National Insurance Numbers (NINOs): Confirming a NINO: Employee does not have a NINO
Identifier: NIM39215
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39215
Published: 2016-04-11T17:52:24+01:00
Updated: 2026-09-14T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:17:44.413060+00:00

If a new employee has never had a NINO, the employer should:

- keep a record of their full name (surname and first name(s)), address, date of birth and gender, and

- first tell them to check guidance on gov.uk: https://www.gov.uk/find-national-insurance-number (https://www.gov.uk/find-national-insurance-number)

For further details of the adult registration process, see https://www.gov.uk/apply-national-insurance-number (https://www.gov.uk/apply-national-insurance-number)

For details of special cases see NIM39600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39600).
