There are time limits for the payment of Class 3 NICs, see NIM25028 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25028). In addition, they may be payable at more than their original rate, see NIM25029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25029).

Class 3 NICs are precluded if the contributor pays them too late to count for benefit, these contributions may be refunded or appropriated to another year, see NIM25005 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25005).

Under regulation 62 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, Class 3 NICs can be paid by a third party on behalf of a deceased person

- if they were entitled to pay before the date of death, and

- if the payment would benefit the next of kin.

The same time limits and higher rate provisions apply.
