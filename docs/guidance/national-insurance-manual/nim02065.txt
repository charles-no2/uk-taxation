Class 1 NICs : Earnings of employees and office holders : Arrears of pay
Identifier: NIM02065
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02065
Published: 2016-04-11T17:01:41+01:00
Updated: 2021-12-20T11:23:36+00:00

Any arrears of pay, for example a backdated pay increase, must be added to any other earnings received by the employee in the pay period under consideration and NICs assessed on the total amount.

Example

In a particular month an employee who is regularly monthly paid receives :

Description  | Amounts |

Normal earnings | £1800 |

Arrears of pay  | £1000 |

Total | £2800 |

NICs must be assessed on the total of £2800 using the monthly secondary threshold, lower earnings limit (LEL), primary threshold and monthly upper earnings limit (UEL). See NIM01000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01000).
