Section 121C of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (SSAA 1992)

Decisions by the independent Tax Tribunal in respect of Personal Liability Notice appeals include:

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: Michael Denmark v HMRC, TC06096, Released 6 September 2017 (http://financeandtax.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk//judgmentfiles/j10064/TC06096.pdf)

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: Charles O’Rorke v HMRC, TC06008, Released 14 July 2017 (http://financeandtax.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk//Aspx/view.aspx?id=9976)

- Upper Tribunal, Tax: HMRC v Charles Michael O’Rorke, FTC/39/2012, Released 4 October 2013 (http://taxandchancery_ut.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk/Documents/decisions/hmrc-v-o%27rorke.pdf)

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: Humayun Zubair v HMRC, TC02324, Released 22 October 2012 (http://www.bailii.org/cgi-bin/format.cgi?doc=/uk/cases/UKFTT/TC/2012/TC02324.html&query=(Humayun)+AND+(Zubair))

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: John Peter Smith v HMRC, TC02110, Released 28 June 2012 (http://www.bailii.org/cgi-bin/format.cgi?doc=/uk/cases/UKFTT/TC/2012/TC02110.html&query=(TC02110))

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: Mrs Christine Roberts v HMRC, TC01994, Released 11 April 2012 (http://www.bailii.org/cgi-bin/format.cgi?doc=/uk/cases/UKFTT/TC/2012/TC01994.html&query=(TC01994))

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: Stephen Roberts & Alan Martin v HMRC, TC01130, Released 26 April 2011 (http://www.bailii.org/cgi-bin/format.cgi?doc=/uk/cases/UKFTT/TC/2011/TC01130.html&query=(TC01130))

- First-Tier Tribunal, Tax: Leslie Livingstone v HMRC, TC00369, Released 3 February 2010 (http://www.bailii.org/cgi-bin/format.cgi?doc=/uk/cases/UKFTT/TC/2010/TC00369.html&query=(TC00369))

Whilst it’s recognised that the First-tier Tribunal decisions are not binding and do not set case precedent, as noted by Judge Amanda Brown in Charles O’Rorke v HMRC (http://financeandtax.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk//Aspx/view.aspx?id=9976), they can be ‘persuasive and illustrative of the approach to be taken’.

You can see the significance of the Upper Tribunal decision in HMRC v Charles Michael O’Rorke (http://taxandchancery_ut.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk/Documents/decisions/hmrc-v-o%27rorke.pdf), at NIM12204 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12204).
