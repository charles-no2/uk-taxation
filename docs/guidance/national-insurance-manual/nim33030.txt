Regulation 883/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004 on the coordination of social security systems

The United Kingdom does not apply Regulation 883/2004 to third country nationals although most other Member States do.

A third country national, is somebody who is not a national of any of the Member States but who is resident in a Member State

By resident we mean legally resident in a Member State.

If you have a third country national moving within the Member States you should apply the rules in Regulation 1408/71 see NIM33300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33300)

If you see a Portable Document A1 issued to a third country national by the competent authority of another Member State then this should be accepted.
