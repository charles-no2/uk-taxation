Article 13.2(c) of Council Regulation (EEC) No 1408/71

Periods up to 1 May 2010

A person who is a person employed on board a vessel flying the flag of a Member State shall be subject to the legislation of that State. The conditions of domicile or residence under UK legislation - see NIM29009 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29009) - do not apply where EC legislation applies.

Example

Albert is a French national and is employed on board a vessel flying the flag of the UK. Even though he is resident in France, Albert is liable for Class 1 NICs under UK legislation.
