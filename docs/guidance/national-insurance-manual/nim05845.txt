Class 1 NICs: Expenses and Allowances: Motoring expenses (including mileage allowances) paid on or after 6 April 2002: Determining whether payments are for an employee's use of a qualifying vehicle: Background
Identifier: NIM05845
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05845
Published: 2016-04-11T17:14:07+01:00
Updated: 2019-10-24T17:21:02+01:00

To disregard some or the entire qualifying amount (QA) (see NIM05830 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05830)) from earnings, the employer must make payments to or for the benefit of an employee which are relevant motoring expenditure (RME) (see NIM05815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05815)). For Class 1 NICs purposes, the employer can only deduct QA from RME, not other earnings.

Mileage allowance payments (MAPs, see EIM31205 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31205)) are exempt from PAYE income tax. The employer, therefore, does not have to include the excess in gross pay for PAYE tax purposes. See EIM31235 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31235).

To determine whether payments are RME may involve trying to establish whether the paymentsare made for the employee’s use, or potential, or anticipated use, of a qualifying vehicle. See EIM31255 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31255). If the payments are not for the employee’s use, or potential, or anticipated use, of a qualifying vehicle,they are not RME and are unlikely to be MAPs. If the payments are not RME the employer must include the payments in gross pay for Class 1 NICs. If the payments are not MAPs the employer must include the payments in gross pay for PAYE income tax.

See

- NIM05825  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05825)for some examples of payments that are not RME.

- EIM31215 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim31215) for some examples of payments that are not MAPs.

- NIM05847  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05847)for guidance about establishing the facts to determine the nature of the payment

- NIM05848  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05848)which tells you what to do once you have those facts.
