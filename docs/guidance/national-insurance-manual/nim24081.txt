Regulation 105, SS(C)R 2001

Earnings for the purposes of special Class 4 NICs are the same as those for the purposes of Class 1 NICs (see NIM02000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02000) onwards).

However, the Class 4 NICs payable are calculated at the same percentage rates as ordinary Class 4 NICs and not the equivalent Class 1 rates, see NIM24200.
