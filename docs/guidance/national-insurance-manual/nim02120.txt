Class 1 NICs:  Earnings of employees and office holders:  Earnings paid in later year
Identifier: NIM02120
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02120
Published: 2016-04-11T17:02:38+01:00
Updated: 2021-09-23T14:17:07+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:13:14.661127+00:00

Employers must enter payments made after the end of the tax year in which the employee earned them on the Full Payment Submission (FPS) in the tax week or month in which the employer paid them. NICs only become due when the earnings are paid (see NIM01002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01002)).

Examples of earnings paid in a later tax year include:

- arrears of pay from a backdated pay increase

- a director’s earnings paid in a tax year later than that in which the director earned them.

See NIM08000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim08000) for general information regarding earnings periods.
