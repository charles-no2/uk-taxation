Class 1B NICs: liability: overpayments

Where there is an overpayment of Class 1B NICs, this can be:

- offset against any outstanding or future NIC liabilities, or

- if there are no such liabilities, refunded to the employer (together with any interest payable on the sum). See NIM38000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim38000) for information regarding refunds of Class 1B NICs.

Repayments of Class 1B NICs fall within regulation 52 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 which cover all Class 1 NICs. The time limit for making a repayment claim for these contributions is covered in the National Insurance Manual at NIM37003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim37003).

See PSA2110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-settlement-agreements/psa2110) for information about overpaid tax in PAYE Settlement Agreements.
