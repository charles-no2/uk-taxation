National Insurance contributions for people going to work abroad - European Economic Area (EEA)

Step 1

|

Is the person going to work in the EEA?

|

|

Yes - Go to step 4

|

|

No - Go to step 2

|

Step 2

|

Is the person going to work in a RA country?

|

|

Yes - See NIM33400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33400)

|

|

No - Go to step 3

|

Step 3

|

Is the person going to a ROW country?

|

|

Yes - See NIM33500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33500)

|

|

No - Go back to step 1

|

Step 4

|

Is the person a national of an EEA country

|

|

Yes - EC Regulation 883/04 may apply. It is possible that they may remain subject to UK NICS for a period of up to 24 months, provided certain criteria are met. See NIM33065 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33065)

|

|

No - Go to step 5

|

Step 5

|

Is the person legally resident in an EEA country?

|

|

Yes - EC Regulation 1408/71 may apply. It is possible that they may remain subject to UK NICS for a period of up to 12 months, provided certain criteria are met. See NIM33300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33300).

|

|

No - EC Regulations do not apply. If the person is going to an EEA country with which the UK also has reciprocal agreement, then this may be applied. See NIM33012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33012). If it is an EEA country with which the UK does not have a reciprocal agreement then it may need to be treated under ROW rules, see NIM33500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33500)

|
