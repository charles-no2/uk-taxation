Section 10(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (SSCBA 1992)

All removal benefits exempt from income tax under ITEPA 2003 are exempt from Class 1A NICs, see NIM16265 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16265). That is because Class 1A NICs are only due on general earnings chargeable to income tax under ITEPA 2003.

Similarly, all removal benefits subject to income tax under ITEPA 2003 attract a Class 1A NICs liability, see NIM16270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16270).

In addition to Class 1A NICs on taxable removal benefits, Class 1A NICs are also due on:

- all removal benefits and expenses which exceed £8,000, see NIM16275  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16275)and NIM16290;  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16290)and

- any taxable removal expense where no Class 1 NICs liability arises, see NIM16270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16270)

- for guidance on taxable relocation expenses which are not liable for Class 1 NICs see NIM06115 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06115).
