Regulation 149(2)(a) Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

The words “ordinarily resident” are not defined in the above legislation. For guidance on:

- the meaning of the words, see NIM33031

- the factors to take into account in considering whether a person is “ordinarily resident”, see NIM33032.
