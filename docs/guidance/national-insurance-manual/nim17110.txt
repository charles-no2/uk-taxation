Regulation 77 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Where Class 1A NICs for the year ending 5 April 1999 or subsequent tax years are refunded, and the repayment is made after the relevant date, the repaid Class 1A NICs carries interest from the relevant date to the date the order for repayment is issued.

In the case of Class 1A NICs, the relevant date means:

- the 14th day after the end of the tax year in respect of which Class 1A NICs were paid, or

- if later than that day, the date on which the Class 1A NICs were paid.
