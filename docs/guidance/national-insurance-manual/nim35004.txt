Special Cases: Share fishermen: Class 2 liability: Other self-employment during the tax year
Identifier: NIM35004
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim35004
Published: 2016-04-11T17:50:57+01:00
Updated: 2022-12-16T11:34:20+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:17:30.374549+00:00

If a share fisherman has other self-employment during the tax year when they cannot go to sea, they must pay ordinary Class 2 NICs – for periods before tax year 2015-16 see NIM20000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim20000) onwards and for periods from 2015-16 onwards see NIM70000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim70000).
