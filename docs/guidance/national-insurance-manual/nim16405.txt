Class 1A National Insurance contributions: Special Class 1A  NICs cases: Workers going or coming from abroad who are provided with  benefits: Conditions applying to Class 1A NICs liability
Identifier: NIM16405

Published: 2016-04-11T17:34:13+01:00
Updated: 2022-06-29T11:39:19+01:00

If a worker coming from or going abroad is subject to UK NICs legislation, (see NIM16403 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16403) for more information), the general rule on liability for Class 1A NICs is that:

- the benefit must be chargeable to tax - see NIM16425 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16425)

- there must be liability for secondary Class 1 NICs - see NIM16420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16420).

Where both of these conditions are satisfied, Class 1A NICs will be due providing the further conditions giving rise to a Class 1A NICs liability are satisfied, see NIM13021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13021).
