Not everyone who wants a NINo will get one. Some people do not fall into the category of needing a NINo, including:

-

people over pension age who are neither:

- working, nor

- claiming benefit, credits or tax credits, or need one for other HMRC/DWP purposes

- children under age 16.

-

For reasons why people need a NINO, see NIM39125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39125).

For legislation see NIM39330 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39330).

Further information on the NINo Application process can be found at https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-insurance-number-allocations-staff-guide (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-insurance-number-allocations-staff-guide)
