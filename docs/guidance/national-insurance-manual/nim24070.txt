Class 4 NICs: Structure from 6 April 2003: Effect of  changes on entitlement to contributory benefits
Identifier: NIM24070
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24070
Published: 2016-04-11T17:40:13+01:00
Updated: 2019-07-22T11:43:31+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:20:47.698191+00:00

The removal of the Upper Profits Limit for the purposes of calculating the amount of Class 4 NICs due has no effect on a person’s entitlement to contributory benefits.

Payment of Class 4 NICs continue to derive no entitlement to contributory benefit.
