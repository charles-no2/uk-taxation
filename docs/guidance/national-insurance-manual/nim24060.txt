Class 4 NICs: Structure from 6 April 2003: Effect of changes on Class 2 NICs
Identifier: NIM24060

The National Insurance Contributions Act 2002 made changes to the structure of Class 4 NICs from 6 April 2003, but no changes were made to the structure of Class 2 NICs.
