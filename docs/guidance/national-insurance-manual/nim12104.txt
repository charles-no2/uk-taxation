Class 1: Deeming: Who can deem NICs
Identifier: NIM12104
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12104
Published: 2016-04-11T17:27:12+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-16T11:45:47+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:19:50.139897+00:00

The authority to record NICs as deemed must always be provided by at least a band D officer.

In cases where the investigating officer is band D or above, that officer must obtain authority from their immediate manager before NICs can be deemed.

A record of the authority must be maintained for Audit purposes.
