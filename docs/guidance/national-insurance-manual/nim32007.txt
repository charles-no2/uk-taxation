Regulation 143 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

The earnings period of a serving member of the forces is in the case of a member of the regular forces:

- the accounting period under the Naval Pay Regulations, Army Pay Warrant or Queen’s Regulations; or

in the case of a person undergoing training in any of the prescribed establishments in paragraphs 2-9 of Part 1 of Sch 6 to SSCR 2001

- a month
