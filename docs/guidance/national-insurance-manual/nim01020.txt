Regulation 21 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No 1004)

An employee can apply to ‘defer’ paying some of their primary NICs (see NIM01001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01001)) for any tax year except that in which they reach State pension age if they:

- have more than one employment; and

- expect to pay enough NICs in one or more of their employments to reach the annual maximum (see NIM01009 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01009)).

Guidance on the deferment arrangements can be found at NIM01180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01180).
