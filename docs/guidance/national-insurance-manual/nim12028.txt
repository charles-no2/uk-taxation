If the employer decides to ‘pay on account’ (NIM12026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12026)) Class 1 NICs are due from the first month and the employer adjusts the final payment at the end of the year to ensure the same amount of NICs is paid as in the example.
