Sections 8 and 9 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act (SSCBA) 1992

From 6 April 2003, the structure of primary Class 1 NICs provides for two primary percentage rates, although a single secondary percentage rate was maintained. Guidance on these changes can be found at NIM01100 onwards.

Further changes to the structure of primary Class 1 NICs were introduced from 6 April 2009. Guidance on these changes can be found at NIM01200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01200) onwards.
