As explained in NIM07082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07082), the Secretary of State for Business and Trade can make certain specified payments out of the National Insurance Fund. But not all of those payments are earnings within the definition in section 3(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992.

In order to decide whether a certain payment should or should not be included in the gross amount of earnings, it is necessary to establish precisely what payments are being made.The following table shows what payments are liable to NICs and which ones are not.
