Regulation 25 and 40(2)(za) and paragraph 6A of Part 5 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

NIM02437  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02437)explains when meal vouchers can be disregarded from earnings so that Class 1 NICs liability cannot arise. If the payments are disregarded from earnings for Class 1 NICs purposes, regulation 40(2)(za) of the SSCR 2001 exempts such payments from Class 1A NICs liability. Therefore, there can be no Class 1A NICs liability.

EIM02650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim02650) explains the income tax position.
