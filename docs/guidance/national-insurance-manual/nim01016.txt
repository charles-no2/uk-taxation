Class 1 structural overview: types of National Insurance contributions: not contracted-out rate NICs
Identifier: NIM01016
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01016
Published: 2016-04-11T16:58:00+01:00
Updated: 2021-09-16T13:02:55+01:00

Since 6 April 2016 most people pay the same standard rate of NICs following the ending of contracted out NICs schemes.

Before 6 April 2016 non contracted-out (full) rate NICs were paid by those who were included in the additional State Pension. The primary element (see NIM01001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01001)) was payable at either a standard rate or, for certain married women and widows, at a reduced rate.

For further information about the fiscal structure of NICs, see NIM01011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01011) onwards. For further information about married womens’ and widows’ reduced rate NICs, see NIM30000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim30000) and NIM31000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31000), respectively.

For information on the structure of primary Class 1 NICs from 6 April 2003, see NIM01100onwards.

For information on the structure of primary Class 1 NICs from 6 April 2009, see NIM01200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01200) onwards.
