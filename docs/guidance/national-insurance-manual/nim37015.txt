Refunds: Class 1: NICs paid in error: employers who pay tax centrally
Identifier: NIM37015
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim37015
Published: 2016-04-11T17:51:28+01:00
Updated: 2022-04-13T10:55:13+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:17:35.546970+00:00

All employers who pay tax centrally to Public Departments, Cardiff, pay NICs centrally - to Central Payments Section within PT Operations. These employers will correct any mistakes themselves, even if the wrongly paid NICs are for a past year.
