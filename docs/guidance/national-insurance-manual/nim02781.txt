Payment out of the EFRBS is a Pension.

Step 1

If the scheme had been a registered pension scheme, would the payment satisfy pension rules:

-

1 and 3, and

-

4 or 6? (NIM02767 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02767) and NIM02768 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02768))

Yes Go to step 2

No Go to step4

Step 2

Is the pension payable until the employee’s death at least once a year?

Yes Go to step 3

No Go to step 4

Step 3

Payment can be disregarded in the calculation of earnings. See NIM14510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim14510) for Class 1A position.

Step 4

Payment cannot be disregarded in the calculation of earnings.
