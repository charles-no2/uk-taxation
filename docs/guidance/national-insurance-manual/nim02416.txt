Regulation 25 and Part 5 of Schedule 3 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

As mentioned in NIM02411 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02411), certain types of non-cash voucher (NIM02413 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02413)) are specifically disregarded in calculating an employee’s earnings for Class 1 NICs purposes. The following table shows those types.
