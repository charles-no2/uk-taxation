Where to find information about Local Authority (LA) Essential and Casual car users

The rules and method for determining whether any Class 1 NICs liability arises on motoring expenses payments made by LA employers are the same as any other employer. See below for where to find further information.

- For information about calculating Class 1 NICs liability on motoring expenses payments paid on or after 6 April 2002 see NIM05800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05800) onwards.

- For information about calculating Class 1 NICs liability on mileage allowance payments paid before 6 April 2002 see NIM05700  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05700)onwards.

- For general information about Local Authority (LA) Essential and Casual car users see NIM05740 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05740).
