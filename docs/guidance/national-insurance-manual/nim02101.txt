Class 1 NICs: Earnings of employees and office holders: Council Tax, water or sewerage charges: Employer pays all or part of an employee's Council Tax, water or sewerage charges
Identifier: NIM02101
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02101
Published: 2016-04-11T17:02:10+01:00
Updated: 2021-09-23T13:25:53+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:18:26.239390+00:00

If an employer pays all or part of an employee’s Council Tax and/or water or sewerage charges, the payment is earnings and NICs are due. The employer is simply meeting the employee’s debt.

See NIM02010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02010) for the meaning of “earnings”.
