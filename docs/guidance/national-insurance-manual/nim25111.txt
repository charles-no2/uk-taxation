Class 3 NICs: Additional Class 3 NICs: Methods of payment

Published: 2016-04-11T17:43:43+01:00
Updated: 2020-01-21T10:03:29+00:00

A person can pay additional Class 3 NICs by cheque or online payment, see GOV.UK (https://www.gov.uk/pay-voluntary-class-3-national-insurance) for more information.

Please note they cannot pay by direct debit or quarterly bill as those methods can only be used for paying Class 3 NICs for the current or a future year, rather than for past years.
