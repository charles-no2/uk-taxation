Regulation 7 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

If an employer makes a regular additional payment outside the earnings period in which it would normally fall, treat it as belonging to its normal earnings period, except when this is in a different tax year (See NIM08720 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim08720)). The employer must revise any NICs already paid on the other earnings, see NIM08700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim08700) onwards.
