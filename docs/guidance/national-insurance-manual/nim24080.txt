Section 18 SSCBA 1992

Schedule 1, Part II Categorisation of Earners Regulations 1978 and regulations 103 & 125, SS(C)R 2001

Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003 (ITEPA)

Section 18 of SSCBA 1992 provides for contributors who pay tax under ITEPA but who are treated by Schedule 1, Part II of the Categorisation of Earners Regulations 1978 (see ESM0122) as self-employed and are required to pay special Class 4 NICs. Examples include certain lecturers and examiners.

The calculation of special Class 4 NICs for all tax years uses the same Class 4 rates and limits as are used in the calculation of ordinary Class 4 NICs

See

- NIM24010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24010) for the Class 4 rates and limits applicable to tax years before 2003/2004

- NIM24050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24050) for the Class 4 rates and limits applicable to tax years from 2003/2004.

The introduction of a Class 4 NICs liability on profits above the Upper Profits Limit from 6 April 2003 (see NIM24030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24030)) applies equally to special Class 4 NICs, see NIM24082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24082).
