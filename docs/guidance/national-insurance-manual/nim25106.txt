Section 13A(10) - (13) Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, as inserted by section 135 Pensions Act 2008

NIM25103  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25103)sets out the conditions of eligibility that a person needs to satisfy to be entitled to pay Class 3 NICs under the above legislation. The third condition applies only if the individual reaches State Pension age (“SPa”) before 6 April 2010.

The condition is that the individual must, before they reach SPa, have at least one qualifying year which is derived from:

- paid, or treated as paid, Class 1 NICs; and/or

- paid Class 2 or Class 3 NICs.

For:

- the meaning of “qualifying year”, see NIM25001  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25001)and NIM25109 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25109)

- details of:

- the first condition, see NIM25104 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25104)

- the second condition, see NIM25105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim25105)

- treated as paid Class 1 NICs, see NIM12110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12110) (contents)
