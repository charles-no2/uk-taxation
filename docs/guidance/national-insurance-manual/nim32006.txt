Regulation 143 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

The earnings of serving members exclude:

- Emergency Service grants

- bounties in recognition of liability for immediate call-up in times of emergency

- gratuities at the end of a voluntarily-undertaken further period of service

- payments in lieu of food and drink normally supplied in kind to members of the armed forces

- contributions to mess expenses
