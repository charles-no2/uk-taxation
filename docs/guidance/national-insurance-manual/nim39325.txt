National Insurance Numbers (NINOs): Registration of a new NINO: Adult Registration: applying for a NINO through DWP's Jobcentre Plus

This guidance applies to:

- adults who were not issued with a number under the Juvenile Registration processes, see NIM39300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39300), and

- children who have not been part of a Child Benefit award, see NIM39320 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39320)

- People coming in to the UK from abroad.

In order to get a NINO, they must satisfy legislative criteria.

They must be either:

- working, about to start work, or actively seeking employment, and have the right to work in the UK, or

- be liable to UK Class 1 NICs

- making a claim to benefit, or

- be entitled to and wish to pay voluntary Class 3 NICs or be entitled to other credits

- be referred by student loan company, see NIM39130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39130).

These customers need to apply for a NI number. Details of the process can be found on the gov.uk website at:

https://www.gov.uk/apply-national-insurance-number (https://www.gov.uk/apply-national-insurance-number)
