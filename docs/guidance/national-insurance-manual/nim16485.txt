Regulation 145 to 148 of the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Where a UK worker is posted by an employer to work in

- another European Economic Area (EEA) country, or

- a country which has a Reciprocal Agreement (RA) or a Double Contribution Convention (DCC) with the UK

Class 1 and Class 1A NICs are due in the normal way, subject to the rules in NIM33001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33001) onwards. Where Class 1A NICs liability ceases part way through a tax year, to calculate the amount of earnings subject to Class 1A NICs in that year see NIM16500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16500).

If the worker remains liable for UK NICs under European Community legislation or the terms of a RA or DCC, Class 1A NICs are due on any benefits made available, providing the conditions listed in NIM13021  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13021)are satisfied.
