Regulation 14 of the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Regulation 14 provides that the earnings from one or more employments with the same employer need not be aggregated if the earnings from those employments are separately calculated, and as a consequence of the separate calculation, it would not be reasonably practicable to do so. See NIM10009  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim10009)for a discussion of the ‘not reasonably practicable’ test.
