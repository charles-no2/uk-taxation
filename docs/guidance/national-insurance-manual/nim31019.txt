Regulation 127(8) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

The rules and procedures about reduced liability for ordinary widows also apply to war widows - whether or not the war widow is getting Bereavement Allowance (BA) - see NIM31018 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31018).
