Regulation 78 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

If a person liable to pay Class 1A NICs has paid interest on late paid Class 1A NICs, that interest is repayable where:

- the interest paid is found not to have been due, although the Class 1A NICs in respect of which the interest was paid are due; or

- the Class 1A NICs in respect of which interest was paid are returned to the employer in accordance with either regulation 52 or 55 SSCR 2001, see NIM17060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim17060) and NIM17080 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim17080).

There are no legislative provisions that allow interest to be paid on interest refunded.
