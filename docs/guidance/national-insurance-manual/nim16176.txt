Regulation 25 of, and paragraph 7D of Part 8 of Schedule 3 to, the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI2001 No 1004)

With effect from 6 April 2002, payments in respect of fuel are disregarded from earnings under paragraph 7D of Part 8 of Schedule 3 to the SSCR 2001, so that Class 1 NICs liability does not arise where the fuel provided

- is for use in a car which attracts a car benefit charge under section 120 ITEPA 2003 and

- attracts a fuel scale charge under section 149 ITEPA 2003 , see NIM16177  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16177)onwards.

General rule

NIM16173 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16173) explains when Class 1 NICs liability arises on the cost of fuel provided to an employee where the fuel is used for

- private purposes

- business purposes but the actual cost of the business expense is less than the amount the employee receives.

Common methods of meeting fuel costs are shown at NIM06052 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06052).

NIM16173 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16173) applies equally to fuel provided for use in

- a privately owned car, see NIM06050  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06050)onwards; and

- a provided car which attracts a car benefit charge under the special rules in Chapter 6 of Part 3 ITEPA 2003.

Exceptions to the general rule

If a charge to income tax does not arise on fuel for a provided car, where

- the fuel is provided direct from the employer’s own fuel pump; or

- the employee purchases the fuel on behalf of his employer, see NIM06054 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06054) (purchases on behalf of the employer may be made by cash, credit card, garage account, cheque or fuel agency card)

what the employee has received is a payment in kind.

Payments in kind are disregarded from earnings so that no Class 1 NICs are due. Class 1A NICs liability does not arise on fuel provided in this way because there are no general earnings chargeable to income tax under ITEPA 2003. That is because sections 239 and 269 ITEPA 2003 excludes from charge to income tax, under ITEPA 2003, the discharge of any liability of the employee in connection with the car, see EIM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim25500).

NIM16177  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16177)explains when Class 1A NICs liability arises on fuel for use in a provided car.
