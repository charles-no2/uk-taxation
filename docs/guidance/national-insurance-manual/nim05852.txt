Where to find information about Civic Dignitaries

The rules and method for determining whether any Class 1 NICs liability arises on motoring expenses paid by Local Authority employers to or on behalf of Civic Dignitaries are the same as any other employer. See below for where to find further information.

- For information about calculating Class 1 NICs liability on motoring expenses payments paid on or after 6 April 2002 see NIM05800 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05800) onwards.

- For information about calculating Class 1 NICs liability on mileage allowance payments paid before 6 April 2002 see NIM05700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05700) onwards.

- For general information about Civic Dignitaries see NIM05750 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05750).
