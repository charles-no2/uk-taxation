Regulation 111 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulation 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

Regulation 111 of the SSCR 2001 sets out the definition of British aircraft as:

- belonging to Her Majesty ; or

- registered in the UK and owned or hired by someone who is resident in or has a main place of business in UK
