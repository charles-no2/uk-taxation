Class 1 NICs: Employment Related Securities (ERS): Internationally Mobile Employees (IMEs): general terms

In general terms, ‘internationally mobile employees’ are considered to be:

- UK residents going overseas to work

- Overseas residents coming to the UK to work; and

- UK or overseas residents who move in and out of the UK.
