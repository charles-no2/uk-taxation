Paragraph 6 of Schedule 2 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (SSCBA 92)

Paragraph 6 of Schedule 2 to SSCBA 92 provides for the application of Section 101 (https://www.lexisnexis.com/tolley/library/search/runRemoteLink?linkInfo=F%23GB%23UK_ACTS%23sect%25101%25num%252009_10a%25section%25101%25&A=0.9340196807788724&service=citation&langcountry=GB) of the Finance Act 2009 (late payment interest on sums due to HMRC) to any amount due in respect of Class 4 contributions as it applies in relation to income tax.
