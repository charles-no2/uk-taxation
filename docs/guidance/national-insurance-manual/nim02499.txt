Regulation 24 and paragraph 15 of Schedule 2 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

NIM02498 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02498) explains how to calculate the amount of earnings included in any payment made by way of a cash voucher (see NIM02497 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02497)) which is provided to or for the benefit of one employee. But where such a voucher is provided to two or more employees, the amount of earnings is to be calculated or estimated in the same way as that for a non-cash voucher.

For details of:

- the method of calculation, see NIM02464 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02464)

- examples illustrating the method of calculation, see NIM02465 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02465).

For income tax purposes, the method of apportioning the chargeable expense is different. For details, see EIM16160.
