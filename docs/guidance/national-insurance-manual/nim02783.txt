You must read this page in conjunction with NIM02780 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02780) (first considerations) and NIM02782 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02782) (lump sums).

Payment out of EFRBS is a pension commencement lump sum (PCLS).

Step 1

If the scheme has been a registered pension scheme, does the individual become entitled to the PCLS in connection with becoming entitled to a “relevant person” under the same scheme (NIM02769) (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02769)?

Yes Go to step 2

No Go to step 7

Step 2

Is the payment made within three months of the individual becoming entitled to it?

Yes Go to step 3

No Go to step 7

Step 3

Is the lump sum paid:

- when the individual has reached normal minimum pension age (NIM02769) (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02769); or

- the ill-health condition is satisfied (NIM02769) (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02769)?

Yes Go to step 4

No Go to step 7

Step 4

Is the payment made before the individual is aged 75?

Yes Go to step 5

No Go to step 7

Step 5

Does the amount exceed the permitted lump sum (NIM02770) (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02770)?

Yes Go to step 7

No Go to step 6

Step 6

Payment can be disregarded in the calculation of earnings. See NIM14510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim14510) for Class 1A position.

Step 7

Payment cannot be disregarded in the calculation of earnings.
