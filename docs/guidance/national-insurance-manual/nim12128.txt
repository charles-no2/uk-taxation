Regulation 60 of the Social Security Contributions Regulations 2001 (SSCR 2001) (SI 2001 No 1004)

If no response is received following action taken under NIM12126 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12126), or the response does not dispute the opinion, the unpaid NICs cannot be recorded on the employee’s NI account.

In some cases, the unpaid Class 1 NICs may already be recorded as paid. If so, any NICs that are erroneously shown as paid must be removed from the NI account. NIM12119 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim12119) provides further information on removing NICs from the NI account.
