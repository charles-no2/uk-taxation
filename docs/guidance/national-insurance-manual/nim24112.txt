Regulation 100 - Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

NIM24105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24105) provides guidance on the calculation method used to determine the maximum amount of Class 2 and 4 NICs payable for all tax years up to and including 2002/2003 by a contributor who is self-employed only.

The following example illustrates the calculation for a contributor whose profits for the tax year do not exceed the Upper Profits Limit (UPL).

The calculation method shown applies only to tax years up to and including the 2002/2003 tax year. For guidance on the Class 2 and 4 NICs maximum liability for these contributorsfor tax years from 2003/2004 see NIM24150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim24150).

The example uses the rates and limits in force during the 2002/2003 tax year.

Ms J Walters has been self-employed for the whole of the 2002/2003 tax year. Her profits for the year amount to £23,000 Ms J Walters held no other employments during the year.

Step 1

Multiply by the appropriate Class 4 percentage, the amount of profits between the LPL and the contributor’s actual profits for the year.

£23,000 (actual profits) - £4,615 (LPL) = £18,385 x 7% (Class 4 percentage rate) =£1,286.95

Step 2

Multiply the relevant Class 2 rate by the number of weeks of self-employement in the tax year

£2.00 x 52 = £104

Step 3

Add together the results of steps 1 and 2

£1286.95. + £104.00 = £1390.95

Ms J Walters is liable to pay a total of £1,390.95 in Class 2 and 4 NICs for the 2002/2003tax year.
