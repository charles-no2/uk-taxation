Social Security (Benefit) Married Women and Widows Special Provisions Regulations 1974 (SI 1974 no 2010) Reg 3 cover special provisions for starter credits for married women and widows. See NIM41210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41210).

Social Security Credits Regulations 1975 (SI 1975/556) set out the rules for crediting contributors with earnings from 6 April 1975. See NIM41200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41200).

Section 23A, Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 set out rules for Parent and Carers see NIM41250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41250)

The Social Security Act 1998 provides the appeal rights.

Social Security & Child Support (Decisions & Appeals) Regulations 1999 (SI 1999 No. 991) provides for the requirement of a Manadatory Reconsideration before appeal.

Social Security (Credits) Regulations 1975 (SI 1975 No. 556) for crediting NICs for periods before 6 April 2016

State Pension Regulations 2015 (SI 2015 No. 173) for crediting NICs for periods after 5 April 2016

Northern Ireland

There is equivalent legislation for Northern Ireland.
