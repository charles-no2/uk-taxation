Regulation 128 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

The initial period of reduced liability (see NIM31003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31003)) is not affected by a widow living with a man as his wife for part or all of that period.
