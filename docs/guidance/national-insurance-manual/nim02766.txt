Paragraphs 8(b) and 10 of Part 6 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (as inserted by regulation 8(7) of the Social Security (Contributions)(Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2006)

Sections 164(1)(a) and 165(1) of Finance Act 2004

NIM02765 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02765) explains that, subject to the satisfaction of other conditions, a payment out of an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme is disregarded in the calculationof earnings if, had the scheme been a registered pension scheme (NIM02715 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02715)), the payment would have been an authorised member payment of the type shown there. One of those types of payment is a pension which would have satisfied the following conditions:

- pension rules 1, 3 and 4 (section 165(1)); and

- would be payable in instalments at least once a year until the employee’s death.

For details of pension rules:

- 1 and 3, see NIM02767 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02767)

- 4, see NIM02768 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02768).
