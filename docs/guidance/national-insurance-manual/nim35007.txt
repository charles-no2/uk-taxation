Special Cases: Share Fishermen: Residency abroad

Note: This page is under review and the content may only be appropriate to periods beginning before 1 January 2021 and for those covered by a Withdrawal Agreement

Share fishermen who are European Economic Area nationals, resident in another EEA country who work on a UK-registered ship are subject to the same EEC legislation as ‘Mariners’ – see NIM29003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim29003).
