Class 1 NICs: Earnings of employees and office holders: Payment for changes in conditions of employment
Identifier: NIM02280
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02280
Published: 2016-04-11T17:04:05+01:00
Updated: 2021-11-11T13:58:49+00:00

Include payments made to employees in return for a change in the terms and conditions of service in gross pay. This will apply, for example, where a cash payment is made for increasing the working week from 36 hours to 37 hours.
