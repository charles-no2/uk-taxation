Regulations 126 to 139, Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

A widow’s right to reduced liability ends if she:

- cancels her election (see NIM31017 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31017));

- is not a ‘qualifying widow’ (see NIM31007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31007));

- fails the two year test (see NIM31008 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31008)); or

- reaches State Pension age (see NIM31009 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim31009))
