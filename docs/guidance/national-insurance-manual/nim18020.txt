Class 1B NICs: liability: what is a PAYE settlement agreement (PSA)?

A PAYE Settlement Agreement (PSA) is a formal agreement between HMRC and an employer. It allows the employer to account for tax on certain expense payments and benefits in a lump sum after the end of the tax year.

See PSA1000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-settlement-agreements/psa1000) for guidance about PSAs.
