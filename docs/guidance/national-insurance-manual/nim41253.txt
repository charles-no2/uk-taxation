Section 23A of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (SSCBA 1992)

The above legislation provides for contributors reaching State Pension age on or after 6 April 2010 to be awarded a Class 3 National Insurance (NI) credit for each week falling after that date in respect of which they are a relevant carer. For an overview of credits for parents and carers, see NIM41252 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41252).

A person is a relevant carer in respect of a week if they:

- are awarded Child Benefit for any part of that week in respect of a child under the age of 12, or; (see NIM41256 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41256))

- are a foster parent for any part of that week (see NIM41261 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41261)), or;

- are engaged in caring, within the meaning given by regulations, in that week (see NIM41262 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41262))

In some circumstances a person can also be a relevant carer if they are the partner of a person who is awarded Child Benefit (see NIM41257 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41257)) or provide care for a child under the age of 12 (see NIM41258 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim41258)).

A person is not a foster parent or engaged in caring for the award of NI credits during any period in which they are:

- not ordinarily resident in Great Britain (see NIM33031  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33001)for more information about ordinarily resident), or:

- undergoing imprisonment or detention in legal custody.
