Whether payments of tips, gratuities or service charges are earnings

Where a payment of tips, gratuities, and or service charges is made to or for the benefit of an earner, those payments fall within the definition of earnings NIM02010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02010). Certain payments of or in respect of gratuities are disregarded from earnings when calculating Class 1 NIC liability. See NIM02905 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02905).
