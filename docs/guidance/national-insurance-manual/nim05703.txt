Class 1 NICs: Expenses and Allowances: Mileage allowances: Class of NICs due
Identifier: NIM05703
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05703
Published: 2016-04-11T17:12:30+01:00
Updated: 2022-02-22T15:09:19+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:18:57.856965+00:00

If an employer

- reimburses an employee the costs of using their own car for business travel, either by way of a mileage allowance or by exact reimbursement:

- see NIM05708 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05708) for payments made before 6 April 2002

- see NIM05815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05815) and NIM05816 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05816) for payments made on or after 6 April 2002

- provides fuel and meets other costs directly, for example by supplying fuel from the employer’s own fuel pump or by contracting direct with a garage for servicing and repairs, Class 1A NICs may be due, see NIM05660 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim05660).
