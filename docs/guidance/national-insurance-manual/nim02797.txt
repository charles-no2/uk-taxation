Paragraphs 9(1)(b) and 10 of Part 6 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security(Contributions) Regulations 2001 (as inserted by regulation 8(7) of the Social Security(Contributions) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2006)

To enable a payment out of an employer-financed pension only scheme (“EFPOS”; NIM02795 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02795)) to be disregarded in the calculation of earnings, the same conditions need to be satsfied that apply to a payment out of an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme (“EFRBS”; NIM02756 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02756)).So you should follow the guidance in NIM02760  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02760)(contents) -NIM02784, but reading any reference to an ERFBS as if it were a reference to an EFPOS.

For details of:

- the Class 1 NICs position on an employer’s payments into an EFPOS, see NIM02796 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02796)

- the Class 1A NICs position on a payment out of an EFPOS, see NIM14510 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim14510).
