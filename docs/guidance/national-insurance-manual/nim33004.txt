For people going to and coming from the European Economic Area (EEA) and Switzerland, National Insurance rules are governed by the EU Treaty and EC Regulations

On or after 1 May 2010 see NIM33020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33020)

Council Regulations (EC) No 883/04 and 987/2009

Before 1 May 2010 see NIM33300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33300)

Council regulations (EC) No 1408/71 and 574/72

Under transitional rules a National Insurance liability determined under these rules can continue to apply for a time. These rules can still apply in relation to third party nationals.
