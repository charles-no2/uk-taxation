First considerations.

Step 1

Has the individuals employment with:

- the secondary contributor (SC); or

- a subsidiary of the SC; or

- a person connected with the SC or a subsidiary of the SC ended? NIM02765 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02765)

Yes Go to step 2

No Go to step 3

Step 2

Is the individual:

- re-employed as an employee; or

- engaged as a self-employed person with the SC from employment with whom the benefits are derived? NIM02765 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02765)

Yes Go to step 3

No Go to step 4

Step 3

Payment from EFRBS cannot be disregarded in the calculation of earnings.

Step 4

Is the payment a pension or a lump sum?

Pension Go to NIM02781 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02781)

Lump sum Go to NIM02782 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02782)
