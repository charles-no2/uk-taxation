Class 2 National Insurance Contributions: Small Earnings Exception: How Net Profit is calculated: Admissible expenses: Personal drawings
Identifier: NIM21015

Personal drawings (for example for living expenses) were not an admissible deduction. Sometimes drawings were shown as expenses in the Profit and Loss Account where the accounts had not been completed with professional help. Therefore, such drawings should have been re-included within the declared profit.
