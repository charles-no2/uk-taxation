Class 1 NICs: Earnings of employees and office holders: Offerings, gifts or fees to ministers of religion
Identifier: NIM02260
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02260
Published: 2016-04-11T17:03:57+01:00
Updated: 2021-11-11T13:38:14+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:13:24.689135+00:00

If a minister of religion gets offerings, gifts or fees direct, which are not part of their stipend or salary, there are no NICs due on them.
