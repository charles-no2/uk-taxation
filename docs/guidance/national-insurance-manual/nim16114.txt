From 6 April 2005 the position for childcare provided in Northern Ireland is the same as that provided in Great Britain. See NIM16110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16110) for guidance. For the position before 6 April 2005 see NIM16103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16103).
