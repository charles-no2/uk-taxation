General

Some employees do not have a single site as a permanent workplace and have a job where the duties are in relation to a particular geographic area. The geographic area will be their permanent workplace where certain conditions are met.

See EIM32190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim32190) for more detailed guidance.
