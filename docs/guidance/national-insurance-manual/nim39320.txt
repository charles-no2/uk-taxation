National Insurance Numbers (NINOs): Registration of a new NINO: Juvenile Registration: exclusions from the automatic process

Some young people may not be included in the automatic registration process. See NIM39305 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39305).

Providing they are over age 15 years and 9 months they should

- use the "find my NINo" digital service to trace their National Insurance Number or

- contact the National Insurance registration Helpline on 03002003500.

Children who have not been part of a Child Benefit claim or a Tax Free Childcare award will need to apply through DWP. For details see NIM39325 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim39325).
