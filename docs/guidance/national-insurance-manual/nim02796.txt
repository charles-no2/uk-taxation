Paragraph 9(1)(a) of Part 6 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security (Contributions)Regulations 2001 (as inserted by regulation 8(7) of the Social Security (Contributions)(Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2006)

An employer’s payment into an employer-financed pension only scheme (“EFPOS”) is disregarded in the calculation of earnings for the purposes of Class 1 NICs.

For:

- the meaning of an “EFPOS”, see NIM02795 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02795)

- details of the Class 1 NICs position on a payment out of an EFPOS, see NIM02797 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02797).
