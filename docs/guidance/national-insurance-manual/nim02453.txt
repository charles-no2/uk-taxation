Regulation 25 and paragraph 7(6)(b) of Part 5 of Schedule 3 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

Sections 270A(7) ITEPA 2003

The Social Security (Contributions)(Amendment No 4) Regulations 2011 (SI 2011 no 1000)

NIM02440 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02440) explains that a qualifying childcare voucher of up to £55 per qualifying week per employment can be disregarded in calculating an employee’s earnings for Class 1 NICs purposes. For those new to a childcare voucher scheme from 6 April 2011 the amount that is disregarded for NICs purposes is £55 a week for ordinary rate tax payers £28 for higher rate tax payers and £25 for additional rate tax payers

A “qualifying week” means a tax week in respect of which a qualifying childcare voucher (see NIM02440 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim02440)) is received.

“Tax week” means one of the successive periods in the tax year beginning with the first day of that year and every seventh day after that. This means that:

- the last day of the tax year; or

- the last two days of the tax year where it is a leap year,

is treated as a separate week.
