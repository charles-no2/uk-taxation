Class 1A NICs: liability for Class 1A NICs: Class 1A NICs on sporting testimonials: testimonial arranged after sportsperson’s death
Identifier: NIM13307
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13307
Published: 2020-03-25T12:00:24+00:00
Updated: 2022-05-12T10:45:20+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:20:07.812997+00:00

If a testimonial is arranged after the sportsperson has died (for instance, for the benefit of the deceased player’s family) no NICs liability exists.
