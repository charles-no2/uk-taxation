General

NIM16681  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim16681)explains there are two methods for calculating the amount of interest chargeable to income tax on beneficial loans, the averaging and the alternative methods.

Using the averaging method – effect on Class 1A NICs

If at the time Class 1A NICs are due to be paid (see NIM13003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim13003))

- HMRC has not issued a notice to the employee telling him to use the alternative method as per section 183(1) of ITEPA 2003 , or

- the employee has not elected, by notice to HMRC, that he wants to use the alternative method as per section 183(2) of ITEPA 2003

the averaging method must be used to calculate the amount of general earnings chargeable to income tax. This is the same amount that Class 1A NICs are due on.

Income tax guidance

For guidance about the calculation methods see EIM26200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim26200).
