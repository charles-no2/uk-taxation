Aircrews who are not covered by EC Regulations may be covered by a Reciprocal Agreement (RA). The UK has RAs with all EC countries except Greece and Liechtenstein.

The UK also has a number of other RAs with certain countries containing provisions concerning aircrew contribution liability - see NIM33014 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim33014).
