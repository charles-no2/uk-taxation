Class 1 NICs: Payments made under employment legislation: Earnings period to be used in assessing NICs liability: Payments made under a protective award

Regulation 5(a) Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001

As mentioned in NIM07060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07060), a payment made under a protective award (see NIM07050  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07050)onwards) has its own specific earnings period.

The earnings period to be used in assessing the amount of NICs on a payment made under a protective award is the longer of:

- the protected period (see NIM07050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07050)); or

- that part of the protected period in respect of which the amount is paid; or

- a week.

Example

Employer BBBB makes a payment under a protective award to employee B. The payment is for the period 6 April to 31 May (56 days). The protected period (as decided by the employment tribunal – see NIM07050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim07050)) runs from 9 March to 31 May (84 days).

As the longest period is the length of the protected period, the length of the earnings period is 84 days.

- upper earnings limit

- earnings thresholds

for the length of earnings period shown in the above example, see NIM01010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim01010).
