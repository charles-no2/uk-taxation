Regulation 151 Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001 (SI 2001 No. 1004)

A volunteer development worker who is regarded as self-employed – see NIM34025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim34025) – is not liable to pay Class 2 National Insuance contributions (NICs) but is entitled to pay Class 2 NICs at the special rate if they so wish – see NIM34040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim34040).
