Key Points on current rules

-

Current legislation for property businesses carried out by individuals or partnerships is within Part 3 ITTOIA2005

-

Income from property profits and losses are mostly computed using the cash basis (see PIM1090 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1090) onwards) from tax year 2017-18, subject to certain exceptions. Prior to this they were calculated using normal trading income principles.

-

The basis period for a property business carried out by an Income Tax customer is the tax year (6 April to 5 April) with only two exceptions (see PIM1010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1010) for more details. NB The trade basis period rules cease to apply from 6 April 2024 following transitional arrangements in the tax year 2023-24) – see the Business Income Manual from page BIM81200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim81200).

-

For rules on how individuals and partnerships can use losses from a property business please see PIM4200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4200) onwards (PIM4120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4120) for Furnished Holiday Lets). Furnished holiday lettings rules cease to apply in tax years commencing on or after 6 April 2025.

-

Relief for finance costs (mortgage interest etc.) in respect of a dwelling related loan is restricted for tax year 2017-18 and onwards (see PIM2050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim2050) onwards)
