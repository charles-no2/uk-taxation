The following rents and adjustments are treated as property income and charged under Part 3 of ITTOIA05 (income tax), but do not form part of a property business:

-

Chapter 7: adjustment income, see BIM34000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim34000).

-

Chapter 8: rent receivable in connection with a UK section 12 (4) concern, see PIM1117 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1117).

-

Chapter 9: rent receivable for UK electric-line wayleaves, see PIM1118 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1118).

-

Chapter 10: post-cessation receipts, see PIM3000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim3000).

The following rents and adjustments are treated as property income and charged under Part 4 of CTA09 (corporation tax), but do not form part of a property business:

-

CTA09/S261: adjustment on change of basis, see BIM34000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim34000).

-

Chapter 7: rent receivable in connection with UK section 39(4) concern, see PIM1117 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1117).

-

Chapter 8: rent receivable for UK electric-line wayleaves, see PIM1118 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1118).

-

Chapter 9: post-cessation receipts, see PIM3000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim3000).
