The profits of a property business are calculated in the same way as the profits of a trade using generally accepted accounting practice if one of condition A to E in section 271A of the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005 (ITTOIA05) applies. Conditions A to E are outlined at page PIM1092 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim1092).

Certain trading income rules in Part 2 of ITTOIA05 apply for the purposes of computing the profits of a property business. They are set out in S272(2) and are listed below:
