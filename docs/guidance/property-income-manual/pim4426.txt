Property allowance: contents: definitions: relevant property income

An individual’s ‘relevant property income’ is their total ‘relievable receipts’ for all of their ‘relevant property businesses’ for a tax year as defined by ITTOIA05/S783BC.

See example at PIM4481 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4481) for more information.

It should be noted that if any of an individual’s relevant property income in a tax year is made up of receipts from:

- A connected employer (see PIM4452 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4452))

- A connected firm (see PIM4454 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4454))

- A connected close company (see PIM4456 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4456))

then the property allowance will not be available in that tax year.

For Example, if £1 of rent is received by an individual from a connected firm during the tax year, the individual is not eligible for any of the £1,000 relief for that year.

The property allowance will also not be available against the relevant property income of an individual with rent a room income who is not claiming rent a room relief (See PIM4458 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/property-income-manual/pim4458))
