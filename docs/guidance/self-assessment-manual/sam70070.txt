Customers can receive extra support, if a health condition or personal circumstance, make it difficult to contact HMRC. For example:

- dyslexia, autism or cognitive difficulties

- reduced mobility or physical disabilities

- sensory disabilities, like a visual, hearing or speech impairment

- mental health conditions, like depression, stress or anxiety

- financial hardship – unable to afford essentials like food, bills or rent

- victim of domestic abuse, including economic abuse

- receiving treatment in hospital

They can access this, by asking an advisor when they contact a HMRC helpline, or use the webchat service.

When answering customer queries by either method, you should follow the relevant guidance located at:

https://csg.guidance.prod.dop.corp.hmrc.gov.uk/cms/process/viewer/?p=adv90114&lob=nes#
