Plant and Machinery Allowances (PMA): meaning of plant and machinery: pontoons
Identifier: CA21215
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca21215
Published: 2016-04-16T08:44:17+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-06T16:53:03+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:34.253288+00:00

Treat pontoons that float as plant, even if they are attached to a pile or other fixed structure. Do not treat fixed pontoons as plant.

Treat Linkspans as items of plant.
