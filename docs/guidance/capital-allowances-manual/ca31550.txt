CAA01/S571

The IBA legislation treats a part of building as a building unless the legislation refers to the whole building. For example, the legislation about a proportion of non-qualifying use being disregarded CA32700  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca32700)refers to the whole building. This means that the reference to whole building in that legislation does not apply to part buildings.
