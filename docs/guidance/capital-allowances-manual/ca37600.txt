If you need to check that a building or structure falls within a particular enterprise zone, the local authority in which the zone is situated should be asked for confirmation that the building or structure is within the boundary of the enterprise zone. A list of local contact points is given in CA37650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca37650). Cases of difficulty that cannot be resolved locally should be referred to CTIAA
