Structures and buildings allowance (SBA): excluded expenditure: planning permission
Identifier: CA94100
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca94100
Published: 2019-10-30T11:07:38+00:00
Updated: 2019-10-30T14:07:07+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:40.471078+00:00

CAA01/S270BG(2),(9)

Expenditure incurred on, or in connection with, seeking planning permission, including fees and related costs, is not expenditure incurred on the construction of a building and is therefore excluded.

‘Planning permission’ has the meaning given by one of the planning enactments listed in CAA01/S436(2).
