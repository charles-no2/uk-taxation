Structures and buildings allowance (SBA): use: non-residential use

CAA01/S270AA(1)(c),(2)(b)(ii), S270EA

For a building to qualify for SBA CA90100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca90100), the building’s first use must be non-residential. In most cases it is this first non-residential use of a building which commences the up to 33 1/3 year period of SBA entitlement CA92010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca92010).

Non-residential use is wider than qualifying use. Specifically, it does not require there to be a qualifying activity CA92100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca92100).

The SBA is proportionately reduced where the building is not in non-residential use throughout the whole of a chargeable period CA91300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca91300).

A building is in ‘non-residential use’ if it is in use which is not ‘residential’ use CA92500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca92500).

Non-residential use includes periods of disuse, provided these follow immediately after a period of non-residential use CA92900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca92900).
