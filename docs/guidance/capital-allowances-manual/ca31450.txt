IBA: Qualifying expenditure: Capital repairs to a building
Identifier: CA31450
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca31450
Published: 2016-04-16T08:55:05+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-06T16:57:15+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:10.532562+00:00

CAA01/S272 (2) - (3)

Capital repairs to a building are repairs the cost of which is capital expenditure. For a trader, a capital repair is a repair whose cost could not be deducted in calculating trading profits.

Expenditure incurred on capital repairs to a part of a building is treated as capital expenditure on constructing that part of the building. This means that it qualifies for IBA if the normal conditions are satisfied.
