IBA: Qualifying trade: Fishing
Identifier: CA32227

Published: 2016-04-16T08:56:00+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-06T16:57:20+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:13.267771+00:00

A trade of fishing is a trade consisting in catching or taking fish or shellfish. This lets buildings used by the fishing industry qualify for IBA.
