Where to find the capital allowance legislation

The capital allowances legislation is in the Capital Allowances Act 2001 (http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/2/contents) (CAA01). Before that it was mainly in CAA90 apart from the legislation about patents and know-how, which was in ICTA88 Chapter 1 Part XIII.
