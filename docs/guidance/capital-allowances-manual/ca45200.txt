Note: The BPRA guidance has not been updated to reflect the amendments made by s.66 FA 2014 which came into effect on 6 April 2014 for income tax purposes and 1 April 2014 for corporation tax purposes.

CAA01/S360A

Conditions

BPRA is available where a person incurs qualifying expenditure on converting or renovating a qualifying building CA45300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca45300). The allowance may be claimed by a person who incurred qualifying expenditure CA45300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca45300) and has the relevant interest CA45400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca45400) in the qualifying building. This means that BPRA is only available to the person who does the converting or renovating. A person who buys the relevant interest after the qualifying expenditure has been incurred cannot claim BPRA because their expenditure on buying the building is not qualifying expenditure.

Commencement

There is a start date. Expenditure must be incurred on or after 11 April 2007 to qualify.

Expiry

The scheme originally had a life of 5 years but this was extended for a further 5 years so expenditure must be incurred before 5 April 2017 for income tax purposes and 31 March 2017 for corporation tax purposes to qualify.
