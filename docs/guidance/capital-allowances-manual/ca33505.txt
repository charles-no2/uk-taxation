CAA01/S292

The qualifying expenditure on a building is the expenditure on which IBA is based. Itis fixed when the building is first used and does not change even if the building changes hands, apart from one exceptional case CA37375 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca37375).
