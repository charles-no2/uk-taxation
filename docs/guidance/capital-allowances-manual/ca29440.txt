CAA01/S254 - S257

There are special rules for capital allowances and life assurance companies (CAA01/S254 to S257) and friendly societies (CAA01/S257A). Guidance on these rules is contained in the Life Assurance Manual (http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/life-assurance/manual.htm).
