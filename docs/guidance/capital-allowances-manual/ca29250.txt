CAA01/S159 - S171

There are plant and machinery provisions in Chapter 13 Part 2 CAA01 (Sections 159 to 171) about mining and oil industries. You will find guidance about them in the Oil Taxation Manual  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/oil-taxation-manual) .
