IBA: Buildings and Structures: Meaning of building

Published: 2016-04-16T08:54:30+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-06T16:57:11+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:08.948098+00:00

There is no definition of building in the IBA legislation. The dictionary defines a building as a substantial structure for giving shelter and so you should treat anything with four walls and a roof as a building provided that it is of reasonably substantial size. Something that is too small or insubstantial to be a building, such as a garden shed, will be a structure.
