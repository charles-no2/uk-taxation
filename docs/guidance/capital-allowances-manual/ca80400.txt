Dredging: Marinas
Identifier: CA80400
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca80400
Published: 2016-04-16T09:06:37+01:00
Updated: 2020-01-13T13:10:32+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:20:18.136877+00:00

You may get a claim for dredging allowances from a person who operates a marina. The operation of a marina would normally have been a dock undertaking for IBA purposes CA32232 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca32232). The operation of a marina which would have been a dock undertaking for IBA purposes will be a qualifying trade for dredging purposes and dredging allowances will be due.
