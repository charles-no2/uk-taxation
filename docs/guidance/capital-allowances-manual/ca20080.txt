CAA01/S270 & CAA01/S571

A part of an asset is treated as an asset for the purposes of the plant and machinery legislation CA11600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca11600).

The plant or machinery legislation applies to a share in plant or machinery in the same way as it applies to a part of plant or machinery. A share in plant or machinery is treated as used for the purposes of a trade so long as, and only so long as, the plant or machinery is used for the purposes of a trade CA29210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca29210).
