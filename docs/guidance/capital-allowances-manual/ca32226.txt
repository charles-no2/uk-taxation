IBA: Qualifying trade: Foreign plantation
Identifier: CA32226
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca32226
Published: 2016-04-16T08:55:57+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-06T16:57:20+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:21:13.119189+00:00

A foreign plantation is land outside the United Kingdom used for husbandry, forestry, or for the growing and harvesting of crops.

Crops are any form of vegetable produce and harvesting includes the collection of crops, however effected.
