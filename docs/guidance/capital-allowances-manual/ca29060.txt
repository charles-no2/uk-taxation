PMA: Partnerships and successions: Death of a spouse or civil partner
Identifier: CA29060
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca29060
Published: 2016-04-16T08:53:30+01:00
Updated: 2016-04-06T16:57:06+01:00

On the death of a spouse or civil partner, a qualifying activity carried on by the deceased person as a sole business may pass to the surviving spouse or civil partner.

In such a case, assessments on the surviving spouse or civil partner are sometimes made on a continuing basis as if the business carried on by the person who carried on the qualifying activity alone had not ceased (see BIM70670 (http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/manuals/bimmanual/BIM70670.htm)). Whether or not the assessments on the surviving spouse or civil partner are made on a continuing basis, capital allowances due to the person who carried on the qualifying activity alone for periods up to the date of death cannot be given in those assessments.
