Updated: 2016-04-06T16:58:00+01:00

The main conditions for relief are as follows:

- The person carries on a trade of mineral extraction (as defined in CAA01/S394 - see CA50110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca50110) and CA50120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca50120)), and

- The expenditure is incurred for the purposes of that trade, and

- The expenditure is qualifying expenditure within CAA01/S395 (see CA50200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca50200) onwards).
