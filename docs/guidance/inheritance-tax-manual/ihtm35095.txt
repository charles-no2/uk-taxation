Property redirected to the spouse or civil partner: limited interest slightly longer than two years
Identifier: IHTM35095
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35095
Published: 2016-04-09T10:14:01+01:00
Updated: 2017-09-12T08:50:30+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:41.293099+00:00

Where an instrument of variation (IoV) creates a short-term limited interest, that interest is ignored if it ends no more than two-years after the death (IHTM35133 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35133)).

Where an IoV within IHTA84/S142(1) gives the deceased’s spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) a limited interest in possession for a period slightly longer than the two years covered by IHTA84/S142(4), you should refer the case to Technical for noting (even if you do not consider such a reference appropriate under IHTM35093 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35093)).
