Clearance certificates: who is covered by a  clearance certificate?
Identifier: IHTM40012
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40012
Published: 2016-04-09T10:22:25+01:00
Updated: 2017-01-19T11:55:33+00:00

The form IHT30 protects all liable persons (IHTM30011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30011))

- at the same title

- on the same occasion

- for the tax on the property specified in the certificate

even though the form may not have been signed (IHTM40071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40071)) by all of them.

A certificate given to, say, the trustees (IHTM16050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16050)) of a settlement will not protect the deceased’s personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)). Similarly a certificate given to the personal representatives will not protect the settlement’s trustees. If there is more than one title a separate application must be made at each title. This applies even if the personal representatives and the trustees are the same people.
