Annuity to spouse or civil partner: annuity secured by personal covenant
Identifier: IHTM11075
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11075
Published: 2016-03-20T09:32:47+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:59:55+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:56:00.343635+00:00

If the annuity (IHTM16211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16211)) is secured merely by personal covenant and is not a charge on any property, the gift to the annuitant should be treated as a legacy equivalent to the open market value of the annuity. The Actuarial Group is responsible for the valuation.

You should first ask the taxpayer or their agents to

- state the date of birth of the annuitant (IHTM16211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16211))

- state the gender of the annuitant (if unknown), and

- provide a reasoned estimate of the open market value of the annuity

When that information is received, you should refer to the case to the Actuarial Group.
