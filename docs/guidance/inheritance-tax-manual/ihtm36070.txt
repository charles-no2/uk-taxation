Reasonable excuse: lack of information
Identifier: IHTM36070
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36070
Published: 2016-04-09T10:16:36+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:18+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:10.580974+00:00

You should not normally accept as a reasonable excuse that an account could not be completed before the deadline because certain information was not available. IHTA84/S216(3) allows personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) to include estimated figures in accounts provided that they have made the fullest enquiries that are reasonable practicable.
