Published guidelines: who must make the instrument
Identifier: IHTM35023
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35023
Published: 2016-04-09T10:12:48+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:20:34+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:27.371329+00:00

The guidelines say that the instrument must be made by the person or any of the persons who benefit, or would benefit under the dispositions of the property comprised in the deceased’s estate (IHTM04029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029)) immediately before their death. This requirement is considered in more detail at IHTM35041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35041).
