Property in Registered Pension Schemes, Qualifying Non-UK Pension Schemes and ‘S615(3)’ schemes is not relevant property and no proportionate or anniversary charges can arise.

Further guidance on Pensions can be found at IHTM17000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17000).
