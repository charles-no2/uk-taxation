Assessing: CTDs: if the taxpayer has lost the CTD
Identifier: IHTM31777
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31777
Published: 2016-04-09T10:07:00+01:00
Updated: 2018-01-24T15:33:49+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:34.034684+00:00

When the taxpayer states that they have not received or cannot find their CTD, refer the matter to CTD Team (https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs/contact/the-certificate-of-tax-deposit-scheme-enquiries) with a copy of the taxpayer’s letter or, if no letter is available, full details of the taxpayer or agent dealing with the case.
