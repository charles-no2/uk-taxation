Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime reliefs: potentially exempt transfers (PETs): when double charges relief arises
Identifier: IHTM14701
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14701
Published: 2016-04-09T09:21:20+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:47+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:14.216770+00:00

The relief Inheritance Tax (Double Charges Relief) Regulations 1987 (SI1987/1130) Reg.4 covers cases where the deceased had made a PET (IHTM14511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14511)) which has become chargeable, and immediately before the death was beneficially entitled to assets which:

- the deceased had acquired from the transferee otherwise than for full consideration in money or money’s worth

- are the assets transferred by the PET or represent that property

- are comprised in the deceased’s estate and is the subject of a chargeable transfer on death.

Apply the relief (IHTM14692 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14692)) in these circumstances as usual.
