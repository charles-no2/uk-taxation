Life Policies: life policy linked with a loan and inheritance trusts: what to do

Firstly you should obtain full copies of

- all the policies

- the trust/settlement documentation

- the loan documentation

- any documentation evidencing repayment of the loan.

Then follow the instructions at IHTM20503 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20503).
