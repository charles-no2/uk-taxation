Liabilities: law relating to debts: deducting business debts
Identifier: IHTM28393
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28393
Published: 2016-04-09T09:55:20+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:14:38+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:31.647421+00:00

Where the deceased is in business as a sole trader or in a partnership any business debts, for example liabilities incurred for the purposes of the business, should be deducted firstly against any business property, IHTA84/S110 (b).
