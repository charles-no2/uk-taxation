Erroneous or excessive repayments of tax: interest  supplement

Any repayment of tax or interest carries interest known as the ‘interest supplement’ (IHTM31665 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31665)). When an interest supplement is erroneous or excessive if the error is made because of inaccurate information given by us on form Sect 6 (IHTM31641 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31641)), these instructions apply.
