Definition and extent of liability (PETs): objects of a relevant property trust
Identifier: IHTM30054
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30054
Published: 2016-04-09T09:56:50+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-06T14:44:12+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:47.539082+00:00

Where property is settled by the transfer, IHTA84/S199 (1)(d) imposes liability on any person for whose benefit the settled property, (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)) or income from it, is applied (IHTM30037 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30037)). The liability extends to the amount of the property or income. The amount is net of any income tax paid.
