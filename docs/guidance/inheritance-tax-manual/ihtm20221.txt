Life Policies: policy on the deceased’s life not connected with any other transaction (except a trust): policy effected by the deceased for their own benefit but gifted later: introduction
Identifier: IHTM20221
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20221

Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:16+00:00

The transfer of a policy is a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)), and the claim is on the value of the policy at the date of transfer for Inheritance Tax purposes. The guidance at IHTM20231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20231) explains what this value is.

In addition if the deceased paid any premiums on the policy after the transfer, each payment is also a transfer of value. The claim is on the amount of each premium (IHTM20082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20082)).

The transfer should be notified on form IHT403 - there are procedural instructions at IHTM20041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20041) onwards.

If there is a reservation to the donor, there may be an alternative claim under the Gift With Reservation (GWR) (IHTM14025) rules if the policy was given to a non-exempt beneficiary.
