Qualifying investments: reversionary interests
Identifier: IHTM34138
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34138
Published: 2016-04-09T10:11:03+01:00
Updated: 2017-11-02T11:51:20+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:06.121234+00:00

You should not normally treat shares comprised in a reversionary interest (IHTM16231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16231)) as part of the estate for the purposes of this relief. This is because a reversionary interest is normally excluded property (IHTM04251 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04251)) under IHTA84/S48 and does not form part of the deceased’s estate under IHTA84/S5 (1). The qualifying investments (IHTM34131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34131)) must be in the deceased’s estate in order to be considered for relief, IHTA84/S178 (2).

However where a reversionary interest is not excluded property (IHTM04281 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04281)) the shares comprised in that reversionary interest do form part of the estate for the purposes of loss on sale of shares relief.
