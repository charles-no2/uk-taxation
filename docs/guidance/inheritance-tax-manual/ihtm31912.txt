The Accounts Team, Nottingham (Inheritance Tax) are based in Ferrers House.

Their responsibilities include:

- Validating the accounting records.

- Making sure that figures reported to the Corporate Accounts in Worthing are accurate.

- Compiling the in year and year end accounts.

- Producing financial reports for management.

- Assisting in the implementation of the office’s financial policy.

- Assisting caseworkers in reconciling case accounting records that do not balance.

- Assisting with pre-day one accounting issues.
