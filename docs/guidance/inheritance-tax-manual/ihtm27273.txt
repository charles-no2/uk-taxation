Foreign property: specific property of visiting forces and staff of Allied Headquarters: assets excluded from UK Inheritance Tax
Identifier: IHTM27273
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27273
Published: 2016-04-09T09:51:46+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:12:38+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:03:50.667632+00:00

IHTA84/S155 (1) says that assets of qualifying persons (IHTM27274 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27274)), that are eligible for this exclusion from the tax, are:

- emoluments paid by the government of any designated country to a member of a visiting force of the country

- tangible movable property (for example furniture, cars) which is in the UK solely because of the member’s duty here.
