Any reliefs against tax, such as double taxation relief (DTR) (IHTM27160 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27160)) must be deducted from the total tax to pay at your assessment group (IHTM31012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31012)).

COMPASS will carry forward the figure included in any previous calculations. If this is the first calculation, or you are using the manual template (IHTM31302 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31302)), include the total figure in the relevant box.
