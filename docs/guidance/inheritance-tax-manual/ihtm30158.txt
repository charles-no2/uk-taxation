Due date for payment: request to pay in advance
Identifier: IHTM30158
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30158
Published: 2016-04-09T09:57:51+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-12T15:28:11+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:00.102911+00:00

You must refer any request to pay tax, whether under discount or not, in advance of a liability crystallising (such as where a timber election has been made under IHTA84/S125 but the taxpayers wish to settle their contingent liability in advance of disposal of the timber) to your Manager.
