Pre-owned assets: valuation

The approach for valuing assets for the purposes of the pre-owned assets charge is the same as the general rule for Inheritance Tax in IHTA84/S160 (IHTM09701 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09701)). In other words it is the price that the asset might reasonably be expected to fetch if sold in the open market at that time, without any scope for a reduction on the ground that the whole of the asset is to be placed on the market at one and the same time, FA04/Sch15/Para15.
