Groups of exempt transfers: lifetime and death transfers

This group consists of

- transfers between spouses or civil partners (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031)) and (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032))

- transfers to charities and registered sports clubs (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101))

- transfers to political parties (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191))

- transfers of land to registered housing associations (IHTM11211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11211)) or registered social landlords

- transfers for national purposes (IHTM11221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11221))

The exemptions in the group have the following features in common

- they apply to deemed transfers (IHTM04025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04025)) as well as actual transfers (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)). So they apply to transfers on death and on the ending of a qualifying interest in possession in settled property as well as to actual transfers during lifetime

- where the transfer is only partly exempt (IHTM26001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26001)), the extent to which it is exempt is determined by IHTA84/S36 to IHTA84/S42

- the application of the exemptions is restricted by broadly similar provisions

- to the extent that a gift is an exempt transfer under any of these sections, the gift with reservation (IHTM04071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04071)) provisions do not apply - FA86/S102 (5) and

- there is a general formula (IHTM11013 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11013)) for working out how much exemption is due.
