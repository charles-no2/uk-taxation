Pages 1, 5 and 6 contain a number of questions about the estate. If any of the questions are answered ‘Yes’ then the relevant Schedule should be filled in and sent to us with the IHT400. The only exception to this rule is where the taxpayers deliver a ‘reduced account’ (IHTM10471 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10471)).

The Schedules are listed below. Use the links if you want to know what is included on any of these forms or find guidance on what you should do when these forms are included with the IHT400.

Not a long-term UK resident (IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000)) (death on or after 6 April 2025) (IHT401a)
