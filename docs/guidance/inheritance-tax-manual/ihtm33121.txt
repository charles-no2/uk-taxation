Loss on sale of land: changes in the interest or underlying land: general
Identifier: IHTM33121
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33121
Published: 2016-04-09T10:08:59+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:20:08+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:56.776084+00:00

You will need to adjust the sale price where some changes take place in either

- the interest, or

- the underlying land

at some time between the date of death and the date of sale (IHTM33071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33071)), IHTA84/S193.

IHTA84/S193 also contains provisions to take account of possible statutory compensation (IHTM33130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33130)).
