Selecting this option from the main toolbar takes you to the same options page as the button on the case index (IHTM31112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31112)).

You can turn these options on or off by selecting them (a tick means that the option is on).

You must always have the ‘save on exit’ option activated.
