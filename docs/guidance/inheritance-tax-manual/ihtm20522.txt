Life Policies: reverse loan scheme: legislation designed to counteract reverse loan scheme

FA86/S103 was introduced to disallow the deduction of any debt if, and to the extent that, the money was owing to anyone whose resources included funds derived from the transferor. The effect of this provision was to kill the reverse loan scheme.
