Corrective account (C4): when is an account required (England, Wales and Northern Ireland)?
Identifier: IHTM10702
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10702
Published: 2016-03-20T09:31:01+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-05T09:11:24+01:00

Strictly speaking a C4 should be completed in all cases where there is an amendment to the account.

You can insist on a C4 where:

- we are repaying money but we have any doubts that the repayment is due,

- there are lots of amendments or they are complicated,

- more tax is payable after a Form Cap A 5C (IHTM05083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05083)) has been sent to us, or

- assets that were given an estimated value in a reduced account (IHTM10471 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10471)) are redirected to a chargeable beneficiary (IHTM10478 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10478)).

If you have to ask for a form C4 and there is unpaid tax not in dispute, you might want to:

- raise provisional calculations, or

- suggest making a payment on account.
