Lifetime transfers: future payments: payments outstanding at death
Identifier: IHTM14874
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14874
Published: 2016-04-09T09:22:36+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:55+00:00

Where the transferor dies before all the payments have been made, it falls upon the legal personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) to complete those payments.

You can

- give an allowance in valuing the transferor’s estate immediately before the death for the non-gratuitous part of the remaining payments, but

- charge tax on the gratuitous portion.

Review the separate instructions at the liabilities section of the manual. (IHTM28110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28110))
