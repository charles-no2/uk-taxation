Obtaining mandates: introduction

If possible, you should obtain a mandate from the taxpayer under enquiry before contacting the third party.

There are two forms of mandate

- a mandate for enquiries of banks and building societies CH227120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/compliance-handbook/ch227120) (there is background information on when and how to approach banks directly at CH227110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/compliance-handbook/ch227110))

- a mandate which, suitably amended if necessary according to the facts of the case, may be used for other enquiries of third parties (IHTM09383 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09383)).

If you cannot get a mandate from the taxpayer you should first approach the third party on an informal basis. If you need to consider issuing an information notice (IHTM32191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm32191)) under FA08/Sch36 to a bank or building society you should consult Litigation before taking any action.

You should make the taxpayer aware that the bank may charge them for the information provided.
