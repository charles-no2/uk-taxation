When the value of an estate is reduced by business relief (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)) or agricultural relief (IHTM24001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24001)), its value for Inheritance Tax (IHT) purposes is less than its actual net assets value. In such cases, where the deceased bequeathed specific gifts (IHTM26003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26003)), rules known as the interaction rules apply. The example below illustrates the situation.

Example

Barry’s estate consists of:

A farming business +£800,000

Other non-relievable property +£600,000

Total value = £1,400,000

Less relief at 100% on farming business -£800,000

IHT value of estate = £600,000

By his Will, Barry leaves a pecuniary legacy to his spouse of £550,000, with the residue to the children.

How should the chargeable residue be calculated in this example? The actual benefit to the children is £850,000. But if the full amount of the widow’s legacy of £550,000 was deductible from £600,000, the value of the estate for IHT, the chargeable residue, would be only £50,000, which is well below the tax threshold.

The rules (IHTM26103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26103)) of interaction for dealing with this type of situation are contained in IHTA84/S39A.
