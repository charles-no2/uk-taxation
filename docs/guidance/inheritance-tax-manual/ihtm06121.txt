The regulations apply to four occasions of charge under the IHTA

- all charges in connection with pilot trusts (IHTM06122 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06122))

- a ten year charge, (IHTM06124 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06124))

- a proportionate charge, (IHTM06125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06125)) and

- a charge on an age 18-25 trust under IHTA84/S71E (IHTM06126 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06126))
