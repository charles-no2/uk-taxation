To work out the correct amount of tax to pay on a chargeable occasion you will prepare a calculation, or several calculations.

The calculation of tax may be broken down into several parts. You will need to prepare a separate calculation for each different assessment group (IHTM31012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31012)) at each Entry (IHTM31011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31011)).

Example

Theresa held personal assets and freehold property (on which the instalment option is claimed), and also had a qualifying interest in possession (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)) entitlement in a Will Trust (containing listed shares only) when she died.

You will need to raise separate calculations for the:

- Free estate NIOP

- Free estate IOP, and

- Will trust NIOP
