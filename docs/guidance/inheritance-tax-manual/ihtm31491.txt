Tax will be chargeable

- when property no longer has the benefit of Conditional Exemption (IHTM04112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04112)),

- when property previously subject to woodlands relief is disposed of (IHTM04122 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04122)).

You cannot use COMPASS (IHTM31101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31101)) to prepare your calculations, so you must use the manual calculation template (IHTM31301).
