Business relief: Replacement property: Introduction

The ownership test (IHTM25301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25301)) is not satisfied if the business property was not owned by the transferor (IHTM25302 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25302)) throughout the two years immediately preceding the transfer.

However, you can still treat the test as satisfied if the property replaced other business property and certain conditions are met, IHTA84/S107(1).
