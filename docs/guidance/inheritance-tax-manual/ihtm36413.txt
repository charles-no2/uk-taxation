Public funding for penalty hearings: Public funding and s.245 penalties
Identifier: IHTM36413
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36413
Published: 2016-04-09T10:21:27+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:54+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:04.667881+00:00

We do not believe that Public Funding applies to IHTA84/S245 penalties (IHTM36010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36010)). But if a taxpayer wishes to argue before the First-tier Tribunal that an IHTA84/S245 penalty is criminal they should be given the leaflet.
