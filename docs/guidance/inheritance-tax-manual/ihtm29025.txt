Enquiries: penalty enquiries

Whenever you contact a taxpayer, or their agent, to clarify or question the reasons why an asset was omitted, or under-valued in the original account, that is an enquiry (IHTM29011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm29011)). The penalties section (IHTM36000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36000)) of this manual tells you when you should seek a penalty.

Imposing a penalty for the late delivery of an account (IHTM29013 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm29013)) does not amount to opening an enquiry. If you then challenge aspects of the late account you will need to open an enquiry at that stage.
