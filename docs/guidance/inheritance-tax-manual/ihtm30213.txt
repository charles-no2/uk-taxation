Transfers on death: land
Identifier: IHTM30213
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30213
Published: 2016-04-09T09:58:31+01:00
Updated: 2026-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:06.786674+00:00

Land of any description, wherever situated, is qualifying property for instalments.

Instalments are interest free (IHTM30363 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30363)):

-

From 6 April 2026, to the extent that the value is reduced by Agricultural Relief (AR) or Business Relief (BR), or

-

Is reflected in the value transferred by the transfer of a business or an interest in a business if that value is not so reduced

-

Up to 5 April 2026, to the extent that the land

is reflected in the value transferred by the transfer of a business or an interest in a business

or

-

its value is reduced by AR (IHTM24000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24000)).

Otherwise, the instalments are not interest free.

Business relief (BR)

From 6 April 2026, the value of the land which qualifies for Business Relief (BR) (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)) also qualifies for interest free instalments.

Up to 5 April 2026, land which qualified for BR did not necessarily qualify for interest free instalments. Whether the instalments are interest free depended on the paragraph of IHTA84/S105 (1) under which the land qualifies for BR:

-

if the land (including settled land) qualified for BR on the transfer under IHTA84/S105 (1)(a), the instalments are interest free

but

-

if the land qualified for BR only under IHTA84/S105 (1)(d) or (e), the instalments are not interest free.

Agricultural property

Note that agricultural property (IHTM24030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24030)) qualifies for interest free instalments only to the extent that:

-

its value is reduced by AR (or, from 6 April 2026, BR)

or

-

it is reflected in the value transferred by the transfer of a business or an interest in a business.
