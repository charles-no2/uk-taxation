Life Policies: types of policy: unit-linked policy

A unit-linked policy is similar to a with-profits policy (IHTM20105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20105)) as the amount that is finally payable is not a fixed sum. The premiums (IHTM20082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20082)) paid on the policy are allocated to a number of units in an underlying fund. The sum payable is linked to the value of those units, which can fluctuate from day to day.
