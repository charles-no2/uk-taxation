Liabilities: investigating liabilities: income tax
Identifier: IHTM28150
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28150
Published: 2016-04-09T09:53:30+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:14:16+00:00

You should check that any deduction claimed for income tax liabilities

- has been calculated correctly, and

- does not indicate that business assets or income-producing assets have not been included in the estate.

In most cases you can accept a deduction for income tax as claimed. But you may need to raise further enquiries if:

- the deduction is large (IHTM28151 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28151)) in relation to the estate as a whole or inconsistent with what you know about the deceased’s lifestyle

- the liabilities arise as a result of an investigation settlement (IHTM28152 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28152))

- the deduction claimed is an estimate (IHTM28153 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28153)).

You also need to take special care when dealing with deductions claimed for income tax payable on:

- the disposal of an offshore fund (IHTM28154 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28154))

- the disposal of deep discount securities (IHTM28155 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28155))

- interest bearing securities

- sums received following the discontinuance of a business (IHTM28157 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28157))

- the death of a partner in a business (IHTM28158 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28158))

- voluntary payments of tax by the personal representatives (IHTM28159 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28159))

- the chargeable gains arising when a life insurance policy matures (IHTM28160 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28160)).
