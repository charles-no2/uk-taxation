Foreign property: specialty debts: Treasury Bills, British Savings Bonds, National Savings Income Bonds
Identifier: IHTM27080
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27080
Published: 2016-04-09T09:49:40+01:00
Updated: 2022-11-11T14:19:37+00:00

Treasury Bills and British Savings Bonds are treated as specialty debts and situs will follow the normal principles for unsecured specialty debts (IHTM27079 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27079)).

National Savings Income Bonds are securities registered on the National Savings Stock Register and are situated in the UK.
