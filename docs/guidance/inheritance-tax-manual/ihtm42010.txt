IHT100

The taxpayer is required to report chargeable events to Capital Taxes on form IHT100.

Future Claims

Where we are aware of a continuing trust in an existing inheritance tax case, it should be recorded as a future case on ALF and brought out for review at the appropriate time.

Offshore Trusts

New offshore trusts made by settlors who are long-term UK resident (IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000#)) (on or after 6 April 2025) or were domiciled (IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000)) in the UK (before 6 April 2025) have to be reported to HMRC (WMBC Assets) under IHTA84/S218 and recorded appropriately. (IHTM42604 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42604))
