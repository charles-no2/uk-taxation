Death

COMPASS will allow you to prepare calculations on a death case for transfers after 18 March 1986 (IHTM31014 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31014)) for

- the death estate (this includes free estate, settled property (IHTM04080), GWRs (IHTM14301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14301)) where the reservation ceases on death) and other property aggregable with it

- failed PETs (IHTM14512 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14512)) (including GWRs where the reservation ceases during lifetime), and

- additional charges (IHTM14571 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14571)).

Lifetime

At a lifetime case, COMPASS will allow you to prepare calculations in respect of transfers after 18 March 1986 which are

- immediately chargeable transfers (IHTM04067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04067)) (taxable at half death rate (IHTM14534 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14534))).

But, where you need to make an calculation for such a transfer, you will need to create a new case record (IHTM31235 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31235)) on COMPASS.
