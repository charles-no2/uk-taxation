Other communications received from the VOA:  taxpayer's figure is unacceptable

If the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) is unable to accept the taxpayer’sfigures, they will notify the taxpayer of the revised valuation on form VO 1001.

If there is no response from the taxpayer within 4 weeks the VOA will

- send a reminder on form VO 1002, and

- hold the papers a further 2 weeks

At the end of that period the VOA will send an interim report (IHTM23081),or seek assistance, as appropriate.
