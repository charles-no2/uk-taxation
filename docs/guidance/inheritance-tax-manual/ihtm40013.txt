Clearance certificates: when can an application  for clearance be made?
Identifier: IHTM40013
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40013
Published: 2016-04-09T10:22:27+01:00
Updated: 2017-01-19T12:01:14+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:14.769601+00:00

The liable persons (IHTM30011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30011)) should apply for a clearance certificate only when they believe that they have reported all the assets, liabilities and variations in the estate and they believe that the Inheritance Tax position will not require further amendment.

There are separate instructions for dealing with premature applications (IHTM40051 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40051)).
