Special valuation matters: benefit of building contracts
Identifier: IHTM23173
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23173
Published: 2016-04-09T09:37:40+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:08:58+00:00

Where it is known that

- the deceased had entered into a binding contract for work to be done on his property, and

- at the date of death the work had not been completed

you should

- inform the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) accordingly, and

- indicate the cost of the work, where known.

The VOA's opinion of value (IHTM23141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23141)) should reflect

- the benefit of all work to be done under the contract, but

- without deduction for payment to be made under the contract.

The payments to be made under the contract will therefore be deductible as liabilities (IHTM28000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28000)) of the estate.
