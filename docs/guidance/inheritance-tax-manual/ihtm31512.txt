In most cases you can simply charge interest on:

- the tax now due

- from the due date (IHTM30151)

- to the date of the calculation.

But you will have to reduce the interest charge where:

- there are deposits (IHTM31751 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31751)) on account to use against the tax, or

- the estate contains newly advised Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax repayments (IHTM31515 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31515)).
