Dispositions for the maintenance of the transferor’s  family: outline of IHTA84/S11

IHTA84/S11 provides that there are five types of disposition (IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023)) that are not transfers of value. (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) They are those for

- maintenance of the spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), or former spouse or civil partner, (IHTM04173 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04173))

- maintenance etc., of the transferor’s children, (IHTM04175 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04175))

- maintenance etc., of other people’s children, (IHTM04176 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04176))

- care or maintenance of a dependent relative, (IHTM04177 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04177)) or

- maintenance of the transferor’s illegitimate children (IHTM04175 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04175))

If only part of a disposition (IHTM04180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04180)) satisfies the requirements of IHTA84/S11 you should treat the part that does and the part that does not as separate dispositions.

The provisions of IHTA84/S11 are expressly applied to the lifetime disposition (IHTM04085 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04085)) of an interest in possession (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)) in settled property by IHTA84/S51 (2).
