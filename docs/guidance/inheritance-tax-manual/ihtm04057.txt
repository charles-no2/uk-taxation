Lifetime transfers: what is a potentially exempt  transfer?
Identifier: IHTM04057
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057
Published: 2016-03-20T09:15:50+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-09T16:47:05+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:52:29.443398+00:00

Subject to certain exceptions, a potentially exempt transfer (PET) is a lifetime transfer of value that satisfies three conditions. They are that

- the transfer is by an individual (IHTM04053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04053)) on or after 18 March 1986

- it would be a chargeable transfer (IHTM04027 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04027)) apart from IHTA84/S3A (or, if only partly chargeable, is a PET to the extent that it would be chargeable), and

- it is a gift to another individual or to a specified trust, (IHTM04058 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04058)).

So, the starting point is a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) within IHTA84/S3 (1). This is reinforced by IHTA84/S3A (6) which provides that, except as mentioned below, when tax is charged as if a transfer of value had been made, that deemed transfer (IHTM04025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04025)) cannot be a PET. So, as a general rule

- any transfer of value within IHTA84/S3 (1) can be a PET if the three conditions are satisfied, and

- a deemed transfer of value cannot be a PET even if those conditions are satisfied.

- a transfer of value which is wholly covered by an exemption cannot be a PET, it is an exempt transfer. (IHTM04026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04026))

But the legislation also specifically provides for

- transfers that cannot be PETs, (IHTM04061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04061))

- deemed transfers which can be PETs, (IHTM04063 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04063)) and

- deemed PETs (IHTM04064 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04064)).
