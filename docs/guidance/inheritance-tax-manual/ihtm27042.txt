Foreign property: valuation of assets: loss on sale of foreign land
Identifier: IHTM27042

Published: 2016-04-09T09:49:12+01:00

The relief for loss on sale of land (IHTA84/S190) also applies to foreign land. The procedure is the same as that for relief for loss on sale of land in the UK (IHTM33001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33001)).
