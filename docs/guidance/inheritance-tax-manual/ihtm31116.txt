Selecting ‘assessment’ from the main toolbar will give you a number of drop-down options. This will allow you to raise, view and update your calculations.

Raise |  This enables you to raise new calculations once you have set-up or amended your estate records |

Browse |  You can review previous calculations |

Paid/repaid |  You will use this to record any payments or completed repayments on your calculations |

Withdraw |  You can withdraw any raised or issued, but not paid calculations |

Cancel payment |  If you have this option you will be able to unpay (IHTM31212 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31212)) calculations in certain circumstances only. |
