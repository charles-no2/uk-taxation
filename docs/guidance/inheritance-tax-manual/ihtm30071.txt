Definition and extent of liability (lifetime transfers  chargeable when made): transferor
Identifier: IHTM30071
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30071
Published: 2016-04-09T09:56:56+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-07T10:52:22+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:48.672397+00:00

Under IHTA84/S3 (1) the main meaning of transferor is a person who makes a disposition (IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023)) which reduces the value of their estate. For deemed transfers of value, see IHTA84/S3 (4).

The transferor is primarily liable for the tax payable. IHTA84/S204, limitation of liability section, contains no provisions limiting the liability of the transferor under IHTA84/S199 (1)(a).
