Interest period: instalments without interest relief
Identifier: IHTM30362
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30362
Published: 2016-04-09T09:59:56+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-15T11:01:02+00:00

In most circumstances instalments (IHTM30191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30191)) of tax are without interest relief, i.e. interest on the unpaid tax is added to each instalment and paid accordingly (IHTA84/S227 (3)).

Thus in the case of the first instalment interest is charged

- on that instalment only

- from the due date to the date of payment.

However when the second instalment is assessed (IHTM31184 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31184)) interest is charged

- on that instalment only from its due date to the date of payment (in practice, date of assessment)

and

- on the whole of the tax outstanding between the due date of the first instalment and the due date of the second (i.e. for one year).
