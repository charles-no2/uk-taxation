Rules about excepted estates: foreign domiciliaries or non long-term UK residents

For all deaths before 6 April 2002 an IHT400 (IHTM10021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10021)) has to be delivered if the deceased was domiciled (IHTM13021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13021)) outside the UK at the date of their death. If the death was before 18 March 1986 the appropriate form is form Cap 201.

SI2002/1733 Inheritance Tax (Delivery of Accounts) (Excepted Estates) Regulations 2002 relaxed this rule for deaths occurring on or after 6 April 2002. Where all the conditions listed below are met, the estate will qualify as an excepted estate. The conditions are that the deceased:

Deaths before April 2025

-

was domiciled outside the UK  at the date of their death

-

had never been domiciled or deemed domiciled in the UK during their lifetime

Death on or after 6 April 2025

-

was not a long term UK resident  at the date of their death

-

Had never been domiciled or deemed domiciled in the UK (before 6 April 2025) and had never been a long-term UK resident (on or after 6 April 2025)

-

the value of their estate situated in the UK consists only of cash or quoted shares or securities passing under their will (IHTM12041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12041)) or intestacy (IHTM12101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12101)) or by survivorship, the gross value of which does not exceed the cash limits shown in the table below.

-

did not make any gifts from their UK assets of more than £3,000 per year within 7 years of the date of death.

For deaths on or after 1 January 2022 there is an additional condition. The deceased

-

did not own any indirect interests in UK residential property under IHTA84/Sch A1 (IHTM04311 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04311))

Deaths (all dates inclusive)

|

Gross value of estate

|

6 Apr 2002 to 31 Aug 2006

|

£100,000

|

1 Sept 2006 onwards

|

£150,000

|

If any of the above conditions are not satisfied a form IHT400 must be delivered.
