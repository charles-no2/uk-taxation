Lifetime transfers: future payments: exemptions
Identifier: IHTM14873
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14873
Published: 2016-04-09T09:22:34+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:55+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:29.033876+00:00

It follows that by treating the future payments as separate dispositions, the question of any exemption must be considered at the time of each payment.

Accordingly, the annual (IHTM14141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14141)), small gifts (IHTM14180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14180)), or normal expenditure out of income (IHTM14131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14131)) exemptions may be available on each disposition.
