Valuing the partnership interest: Sales
Identifier: IHTM25111
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25111
Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:19+01:00
Updated: 2016-09-29T10:15:12+01:00

The price which an asset fetches when sold is usually a good indication of its open market value. This is the case whether it is a sale of the deceased’s partnership interest as a whole, or of particular assets, e.g. plant and machinery/stock.

However you should always investigate sales to relatives to ensure that they were at arm’s length. You should refer any freehold/leasehold property to the Valuation Office Agency (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)).

Note that sales to surviving partners may be below market value (IHTM25120 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25120)).
