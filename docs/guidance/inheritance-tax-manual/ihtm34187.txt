Changes in shareholdings: rights issue
Identifier: IHTM34187

Published: 2016-04-09T10:11:55+01:00
Updated: 2017-11-07T16:15:40+00:00

If a rights issue is declared in respect of a qualifying investment (IHTM34131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34131)) the appropriate person (IHTM34161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34161)) may

- sell the rights (IHTM34188 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34188))

- take up and sell the allotted shares (IHTM34189 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34189))

- neither take up nor sell the rights (IHTM34190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34190))

- dispose of the rights without consideration (IHTM34191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34191)), or

- sell part of the rights and accept the balance (IHTM34192 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34192)) of the shares.

The impact on the calculation of the loss will depend on what option the appropriate person takes.
