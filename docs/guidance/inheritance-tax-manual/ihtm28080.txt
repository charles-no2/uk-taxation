The manual provides guidance on the following

- costs of sale (general) (IHTM28081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28081))

- costs of sale of heritage property that has been conditionally exempted (IHTM31493 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31493))

- costs of sale of woodlands that were left out of account for tax under IHTA84/S125 (IHTM31492 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31492))

- costs of realisation in partnership cases (IHTM28158 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28158))

- costs of discharge of receiver/deputy for a person lacking capacity (IHTM28082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28082)) (England and Wales only)

- costs of discharge of Curator Bonis (IHTM28083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28083)) (Scotland only)

- an agent’s commission for recovery of an asset(IHTM28084 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28084)).
