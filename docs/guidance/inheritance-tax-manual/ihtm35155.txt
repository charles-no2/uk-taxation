IHT implications of an Instrument of Variation: excepted estates
Identifier: IHTM35155
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35155
Published: 2016-04-09T10:14:31+01:00
Updated: 2017-09-13T13:34:56+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:47.373402+00:00

Where a variation is effected on or after 1 August 2002, it is no longer necessary to send us the instrument unless it contains a valid statement of intent (IHTM35058 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35058)) and it results in additional inheritance tax being payable. So where we receive an instrument of variation (IoV) in an excepted estate (IHTM06011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06011)) we will return it with a covering letter explaining the new provisions.
