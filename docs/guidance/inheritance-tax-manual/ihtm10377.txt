Funeral expenses (box 81): refreshments 
Identifier: IHTM10377

Published: 2016-03-20T09:29:12+00:00

You may allow a deduction for the reasonable costs of refreshments for mourners. You should look at the size of the estate and the deceased’s background when considering what is reasonable.
