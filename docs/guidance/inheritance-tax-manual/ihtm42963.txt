Employee benefit trusts: dispositions by close companies:  procedure where exemption is restricted
Identifier: IHTM42963
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42963
Published: 2016-04-09T10:27:09+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:26:09+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:11:34.748136+00:00

Where IHTA84/S13(2) stops S13(1) from applying, the disposition by the close company to the employee benefit trust is a transfer of value (IHTM04068 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04068)) unless IHTA84/S10 (IHTM42957 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42957)) or IHTA84/S12 (IHTM42958 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42958)) apply.

Where there is a transfer of value you will need to consider whether or not business relief applies (IHTM42965 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42965)).
