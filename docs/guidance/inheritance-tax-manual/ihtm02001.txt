This section deals with all aspects of communication with taxpayers. It includes

- information on the rules of confidentiality and disclosure and how they operate

- advice and instructions on how to use different methods of communication

- areas of difficulty, such as complaints, and

- guidance on the level of service we are required to deliver to taxpayers
