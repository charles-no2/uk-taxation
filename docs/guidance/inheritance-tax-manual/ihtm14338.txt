Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): the reservation: benefit by associated operations

Treat a benefit which a donor obtains through associated operations (IHTM14822 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14822)) as a benefit to him ‘by contract or otherwise’ (IHTM14831).

Where a person gives away property with a reservation to their spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), that will not in itself be a gift with reservation (GWR). However, if they share the spouse’s or civil partner’s enjoyment or benefit, it may be said that the donor has not been entirely excluded.
