Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime exemptions: order in which exemptions apply
Identifier: IHTM14132
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14132
Published: 2016-04-09T09:16:45+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:03+00:00

Where several of the exemptions that apply solely to lifetime transfers (IHTM14131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14131)) are available, and where the transfers in that year exceed the annual exemption limit:

- apply IHTA84/S21, normal expenditure out of income (IHTM14231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14231)) exemption, first on a purely factual basis, without regard to any other exemption, limited or unlimited

- apply IHTA84/S19, the annual exemption (IHTM14141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14141)), last after all the other available exemptions have been applied to the transfer.

If the taxpayer asks you to adopt a different order of application of the exemptions, refer the case to your manager.
