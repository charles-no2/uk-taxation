Sales of related property, etc: introduction

The personal representatives may claim relief where property (IHTM04030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04030)) which was valued in conjunction with

- related property (IHTM09733 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09733)), or

- other property in the deceased’s estate (IHTM09712 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09712)) that passes under another title

is sold within three year’s of death for less than the date of death value,IHTA84/S176.

To qualify for relief a number of conditions (IHTM09754 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09754)) have to be met. There are also special rules (IHTM09762 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09762)) that apply when the property also qualifies for relief under the loss on sale of land (IHTM33000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33000)) provisions.
