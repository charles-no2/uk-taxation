Procedures for taking penalties on late accounts: initial  action in CG and PC&S
Identifier: IHTM36034
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36034
Published: 2016-04-09T10:16:06+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:13+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:04.652178+00:00

When you receive a late account or a late account is referred to you, you should

- treat the penalty aspect as a priority

- check that the time limit (IHTM10803 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10803)) has been exceeded

- check that the taxpayer or their agent has not provided a reasonable excuse

- issue the standard penalty letter (IHTM36041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36041)) with the leaflet IHT13 if appropriate

- raise any other enquiries that are necessary

The delivery of a late account does not constitute an enquiry (IHTM29012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm29012))
