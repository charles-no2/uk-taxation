Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): insurance policies: normal out of income exemption on regular premiums
Identifier: IHTM14432
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14432
Published: 2016-04-09T09:18:57+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:33+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:57:47.084133+00:00

Gifts made on or after 18 March 1986 which are covered by normal expenditure out of income exemption (IHTM14231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14231)) may be chargeable as GWRs despite that exemption. The exemption is not one of the stated exempt transfers which cannot be GWRs (IHTM14318 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14318)).
