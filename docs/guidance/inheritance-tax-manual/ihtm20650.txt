In May 2007 HMRC issued a Technical note on the subject of discounted gift schemes. The original note can be found on the HMRC website (http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/cto/dgs-tech-note.pdf) External customers can find this guidance at http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/cto/dgs-tech-note.pdf (http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/cto/dgs-tech-note.pdf). The pages of guidance that follow consolidate the contents of that original note.
