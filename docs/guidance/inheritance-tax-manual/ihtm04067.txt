Lifetime transfers: what is an immediately chargeable  transfer?
Identifier: IHTM04067
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04067
Published: 2016-03-20T09:16:06+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-12T16:47:51+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:52:33.144773+00:00

Any lifetime transfer that does not qualify as a potentially exempt transfer (PET) (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)) will be immediately chargeable to Inheritance Tax under IHTA84/S3 (1). Two transfers that do not qualify are a

- transfer into a relevant property trust, because the gift is not to an individual or one of the specified trusts, see IHTM04058 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04058) and

- transfer to a company, see example 2 at IHTM04060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04060)

There may also be an alternative charge on the property transferred under the gift with reservation rules. (IHTM04071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04071))

There are four other transfers of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) that are specifically prevented from being PETs. These are a

- transfer by a close company, (IHTM04068 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04068)) IHTA84/S94 (1),

- the deemed disposition on the alteration in the capital or share rights (IHTM04069 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04069)) of close companies, IHTA/S98 (3),

- the release of a life interest (IHTM04063 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04063)) between 18 March 1986 and 16 March 1987, and

- the transfer of woodlands (IHTM04062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04062)) subject to an outstanding Estate Duty charge, which only qualifies for partial PET treatment.
