Other holdings of unquoted shares: Meaning of  unquoted for business relief purposes
Identifier: IHTM25192
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25192
Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:49+01:00
Updated: 2022-10-18T13:58:43+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:21.200348+00:00

The meaning of unquoted for the purposes of IHTA84/S105(1) is that shares are not listed on a recognised stock exchange, IHTA84/S105(1ZA). Further information can be found at IHTM18131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm18131).
