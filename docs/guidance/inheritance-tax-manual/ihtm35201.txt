The Inheritance (Provision for Family and Dependants) Act 1975 contains

- provisions enabling the courts to order financial provision out of the estate of a deceased person for his or her family and dependants, and

- one section, s.19(1), on the relationship between the Act and IHT. The effect of s.19(1) on that relationship is duplicated and extended by IHTA84/S146.

How the I(PFD)A 1975 works is explained further at IHTM35231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35231). The IHT consequences of an order under the Act will depend on the nature and extent of the order.
