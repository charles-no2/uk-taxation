Lifetime transfers: the charge to tax: other charges: death subsequent to the transfer
Identifier: IHTM14551
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14551
Published: 2016-04-09T09:19:56+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:24+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:57:58.441522+00:00

If the transferor did not survive the transfer by seven years or more, there is a further charge to Inheritance Tax because of the death. You have to recalculate tax on the transfer and consider any additional charges (IHTM14571 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14571)).
