Lifetime transfers: transfers by close companies: exemptions
Identifier: IHTM14853

Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:53+00:00

Any transfer by a close company is a deemed transfer (IHTM04061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04061)) for Inheritance Tax purposes and is not a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)), but

- the annual exemption (IHTM14141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14141)) is available as the restriction in IHTA84/S19(5) to deemed transfers of value does not extend to transfers under IHTA84/S95(5).

as is

- spouse or civil partner exemption (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031)) to the extent that the estate of the spouse or civil partner of a participator is increased.

The exemptions for

- small gifts (IHTM14180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14180)),

- normal expenditure out of income (IHTM14231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14231)), and

- gifts in consideration of marriage or civil partnership (IHTM14191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14191))

are not available since they cannot apply to deemed transfers.
