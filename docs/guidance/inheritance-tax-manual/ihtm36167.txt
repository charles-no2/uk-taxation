Investigating incorrect accounts, information or  documents: deciding the amount of penalty due
Identifier: IHTM36167
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36167
Published: 2016-04-09T10:17:51+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:29+00:00

For a penalty to be due on an incorrect account, information or document (IHTM36101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36101)) you need

- to first satisfy yourself through your enquiries and investigations that there is fraud (IHTM36290) or negligence (IHTM36301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36301))

- decide which penalty provision (IHTM36102 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36102)) applies so that you can calculate the maximum penalty payable (IHTM36177 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36177))

- you then have to establish by careful evaluation of the facts the degree of culpability so you can decide to what extent the maximum penalty can be abated (IHTM36177 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36177)).

If there is no evidence of fraud or negligence then no penalty is due in respect of delivering, furnishing or producing incorrect accounts, information or documents. But if there was a failure to correct an error without unreasonable delay (IHTM36208 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36208)) then you will need to consider a penalty under IHTA84/S248.

Before negotiating a penalty (IHTM36221) with the taxpayer you will need to get prior approval (IHTM36223 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36223)) from your manager for the amount of penalty (‘the expected offer’ (IHTM36174 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36174))) you are seeking.

Even if you decide not to take a penalty you should consider writing to or telephoning the taxpayer (IHTM36271 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36271)) to explain what might be done in future when providing an account, information or document so that the question of penalties does not arise.
