The ownership test: Meaning of ownership
Identifier: IHTM25302
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25302
Published: 2016-04-09T09:45:49+01:00
Updated: 2017-08-22T12:00:39+01:00

For the purposes of the ownership test (IHTM25301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25301)) (including the three situations where the general rule is relaxed) we consider ownership to have the following meaning

- in the case of settled property in which there is no qualifying interest in possession, legal ownership by the trustees,

- in all other cases, beneficial entitlement (IHTM04031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04031)), which includes entitlement to settled property in which the deceased/transferor had a qualifying beneficial interest in possession (IHTM16060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16060)).

In view of IHTA84/S91, where an unadministered residuary estate (IHTM22025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm22025)) includes business property, a residuary beneficiary is treated as owning the business property, or an appropriate share of it.
