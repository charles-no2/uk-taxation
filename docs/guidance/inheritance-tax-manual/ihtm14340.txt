Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): the reservation: when occupation is not a reservation
Identifier: IHTM14340
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14340
Published: 2016-04-09T09:18:21+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:29+00:00

There are two circumstances in which the donor’s occupation of gifted land is not treated as a reservation. They are, very broadly

- where the occupation was for full consideration (IHTM14341 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14341)) (this also applies to chattels), or

- where it represents reasonable provision for an infirm relative (IHTM14342 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14342)).
