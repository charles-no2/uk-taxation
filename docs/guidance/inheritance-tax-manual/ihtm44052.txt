There are a number of exemptions that apply

-

to an individual who is not resident in the UK for any year of assessment (IHTM44053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44053)), and

-

to individuals who are not long-term UK resident (IHTM44054 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44054) & IHTM44055 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44055)).
