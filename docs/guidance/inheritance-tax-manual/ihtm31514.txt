Assessing: calculating interest: transfer of deposit between files
Identifier: IHTM31514
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31514
Published: 2016-04-09T10:05:05+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:19:15+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:12.895069+00:00

You should disregard the date on which a deposit was transferred from one file to another.

The material date for calculating interest is the EDP (IHTM31211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31211)) of the money.
