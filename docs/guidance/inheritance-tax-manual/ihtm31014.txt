You can prepare calculations on COMPASS (IHTM31101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31101)) for all charges where the date of death, lifetime or settlement chargeable event is on or after 18 March 1986. You cannot use COMPASS to prepare calculations where:

- the date of death is before 18 March 1986

- the date of the chargeable transfer on which tax is due is before 18 March 1986.
