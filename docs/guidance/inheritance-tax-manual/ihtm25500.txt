Finance Act 2026 Schedule 12 also made the instalment option available on property which qualifies for business relief and details can be found at: IHTM25580 - AR/BR 50% rate: instalment option (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25580)
