Control holdings of quoted shares or securities: Transfers and shares within IHTA84/S105 (1)(cc)
Identifier: IHTM25202
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25202
Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:53+01:00
Updated: 2016-09-30T14:34:15+01:00

IHTA84/S105(1)(cc) applies to transfers and other events occurring on or after 10 March 1992.

It applies to quoted shares or securities which

- either by themselves or together with other such shares or securities owned by the transferor

- gave the transferor control of the company immediately before the transfer.

The instructions on shares and securities within IHTA84/S105 (1)(b) at IHTM25171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25171), and the time at which the transferor is required to have control (IHTM25172 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25172)), also apply to IHTA84/S105 (1)(cc).

Shares and Assets Valuation (SAV) will advise whether a holding of shares or securitiess atisfies the requirements of IHTA84/S105 (1)(cc) but may not be able to indicate whether or not business relief is due.
