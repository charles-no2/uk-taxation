COMPASS runs through the calculations to raise in the order they are shown on the case record.

If you want to raise a calculation at a later assessment group without raising a calculation at the current assessment group, for instance B/NIOP and not A/NIOP,

- do not press the Cancel button (IHTM31193 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31193)) as this will cancel all calculations you have raised so far and will not allow you to progress.

Instead,

- raise calculations for all the assessment groups in the estate,

- issue (IHTM31210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31210)) the calculations you want to send out,

- withdraw (IHTM31213 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31213)) the calculations you do not wish to send out.
