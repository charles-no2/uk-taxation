Intestacy arises where a deceased person does not effectively dispose of their property by Will (IHTM12041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12041))

There is either:

- a total intestacy where none of the estate is disposed of, or

- a partial intestacy where part of the estate is not disposed of.

There are legal rules (IHTM12111 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12111)) for the distribution of an estate on intestacy. The rules in England & Wales differ from those in Scotland (IHTM12142 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12142)) and Northern Ireland (IHTM12162 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12162))
