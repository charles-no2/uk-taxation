As indicated at IHTM20401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20401) these plans are not aimed at avoiding or mitigating Inheritance Tax. They are also quite rare. If you come across any such plans, refer the matter to the Actuarial Team, who will advise on what queries to raise and the tax position
