Investigating the assets on page 7: Farms, farmhouses and farmland (box 68)
Identifier: IHTM10102
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10102
Published: 2016-03-20T09:27:33+00:00
Updated: 2026-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:02.542208+00:00

The value of all the farmland, farmhouses and farm buildings owned by the deceased should be included in box 68 of the IHT400.

Details of property included in this box should also be found on form IHT405. If agricultural relief has been deducted this will also be shown on form IHT414. Guidance on agricultural relief can be found from IHTM24001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24001) onwards and details of the 100% relief allowance (from 6 April 2026) can be found from IHTM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25000).
