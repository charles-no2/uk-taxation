Payment by instalments: when instalments are payable
Identifier: IHTM30201
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30201
Published: 2016-04-09T09:58:18+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-13T10:00:10+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:04.544923+00:00

The facility provided by IHTA84/S227 (1) is for payment by ten equal yearly instalments. The first instalment is due:

- in the case of a transfer on death, six months after the end of the month in which the death occurred,

- in other cases, at the time the tax would be due if it were not being paid by instalments.
