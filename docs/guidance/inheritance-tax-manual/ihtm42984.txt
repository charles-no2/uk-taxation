Employee benefit trusts: property leaving employee benefit trusts: approved Profit Sharing Schemes 
Identifier: IHTM42984
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42984
Published: 2016-04-09T10:27:40+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:26:16+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:11:41.257488+00:00

If the trusts of the employee benefit trust are those of a profit-sharing scheme approved under TA88/Sch9 (IHTM42940 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42940)), IHTA84/S72(4) specifically waives the charge under IHTA84/S72(3)(b) on an appropriation of shares under the scheme.
