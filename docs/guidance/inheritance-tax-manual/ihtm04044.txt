Transfers on death: what is the value transferred on death?
Identifier: IHTM04044
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04044
Published: 2016-03-20T09:15:35+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-06T15:30:33+01:00

The starting point is the value of all the property that makes up the person’s estate (IHTM04029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029)) less the value of deductible liabilities (IHTM28010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28010)). The rules about the valuation of property (IHTM09701 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09701)) and the deduction of liabilities (IHTM28002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28002)) are covered in other sections, but there are some special rules that effect the value of the estate on death. These are that

- in valuing property in the death estate, the estate has to be considered in it’s entirety, so items of property may be taken together (IHTM04045 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04045)) even though they may be held under separate titles,

- although the deemed transfer takes place immediately before the death, certain changes in value caused by the death (IHTM04046 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04046)) are taken into account,

- dividing the property into natural lots (IHTM09701 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09701)) or valuing two or more items of property as a single unit (IHTM09701 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09701)) may produce a higher value,

- the value of certain categories of property is left out of the account, (IHTM04331 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04331))

- there are special rules for deducting certain liabilities on death such as funeral expenses (IHTM10371 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10371)) and expenses incurred abroad (IHTM27050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27050)).
