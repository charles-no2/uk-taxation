Liability on death transfers: other unsettled  property (except GWRs)

This category includes survivorship property (IHTM15081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15081)), nominated property (IHTM15101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15101)) and partnership interests (IHTM25091 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25091)) subject to restricted powers of disposition under the partnership contract.

The persons liable are the same as for the free estate (IHTM30021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30021)). The surviving joint owner or partner or other person entitled under the contract will normally be liable under IHTA84/S200 (1)(c) as vestee or beneficiary or both.

There are separate instructions for gifts with reservation (GWRs) (IHTM30081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30081)).
