Life Policies: investigating form IHT403: introduction

There are two situations in which transfers of value in connection with life policies should be recorded on form IHT403. These are:

- a gift of an existing life policy, or the effecting and immediate gift of a new life policy (IHTM20042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20042))

- the payment of premiums on a life policy for the benefit of someone else (IHTM20043 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20043)).
