Written correspondence is probably the most used method of communication between us and our customers. So, the instructions on correspondence form a large part of the section on communications. To make the instructions more accessible they have been divided up into the sub-headings shown on the contents page at (IHTM02000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02000)).

Further relevant information on correspondence may be found in the sections on Customer Service Standards (IHTM02271), personal applicants (IHTM02161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02161)) and disabled customers (IHTM02171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02171)).
