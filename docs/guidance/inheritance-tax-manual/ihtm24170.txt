Lifetime transfers: Introduction

Except where otherwise stated, the instructions contained in IHTM24001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24001) through to IHTM24167 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24167) apply to both death and lifetime transfers. However there are a number of additional considerations to take into account with lifetime transfers. These are the extent of relief where the value transferred exceeds the value of the property transferred (IHTM24174 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24174)), and whether the property transferred satisfies the definition of agricultural property (IHTM24030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24030)) may depend on whether it is considered in isolation or as part of the transferor’s estate as a whole (IHTM24171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24171)).

There are also additional conditions (IHTM24172 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24172)) that apply to lifetime transfers when calculating

- the taxable value transferred by a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)),

- the additional tax on other lifetime chargeable transfers payable by reason of the transferor’s death, and

- the special rules (IHTM24200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24200)) for charges under the gift with reservation provisions.
