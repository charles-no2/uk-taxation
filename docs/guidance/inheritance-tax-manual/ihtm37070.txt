Non - Land valuation cases: other valuation cases
Identifier: IHTM37070

Published: 2016-04-09T10:21:55+01:00
Updated: 2023-01-19T13:51:02+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:09.380556+00:00

Where there is an impasse regarding the valuation of an asset that is not land or unlisted shares, for example a valuable painting or antique, HMRC will consult, where appropriate, with a specialist valuer to establish the definite opinions of the tax at stake and the valuation arguments, and then follow the same procedures as for unlisted shares (IHTM37060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37060)).
