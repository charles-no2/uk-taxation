Pre-owned assets: exemptions: reservation of benefit in property but for certain exemptions
Identifier: IHTM44046
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44046
Published: 2016-04-09T10:31:05+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:26:33+00:00

The reservation of benefit provisions do not apply to disposals by way of gift that are exempt from Inheritance Tax (IHTM11000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11000)). FA04/Sch15/Para11(5)(b) provides that the POA charge does not apply where property would be subject to a reservation of benefit (IHTM14301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14301)) but for the following exemptions

- gifts to charities (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101)),

- gifts to political parties (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191)),

- gifts to housing associations (IHTM11211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11211)),

- gifts for national purposes (IHTM11221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11221)),

- maintenance funds for historic houses (IHTM11250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11250)), and

- employee trusts.

Note that where the disposal is to a maintenance fund, the POA charge is only disapplied when the property remains subject to the trusts complying with the requirements for maintenance funds, FA04/Sch15/Para11(10).

Note also that a gift to a spouse or civil partner which is exempt from the reservation of benefit provisions (IHTM14318 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14318)), is not exempt under this provision, but is instead an excluded transaction under FA04/Sch15/Para10(1)(b) (IHTM44032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44032)).
