Investigation of form IHT404: introduction to form IHT404

Details of a deceased person’s beneficial interest (IHTM15011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15011)) in joint property should be shown on form IHT404. The more detailed information asked for on this form is to help us decide:

- The extent (IHTM15031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15031)) of the deceased’s beneficial interest in the joint property immediately before the death. In other words, the share or proportion that should be included as part of the estate on death.

-

The way in which the joint property was held (IHTM15081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15081)) (whether the deceased’s interest passes by survivorship (IHTM15081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15081)), by Will (IHTM12041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12041)) or intestacy (IHTM12101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12101))). We may need to know this to work out:

- whether exemptions apply (IHTM11000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11000)),

- who the liable persons are (IHTM30011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30011)),

- whether an Instrument of Variation (IHTM35011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35011)) is valid, and

- whether grossing-up (IHTM26121 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26121)) applies

It will also tell us whether the start of the joint ownership arrangement included any lifetime transfer (IHTM15060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15060)) by the deceased

The information given on the IHT404 may also identify areas where we may need to raise an enquiry (IHTM29011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm29011)).
