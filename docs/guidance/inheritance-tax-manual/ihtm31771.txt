A certificate of tax deposit (CTD) (IHTM30186 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30186)) is a form of deposit made by a purchaser to cover present or future liabilities to HMRC.

Because the certificate itself usually carries interest, you must treat it in a different way to a normal deposit.
