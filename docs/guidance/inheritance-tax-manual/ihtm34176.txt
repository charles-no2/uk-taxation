Calculating the loss: sale value
Identifier: IHTM34176
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34176
Published: 2016-04-09T10:11:40+01:00
Updated: 2017-11-07T14:26:57+00:00

The general rule is that the sale value of any investment is

- the price for which it is sold (or, if it is greater, the best consideration that could reasonably have been obtained for it at the time of the sale), IHTA84/S179 (1)(b), plus

- the amount of any capital payments (IHTM34177 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34177)) received at any time after the death, IHTA84/S181.

The sale price is the gross proceeds of sale. All expenses are ignored.

A dividend received from a holding quoted as ex-dividend (xd) at the date of death is not included in the sale value.

The value of Government securities marked ‘1k’ or ‘1k-xd’ at the date of sale should be calculated (IHTM34174 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34174)) in the same way as for the date of death.
