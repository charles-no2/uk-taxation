The Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs have determined -

In relation to the deemed disposal for the purposes of inheritance tax on the death on 10 September 2018 of Alan Bertram Carr (“the Deceased”)

That at that date, the Deceased’s freehold interest in the 25 acres of land at Carr Farm, Little Michelton, Lancashire had

a) an open market value, having regard to the terms of section 160 Inheritance Tax Act 1984, of £375,000;

b) an agricultural value of £150,000.
