Deductions from the estate - Mortgages and secured loans (box80)

Box 80 of the IHT400 includes any liabilities that were secured by a mortgage on assets that qualify for the instalment option. Full details of the mortgages should be provided on form IHT410.

Guidance on how to investigate mortgage debts (IHTM28210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28210)) can be found in section 28 (IHTM28000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28000)).
