Lifetime transfers: what is the value  transferred by an immediately chargeable transfer?
Identifier: IHTM04070
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04070
Published: 2016-03-20T09:16:10+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-17T16:17:06+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:52:34.204169+00:00

The value transferred (IHTM04028 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04028)) is calculated by reference to the loss to the transferor’s estate (IHTM04054 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04054)). However, note particularly the need to gross up (IHTM14541 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14541)) the transfer if the transferor is paying the tax.
