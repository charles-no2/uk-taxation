Definition and extent of liability (settled  property): beneficiaries
Identifier: IHTM30103
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30103
Published: 2016-04-09T09:57:16+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-12T13:15:46+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:52.729145+00:00

The limitation of liability provisions in IHTA84/S204 (3) apply but only to those persons liable under IHTA84/S201 (1)(b) because they are beneficially entitled to a qualifying interest in possession (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)) in any property. The limitation is to the extent of those assets.

The liability of persons within IHTA84/S201 (1)(b) because they are entitled otherwise than beneficially to a qualifying interest in possession is not limited by IHTA84/S204 (3).
