Succession: Intestacy: distributions in Northern Ireland: beneficiary does not take up an entitlement under intestacy
Identifier: IHTM12177
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12177
Published: 2016-03-20T09:37:09+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:00:33+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:56:54.434955+00:00

A beneficiary cannot be forced to take a benefit under the intestacy and has the right to disclaim their interest. If the interest is disclaimed then the disclaimer is treated as a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) unless the provisions (IHTM35162 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35162)) of IHTA84/S142 are satisfied.

A beneficiary may also choose to redirect a benefit using an instrument of variation (IHTM35011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35011)).
