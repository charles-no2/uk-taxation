Employee benefit trusts: sub-trusts: revocation of sub-trust 
Identifier: IHTM42973
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42973
Published: 2016-04-09T10:27:24+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:26:12+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:11:37.784203+00:00

Where a sub-trust which does not meet the requirements of IHTA84/S86 (IHTM42911 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42911)) is revoked then, assuming that the main trust continues to meet the requirements of IHTA84/S86, the sub-trust fund will, once again, be held on those trusts.

As the sub-trust itself was a relevant property trust (IHTM42970 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42970)) the revocation will give rise to an exit charge (IHTM42110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42110)) on the property ceasing to be relevant property under IHTA84/S65(1)(a), unless the trust property is shares or securities in a company and the provisions of IHTA84/S75 apply (IHTM42948 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42948)).
