Liabilities: investigating form IHT419: when property derived from the deceased can be disregarded for FA86/S103 purposes?
Identifier: IHTM28368
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28368
Published: 2016-04-09T09:55:01+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:14:35+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:28.016207+00:00

The definition of property derived from the deceased (IHTM28367 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28367)) is wide-ranging. But, an earlier disposition should be ignored if

- it was not a transfer of value (IHTM04151 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04151)), and

- it is shown that it was not part of associated operations (IHTM09152) which contained any element of direct or indirect bounty, FA86/S103 (4).

An exempt transfer should be treated as a transfer of value for the purposes of this legislation.
