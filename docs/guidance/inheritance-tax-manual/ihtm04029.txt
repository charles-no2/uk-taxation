Structure of the charge: what makes up a  person's estate?
Identifier: IHTM04029
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029
Published: 2016-03-20T09:15:19+00:00

The value transferred by a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) is quantified by reference to a person’s estate. This is so whether you are dealing with a lifetime transfer (IHTM04051 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04051)) or the deemed transfer on death. (IHTM04041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04041))

The word ‘estate’ is given its general meaning and includes all property (IHTM04030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04030)) to which the transferor is beneficially entitled, (IHTM04031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04031)) IHTA84/S5(1). The legislation then goes on to both extend (IHTM04032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04032)) and restrict (IHTM04033 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04033)) the meaning of the word.
