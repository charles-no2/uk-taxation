Lifetime transfers: the charge to tax: cumulation

When you consider how to apply cumulation, there is a variety of factors for you to take into account. Your approach will depend on whether you are looking at

- the chargeable value of the death estate (IHTM14503 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14503))

- tax directly attributable to a Potentially Exempt Transfer (PET) (IHTM14513 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14513))

- tax on an immediately chargeable transfer (IHTM14533 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14533)), or

- additional charges on an immediately chargeable transfer arising on death (IHTM14573 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14573))
