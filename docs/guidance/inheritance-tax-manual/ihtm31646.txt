You should give supplementary interest in all cases, unless you have been directed to exclude interest supplement (IHTM31665 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31665)). The date to which interest is to be calculated will normally be 5 working days after the date you prepare the Sect 6. Select the ‘calculate interest’ button to calculate the amount of the interest supplement.
