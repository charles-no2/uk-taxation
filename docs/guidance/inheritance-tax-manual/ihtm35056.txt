Has an election been made?: the six month time limit
Identifier: IHTM35056

Published: 2016-04-09T10:13:29+01:00

A written notice of election should have been given within six months of the date of the instrument. So it was quite in order for the variation to be made within two years of the death, and the election to be made more than two years after the death, but within six months of the date of the instrument.
