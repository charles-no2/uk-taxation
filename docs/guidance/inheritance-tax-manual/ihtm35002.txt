Alterations to the devolution of an estate: provisions which relate to changes in the devolution of an estate
Identifier: IHTM35002
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35002
Published: 2016-04-09T10:12:37+01:00
Updated: 2017-09-07T11:53:59+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:25.126345+00:00

The provisions in the IHTA which relate to changes in the devolution of an estate are

- IHTA84/S142 - where the devolution is changed by instruments of variation (IHTM35011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35011)) or disclaimers (IHTM35161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35161)) made within two years of the death

- IHTA84/S143 - where a legatee redistributes assets in compliance with the testator’s request (IHTM35171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35171)) within two years of the death

- IHTA84/S144 - where the will creates a discretionary trust (IHTM42000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42000)) and there is a distribution (IHTM35181 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35181)) from it within two years of the death

- IHTA84/S145 - when a surviving spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) is entitled to a life interest under intestacy and elects to take a lump sum (IHTM35190) instead

- IHTM/S146 - where the devolution is changed by an Order under the Inheritance (Provision for Family and Dependants) Act 1975 (IHTM35201 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35201))

- IHTM/S147 - where, in Scotland, the executors elect that the estate should devolve without regard to legal rights or legitim. (IHTM35211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35211))

The disclaimer of interests (IHTM35165 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35165)) in settled property (IHTM16180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16180)) is the one provision that can apply following a lifetime transfer as well as a death.
