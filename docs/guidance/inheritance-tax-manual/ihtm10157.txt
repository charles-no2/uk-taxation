Other assets due to the deceased: deferred legacies
Identifier: IHTM10157
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10157
Published: 2016-03-20T09:28:08+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:39+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:09.757523+00:00

A Will may provide for the specific deferment of a legacy with no compensatory right to interest. If

- the prospective recipient of such a legacy dies before any benefit is received, and

- a discount is claimed to reflect the non-receipt of capital and interest

you should refer the file to Litigation for advice.
