Lifetime transfers: omissions: possible omissions of lifetime transfers
Identifier: IHTM14802
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14802
Published: 2016-04-09T09:21:47+01:00
Updated: 2018-07-09T16:23:17+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:19.362854+00:00

When considering an estate, you should be alert to the possibility that chargeable lifetime transfers may have been made but have not been declared.

If an omitted lifetime transfer does come to light after the taxpayer delivers an account, you must consider whether penalty action is appropriate (IHTM36101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36101)).
