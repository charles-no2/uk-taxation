Interest in possession?

Assets which do not produce income may have an interest in possession attached to them which defeats any otherwise discretionary provisions of a trust in which they are held. See IHTM16066 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16066)

Rights of residence – joint ownership

There are also potential interest in possession issues to bear in mind for joint residential property. See IHTM16139 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16139).
