The statutory rules for making a Will in England & Wales are to be found in the Wills Act 1837/S9, as amended by the Administration of Justice Act 1982/S17 which substitutes new wording into the original S9.

The Wills Act/S7 as amended by the Family Reform Act 1969/S3 (1)(a) stipulates the age requirement (IHTM12045 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12045)).

In Northern Ireland, the position is the same in both respects, under the Wills and Administration (Proceedings (NI) Order 1994/S5 & S4.

In Scotland, (IHTM12047 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12047)) there are different rules depending on whether the Will was made on or after 1 August 1995.
