Assessing: deposits: retention of deposits
Identifier: IHTM31754
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31754
Published: 2016-04-09T10:06:40+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:19:35+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:30.296773+00:00

We should not hold on to large sums of money on deposit for long periods of time. You should make sure that money on deposit is either applied against outstanding tax or repaid. But, you should not repay money where this might prejudice the interests of HMRC.
