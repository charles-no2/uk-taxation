Valuing the partnership interest: Goodwill
Identifier: IHTM25108
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25108
Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:13+01:00
Updated: 2016-09-29T09:48:57+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:13.927530+00:00

If it is necessary to value goodwill, refer the file to Shares and Assets Valuation (Goodwill) once the values of all other partnership assets have been agreed, but if the case involves land do not delay referring if early agreement with the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) looks unlikely.
