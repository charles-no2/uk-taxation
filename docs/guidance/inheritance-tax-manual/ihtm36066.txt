Reasonable excuse: not aware an account had to be  delivered
Identifier: IHTM36066

Published: 2016-04-09T10:16:28+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:17+00:00

Ignorance of the law is not an excuse.

The amount of time allowed to deliver an account is sufficient to cover this sort ofexcuse. You should not be easily persuaded that a reasonable excuse existed.
