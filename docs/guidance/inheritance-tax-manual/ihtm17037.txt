Pensions: IHT exclusions: omission to exercise a right
Identifier: IHTM17037
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17037
Published: 2016-04-09T09:27:02+01:00
Updated: 2023-06-29T12:07:21+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:59:20.062930+00:00

IHTA84/S3(3) treats an omission to exercise a right as a disposition where

- a person’s estate is diminished and

- the value of another person’s estate or of settled property is increased as a result (IHTM14810 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14810)).

In general, this can include a situation where a person does not exercise a right to take pension benefits and so increases the death benefits payable outside the estate. However, for deaths on and after 6 April 2011, where a member of:

- a registered pension scheme (IHTM17021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17021)),

- a qualifying non-UK pension scheme (IHTM17025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17025)) or

- a section 615(3) scheme (IHTM17026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17026))

omits to exercise a right to take available pension benefits from that pension scheme, this provision no longer applies. The provision can still apply to pension schemes other than those above, mainly employer-financed retirement benefit schemes (EFRBS).

IHTM17301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17301) gives further details of the section IHTA84/3(3) charge in relation to pension rights and the circumstances where we would apply such a charge.
