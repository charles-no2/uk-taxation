Page 8 of the IHT400 should include all debts owed by the deceased at the date of death apart from foreign debts (IHTM27032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27032)).

You need to consider whether the deductions claimed are properly allowable.

The general rule (IHTM28381 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28381)) is that we allow a deduction for debts that the deceased actually owed at the date of death. A debt for inheritance tax purposes is one that is capable of being legally enforced (IHTM28383 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28383)) and is either

- imposed by law, or

- a debt for which consideration (IHTM28382 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28382)) was received.

The next page (IHTM10362 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10362)) tells you where you can find instructions for dealing with specific types of liabilities.
