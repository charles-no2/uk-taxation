Qualifying investments: underwriting deposits
Identifier: IHTM34136
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34136
Published: 2016-04-09T10:10:59+01:00
Updated: 2017-11-02T11:43:25+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:05.378649+00:00

Relief is available for the sale for qualifying investments which are part of the underwriting interest held by a Lloyd’s name.

You should refer to Technical (IHTM01081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm01081)) any claim for relief involving a Lloyd’s name, where qualifying investments (IHTM34131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34131)) form part of the underwriting assets.
