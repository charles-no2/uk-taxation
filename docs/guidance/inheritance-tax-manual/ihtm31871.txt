Repayments are effectively processed when the PORF is sent to the accounts office Shipley. You must not withdraw any repayment calculations at this late stage. If you need to issue a revised calculation, you should wait until you know that the Sect 6 repayment warrant has been returned from Shipley.

To withdraw a Payable Order, use the Sect 6A procedure (IHTM31711) as normal.
