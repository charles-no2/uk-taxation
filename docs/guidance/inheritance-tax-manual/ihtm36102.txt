Incorrect account, information or document: types of  penalty
Identifier: IHTM36102
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36102
Published: 2016-04-09T10:16:57+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:21+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:13.145962+00:00

Penalties may arise where

- the taxpayer fraudulently or negligently provides (IHTM36103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36103)) an incorrect account, information or document (IHTM36101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36101)), IHTA84/S247 (1)

- someone who is not liable for tax fraudulently or negligently provides (IHTM36104 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36104)) incorrect information or an incorrect document, IHTA84/S247 (3)

- someone assists in or induces the delivery (IHTM36105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36105)) of an account, information or document which they know to be incorrect, IHTA84/S247 (4).

There are also penalties for failing to remedy an error (IHTM36106 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36106)) or failing to notify us of an error (IHTM36107 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36107)) in an account, information or document within a reasonable time of the error being found, IHTA84/S248.
