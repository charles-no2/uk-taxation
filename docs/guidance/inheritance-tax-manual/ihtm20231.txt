Life Policies: policy on the deceased’s life not connected with any other transaction (except a trust): policy effected by the deceased for their own benefit but gifted later: value of policy: general rule
Identifier: IHTM20231
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20231
Published: 2016-04-09T09:32:59+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:17+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:07.578286+00:00

As a general rule the value of a life policy is its open market value in accordance with IHTA84/S160. The open market value (IHTM20083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20083)) is the amount for which the policy could be sold, and might be more than the surrender value (IHTM20083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20083)).

The Actuarial Team is responsible for the valuation of life policies on an open market basis.

The guidance at IHTM20241 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20241) explains the special rules for the valuation of policies for Inheritance Tax purposes in certain circumstances.
