Dispositions allowable for income tax or conferring retirement benefits: outline of IHTA84/S12
Identifier: IHTM04191

IHTA84/S12 provides that two types of disposition (IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023)) are not transfers of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)). They are:

- those allowable in computing a person’s profits for income tax purposes, (IHTM04192 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04192))

- certain contributions to retirement benefits. (IHTM04193 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04193))

If only part of a disposition satisfies the requirements of IHTA84/S12, you should treat the part that does and the part that does not as separate dispositions, IHTA84/S12 (5).

If the parties claim, or the information before you suggests, that a disposition is the provision of retirement benefits, you should seek advice from Technical.
