Issuing a Notice of Determination or dealing with appeals depends on whether the case is

- concerned with the valuation of land (IHTM37051 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37051))

- concerned with the valuation of unquoted shares (IHTM37060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37060))

- concerned with other valuation (IHTM37070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37070)) issues

- a contentious case (IHTM37080 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37080)).
