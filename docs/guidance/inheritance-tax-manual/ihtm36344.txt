Letters of Offer: interest clause
Identifier: IHTM36344
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36344
Published: 2016-04-09T10:20:34+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:51+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:54.006904+00:00

The standard clause providing for interest at the current statutory rate in the event of delay in payment should be included in all draft letters of offer (except instalment offers (IHTM36251 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36251)), which contain their own interest clause) unless there is nothing left to pay.
