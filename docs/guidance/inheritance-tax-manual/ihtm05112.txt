Exceptions where no grant is needed: no grant cases
Identifier: IHTM05112
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05112
Published: 2016-03-20T09:21:32+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:56:31+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:53:44.005889+00:00

Sometimes Personal Representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) will need to submit a form IHT400 (IHTM10021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10021)) even though they do not need or intend to take out a grant (IHTM05001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05001)). For example, where all the property passes by survivorship, but Inheritance Tax is due on the estate.
