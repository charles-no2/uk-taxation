Raising enquiries: when to make your initial enquiries
Identifier: IHTM09051
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09051
Published: 2016-03-20T09:24:09+00:00
Updated: 2017-08-22T15:20:52+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:20.158654+00:00

You should always raise your enquiry as soon as it is appropriate to do so. This is a matter of judgement. You will need to balance the office targets for raising enquiries into new accounts with your wish to obtain as much background information as possible about the deceased or transferor.
