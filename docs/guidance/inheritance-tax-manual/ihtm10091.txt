National Savings investments (box 54): investments to be included in the estate
Identifier: IHTM10091
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10091
Published: 2016-03-20T09:27:28+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:35+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:01.471319+00:00

The following should be included in box 54 of the IHT 400 and in box 4 of form IHT206

- National Savings Certificates

- National Savings Capital or Deposit Bonds

- National Savings Income Bonds

- Pensioners Guaranteed Income Bonds

- Children’s Bonus Bonds

- First Option Bonds

- Save As You Earn Contracts

- Year Plans

National Savings Bank accounts should be included at box 2 of the IHT406.

Taxpayers are asked to include details of the investments and figures for the capital and interest owed, but not paid, up to the date of death.
