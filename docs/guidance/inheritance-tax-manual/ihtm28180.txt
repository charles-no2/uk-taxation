Liabilities: investigating liabilities: insurance premiums
Identifier: IHTM28180

Published: 2016-04-09T09:53:54+01:00
Updated: 2023-09-29T16:13:55+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:15.067922+00:00

You should make sure that any policies to which the payments relate are included in the estate.

You should not allow a deduction for any liability arising under or in connection with any life insurance policy taken out on or after 1 July 1986 unless all the sums assured under that policy form part of the estate, FA86/S103 (7).
