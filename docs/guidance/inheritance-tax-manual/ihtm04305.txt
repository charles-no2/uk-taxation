Government securities in foreign ownership:  FOTRA gilts as partnership assets
Identifier: IHTM04305
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04305
Published: 2016-03-20T09:18:56+00:00
Updated: 2018-05-03T09:38:31+01:00

Very occasionally the assets of a partnership (IHTM25091 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25091)) may include FOTRA gilts (IHTM04291 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04291)) which will normally constitute excepted assets (IHTM25351 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25351)) and so fall outside the scope of business relief (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)).

In this situation if a partner who is not resident in the UK (IHTM04295 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04295)) and, where appropriate, meets any conditions concerning ordinary residence and domicile (IHTM04296 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04296)), transfers their interest in the partnership, the interest will be excluded property (IHTM04251 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04251)) to the extent that it is attributable to the FOTRA gilts. You should calculate the amount to be excluded as follows:

(Value of exempt securities X Value of transferor’s partnership interest) ÷ Total value of partnership
