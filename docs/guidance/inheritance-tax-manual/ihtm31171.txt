You need to keep the COMPASS record up to date to ensure that any calculations issued are accurate. Incorporate amendments (IHTM31131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31131)) to the assets and liabilities as you become aware of them.

Check whether:

- there are any amendments to the previous lifetime cumulative total (PLCT)

- Quick Succession Relief (QSR) (IHTM22041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm22041)) is due

- any Double Taxation relief (DTR) (IHTM27181 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27181)) applies

- there is any money held on deposit to use against the calculations
