Sales: date of sale

The date of sale is either

- the date of the contract note, or

- the date on which any option to sell or purchase was granted, (IHTA/S189)

In the case of appropriations (IHTM34153 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34153)) the date of sale is the date of appropriation (Re Charteris, Charteris v Biddulph [1917] 2 Ch 379). Formal evidence, such as an instrument of appropriation, will normally exist to demonstrate that steps were taken to vest assets in the legatee.
