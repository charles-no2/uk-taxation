Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): the reservation: initial requirements

In considering the reservation you are concerned with establishing whether:

- the donee assumed bona fide possession and enjoyment (IHTM14332 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14332)),

- the donor was entirely excluded (IHTM14333 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14333))..

If either requirement is not satisfied, the gift is one with reservation.

You will also have to consider the impact of any changes in the property given (IHTM14313 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14313)).

There are separate instructions relating to insurance policies (IHTM14451 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14451)).
