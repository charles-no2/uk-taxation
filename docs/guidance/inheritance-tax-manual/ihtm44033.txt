Pre-owned assets: excluded transactions: the disposal condition - family maintenance
Identifier: IHTM44033
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44033
Published: 2016-04-09T10:30:29+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:26:32+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:10:14.909820+00:00

For the purposes of the disposal conditions relating to land and chattels, the disposal of any property is an excluded transaction in relation to the chargeable person if it was a disposition that meets the conditions of IHTA84/S11, dispositions for maintenance of family (IHTM04171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04171)), FA04/Sch15/Para 10(1)(d).

This provision is likely to be of limited application given that it is not easy for a disposition of a capital asset, such as land or chattels, to be shown to be for the maintenance of the donee. However, the decision in McKelvey v HMRC [2008] UKSPC (SPC00694) demonstrates that this may be so and any case where this exclusion is claimed should be referred to Technical.
