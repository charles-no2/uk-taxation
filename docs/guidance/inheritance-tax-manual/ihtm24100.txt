To satisfy the second alternative condition in IHTA/S117, the deceased/transferor must have owned the property concerned throughout the seven years immediately before the transfer and the property must have been occupied (IHTM24070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24070)) throughout the period for the purposes of agriculture (IHTM24060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24060)). The identity of the occupier is not material, but the continuity of such occupation is vital.

This general rule is relaxed where

- where the property transferred replaced other relievable property, IHTA84/S118 (IHTM24111 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24111))

- where the deceased/transferor became entitled to the property on a death, IHTA84/S120 (IHTM24121 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24121))
