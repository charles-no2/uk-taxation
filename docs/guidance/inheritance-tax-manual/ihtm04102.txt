Settled property: charges on trusts for  disabled persons
Identifier: IHTM04102
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04102
Published: 2016-03-20T09:16:51+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:54:20+00:00

Property (IHTM04030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04030)) transferred into trust after 9 March 1981 which is held on discretionary trusts primarily for the benefit of a disabled personis treated for the purposes of IHT as if the disabled person had an interest in possession (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)) (IIP) in the property, IHTA84/S89.

Distributions out of such property to the disabled person are not taxable, but a charge to IHT will arise on their death and the trust fund will form part of their estate. (IHTM04029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029))
