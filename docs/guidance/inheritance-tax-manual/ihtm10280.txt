Schedule 15 to the Finance Act 2020 provides for Inheritance Tax (IHT) relief where scheme payments are received and those payments meet the requirements as set out in the Schedule.

The relief is calculated in the same way as for payments made under compensation schemes for wrongs suffered during World War II and the instructions at IHTM04423 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04423)  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04423) should be followed.

The current schemes are:

Windrush Compensation Scheme IHTM (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10281)10281 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10281)

The Troubles Permanent Disablement Scheme IHTM (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10282)10282 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10282)

Post Office Horizon Compensation Scheme IHTM (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10283)10283 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10283)

Infected Blood Interim Compensation Payment Scheme (IBICPS) IHT (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10284)10284 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10284)
