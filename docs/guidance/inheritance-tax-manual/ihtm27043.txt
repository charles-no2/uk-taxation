Foreign property: valuation of assets: fall in value relief on foreign land or buildings

Fall in value relief (IHTM14621 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14621)) also applies to foreign land. The procedure is the same as for a claim for fall in value relief on UK land - except the value of the sold land must be converted to sterling using the exchange rate as at the date of sale (IHTM14626 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14626)).
