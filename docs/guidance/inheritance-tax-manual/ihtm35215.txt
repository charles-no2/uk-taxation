Where the executors wish to elect (IHTM35213 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35213)) for the provisions of IHTA84/S147(4) to apply, you should send form 123 to them for completion. All of the executors, not just the deponing executor, who confirmed to the estate must sign the election form.
