Settled property: on death
Identifier: IHTM22091

Published: 2016-04-09T09:36:20+01:00
Updated: 2016-09-14T14:46:29+01:00

When a qualifying interest in possession (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)) in settled property (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)) terminates on death, the settled property forms part of the death estate. Subject to one qualification, you may allow any appropriate quick succession relief (IHTM22041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm22041)).

The qualification is that you will have to restrict the tax available for deduction if part (or all) has been deducted against tax on a lifetime (IHTM22092 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm22092)) termination of your deceased’s interest in part of the settled property. You can see an example of this calculation at IHTM22093 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm22093).
