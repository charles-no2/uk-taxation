Transfers on death: introduction

Instalments are available for chargeable transfers made on death for the tax attributable to the value, including any addition for grossing up (IHTM26121 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26121)), of certain categories of qualifying property (IHTM30212 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30212)).

By reason of FA86/S102 (3), the transfer on death includes property taxable as a gift with reservation (GWR) (IHTM04071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04071)) because it was subject to a reservation at the death of the donor. However, because GWRs involve a lifetime gift, the application of the instalment provisions to them (IHTM30271 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30271)) needs separate consideration.
