Sales of related property, etc: conditions for relief
Identifier: IHTM09754
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09754
Published: 2016-03-20T09:25:47+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:23+00:00

The following conditions must be satisfied before relief can be given for sales of related property

- the relief must be claimed, IHTA84/S176 (2)

- the sold property must have originally been valued under the related property provisions or in certain circumstances in conjunction with other property in the deceased’s estate (IHTM09755 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09755))

- the sales must be by the personal representatives or by the person’s in whom the property was vested (IHTM09756 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09756)) immediately after the death

- the sale must be at arm’s length, IHTA84/S176 (3)(b)

- the sale price must be freely negotiated at the time of the sale, IHTA84/S176 (3)(b)

- the sale must not be made in conjunction with a sale of the related or other property with which it was originally valued (IHTM09757 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09757))

- there must be no connection between those selling the property and the purchaser (IHTM09758 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09758))

- those selling the property must not have a right to acquire the property or an interest in it (IHTM09759 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09759))

- the sale price must be less than the original value (IHTM09760 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09760)).
