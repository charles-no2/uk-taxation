While it is ongoing, income and capital gains generated by the trust assets are subject to Income Tax and Capital Gains Tax. You can find details of the taxing regimes at the Trusts, Settlements and Estates Manual, from TSEM3000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem3000).
