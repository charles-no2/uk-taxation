Step 1 - the starting value of specific gifts: gift of an annuity

Updated: 2016-02-11T17:11:55+00:00

Where a specific gift (IHTM26011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26011)) is a gift of an annuity you should follow the instructions at IHTM11071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11071) to value the gift. Although those instructions apply most commonly to gifts of annuities to spouses or civil partners (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), they also apply to gifts of annuities to other persons.
