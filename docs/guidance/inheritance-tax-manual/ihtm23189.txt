Special valuation matters: lease for life

There are three different situations which may lead to a lease for life. These are where

- a lease is reserved by the transferor or granted (IHTM23190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23190)) to him by the transferee in fulfilment of a condition

- a lease is granted gratuitously (IHTM23191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23191))

- occupation of settled property amounts (IHTM23192 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23192)) to a lease for life
