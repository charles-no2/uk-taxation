The general reservation rules (IHTM14331 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14331)) are supplemented for policies by a special rule aimed at PETA schemes (IHTM20410 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20410)) and the original discounted gift schemes (IHTM20421 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20421)).

Details of the special rule are explained at IHTM14452 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14452)

The examples which follow illustrate both the special rule and the application of the general reservation rules to policies (IHTM14453 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14453)).

You can also review examples 3 and 5 at the section dealing with the property given (IHTM14440 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14440)).
