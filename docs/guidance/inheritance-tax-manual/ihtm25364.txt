Lifetime transfers - additional conditions:  Continuing ownership
Identifier: IHTM25364
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25364
Published: 2016-04-09T09:46:21+01:00
Updated: 2016-10-20T10:23:36+01:00

The requirement in the first condition is that the transferee (IHTM25367 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25367)) has owned the original property throughout the period from the date of transfer to the death of the transferor, IHTA84/S113A(3)(a) or the earlier death of the transferee, IHTA84/S113A(4).

This original asset requirement is relaxed by the replacement property (IHTM25369 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25369)) provisions, and you need to consider carefully whether the original transferee has retained the asset.
