Is an account required? - other occasions where forms of account are not appropriate (England, Wales and Northern Ireland)
Identifier: IHTM05023
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05023
Published: 2016-03-20T09:20:34+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:56:15+00:00

As well as cases where the excepted estates (IHTM05021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05021)) regulations apply, the standard form of account (IHTM05031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05031)) is not appropriate where the following types of grant are being taken out:

- a grant of double probate (IHTM05103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05103))

- a grant of administration de bonis non (IHTM05102 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05102))

- a fresh grant following a cessate grant (IHTM05103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05103)) - as long as the property was within the operation of the previous grant (IHTM05001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05001)).

In these cases, Cap Form A5C (IHTM05081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05081)) should be completed.

Cap Form A5C is not appropriate where application is made for

- a full grant following a grant pendente lite (IHTM05103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05103)) or ad colligenda bona (IHTM05103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05103))

- a grant caeterorum (IHTM05103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05103))

- a new grant following the revocation of a previous grant

In these cases the standard form of account, usually IHT400, (IHTM10021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10021)) should be completed.
