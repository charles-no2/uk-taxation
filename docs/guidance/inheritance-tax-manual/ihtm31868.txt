Deposit (IHTM31751 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31751)) is the term we use word for a payment on account. It is also any money recorded on SAGE that is not matched to a calculation or recorded as a penalty (IHTM36010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36010)).

In the case of amounts placed on deposit as a result of a repayment set-off, the deposit number is the warrant number allocated to the repayment.
