Exempt transfers (IHTM11001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11001)) in this manual are divided into different categories depending on the type of transfer they apply to. These categories are

- lifetime and death (IHTM11012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11012)) transfers

- transfers of Heritage property (IHTM11014 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11014))

- transfers on death (IHTM11015 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11015)) only

- lifetime transfers (IHTM14000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14000)) only
