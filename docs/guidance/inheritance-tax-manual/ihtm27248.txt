Foreign property: discretionary trusts and exempt securities: unknown persons
Identifier: IHTM27248
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27248
Published: 2016-04-09T09:51:23+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:03:46.139282+00:00

The legislation refers to "known persons". So, when considering the conditions specified by the securities (IHTM04291 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04291)) you should not consider the possibility that some (currently) unknown person (for example an unborn child or future spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) of an existing beneficiary) might become a beneficiary in the future.
