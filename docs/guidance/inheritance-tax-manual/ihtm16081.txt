Settled property exemptions: exemptions on death
Identifier: IHTM16081
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16081
Published: 2016-04-09T09:24:44+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:04:56+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:53.449069+00:00

Where an interest in possession comes to an end on death the following exemptions are available:

- transfers between spouses or civil partners (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031))

- gifts to charities (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101))

- gifts to political parties (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191))

- gifts for national purposes (IHTM11221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11221))

- gifts for public benefit (IHTM11240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11240))

- for maintenance funds (IHTM11250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11250))

- gifts to Registered Housing Associations on or after 14 March 1989 (IHTM11211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11211)).
