Settled property: charges on accumulation and  maintenance trusts
Identifier: IHTM04099
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04099
Published: 2016-03-20T09:16:46+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:54:18+00:00

Property (IHTM04030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04030)) held on discretionary trusts which come within the inheritance tax definition of an accumulation and maintenance settlement is not relevant property and therefore exempt from the ten-yearly and proportionate charges.

Tax is charged

- where property held on accumulation and maintenance trusts ceases to be held on those trusts, IHTA84/S71 (3)(a) or

- when the trustees make a disposition (IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023)) which reduces the value of such property, IHTA84/S71 (3)(b).

The charge is on the amount by which the value of the settled property is reduced as a result of the event giving rise to the charge, IHTA84/S70 (5). Grossing up is required if the trustees pay the tax out of property remaining in the settlement. Under IHTA84/S70 (4) relief for dispositions not intended to confer bounty (IHTM04161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04161)) and a grant of a tenancy of agricultural property (IHTM04230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04230)) can apply.

There is no charge where settled property ceases to be held on accumulation and maintenance trusts

- because a qualifying beneficiary becomes entitled to the property or to an interest in possession in it, IHTA84/S71 (4)(a) or

- because a qualifying beneficiary dies before becoming entitled, IHTA84/S71 (4)(b).

Distributions to charities or other exempt bodies are not liable to the charge.
