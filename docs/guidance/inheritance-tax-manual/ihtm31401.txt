The previous two parts of this section of the Manual describe the mechanics of preparing a calculation on COMPASS (IHTM31101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31101)) and the processes you have to undertake.

This part is concerned with the calculations of tax and interest (IHTM31511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31511)) that you will need to include in your calculation.

Repayments (IHTM31601 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31601)) are dealt with separately.
