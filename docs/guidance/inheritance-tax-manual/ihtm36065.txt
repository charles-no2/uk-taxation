Reasonable excuse: bereavement
Identifier: IHTM36065
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36065
Published: 2016-04-09T10:16:26+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:17+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:08.740851+00:00

You should accept the death of a close relative or partner shortly before the deadline is a reasonable excuse as long as the necessary steps had already been taken to have the account ready on time.
