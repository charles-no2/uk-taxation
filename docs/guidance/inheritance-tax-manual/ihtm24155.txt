Agricultural relief reduces the value transferred (IHTM04028 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04028)) by a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)). Consequently it is applied before any exemptions are deducted and before the value of any agricultural property that is chargeable to tax is grossed up. (IHTM26101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26101))

However, with effect for deaths and other transfers of value from 6 April 2026 the allowance for 100% agricultural relief will only apply to chargeable transfers of qualifying property. A transfer of qualifying property to an exempt beneficiary such as a spouse/civil partner, or a charity, will not use any part of the 100% allowance.
