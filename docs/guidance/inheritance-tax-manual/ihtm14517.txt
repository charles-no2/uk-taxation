Lifetime transfers: the charge to tax: potentially exempt transfers (PETs): taper relief
Identifier: IHTM14517
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14517
Published: 2016-04-09T09:19:28+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:35+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:57:52.946039+00:00

If the chargeable PET is over three years from the date of death, apply taper relief (IHTM14611 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14611)) against the tax that is payable on the PET.
