Exclusions from exemption: gifts not limited to charitable and other exempt purposes
Identifier: IHTM11172
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11172
Published: 2016-03-20T09:33:52+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:00:10+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:56:14.165731+00:00

Section IHTA84/S23 (5) provides that a gift to a charity (IHTM11112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11112)) is not exempt if any part of the property given is used for purposes that are not

- charitable purposes (IHTM11112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11112)), or

- those of a body mentioned in

- IHTA84/S24 (political parties (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191)))

- IHTA84/S25 (national purposes (IHTM11221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11221)))

- IHTA84/S26 (public benefit (IHTM11240 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11240))), or

- if the property is land, IHTA84/S24A (registered housing associations (IHTM11211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11211)))

And IHTA84/S23 (5A), similarly, provides that a gift to a registered club is not exempt if any part of the property given is used for purposes that are not

- the purposes of the club in question;

- the purposes of another registered club;

- the purposes of the governing body of an eligible sport where the club in question exists for the purposes of the same sport, or

- charitable purposes.
