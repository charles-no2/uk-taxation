An election to merge components (IHTM45035 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45035)) or to opt out of the reduced rate (IHTM45040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45040)) must be made in writing and delivered to HMRC by the appropriate persons (IHTM45042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45042)) and within the time limits (IHTM45043 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45043)). If the election for either merger or opt out is made before delivery of form IHT400, a separate schedule - IHT430 - can be used. Otherwise, there is no specified form for either election.

Either election can be withdrawn by a notice in writing made by all those entitled to make the election. An officer of HM Revenue & Customs has the discretion to admit a late claim (IHTM45042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45042)).
