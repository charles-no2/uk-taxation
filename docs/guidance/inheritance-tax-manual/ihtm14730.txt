Where you are considering additional charges (IHTM14571 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14571)), you can allow double charges relief as you would for a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM14701 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14701)), and follow the same calculations.

As the calculations are of tax payable as a result of the death, the lifetime tax paid at half-death rates is not affected.
