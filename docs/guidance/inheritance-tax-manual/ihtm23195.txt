Special valuation matters: machinery and plant

The Valuation Office Agency (VOA) (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) may be asked for a valuation of machinery and plant (other than in going concern (IHTM23179 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23179))

When considering whether 100% business relief is available, bear in mind that from 6 April 2026, there is a £2.5 million 100% relief allowance on the combined value of agricultural and business property (apportioned between all items of such property) and any value over that amount gets relief at 50% (see IHTM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25500)).

When making such a request, send the VOA an itemised schedule of the assets to be valued.
