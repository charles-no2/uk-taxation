Life Policies: trusts of life policies: policy settled on its own trusts: conditions required for policy to be comprised in a settlement
Identifier: IHTM20152
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20152
Published: 2016-04-09T09:32:26+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:13+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:01.195780+00:00

A policy is comprised in a settlement if

- a beneficiary is given a life interest (IHTM16062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16062)), or

-

it is held for a person absolutely, subject to being defeated, for example

- by failure to survive to a given date, or

- by failure to survive to a given event, or

- by the exercise of a special power of appointment

- it is held for persons contingently (IHTM16066 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16066)), for example

‘for such of the children of Xavier as survive Xavier’.

See the examples at IHTM20154 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20154) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) and IHTM20155 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20155) (Scotland).
