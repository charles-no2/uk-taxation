Liabilities: investigating liabilities:  Agents' commission for recovery of an asset
Identifier: IHTM28084
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28084
Published: 2016-04-09T09:53:05+01:00

In view of IHTA84/S160, you should allow a deduction for commission paid to a professional agent in connection with the recovery of an asset.
