Other income due to the deceased - general
Identifier: IHTM10120
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10120
Published: 2016-03-20T09:27:47+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:35+00:00

The following income should be included in box 76 of IHT400

- any money owed in salaries, wages, director’s fees or arrears of pension - there is separate guidance on the treatment of retrospective pay awards (IHTM10125 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10125)), redundancy payments (IHTM10123 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10123)), outstanding barrister’s fees (IHTM10122 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10122)) and arrears of Civil Service pay and pensions (IHTM10124 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10124))

- rents due to the deceased

- guaranteed payments due to the estate under a purchased life annuity (IHTM20632 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20632)).
