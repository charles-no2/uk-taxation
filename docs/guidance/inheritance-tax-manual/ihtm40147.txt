Changes after the issue of the clearance certificate:  statutory amendments to the estate
Identifier: IHTM40147
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40147
Published: 2016-04-09T10:23:19+01:00
Updated: 2017-01-19T16:46:27+00:00

A certificate does not stop you from collecting any additional tax that becomes due if the value of the estate changes as a result of any of the following

- disclaimer of an interest in settled property (IHTM35165 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35165)), IHTA84/S93

- variations (IHTM35011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35011)) and disclaimers (IHTM35161), IHTA84/S142

- compliance with testator’s request (IHTM35171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35171)), IHTA84/S143

- distribution from property settled by Will (IHTM35181 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35181)), IHTA84/S144

- redemption of surviving spouse’s or civil partner’s (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) life interest on intestacy (IHTM35190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35190)), IHTA84/S145.

This applies irrespective of the title at which the amendment is made. If this view is challenged you should refer the case to Technical.
