Pensions: IHT exclusions: rights to a pension or annuity
Identifier: IHTM17036
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17036
Published: 2016-04-09T09:27:00+01:00
Updated: 2022-10-24T12:51:15+01:00

Under IHTA1984/S151, the value of an interest in:

- a registered pension scheme (IHTM17021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17021)),

- a qualifying non-UK pension scheme (IHTM17025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17025)), or

- a section 615(3) scheme (IHTM17026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17026)),

that comes to end on the death of the person entitled to it is excluded from the value of their estate if the interest:

- is, or is a right to, a pension or annuity

- is not a benefit provided otherwise than by way of a pension or annuity.

Death benefits do not fall within this exclusion.
