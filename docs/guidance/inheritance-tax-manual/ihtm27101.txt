Foreign property: money from a life policy: general rule
Identifier: IHTM27101
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27101
Published: 2016-04-09T09:49:47+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:12:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:03:28.684618+00:00

When a life policy is not made by way of deed the policy monies are situated where the debtor (the company) is resident. This is generally the head office of the company.

You can find more information on policies at IHTM20000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20000) onwards.
