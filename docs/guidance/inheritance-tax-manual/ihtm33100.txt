Loss on sale of land: value on death: meaning
Identifier: IHTM33100
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33100
Published: 2016-04-09T10:08:55+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:19:57+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:56.023338+00:00

‘Value on death’ means the value for tax purposes before any adjustments

- under the loss on sale of land provisions, IHTA84/S190 -198, or

- under IHTA84/S176 (relief for sales of related property (IHTM09752 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09752)).
