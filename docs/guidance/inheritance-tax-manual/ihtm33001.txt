Loss on sale of land: summary

This section of the manual tells you about the relief that is available when an interest in land (IHTM33061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33061)) is sold for a price that is different from its date of death value (IHTM33100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33100)).

It also tells you what steps you should take when considering a claim for relief.

The section is in three parts:

- an introduction (IHTM33010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33010)) to the relief and an outline of the basic conditions (IHTM33011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33011)),

- a guide to the procedures (IHTM33022 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33022)) you must follow when considering a claim for relief

- a more detailed background to how the legislation works for those of you who wish to consider particular aspects of the relief in greater depth (from IHTM33050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33050)).

There are links between the different parts.
