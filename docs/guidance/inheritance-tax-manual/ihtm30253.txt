Qualifying property (PETs): control holdings
Identifier: IHTM30253
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30253
Published: 2016-04-09T09:59:02+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:15:17+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:12.378311+00:00

The conditions are the same as with transfers on death (IHTM30217 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30217)). However, you should note:

- for lifetime transfers, this applies to shares or securities of a company which gave the transferor control of the company immediately before the transfer

- instalments are available for any transfer out of a control holding.
