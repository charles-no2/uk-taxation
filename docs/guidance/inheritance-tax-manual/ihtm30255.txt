Qualifying property (PETs): unquoted shares or  securities - 20% tax test
Identifier: IHTM30255
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30255
Published: 2016-04-09T09:59:06+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:15:17+00:00

The 20% tax test (IHTM30219 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30219)) applies only to transfers on death. It does not apply to lifetime transfers (IHTM14001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14001)).
