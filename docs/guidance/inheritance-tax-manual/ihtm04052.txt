Lifetime transfers: what is a person?
Identifier: IHTM04052
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04052
Published: 2016-03-20T09:15:42+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-09T16:05:38+01:00

The disposition (IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023)) has to be by ‘a person’. In law, this includes a corporation or company as well as a natural person. However in relation to the charging provisions, this wide meaning is effectively cut down by the definition of a chargeable transfer. (IHTM04027 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04027)) To be chargeable, a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) has to be either

- by an individual, (IHTM04053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04053)) that is a human being, or

- by a close company (IHTM04068 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04068)) and be treated under IHTA84/S94 to IHTA84/S102 as a transfer of value by an individual.
