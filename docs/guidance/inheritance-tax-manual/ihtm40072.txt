Section B: signatures of corporate or public trustees on form IHT 30
Identifier: IHTM40072

Published: 2016-04-09T10:22:46+01:00
Updated: 2017-01-19T15:17:17+00:00

The signature of an authorised official is necessary in the cases dealt with by

- the Public Trustee

- trust corporations

- bank trustees

If an appropriate person has not signed the form, you should return it and politely explain the correct procedure.
