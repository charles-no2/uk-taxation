You may need to withdraw an issued (but not paid) calculation because

- it is incorrect, or

- further amendments have come to light which need to be incorporated.

In either case, highlight the case record in the Case Index window and choose ‘Withdraw’ from the Assessments menu to take you to the Withdraw Assessments window.

Highlight the relevant calculation and select the Withdraw button. Confirm that you wish to withdraw the calculation.

Once a calculation is withdrawn it cannot be reinstated.
