Definition and extent of liability: trustees
Identifier: IHTM30034
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30034
Published: 2016-04-09T09:56:26+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:14:48+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:42.619761+00:00

The liability of persons liable as trustees (IHTM30091 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30091)) is limited by IHTA84/S204 (2).

There are particular rules where the deceased had and exercised a general power of appointment (IHTM30038 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30038)).
