Loss on sale of land: procedures: issuing form IHT38 
Identifier: IHTM33022
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33022
Published: 2016-04-09T10:08:06+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:19:50+00:00

The relief should normally be claimed on form IHT38 to ensure that all necessary information is provided. In most cases you will find out about a claim for loss on sale of land relief when you receive the form IHT38 from the taxpayers. You will need to follow the instructions at IHTM33031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33031) onwards for handling a claim for relief.

You should send the taxpayers a form IHT38 to complete if:

- they tell you that they wish to claim the relief

- the taxpayers tell us about a lower value than originally returned or agreed even if the relief is not specifically claimed (unless the value is still being considered by the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)).

- the VOA tells you about a possible claim.

Where unrepresented personal applicants (IHTM02161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02161)) are dealing with the case and you become aware that an interest in land (IHTM33061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33061)) is being sold for less than the date of death value, you should:

- bring the relief to their attention

- send them a copy of form IHT38.

- warn them about the risks of making a claim that may not be in their favour (IHTM33013 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33013)), if this might apply.

You should not ask the taxpayer to complete a form IHT38 in the following circumstances

- where the difference between the sale price (IHTM33072 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33072)) and the value on death (IHTM33100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33100)) is small and no relief would be available (IHTM33083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33083))

- the date of death value is still being considered by the VOA, when you should first report the sale price to the VOA (IHTM33035 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33035))

- where there is a claim for relief on a sale of a joint interest in land (IHTM33181 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33181)) unless you are satisfied that the claim will not be disadvantageous (IHTM33182 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33182)) to the taxpayer

- where the taxpayers claim to substitute a higher sale value (IHTM33026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33026)) and no tax is due on the sold land.

- where you are aware, or believe, that more than one interest in land is being sold, unless you are satisfied that the claim will not be disadvantageous (IHTM33013 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33013)) to the taxpayer.
