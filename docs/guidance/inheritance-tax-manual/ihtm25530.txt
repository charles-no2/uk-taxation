AR/BR 100% relief allowance: transfer of unused allowance
Identifier: IHTM25530
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25530
Published: 2026-04-07T07:44:07+01:00
Updated: 2026-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:48.143958+00:00

Unused relief allowance from a late spouse or civil partner may be transferred to the deceased’s estate, provided that a claim is made within the later of four years of the survivor’s date of death or six months of the personal representatives starting their role.
