Land valuation cases: action where there is no appeal
Identifier: IHTM37055
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37055
Published: 2016-04-09T10:21:49+01:00
Updated: 2023-01-19T11:01:27+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:08.290828+00:00

Unless the taxpayer appeals (IHTM37013 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37013)) against it within 30 days after the Notice of Determination was served, the determination will be conclusive, although late appeals may be allowed in some circumstances in accordance with IHTA1984/S223 (IHTM37101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37101)).
