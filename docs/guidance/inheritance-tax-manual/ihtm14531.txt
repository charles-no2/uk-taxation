Lifetime transfers: the charge to tax: immediately chargeable transfers: introduction

As the name suggests, the tax charge on an immediately chargeable transfer (IHTM04067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04067)) arises at the time it is made. It does not depend on a death.

The due date for payment (IHTM30153 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30153)) depends on the date of transfer.

The amount of tax payable depends upon

- the value for tax (IHTM14532 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14532))

- cumulation (IHTM14533 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14533)),

- the rate of tax (IHTM14534 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14534)).
