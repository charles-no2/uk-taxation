Transfers and other events on or after 17 March  1987: the transferee

‘The transferee’ is defined (by IHTA84/S227(1B) for the purposes of the section as a whole) as the person who became the owner of the qualifying property on the transfer or, where the transfer was into a relevant property trust (IHTM16042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16042)), the trustees of the settlement. In view of IHTA84/S 49 (1), when the transfer was into a qualifying interest in possession trust (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)), the life tenant is the transferee.
