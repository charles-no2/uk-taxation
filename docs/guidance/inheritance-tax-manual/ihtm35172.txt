Compliance with a testator's request: what you should do if you are notified of a transfer 
Identifier: IHTM35172
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35172
Published: 2016-04-09T10:14:48+01:00
Updated: 2017-09-13T14:23:09+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:50.757182+00:00

You should satisfy yourself that the transfer meets the requirements (IHTM35171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35171)) to fall within IHTA84/S143. Where it does the IHT implications are that

- the IHTA84 applies, and so tax is charged or exemption given as if the property transferred had been bequeathed by the testator’s will to the transferee, and

- the transfer is not a transfer of value, IHTA84/S17(b).
