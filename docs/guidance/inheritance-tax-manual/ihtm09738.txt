Related property: what happens when both the  general and special rules apply?
Identifier: IHTM09738
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09738
Published: 2016-03-20T09:25:38+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:21+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:39.122035+00:00

If there are three or more items that are combined for valuing property (IHTM09732 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09732)) under the related property rules you may get a situation where both the general (IHTM09735 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09735)) and special (IHTM09736 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09736)) rules apply. When this happens you should first use the general rule to apportion the aggregate value between the various categories of assets. You should then do a further apportionment using the special rule of the amounts apportioned to each category.
