Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): the reservation:  benefit to donor's spouse or civil partner
Identifier: IHTM14339
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14339
Published: 2016-04-09T09:18:19+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:29+00:00

Except as in relation to insurance policies (IHTM14421 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14421)), the gift with reservation (GWR) provisions do not extend to gifts under which a benefit is reserved to the donor’s spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)).

However, if enjoyment of a gift by the spouse or civil partner is shared by the donor the GWR provisions will apply. As is mentioned at (IHTM14338 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14338)), you may need to consider the associated operations provisions (IHTM14821 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14821)) in this context.
