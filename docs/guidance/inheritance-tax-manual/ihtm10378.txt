Funeral expenses (box 81): transport and embalming costs
Identifier: IHTM10378
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10378
Published: 2016-03-20T09:29:13+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:59:18+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:26.298289+00:00

You should allow the reasonable cost of transporting the deceased’s body from a foreign country to this country (or from the UK to the deceased’s home country). You should also allow the cost of embalming the body - see Hart v Griffith-Jones [1948] 2 ALL ER 729.
