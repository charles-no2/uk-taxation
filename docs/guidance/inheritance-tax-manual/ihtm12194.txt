Succession: Simultaneous deaths (commorientes): General law (Northern Ireland)

In Northern Ireland, there is no equivalent of S184 Law of Property Act 1925 (IHTM12192 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12192)) in England. The result is that the common law presumption of simultaneous deaths still applies in Northern Ireland with one exception. Under Article 30 Wills and Administration Proceedings (NI) Order 1994, where a Will contains provision for a substitute executor operative if the one designated in the Will dies before or at the same time as the testator (IHTM12001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12001)) but the deaths occur in circumstances rendering uncertain which of them survived the other, the provision is to take effect.
