Liability in special cases: woodlands
Identifier: IHTM30123

Published: 2016-04-09T09:57:33+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-12T14:18:48+00:00

IHTA84/S208 places liability to pay the tax chargeable under IHTA84/S126 on the person entitled to the proceeds of sale if the disposal is a sale or, if the disposal is not a sale, on the person who would be so entitled if it were a sale.
