Reduced rate for gifts to charity: elections: late claims

Under IHTA84/Sch1A/Para 9(3), an officer of Revenue & Customs has the discretion to admit a late claim for either an election to merge components (IHTM45035 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45035)) or to opt out of the reduced rate (IHTM45040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm45040)). The circumstances in which you may admit a late claim are the same as for a late claim to TNRB and you should follow the guidance at IHTM43009 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm43009).
