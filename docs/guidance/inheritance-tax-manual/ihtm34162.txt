Appropriate person: separate claims by legal  personal representatives and trustees
Identifier: IHTM34162
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34162
Published: 2016-04-09T10:11:26+01:00
Updated: 2017-11-07T09:33:13+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:10.981902+00:00

If there is both free estate and settled property, both the legal personal representatives and the trustees can make claims if there has been a loss on the sale of the qualifying investments (IHTM34131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34131)) for which they are liable. You should treat the claims separately.
