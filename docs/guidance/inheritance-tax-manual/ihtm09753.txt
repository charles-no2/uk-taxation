Sales of related property, etc: what the relief does  not do
Identifier: IHTM09753
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09753
Published: 2016-03-20T09:25:45+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:22+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:40.606264+00:00

The relief does not substitute the date of sale value for the sold property. The date of valuation is immediately before the death.

No relief is available for the property that was valued in conjunction with the sold property unless it has itself been sold.
