Step 4 - grossing up: initial procedure for grossing up

Before starting the grossing calculation, you should make a note of the figures and values you will need. These are

- the lifetime cumulative total (IHTM31413 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31413))

- the total value transferred (IHTM04028 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04028)) at the title where grossing up is required

-

the value (as reduced under interaction (IHTM26101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26101)) provisions if appropriate) of the specific gifts (IHTM26011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26011)) at that title in three separate groups

- the value of the exempt gifts

- the value of the chargeable specific gifts which do not bear their own tax

- the value of the chargeable specific gifts which bear their own tax

- the proportion of the residue that is exempt

After you have this information you should decide which type (IHTM26131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26131)) of grossing calculation to use.
