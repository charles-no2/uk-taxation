Lifetime transfers: lifetime transfers chargeable when made

The transferor is accountable, IHTA84/S216 (1)(a). This is because the transferor is liable for any tax due.

The normal time limits (IHTM10803 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10803)) for delivering an account apply. The proper form of account is the IHT 100a.

An account is not required

- if the transfer is exempt

- where the transfer is an excepted transfer’ (IHTM10823 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10823)).

If a transferor has died and an account is required, the transferor’s personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) should be asked to deliver it. If they say they are not accountable, you should refer the case to Technical

Special rules (IHTM10833 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10833)) apply when there is a chargeable lifetime transfer on the termination of a qualifying interest in possession.
