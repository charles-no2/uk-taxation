Some of the main areas that staff in Customer Services are involved in will include:

- ensuring the correct amount of Inheritance Tax (IHT) is assessed and collected before the grant is obtained;

- processing IHT accounts received in HMRC and entering data into HMRC systems;

- handling more straightforward taxpaying and non-taxpaying cases following a risk assessment process;

- the Service Technical Support team – providing a first level of support on technical and procedural matters;

- the Inheritance Tax Helpline (before 2 October 2023, the Probate and Inheritance Tax Helpline) – a telephone enquiry service for both members of the public and the professions. Both general and specific enquiries are handled. The Helpline telephone number is 0300 123 1072.
