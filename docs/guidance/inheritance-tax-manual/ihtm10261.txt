In Lewisham and Guys Mental Heath NHS Trust v Harris (as legal representatives of Andrews Deceased) [2000] 3 All ER 769 it was held that any claim for compensation under the

- Race Relations Acts

- Sex Discrimination Act 1975, or

- Disability Discrimination Act 1995

was a cause for action under s.1(1) of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1934 and could survive the death of the deceased.

You should refer any case where the personal representatives pursue a claim to TG.
