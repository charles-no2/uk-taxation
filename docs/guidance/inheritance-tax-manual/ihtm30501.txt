We are required to keep records of:

- remissions (IHTM30502 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30502)) of interest on tax

- non-statutory remissions and irrecoverable assessments (IHTM30502 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30502))

- cases compromised (IHTM30531 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30531)) upon a point of law

- losses due to departmental error (IHTM30541)

- erroneous or excessive repayments of tax (IHTM30551 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30551)).

Records are also kept of cases where the value of a holding of quoted stocks and shares (IHTM18061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm18061)) has been reduced because of the size of the entire holding - those are dealt with by Shares Assets and Valuation (SAV).
