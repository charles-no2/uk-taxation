The question at box 7 only needs to be answered if the deceased died domiciled in Scotland. It identifies those cases where a claim may be made for legal rights (IHTM12221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12221)) to apply as opposed to allowing the estate to pass in accordance with the provisions in a Will.

There are separate instructions for valuing an entitlement to claim legal rights as an asset (IHTM10302 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10302)) or a debt (IHTM10303 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10303)) of the deceased’s estate.

Where there is a possible claim for legal rights further information should be given on pages 15 and 16 of the account.
