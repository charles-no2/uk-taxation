Step 3 - interaction: the appropriate fraction

All specific gifts other than those of relievable property are reduced proportionately by using the appropriate fraction at IHTA84/S39A (4). The fraction can be expressed in two alternative ways depending on whether there are

- specific gifts of relievable property (IHTM26110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26110)), or

- no specific gifts of relievable property (IHTM26109 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26109))

The fraction is based solely on property at the title (IHTM26211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26211)) you are concerned with, normally the free estate (IHTM26003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26003)). Property chargeable at any other title, such as settled property (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)), is excluded.
