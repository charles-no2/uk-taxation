Other deductions from the chargeable estate - Exemptions and reliefs (box 93)

Box 93 on the IHT400 should include all reliefs and exemptions that are being deducted from the estate. These include exemptions for

- transfers between spouses or civil partners (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031)) (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032))

- gifts to charities (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101))

- gifts to political parties (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191))

- gifts to housing associations (IHTM11211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11211))

- gifts for national purposes (IHTM11221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11221))

- gifts of, or for the purposes of maintaining, heritage and other historic property (IHTM11250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11250))

You need to consider whether

- the exemption is due, and

- if the estate is partly exempt (IHTM26001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26001)), whether the value of the chargeable estate needs to be adjusted by grossing-up (IHTM26121 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26121)) or interaction (IHTM26101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26101)).
