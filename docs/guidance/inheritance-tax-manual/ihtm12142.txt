Succession: Intestacy: Intestacy (Scotland): Rules of intestacy

The rules of intestacy are found in the Succession (Scotland) Act 1964 as amended from time to time. Under these rules the personal representatives are required:

- to pay the deceased’s debts and expenses and, where appropriate in cases of partial intestacy any legacies bequeathed by the Will, and

- to distribute any of the deceased's free executry estate not disposed of by Will in accordance with the distribution rules (IHTM12151 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12151)) for an intestacy.
