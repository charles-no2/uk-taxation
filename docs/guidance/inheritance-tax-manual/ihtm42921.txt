Employee benefit trusts: conditions for relief: investigation

When investigating whether or not an employee benefit trust satisfies the requirements of IHTA84/S86, there are a number of points to look out for. These are:

- non-qualifying beneficiaries (IHTM42922 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42922))

- former employees (IHTM42923 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42923))

- probationers (IHTM42924 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42924))

- partnerships (IHTM42925 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42925))

- trustees’ remuneration (IHTM42926 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42926))

- splitting funds or ‘hiving off’ (IHTM42927 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42927))

- employees outside the UK (IHTM42928 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42928))

- company groups and subsidiaries (IHTM42929 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42929))

- share price schemes (IHTM42930 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42930))

- part-time staff and casual labour (IHTM42931 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42931))

- best interests of the company (IHTM42932 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42932))

- insolvency (IHTM42933 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42933))

- consultants (IHTM42934 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42934))

- payment of tax liabilities out of trust funds (IHTM42935 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42935))

- power to transfer funds to another settlement (IHTM42936 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42936))

- direction to waive dividends (IHTM42937 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42937))
