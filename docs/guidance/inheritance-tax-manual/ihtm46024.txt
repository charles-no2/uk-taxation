Calculating the RNRB: terms used: the 'default allowance'

The legislation refers to a person’s ‘default allowance’. This is the maximum potential value of the residence nil rate band for that estate before applying the taper threshold (IHTM46023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46023)). It is the total of:

(i) the residential enhancement at the person’s death (IHTM46022 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46022)), and

(ii) the person’s ‘brought forward allowance’ (IHTM46040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46040)).

However, if the value of the estate exceeds the taper threshold the ‘default allowance’ is reduced to become the ‘adjusted allowance’ (IHTM46025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46025)).
