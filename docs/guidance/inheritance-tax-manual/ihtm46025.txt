Calculating the RNRB: terms used: the 'adjusted allowance' 
Identifier: IHTM46025
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46025
Published: 2017-04-05T16:14:47+01:00
Updated: 2018-09-28T07:54:28+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:10:55.614331+00:00

The legislation refers to a person’s ‘adjusted allowance’. This is the reduced maximum possible RNRB when the value of the estate exceeds the taper threshold. The adjusted allowance is calculated by deducting the amount of tapering from the ‘default allowance’ IHTM46023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46023)).
