Business interests: Rate of relief
Identifier: IHTM25151

Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:36+01:00
Updated: 2026-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:18.059573+00:00

The rate of relief is:

-

On or before 9 March 1992: 50%

-

From 10 March 1992 to 5 April 2026: 100%

-

From 6 April 2026 (includes transfers made within 7 years of a death on or after 6 April 2026 if the transfer was made on or after 30 October 2024): 100% up to the value of the transferor’s lifetime allowance, 50% thereafter (IHTM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25500))
