Calculating the chargeable estate: special rule concerning spouse or civil partner exemption
Identifier: IHTM26020
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26020
Published: 2016-04-09T09:46:56+01:00
Updated: 2017-04-11T12:07:47+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:58.049561+00:00

Under IHTA84/S38 (2) there is a special rule where

- there are two or more specific gifts to the spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), and

- the spouse or civil partner exemption (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031)) is limited because of the spouse’s or civil partner’s foreign domicile (IHTM13031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13031))

Any such case should be dealt with as per the instructions at IHTM26130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26130).
