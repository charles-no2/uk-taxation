Excepted transfers and terminations - qualifying trusts
Identifier: IHTM06101
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06101
Published: 2016-03-20T09:23:18+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:02+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:08.264168+00:00

The regulations only apply to the termination of a life interest in

- a trust of settled property where the person became beneficially entitled to an interest in possession before 22 March 2006, (IHTM16062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16062))

- a trust for a bereaved minor within IHTA/S71A, (IHTM42815 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42815))

- a trust in which there is an immediate post-death interest within IHTA/S49A, (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061))

- a trust for a disabled person within s.89; or a self-settlement within IHTA/S89A, or a disabled person’s interest with IHTA/S89B (IHTM42805 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42805)) or

- a trust in which there is a transitional serial interest within IHTA/S49B - 49E (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)).
