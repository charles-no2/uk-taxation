Interest in unadministered estates: payment by  instalments
Identifier: IHTM22030
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm22030
Published: 2016-04-09T09:35:42+01:00
Updated: 2017-07-21T11:39:41+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:41.523005+00:00

You may allow payment of tax by instalments, interest free as appropriate, if the underlying assets comprised in the unadministered residuary estate qualify for the instalment option (IHTM30212 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30212)).

You must also take those assets into account in considering whether the tax chargeable on other property in the beneficiary’s estate may be paid by instalments.
