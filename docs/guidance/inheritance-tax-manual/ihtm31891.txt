A transaction report lists all the information recorded on SAGE in chronological order.

All transactions are shown, together with details about how they have been matched.

You can view the details on the report through COMPASS (IHTM31892 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31892))
