Adjustment of tax paid: underpayments
Identifier: IHTM30401
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30401
Published: 2016-04-09T10:00:12+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-19T14:14:49+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:25.341278+00:00

As a general rule, if too little tax has been paid, the additional tax is payable with interest. This is so even if the tax paid was that stated in a Notice of Determination (NOD) (IHTM37001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37001)) under IHTA84/S221. This general rule may be restricted or excluded by any of the following

- the instructions on reopening IHT liabilities (IHTM30411 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30411))

- the instructions on reconsideration of value (IHTM30421 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30421))

- where there is a change of view of the law or practice (IHTM30451 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30451))

- lapse of time (IHTM30461 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30461))

- a clearance certificate (IHTM40141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40141)) has been issued.

Where a transfer is reported after tax on a subsequent transfer has been settled, you need to consider IHTA84/S264.
