Structure of the charge: what is a chargeable  transfer?

A chargeable transfer is a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) made by an individual (IHTM04053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04053)) that is not an exempt transfer (IHTM04026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04026)). A transfer of value may be wholly or partly exempt. To the extent that it is not exempt it is chargeable to inheritance tax.
