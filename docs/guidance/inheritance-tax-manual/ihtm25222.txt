Land and buildings, machinery and plant: Property  within IHTA84/S105 (1)(d)
Identifier: IHTM25222

IHTA84/S105 (1)(d) applies to

- any land or buildings, machinery or plant which

- immediately before the transfer

- was used wholly or mainly for the purposes of a business (IHTM25153 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25153))

- carried on by either a company which the transferor then controlled or a partnership of which the transferor was then a partner.

There are additional requirements governing the period of user (IHTM25223 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25223)) and the transferor’s interest in the occupying company or partnership (IHTM25224 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25224)).

Used for the purposes of a business is a factual test; there does not have to be a binding contractual relationship between the transferor and the company or partnership.

There are special rules (IHTM25354 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25354)) where part of any land or building within IHTA84/S105 (1)(d) is used exclusively for the purposes of a business.

For the valuation implications of the IHTA84/S105 (1)(d) situation, see the following instructions:

- related property (IHTM23206 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23206))

- partnership property (IHTM23198 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23198))

- property let to a company in which the deceased was interested (IHTM23202 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23202))

- business premises owned by the deceased and occupied by a partnership terminating on the relevant transfer (IHTM23174 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23174))

- machinery and plant (IHTM23195 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23195))
