If appropriate the DV will also provide advice on:

- Whether any land is agricultural land or pasture (IHTM24031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24031))

- Whether there is any woodland (IHTM24032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24032)) or any buildings used in connection with the intensive rearing (IHTM24033 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24033)) of livestock or fish, which is occupied with the agricultural land or pasture and is ancillary to that land.
