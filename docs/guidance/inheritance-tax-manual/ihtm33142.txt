Loss on sale of land: non-qualifying sales: exceptions to the rule for adjusting the price
Identifier: IHTM33142
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33142
Published: 2016-04-09T10:09:15+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:20:10+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:59.725257+00:00

There is no provision under IHTA84/S196 for taking into account a non-qualifying sale

- where the sale price (IHTM33072 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33072)) is less than the value on death (IHTM33100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33100)), (where a loss is made) or

- where the sale takes place in the fourth year after death (IHTM33074 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33074)) for more than the date of death value.
