Repayments of tax (IHTM31601 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31601)) are due to the estate. Penalties are due from the taxpayer personally. This means that if you need to make a repayment of tax you should not delay it or withhold it because a penalty issue remains open. But you may retain part of the repayment to set-off against any penalty that may become due if you are specifically asked to do so by the taxpayers.
