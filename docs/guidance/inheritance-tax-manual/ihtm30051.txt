Definition and extent of liability (PETs):  transferee
Identifier: IHTM30051
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30051
Published: 2016-04-09T09:56:44+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-06T14:08:18+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:46.435887+00:00

‘Transferee’ is shorthand for the wording used in IHTA84/S199 (1)(b) – ‘any person the value of whose estate is increased by the transfer’. Exceptionally it is possible, e.g. by operation of the related property provisions (IHTM09731 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09731)), for a person to have the value of their Inheritance Tax estate increased by a transfer even though they do not receive any property from the transferor.
