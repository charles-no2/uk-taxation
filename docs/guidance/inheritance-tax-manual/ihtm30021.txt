Liability on death transfers: the persons liable
Identifier: IHTM30021

Published: 2016-04-09T09:56:10+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-05T10:22:33+00:00

The persons liable where the chargeable transfer is made on death under IHTA84/S4 are as specified in IHTA84/S200 (1). They are:

### Persons liable |  ### Authority |

|    |

The personal representatives (IHTM30031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30031)) |  IHTA84/S200(1)(a) |

Any person in whom the property is vested at any time after the death (IHTM30035 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30035)) |  IHTA84/S200(1)(c) |

Any person entitled to an interest in possession in the property at any time after the death (IHTM30035 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30035)) |  IHTA84/S200(1)(c) |
