Exclusions from exemption: postponed gifts
Identifier: IHTM11174
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11174
Published: 2016-03-20T09:33:55+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:00:11+00:00

A gift to a charity (IHTM11112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11112)) is not exempt if it takes effect on the termination, after the transfer of value, (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) of any interest or period. We interpret the term ‘takes effect’ to mean ‘takes effect in possession’. (IHTA84/S23 (2) (a))

Example

Ashley dies.

By Will Ashley leaves the residue of his estate to Leonora for life, and then to a charity when Leonora dies.

IHTA84/S23 (1) does not provide exemption on Ashley’s death (though it may on Leonora’s).
