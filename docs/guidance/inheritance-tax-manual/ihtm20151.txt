Life Policies: trusts of life policies: policy settled on its own trusts: express life interest in favour of settlor
Identifier: IHTM20151
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20151
Published: 2016-04-09T09:32:24+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:12+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:00.832584+00:00

If

- the only property settled is a policy on the life of the settlor, and

- the settlor is given an express life interest, and

- there is no clear indication that the settlor is not to receive any income produced during their lifetime

the settlor is considered to have an interest in possession (IHTM16062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16062)) in the policy.

Unless there is a clear indication of that the settlor is not to receive income it is considered impossible that the settlor did not intend to take any benefit from the only property in a settlement under which they have given themselves a life interest.

See the examples at IHTM20154 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20154) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) and IHTM20155 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20155) (Scotland).
