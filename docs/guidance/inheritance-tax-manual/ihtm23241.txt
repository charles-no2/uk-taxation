Centralised management information is made available to us by the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) about workflows and the age of work on hand. This is used to monitor VOA performance, so that we have a central overview of all outstanding cases that are of a specific age (for example, one year). This enables us to ensure that the right procedural steps have been taken and/or to provide advice and assistance, as necessary. This assists in the identification of potential problem cases sooner and enables matters to be got moving before the problem reaches the two-year stage (when our general reviews commence).
