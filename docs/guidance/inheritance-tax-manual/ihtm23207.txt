Special valuation matters: timeshares
Identifier: IHTM23207
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23207
Published: 2016-04-09T09:38:43+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:09:09+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:01:09.990280+00:00

The valuation of timeshares situated within the UK is the responsibility of the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) within whose geographic area the property falls.

Timeshares situated outside the UK should be referred to SAV (Foreign).
