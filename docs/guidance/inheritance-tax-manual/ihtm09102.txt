Investigating no-grant cases: approaching the accountable or liable persons

If you have identified the accountable person (IHTM10801 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10801)) then you should write to them enclosing the relevant IHT account (IHTM10011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10011)) and ask them to complete and return it to this office.

If you cannot identify the accountable person you should consider the options on the next page (IHTM09103 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09103)).
