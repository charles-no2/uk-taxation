Related property: definition of related property
Identifier: IHTM09733

Property (IHTM04030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04030)) is related to property in a person’s estate (IHTM04029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029)) if:

- it is comprised in the estate of that person’s spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), or

-

it is or has within the previous 5 years been the property of

- a charity (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101)), or held on trust for charitable purposes

- a qualifying political party (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191))

- a registered housing association (IHTM11211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11211)), with effect from 14 March 1989

- one of the bodies and institutions (IHTM11221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11221)) listed in schedule 3, or a body to which IHTA84/S26 applies

and became so following an exempt transfer by the deceased, transferor or that person’s spouse or civil partner after 15 April 1976.

For the purposes of transfers to a charity or one of the other bodies listed above,whether a particular person is another’s spouse or civil partner depends on that person’s status at the date of the transfer that is being valued and not the earlier exempt transfer.

If a charity or other body sells on the assets transferred to them to another such body, then

- the property would no longer be the property of a qualifying body, and

- the 5 year period would start to run from the date of the sale.
