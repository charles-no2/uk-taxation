Life Policies: policy on the deceased’s life not connected with any other transaction (except a trust): policy effected for the benefit of someone else from the start: introduction
Identifier: IHTM20251
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20251
Published: 2016-04-09T09:33:08+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:20+00:00

Where a policy is effected for the benefit of someone else from the start, the effecting of the policy is a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)).

The claim is on the first premium and, if the deceased paid any further premiums on the policy, a claim also arises in respect of each one.

There are procedural instructions at IHTM20044 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20044).

If the policy contains an option to surrender bonuses to the transferor before the policy matures, the guidance at IHTM20252 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20252)applies.
