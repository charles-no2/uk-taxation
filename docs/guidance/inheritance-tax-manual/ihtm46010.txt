Although RNRB applies in principle where a person’s estate includes their home and that home is left to their children or other direct descendants, the RNRB legislation (IHTA84/S8D to S8M) sets out the qualifying criteria in detail. It introduces a number of terms with specific meanings which, among other things, determine what qualifies as a ‘home’ (IHTM46011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46011)), who qualifies as a ‘direct descendant’ (IHTM46034 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46034)), and whether the ‘home’ is part of a person’s estate (IHTM46012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46012)). These are explained on the following pages.

There are further terms which apply to situations where RNRB is being transferred (IHTM46040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46040)) between the estates of spouses or civil partners, or where the downsizing provisions (IHTM46050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm46050)) apply. These terms are set out in the relevant parts of the Manual.
