When the required conditions are satisfied, instalments are available

- for the tax payable on the value transferred by the chargeable transfer

- which is attributable to the value of the qualifying property (IHTA84/S227 (1)).

How much of the tax qualifies for instalments? The attribution process can be illustrated by examples in respect of lifetime transfers (IHTM30312 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30312)) and transfers on death (IHTM30313 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30313)).
