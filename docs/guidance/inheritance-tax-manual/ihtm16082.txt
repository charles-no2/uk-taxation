Settled property exemptions: exemptions on lifetime  transfers
Identifier: IHTM16082
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16082
Published: 2016-04-09T09:24:45+01:00
Updated: 2023-03-02T11:23:30+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:53.822876+00:00

Where the interest in possession comes to an end in lifetime, and the circumstances are such that a charge arises under IHTA84/S52 (1) (IHTM04084 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04084)), the exemptions on death (IHTM16081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16081)) can apply, plus

- annual exemptions (IHTM14141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14141))

- marriage or civil partnership gifts (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) (IHTM14191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14191))
