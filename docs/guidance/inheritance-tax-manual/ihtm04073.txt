Lifetime transfers: what is the value  transferred by a gift with reservation of benefit?
Identifier: IHTM04073
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04073
Published: 2016-03-20T09:16:15+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-17T16:39:50+01:00

The value transferred (IHTM04028 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04028)) by a gift with reservation (IHTM04071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04071)) will depend on how the gift is being taxed. If it is being taxed as part of the deceased’s estate on death, the property transferred will be valued as part of the deceased’s estate. (IHTM04044 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04044))

If it is being taxed as a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)) the property transferred will be valued on the basis of the loss to the transferor’s estate. (IHTM04054 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04054))
