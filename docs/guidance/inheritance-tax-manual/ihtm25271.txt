Business relief: Investment businesses: Property letting
Identifier: IHTM25271
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25271
Published: 2016-04-09T09:45:25+01:00
Updated: 2016-10-21T08:34:57+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:30.247171+00:00

You should investigate whether the rule excluding investment businesses (IHTM25261 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25261)) from business relief applies if the business involves a caravan site, letting of furnished accommodation, a furnished holiday letting, letting commercial premises or other letting activities. There may be a wide range of activities within any of these headings.

The two tests that need to be applied in these cases are:

- whether the activities carried on constitute a business for the purposes of IHTA84/S105 (1)(a);

- if they do, is relief precluded because that business was one of ‘wholly or mainly holding investments’, IHTA84/S105 (3).
