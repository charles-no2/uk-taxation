Succession: Intestacy: Distributions under intestacy (Scotland): Ultimus haeres
Identifier: IHTM12154

Published: 2016-03-20T09:36:49+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:00:32+00:00

If there are no surviving relatives (IHTM12153 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12153)) the Crown as ultimus haeres takes the whole. The Inheritance Tax treatment is the same as for property that passes bona vacantia (IHTM12126 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12126)).
