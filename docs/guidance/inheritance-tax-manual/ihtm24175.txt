Lifetime transfers: Where shares or securities are the ‘original property’
Identifier: IHTM24175
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24175
Published: 2016-04-09T09:42:14+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:09:44+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:01:49.510080+00:00

Where the property transferred is shares or securities in a company, the value transferred is treated as attributable to the agricultural value (IHTM24150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24150)) of agricultural property (IHTM24030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24030)) if

- the agricultural property forms part of the company assets and part of the value of the shares or securities can be attributed to the agricultural value of agricultural land, IHTA84/S122 (1)(a) and

- the shares or securities gave the transferor control of the company (IHTM24124 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24124)) immediately before the transfer, IHTA84/S122 (1)(b).

Where the original property consists of shares that fall within the provisions of IHTA84/S122 (1), the first two of the additional conditions (IHTM24173 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24173)) continue to apply, but the third and fourth conditions are replaced by the requirement that the agricultural property within IHTA84/S116 and IHTA84/S122 (1) was owned by the company and occupied (by the company or another) for agricultural purposes.
