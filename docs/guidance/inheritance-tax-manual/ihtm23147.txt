What it means

The VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) has the taxpayer’s agreement to his o/v (IHTM23141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23141)) in writing.

Action

Deal with the case in the normal way.

With increases in value consider the need to raise an assessment immediately. With decreases that result in a repayment of tax you should make the repayment subject to the instructions relating to repayments of tax (IHTM31601 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31601)).
