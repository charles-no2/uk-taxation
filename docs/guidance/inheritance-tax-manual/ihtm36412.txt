We recognise that a limited number of our penalties are subject to Article 6 (IHTM36402 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36402)) of the European Convention on Human Rights (EHCR). The Public Funding leaflet must be issued to all taxpayers who elect for a Hearing before the First-tier Tribunal in England and Wales in the following circumstances

- in all enquiry cases where it appears likely that a IHTA84/S247 or IHTA84/S248 penalty will need to be considered and the leaflet IHT 13 (IHTM36156 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36156)) is issued, or

- if the taxpayer or agent requests it (IHTM36414 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36414)).

The leaflet must be issued even if the taxpayer is represented.
