Negotiating a settlement: examples of negotiating situations

These are some of the situations where a negotiated settlement may be appropriate

- valuing an asset which is not dealt with by a specialist valuer

- the allowable deduction in respect of a debt that is claimed to be due to the relative of the deceased (IHTM28321 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28321))

- what constitutes reasonable funeral expenses (IHTM10371 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10371))

- the valuation of any lifetime transfer resulting from a guarantee debt (IHTM28356 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28356))

- the amount allowable in respect of a guarantee debt where full reimbursement is not expected (IHTM28355 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28355))

- the deduction allowed for foreign administration expenses (IHTM27050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm27050)) under IHTA84/S173 where the 5% limit does not apply

- what the deceased provided in respect of a joint account (IHTM15000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15000))

- the extent to which withdrawals from a joint account (IHTM15000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15000)) give rise to lifetime transfers

- the availability of business relief on bank or building society accounts (IHTM25341 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25341))

- the discount for occupation of the deceased’s property (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002))

- the extent to which a transfer was intended to confer gratuitous benefit

- the extent to which a normal expenditure out of income (IHTM14231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14231)) claim is allowable

- the application of equitable estoppel

- the value of a future (IHTM28110 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28110)) or contingent liability (IHTM28070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28070)) not dealt with by the Actuarial Team

- any case where the facts are uncertain and are impossible to establish.

This list is not exhaustive.
