Value left out of account: overseas pensions
Identifier: IHTM04390
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04390
Published: 2016-03-20T09:19:41+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-23T15:39:59+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:53:11.890731+00:00

Under IHTA84/S153 certain overseas pensions are left out of account in determining the value of a person’s (IHTM04052 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04052)) death estate (IHTM04029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029)) immediately before their death. If you receive a claim for this exemption you should proceed in accordance with the instruction on lump sums in respect of service under the Crown at IHTM17058 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm17058).
