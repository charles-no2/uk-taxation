Government securities in foreign ownership:  domicile
Identifier: IHTM04296
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04296
Published: 2016-03-20T09:18:40+00:00
Updated: 2018-05-02T15:22:21+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:53:05.183307+00:00

For deaths and other chargeable events before 6 April 1998, the beneficial owner (IHTM04031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04031)) not only had to be resident or ordinarily resident (IHTM04295 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04295)) outside the UK, they also had to be domiciled (IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000)) outside the UK. For the purposes of the exclusion, domicile was determined without the aid of the deemed domicile provisions of IHTA84/S267 (IHTM13024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13024)). If FOTRA gilts (IHTM04291 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04291)) are transferred by their beneficial or absolute owner (IHTM04031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04031)), the relevant domicile is that of the owner or transferor.

If immediately before the transfer, the securities are settled property in which a qualifying interest in possession (IHTM16062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16062)) subsists you will need to consider the domicile of the person (IHTM04052 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04052)) entitled to that interest, although the position is different where the person is acompany (IHTM04297 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04297)).
