Liability on lifetime transfers chargeable when made:  introduction
Identifier: IHTM30061

Updated: 2018-03-07T10:36:15+00:00

Where the chargeable transfer is made by a disposition (IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023)) of the transferor (and the transfer is neither a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM30041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30041)) nor on the ending of a qualifying interest in possession in settled property (IHTM30091 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30091))), there may be two separate Inheritance Tax charges to consider

- on the making of the chargeable transfer (IHTM30062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30062))

- an additional liability (IHTM30041 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30041)) on the death of the transferor within 7 years.

Though the persons liable for both charges are specified in IHTA84/S199, there are important differences between the persons liable for the two charges, so they are dealt with separately.
