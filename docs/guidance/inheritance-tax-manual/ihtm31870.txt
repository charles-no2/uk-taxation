Assessing: SAGE: unpaying calculations

When calculations are withdrawn, any payments matched to them will be automatically unmatched by Cashiers. However, if a calculation is ‘unpaid’ on COMPASS (IHTM31212 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31212)) and not withdrawn, the entries stay matched on SAGE.

Calculations must only be unpaid when:

- the calculation has genuinely been marked paid on COMPASS in error

- you are told that an invalid or dishonoured cheque has been received

If you want to unpay a calculation for any other reason you should ask for advice from Accounts, Nottingham.
