You must not seek to increase the value of property where any of the following apply

- any of the bars to assessment of additional tax set out (IHTM30401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30401))

- an agreement on value (IHTM30442 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30442)), or

-

a formal notice of determination (IHTM37001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm37001)) of the value of land.We may however be able to reopen the valuation of property if, since our acceptance or agreement of the valuation, we have received information indicating

- that the property to be valued needs to be redefined (IHTM30432 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30432)) or

- that the valuation was based on wrong or misleading information.Refer any such case to your manager.
