You should use the ‘Adjustment’ box to make adjustments to interest (IHTM31511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31511)) only.

You must never amend the tax charge by this method as it will result in unmatched money on the SAGE (IHTM31851 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31851)) record.

There is a cash limit on the adjustment box; any case where that limit is exceeded will need to be referred to your manager to be authorised before the calculation can be issued.
