Land and buildings, machinery and plant: Rate of  relief
Identifier: IHTM25221
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25221
Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:55+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:11:27+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:22.743500+00:00

The rate of relief for IHTA84/S105 (1)(d) property is 50% for transfers on and after 10March 1992. For transfers before that date the rate of relief was 30%.
