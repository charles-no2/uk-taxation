Lifetime transfers: schemes to exploit annual exemption: transfer of share quantified by loss in value
Identifier: IHTM14164
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14164
Published: 2016-04-09T09:17:03+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:06+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:57:23.460504+00:00

The deed transfers as much of a share of a property so that the retained share is equal in value to the value of the whole minus, say, £3,000.

This effects a transfer equal in value to £3,000. The calculation of the retained share is an asset of the estate and you will need to refer its valuation to the Valuation Office (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)).

If you come across this point refer the matter to Technical.
