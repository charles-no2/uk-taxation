Accountability: time limits
Identifier: IHTM10803

The normal time limit for delivering an account is

- twelve months from the end of the month in which the transfer is made, or

- if later, three months from the date when the accountable person first becomes liable for the tax.

The rule is modified for

- transfers that arise on death (IHTM10811 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10811))

- potentially exempt transfers (PETs) (IHTM10821 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10821))

- gifts with reservations (GWRs) (IHTM10824 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10824))

- relevant property trust charges that arise on or after 6 April 2014 (IHTM10836 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10836))

Penalties (IHTM36021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36021)) may be chargeable if an account is not delivered within the statutory time limit. There are also penalties for failing to deliver an account (IHTM36081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36081)).
