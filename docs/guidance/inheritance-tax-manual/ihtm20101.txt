Life Policies: types of policy: whole life policy
Identifier: IHTM20101
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20101
Published: 2016-04-09T09:32:09+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:09+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:59:57.821210+00:00

A whole life policy is one in which benefits under the policy are paid out on the death of the life assured (IHTM20081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20081)) at any time. If the deceased is also the policyholder, this information should be given on page 1 of form IHT410 (IHTM20022 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20022)).
