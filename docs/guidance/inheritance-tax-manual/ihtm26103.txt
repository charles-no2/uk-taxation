Step 3 - interaction: summary of the interaction provisions
Identifier: IHTM26103
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26103

Updated: 2016-02-11T17:11:58+00:00

In outline, the interaction provisions of IHTA84/S39A state the value of any specific gifts (IHTM26011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26011)) of relievable property is their value after relief (IHTA84/S39A (2)), while the value of any other specific gifts is the ‘appropriate fraction’ (IHTM26108 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26108)) of their value (IHTA84/S39A (3)).

Under IHTA84/S39A (4) the appropriate fraction is:

- The value transferred after business or agricultural relief less any specific gifts of relievable property at their reduced value

divided by

- the unreduced value transferred less any specific gifts of relievable property at their value before reduction

Where grossing up (IHTM26003 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26003)) is necessary, IHTA84/S39A(5) provides that the values to be grossed up are the values as reduced under IHTA84/S39A (2) to (4).

A specific gift of relievable property does not include the value of any other gift payable out of that property and that other gift is not treated as a gift of relievable property (IHTA84/S39A (6)).
