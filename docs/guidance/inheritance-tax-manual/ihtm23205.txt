Special valuation matters: purchaser's interest under a  binding contract to purchase
Identifier: IHTM23205
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23205
Published: 2016-04-09T09:38:39+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:09:08+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:01:09.243901+00:00

Where a purchaser of a property dies between

- entering into an enforceable contract to purchase, and

- the completion of the conveyance

the interest of his estate is in the property itself not the cash used to purchase it.

You should therefore refer the property to the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) in the normal way.

In Scotland if the purchaser has not obtained a registered title to the property, the matter should be referred to TG for advice before any reference to the VOA is made if the point is likely to be material.
