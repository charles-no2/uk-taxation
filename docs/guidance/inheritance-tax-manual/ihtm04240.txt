Changes in the distribution of the deceased's estate

Under IHTA84/S17 none of the following is a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024))

- a variation or disclaimer (IHTM35001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35001)) to which IHTA84/S142 (1) applies

- a transfer made in pursuance of the deceased’s wishes (IHTM35171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35171)), IHTA84/S143

- an election by a surviving spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) (IHTM35190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35190)) under S47A of the Administration of Estates Act 1925 (from 1 October 2014 the surviving spouse or civil partner receives an absolute interest and so there is no longer a need to make an election under S47A),

- in Scotland, the renunciation of a claim to legitim (IHTM35213 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35213)) before the persons making it attained 18 years, or within two years of attaining 18 or such longer period as the Board may permit, IHTA84/S147 (6).
