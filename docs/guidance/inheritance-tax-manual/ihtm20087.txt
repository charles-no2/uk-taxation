Life Policies: definitions: 'back-to-back policies'

These are linked life and annuity policies. They are described as linked because in certain circumstances the insurance company would not issue the life policy without the annuity policy also being taken out. There is further information at IHTM20371 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20371).
