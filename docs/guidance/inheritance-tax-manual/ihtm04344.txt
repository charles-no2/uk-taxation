Estate Duty surviving spouse exemption: application  to lifetime events
Identifier: IHTM04344
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04344
Published: 2016-03-20T09:19:13+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-25T12:13:31+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:53:13.384190+00:00

The relief is extended to cover lifetime events, where the interest of the surviving spouse terminates during his or her lifetime. The latter part of IHTA84/SCH6/PARA2 means that tax is not charged under IHTA84/S52 on the coming to an end (IHTM04083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04083)) of an interest in possession (IIP) (IHTM16060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16060)) in settled property if

- the spouse (or former spouse) of the person beneficially entitled to the interest died before 13 November 1974, and

- the value of the property in which the interest subsists would by virtue of the preceding provisions (IHTM04343 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04343)) of IHTA84/SCH6/PARA2 have been left out of account (IHTM04331 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04331)) in determining the value of the survivor’s estate had they died immediately before the interest came to an end.
