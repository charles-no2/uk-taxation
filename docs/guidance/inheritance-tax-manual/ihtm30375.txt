Special rules: settled property - sales and mortgages of  reversionary interests

There is a further relief, which is included only for completeness as you are unlikely to see it in practice. It relates to the tax charge:

- on the cesser of an interest in possession (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061))

- where a reversionary interest in the settled property (IHTM16231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16231)) was sold or mortgaged before 27 March 1974.

There is a tax relief (IHTM16232 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16232)) in these circumstances.

The relief is applied in the same way as that afforded by IHTA84/S40(2)(d) FA 1969 for Estate Duty. Any sum repayable under this practice would not carry interest.
