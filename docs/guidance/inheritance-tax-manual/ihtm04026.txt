There are a number of exemptions that apply to Inheritance Tax. These are detailed in sections 11 (IHTM11000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11000)) and 14 (IHTM14000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14000)). A transfer may be partially exempt where only part of the transfer is eligible for relief or exemption. Though all the exemptions apply to actual transfers of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) the legislation specifically prevents some of the exemptions applying to deemed transfers of value. (IHTM04025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04025))
