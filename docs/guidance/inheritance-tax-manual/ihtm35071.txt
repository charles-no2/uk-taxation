Meaning of estate for s.142: introduction
Identifier: IHTM35071

The normal meaning of an individual’s estate (IHTM04029 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04029)) is modified for the purposes of IHTA84/S142 (1) in the following way so that it is extended to include any excluded property (IHTM04141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04141)) which would otherwise be taken out of the Inheritance Tax estate by IHTA84/S5 (1). Examples are

- a non-purchased reversionary interest (IHTM04281 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04281))

- certain British Government securities (IHTM04291 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04291)) if the deceased was not ordinarily resident in the UK.

It is restricted by the excluding from the meaning of estate property to which the deceased was entitled under

- IHTA84/S49 (1), settled property, (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)) and

- FA86/S102, a gift with reservation (IHTM04072 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04072)) which is still subject to a reservation at the date of death.

Subject to these modifications, the normal meaning of estate applies. Property that may be redirected by a variation includes

- joint property (IHTM15081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15081)) passing by survivorship,

- joint property passing under a special destination (IHTM15050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15050)) in Scotland, and

- property devolving under legal rights (IHTM12221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12221)) in Scotland.
