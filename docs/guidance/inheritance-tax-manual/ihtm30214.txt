From 6 April 2026, if property qualifies for (BR) (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)) it will also qualify for instalments and these will be interest free (IHTM30363 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30363)) see IHTA/S227(2)(aa) and IHTA/S234(1)(a)).

A business or an interest in a business is also qualifying property for instalments - IHTA84/S227 (2)(c) even if it does not qualify for BR.

The basic definitions are similar to those for BR:

-

the value is the net assets value (compare IHTA84/S110)

-

business includes profession or vocation but does not include a business carried on otherwise than for gain (compare IHTA84/S103 (3)).

Accordingly the instructions on the meaning of business (IHTM25051 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25051)) elsewhere in the Manual apply.

Interest relief

From 6 April 2026, any property which qualifies for BR or Agricultural Relief (AR) is also qualifying property for interest free (IHTM30363 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30363)) instalments.

Before that date, property which qualified for BR under IHTA84/S105 (1)(a) was also qualifying property for interest free (IHTM30363 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30363)) instalments.

(If the BR is at 100%, this is academic. 100% relief might be available because the death was between 10 March 1992 and 6 April 2026 or, after that date, if the value is entirely covered by the 100% relief allowance see IHTM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25500).)

In addition, whenever the death, interest free instalments are available under IHTA84/S227 (2)(c) and IHTA84/S234 (1)(a) for

-

any excepted assets (IHTM25351 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25351)) disallowed for BR under IHTA84/S112

-

businesses excluded from BR because the ownership condition (IHTM25301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25301)) is not satisfied or they are investment businesses (IHTM25261 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25261))

-

agricultural property (IHTM24030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24030)) which is an asset of a qualifying business but is excluded from BR by IHTA84/S114 (1).

These instructions do not apply to holdings of quoted or unquoted shares (IHTM30215 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30215)) in limited companies.
