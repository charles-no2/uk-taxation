Domicile: Finance (No 2) Act 2017 changes: non UK domicile

From 6 April 2025, for inheritance tax purposes, domicile is replaced by long-term UK residence and you can find details of these rules at IHTM (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000)47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000).

Individuals with a non-UK domicile of origin are not affected by the measure at IHTM13062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13062). They are however subject (as now) to the deemed domicile rules at IHTM13061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13061) and the UK residential property rules at IHTM04311 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04311).
