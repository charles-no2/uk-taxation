Where an increase in value is offered follow the instructions at (IHTM30432 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30432)).

In small cases, where a reference has not previously been made to the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) and the taxpayers offer tax on the sale price of real or leasehold property included in the IHT400 at a lower figure, the sale price can be accepted. This is subject to confirmation that the sale was for full consideration and not made to a connected person.
