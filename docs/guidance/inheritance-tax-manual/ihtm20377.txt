Life Policies: life policy linked with an annuity: deferred annuity contract

This is a variant of the typical back-to-back scenario, where a deferred annuity contract is substituted for the life policy. The scope of IHTA84/S263 is extended by S263(3) to deal with this.

You should follow the instructions at IHTM20372 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20372).
