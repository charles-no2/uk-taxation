You calculate the chargeable value on the usual basis of loss to the estate of the transferor. (IHTM04054 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04054))

You will need to gross up the value if the transferor pays the tax. (IHTM14541 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14541))

You can allow business (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)) and agricultural (IHTM24170 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24170)) relief if the conditions for relief are satisfied at the time of the transfer.

Gifts with Reservation (GWRs)

You consider double charges relief if the transfer is a GWR (IHTM14711 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14711)).
