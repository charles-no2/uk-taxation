Sales of related property, etc: vendors must not  acquire rights to sold property
Identifier: IHTM09759
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09759
Published: 2016-03-20T09:25:55+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:24+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:42.848277+00:00

The sale will not qualify for relief if either the vendors or any person having an interest in the proceeds of sale (IHTM09758 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09758)) obtains ‘in connection with the sale’ a right to acquire the property (IHTM04030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04030)) sold or any interest in or created out of it, IHTA84/S176 (3)(d). You should treat the words ‘in connection with the sale’ as covering collateral arrangements as well as provisions included in the sale agreement itself.
