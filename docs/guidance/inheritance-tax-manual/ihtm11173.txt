Exclusions from exemption: defeasible gifts
Identifier: IHTM11173
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11173
Published: 2016-03-20T09:33:53+00:00
Updated: 2026-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:56:14.542691+00:00

Under IHTA84/S23 (2)(c) a gift to a charity (IHTM11112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11112)) is not exempt if it is defeasible. Broadly, a defeasible gift is one which can be defeated by a future event, such as a gift that could be revoked.

Any gift that:

- has not been defeated within the twelve months after the transfer, and

- cannot be defeated at any time after this point in time(even if it could have been defeated before the twelve months had passed)

should be treated as not defeasible.
