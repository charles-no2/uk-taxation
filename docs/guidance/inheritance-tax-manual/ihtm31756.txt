Assessing: deposits: money attributed to the wrong file
Identifier: IHTM31756
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31756
Published: 2016-04-09T10:06:44+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:19:35+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:31.045853+00:00

You must check all payments received at your file to make sure they have been allocated to the right file. If you think that a payment does not belong at your file refer this to Accounts Nottingham.

Do not use a Sect 6 (IHTM31641 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31641)) to transfer the money.
