Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime reliefs: double charges relief: applying the relief

When a double charge arises:

- retain the charge on the transfer which produces the higher overall amount of Inheritance Tax, and

- reduce the other transfer to nil.

There is an exception in certain discretionary trust cases (IHTM14732 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14732)).

Where you:

- retain the later transfer within the Inheritance Tax charge, and

- tax has been paid on the earlier transfer,

you may allow credit for some or all of that tax.
