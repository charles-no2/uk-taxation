IHT implications of an Instrument of Variation: effect of not coming within s.142
Identifier: IHTM35154
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35154
Published: 2016-04-09T10:14:29+01:00
Updated: 2017-09-13T13:26:40+01:00

A variation or disclaimer may not come within IHTA84/S142(1), because, for example

- there is extraneous consideration (IHTM35100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35100))

- there is insufficient link with the original disposition (IHTM35025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35025)), or

- there is no valid statement of intent (IHTM35058 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35058)) (for variations effected on or after 1 August 2002) or

- there is no valid election (IHTM35051 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35051)) (for variations executed before 1 August 2002)

Or a variation or disclaimer may be only partly within IHTA84/S142(1), for example

- where it also deals with property which is not part of the death estate (IHTM35071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35071)) for the purposes of IHTA84/S142(1) or

- where it purports to reduce the benefits of persons who are not parties to it (IHTM35045 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35045)).

If a variation does not come within IHTA84/S142(1), or to the extent to which it does not come within IHTA84/S142(1), it operates for IHT purposes in the same way as any other transaction affecting an individual’s own property. It may be a transfer of value and will not be written back into the will or other disposition for the purpose of tax on the death. As a transfer of value it will be either a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)) or a transfer chargeable when made (IHTM04067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04067)) and will cumulate should the beneficiary making the instrument fail to survive for 7 years after the date of the instrument.

The above also applies to any disclaimer which is not within IHTA84/S142(1), except that in the alternative a disclaimer of an interest in settled property may come within IHTA84/S93.
