You should refer any cases to Technical

- which do not satisfy the requirements (IHTM11124 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11124)), or

- about which you are doubtful

You should also refer

- gifts to charities by an instrument of variation (IHTM35011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35011)) or an appointment by executors/ trustees where property is held on trusts (IHTM11161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11161)) for charitable purposes but which include objects outside the UK.

- any case in which a charity beneficiary settles a claim (IHTM11026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11026)) against the deceased’s estate.
