Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime reliefs: disallowed debts: when double charges relief arises
Identifier: IHTM14721
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14721
Published: 2016-04-09T09:21:37+01:00
Updated: 2017-04-11T11:36:50+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:17.505500+00:00

Relief under the Inheritance Tax (Double Charges Relief) Regulations 1987 (SI 1987/1130) Reg.6 will apply where the deceased made a failed potentially exempt transfer (IHTM14511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14511)) or immediately chargeable lifetime transfer (IHTM04067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04067)) which:

- increased the estate of the transferee, or

- resulted in property becoming comprised in a settlement,

and before their death the deceased incurred a liability to:

- the transferee, or

- the trustees of the settlement,

- which is a liability reduced or disallowed under FA86/S103. (IHTM28361 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28361))
