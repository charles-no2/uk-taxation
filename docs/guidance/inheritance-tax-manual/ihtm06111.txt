Excepted transfers and terminations - death of the transferor
Identifier: IHTM06111
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06111
Published: 2016-03-20T09:23:35+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:06+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:11.979990+00:00

Excepted transfers and terminations should be taken into account when considering whether an estate falls within the excepted estates rules (IHTM06011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06011)). If the estate is not an excepted estate, details of the lifetime transfers and terminations should be included in the Inheritance Tax account as normal
