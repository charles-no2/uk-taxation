Transfers on death: the charging provisions
Identifier: IHTM04041

Published: 2016-03-20T09:15:31+00:00

The basic provision governing the tax charge on death is IHTA84/S4 (1). The charge may be divided into two parts. The subsection provides that on the death of any person (the deceased) tax is charged as if the deceased

- had made a transfer of value (IHTM04042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04042)) immediately before death, and

- the value transferred had been equal to the value of the deceased’s estate (IHTM04043 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04043)) immediately before his death.
