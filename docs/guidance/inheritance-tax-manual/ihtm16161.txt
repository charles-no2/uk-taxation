Foreign Property in a trust: introduction

The rules for determining whether foreign property is excluded property are revised from 6 April 2025. The general rule is that foreign property comprised within a settlement will be excluded property if the settlor was not a long-term UK resident (IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000)) at the time of the chargeable event (on or after 6 April 2025).

If the chargeable event was before 6 April 2025, any foreign property which is comprised in a settlement is excluded property if the settlor was not domiciled in the UK at the time the settlement was made (or in relation to property that was added later, at the time the addition was made).

See IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000) for domicile guidance and IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000) for long-term UK residence guidance.
