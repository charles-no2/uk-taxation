Rules about excepted estates: low value estates
Identifier: IHTM06012
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06012
Published: 2016-03-20T09:22:22+00:00
Updated: 2025-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:53:55.905054+00:00

The conditions for these estates are that:

-

the deceased died on or after 6 April 2004, and

-

for deaths on or after 6 April 2025, was a long-term UK resident immediately before their death (IHTM47 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000)000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000))

-

for deaths before 5 April 2025, is domiciled (IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000)) in the United Kingdom, and

-

the gross value of the estate, including the deceased’s share of any jointly owned assets, any specified transfers (IHTM06018 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06018)) and any specified exempt transfers (IHTM06019 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06019))

does not exceed the Inheritance Tax nil rate band (IHTM06011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06011)), and for deaths on or after 1 January 2022

-

if the estate includes any assets held in trust, they are held in a single trust and the gross value does not exceed £250,000 (IHTM06020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06020)),

-

if the estate includes foreign assets, their gross value does not exceed £100,000,

-

if there are any specified transfers, their chargeable value does not exceed £250,000,

-

the deceased had not made a gift with reservation of benefit.

For deaths before 1 January 2022 the maximum value for assets held in trust (IHTM06020 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06020)), and the limit on the chargeable value of any specified transfers (IHTM06018 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06018)), is lower.
