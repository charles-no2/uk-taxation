Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime reliefs: potentially exempt transfers (PETs): partial consideration
Identifier: IHTM14702
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14702
Published: 2016-04-09T09:21:22+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:47+00:00

Do not exclude or limit relief because the deceased had reacquired property from the transferee for partial consideration.

Refer any case to Technical where the deceased had made a substantial payment, but it falls short of full consideration.
