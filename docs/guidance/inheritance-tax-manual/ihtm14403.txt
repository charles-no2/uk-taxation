Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): tracing settled property: settlement by the donee
Identifier: IHTM14403
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14403
Published: 2016-04-09T09:18:51+01:00
Updated: 2020-09-29T11:37:49+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:57:45.933169+00:00

Where property is not settled by the gift but is subsequently settled by the donee:

- For the period between the gift and the settlement by the donee, the normal tracing rules apply (IHTM14371 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14371)).

- But, from the date of the settlement by the donee, the settled property tracing rules apply (IHTM14401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14401)). For this purpose, treat property which is settled by the donee’s will or intestacy as settled by the donee.
