Corrective account (C4) England, Wales and Northern Ireland: who should complete and sign the account?
Identifier: IHTM10703
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10703
Published: 2016-03-20T09:31:03+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:59:37+00:00

All accountable persons (IHTM10805 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10805)) should sign the C4.

An agent acting on behalf of the accountable persons must not sign the form.
