Payment by instalments: election
Identifier: IHTM30202
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30202
Published: 2016-04-09T09:58:20+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-13T10:03:40+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:04.938120+00:00

Tax is not payable by instalments unless the taxpayer makes a written election to do so. The HMRC accounts (IHTM10011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10011)) include sections for an election to be made.
