Is an account required?: excepted estates 
Identifier: IHTM05021
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05021
Published: 2016-03-20T09:20:31+00:00
Updated: 2021-12-16T11:51:25+00:00

Over a quarter of a million grants are obtained (IHTM05001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05001)) each year in England and Wales alone. A large number of these are for simple estates where the value of the total assets is much lower than the nil-rate band for Inheritance Tax (IHT). Since 1981, under a power given by IHTA84/S256, the Board of HMRC has made regulations which have enabled a large number of cases to be excepted estates (IHTM06001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06001)).

In these cases, the liable persons do not need to deliver an account of the estate provided certain conditions are met. Instead, for deaths on or after 1 January 2022, they declare that the estate is an excepted estate, confirm if they are claiming transferable nil rate band (IHTM43064 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm43064)), and provide three values for IHT on the Probate or Confirmation forms (IHTM06031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06031)). For deaths before 1 January 2022, they complete a brief return of information about the estate. Details of the initial steps on how they do this can be found at IHTM05024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05024).

The current regulations set out at IHTM06012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06012) and IHTM06013 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06013) apply to deaths on or after 1 September 2006. They came into force under the IHT (Delivery of Accounts) (Excepted Estates) Regulations 2004 (SI2004/2543) as amended. There are some differences in the procedures for applying for an excepted estate grant in Scotland (IHTM05027 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05027)) and the rest of the UK (IHTM05026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05026)).
