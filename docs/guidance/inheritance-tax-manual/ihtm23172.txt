Special valuation matters: agricultural relief claimed on the land
Identifier: IHTM23172
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23172
Published: 2016-04-09T09:37:38+01:00
Updated: 2026-04-07T01:00:00+01:00

Where agricultural relief (IHTM24001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24001)) has been claimed you should determine whether the property qualifies for relief before you ask the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) to consider the taxpayer’s valuation of the property (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)).  This includes whether business relief may be available as an alternative (IHTM24151 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24151)).

Follow the relevant instructions where agricultural property owned by the deceased is occupied by a partnership (IHTM23171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23171)).

You will need to ask the VOA to provide both the open market value and agricultural value (IHTM24150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm24150)) for any property on which agricultural relief is due.

From 6 April 2026, there is a £2.5 million 100% relief allowance on the combined value of agricultural and business property (apportioned between all items of such property) and any value over that amount gets relief at 50% (see IHTM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25500)). You should therefore consider the extent of potential chargeable values before proceeding to ask for a formally agreed value.
