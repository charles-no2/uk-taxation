Loss on sale of land: changes in the interest or underlying land: leases
Identifier: IHTM33131

You must make an addition to the sale price (IHTM33072 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33072)) where the interest is a leasehold with not more than fifty years to run at the date of death, IHTA84/S194 (1).

The amount of addition is the ‘appropriate fraction’ of the value on death (IHTM33100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33100)) of the interest in land. The appropriate fraction is:

(P1 - P2) ÷ P1

Where:

- P1 is the percentage derived from the table (IHTM14670 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14670)) in TCGA92/SCH8/para1 for the duration of the lease at the date of death, and

- P2 is the corresponding percentage derived from the table in TCGA92/SCH8/para1 for the duration of the lease at the date of sale (IHTM33071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33071)).
