Clearance certificates: what is not covered by a clearance certificate?
Identifier: IHTM40014
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40014
Published: 2016-04-09T10:22:29+01:00
Updated: 2017-01-19T12:06:41+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:15.116783+00:00

The clearance certificate means that the taxpayer will not have to pay any further tax on the transfer described in the certificate, unless IHTA84/S239 (4) applies, where

- there has been fraud or failure to disclose material facts (IHTM40143 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40143))

- additional assets (IHTM40142 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40142)) are later shown to have been included in the transfer

- too great an increase in the nil rate band has been claimed on the death of the surviving spouse (IHTM43067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm43067))

- further tax becomes payable as a result of statutory amendments after issue (IHTM40147 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40147))
