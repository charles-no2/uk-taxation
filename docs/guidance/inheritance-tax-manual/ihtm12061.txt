Usually, a Will is made by one person and deals with the disposal of their individual property on death.

But, Wills can also be made jointly (IHTM12062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12062)) and/or mutually (IHTM12063 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12063)) although this is now fairly uncommon.

Whatever the type of Will, property held jointly (IHTM15001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15001)) and passing by survivorship (IHTM15081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15081)) cannot be subject to any Will but passes automatically to the surviving owner(s).
