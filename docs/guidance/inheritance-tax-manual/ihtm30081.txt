Liability on gifts with reservation (GWRs):  introduction

Updated: 2020-01-20T09:40:44+00:00

The instructions in this section assume that the tax on the gift is chargeable under the gifts with reservation (GWR) (IHTM04071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04071)) provisions in FA 1986/S102. You need to remember, however, that a gift taxable as a GWR may also be taxable as a chargeable transfer either when made or, in the case of a potentially exempt transfer (PET) (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)), as a result of the donor’s death. Where this is the position, the relief for double charges (IHTM14711 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14711)) needs to be considered.

The liability depends on whether the gift is treated as a PET (IHTM30082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30082)) or as part of the death estate (IHTM30083 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30083)).
