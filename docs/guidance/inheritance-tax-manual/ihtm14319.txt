Lifetime transfers: gifts with reservation (GWRs): the gift: exempt transfers which can be GWRs

The main exemptions which do not prevent application of the gift with reservation (GWR) rules are

- IHTA84/S3A (4) - PET made seven years or more before the death of the transferor (IHTM14511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14511))

- IHTA84/S19 - Annual exemption (IHTM14141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14141))

- IHTA84/S21 - Normal out-of-income exemption (IHTM14231 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14231))

A gift which is an exempt transfer under the above exemptions may be chargeable under the GWR rules, as may a gift which is an exempt transfer under any other section not listed at IHTM14318 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14318).

Small gifts exemption

Strictly speaking, the small gifts exemption (IHTM14180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14180)) is only available in respect of outright gifts.
