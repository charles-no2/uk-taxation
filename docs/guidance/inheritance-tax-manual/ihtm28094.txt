Liabilities: investigating liabilities: interim maintenance orders ceasing on the deceased's death
Identifier: IHTM28094
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28094
Published: 2016-04-09T09:53:15+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:12:48+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:07.714408+00:00

You should allow any deduction for any sums which were owed to the former spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) before the death, under the order, but which remained unpaid at the deceased’s death.
