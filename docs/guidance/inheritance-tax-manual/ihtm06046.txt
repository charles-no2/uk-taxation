Compliance investigators may see excepted estates for a number of reasons,

- where the information available to us suggests an excepted estate grant has been incorrectly obtained, or

- where an IHT100 (IHTM10650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10650)) has been received and it is clear that an excepted estate grant has been incorrectly obtained.
