Monitoring the excepted estates rules: automatic discharge
Identifier: IHTM06043
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06043
Published: 2016-03-20T09:22:57+00:00
Updated: 2021-12-08T15:32:15+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:54:04.163796+00:00

All persons personally liable for any Inheritance Tax due in respect of an excepted estate are automatically discharged from liability at the end of the prescribed period (IHTM06042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06042)) and any charge for tax on any assets comprised in the estate is removed.

Automatic discharge will not apply

- if the taxpayers or agents have given us false information or not told us all the material facts

- if it later comes to light that there is further property which causes the excepted estates conditions to be breached

- if within the prescribed period a notice in writing is issued to the personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) asking them for further information.

This discharge also applies to trustees (IHTM06044 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm06044)) and has the same affect as formal clearance.
