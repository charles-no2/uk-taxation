Legal rights have a number of implications for Inheritance Tax. You may need to consider:

- the effect on the deceased’s estate (IHTM12222 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12222))

- the effect where legal rights are discharged (IHTM12223 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12223))

- the value of legal rights as an asset in the estate of someone who has died (IHTM10302 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10302))

- the value of legal rights as a debt in the estate of someone who has died (IHTM10303 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10303))

- the treatment of legal rights for grossing up (IHTM26001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26001))
