Contracts for sale: Introduction
Identifier: IHTM25291
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25291
Published: 2016-04-09T09:45:43+01:00
Updated: 2017-09-04T15:40:00+01:00

Property subject to a binding contract for sale is not relevant business property (IHTM25141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25141)) except where it is

- a business or an interest in a business and the sale is to a company which is to carry on the business and is made wholly or mainly in consideration of shares in or securities of that company, or

- shares in or securities of a company, and the sale is made for the purposes of reconstruction or amalgamation, IHTA84/S113.

Where a lifetime transfer other than a potentially exempt transfer (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)) is made shortly before a sale, you should investigate the circumstances (IHTM14832 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14832)) to ensure the relief is properly due.
