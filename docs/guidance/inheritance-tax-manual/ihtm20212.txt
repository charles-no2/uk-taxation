Life Policies: policy on the deceased’s life not connected with any other transaction (except a trust): policy unconnected with a gift or trust: possible discount
Identifier: IHTM20212
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20212
Published: 2016-04-09T09:32:53+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:15+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:06.460223+00:00

Exceptionally, some policies provide that the claim value (IHTM20084 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20084)) will not be payable until

- a certain period has passed since the death, or

- certain formalities other than those referred to at IHTM20211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20211) have been complied with.

Any claim for a discount in these circumstances should be referred to the Actuarial Team.
