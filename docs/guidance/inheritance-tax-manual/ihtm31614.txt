Assessing: repayments: adjusting for additional interest charges
Identifier: IHTM31614

Published: 2016-04-09T10:05:45+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:19:22+00:00

Remember to account for any additional interest (IHTM31540 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31540)) charged in a separate calculation to the original tax charge.

Treat the additional interest as attributable to the amount of tax outstanding at the date of the previous calculation.
