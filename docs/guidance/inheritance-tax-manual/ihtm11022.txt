As a general rule in order for the exemptions to apply

- the beneficiary (such as, the spouse or civil partner (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031)) (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), charity (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101)), political party (IHTM11191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11191))) must have the required status, and

- the exemption must not be excluded by one of the exceptions

More detailed guidance, including any additional requirements or restrictions, is given in the separate instructions on each exemption.
