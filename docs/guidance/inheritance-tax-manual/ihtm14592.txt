You can find full details about who is liable for Inheritance Tax on lifetime transfers separately at IHTM30011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30011) onwards.

In general terms, the transferor is liable for tax on an immediately chargeable transfer (IHTM14531 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14531)), and the transferee is liable for tax on Potentially Exempt Transfers (PETs) (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)) or additional charges (IHTM14571 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14571)).
