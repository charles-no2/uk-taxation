Discounted Gift Schemes are offered by a wide variety of providers, including most of the major insurance companies. The schemes are marketed with a variety of names, such as

- Discounted Gift Plan

- Discounted Gift Trust

- Discounted Trust

- Estate Planning Bond

- Estate Preservation Plan

- Inheritance Tax Plan

- Inheritance Protection Plan

- PELF Bond

- PETA Bond

- Reserved Interest Trust

- Retained Interest Trust

- Secure Estate Plan

- Wealth Preservation Bond

If you come across an investment with one of these names then you are probably dealing with a Discounted Gift Scheme and should follow the instructions at IHTM20422 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20422).
