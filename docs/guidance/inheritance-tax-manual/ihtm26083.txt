Step 1 - the starting value of specific gifts: settled gift

Property may be settled by a specific gift (IHTM26011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26011)), either

- by giving a person a qualifying interest in possession (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)), such as a life interest to a surviving spouse or civil partner (IHTM11031 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11031)) (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)), or

- on relevant property trusts (IHTM42161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42161)).

In these situations the value of the gift for the purposes of the partly exempt transfer rules (IHTM26071 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26071)) is the value of the property settled.
