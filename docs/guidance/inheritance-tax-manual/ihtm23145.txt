What it means

This endorsement is made when the VOA (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)) is satisfied from information in his possession that

- the value returned is reasonably correct; and

- any likely increase does not justify the expense of inspection (IHTM23223 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23223)) and subsequent dialogue with the parties.

Action

You should

- act upon it as if it were an `agreed’ (IHTM23147 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23147)) valuation; and

- tell the parties that the VOA has ‘no question’ (IHTM23074) to raise on the value offered.

Should the property be sold at a higher figure, we may ask the VOA to reconsider (IHTM23162 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23162)) his opinion of value.
