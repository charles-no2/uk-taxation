Exclusions from exemption: conditional gifts
Identifier: IHTM11175
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11175
Published: 2016-03-20T09:33:57+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:00:11+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:56:15.282504+00:00

A gift to a charity (IHTM11112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11112)) is not exempt if it depends on a condition which is not satisfied within twelve months after the transfer.

However, a gift for the saying of Masses has been held to be charitable in Re Hetherington Deceased [1989] 2 All E.R. 129, so such legacies are not considered to be conditional.

When a condition which could be satisfied outside the twelve month period is in fact satisfied within the period, you should follow the instructions (IHTM11093 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11093)) for transfers between spouses or civil partners (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)). (IHTA84/S23 (2) (b) is in the same terms as IHTA84/S18 (3) (b)).
