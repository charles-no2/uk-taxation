Charging provisions: IHTM04100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04100)

Chargeable events are liable to Inheritance Tax (IHT) at the flat rate. (IHTM42802 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42802))

Distributions to the employees or their relatives or dependants are normally exempt.

IHTA84/S87 specifically allows trusts for newspaper publishing companies or newspaper holding companies to receive the same IHT treatment as employee benefit trusts (EBTs).

There is a full separate section of guidance on EBTs at the end of this chapter of the Manual. (IHTM42900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42900))
