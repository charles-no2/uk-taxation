Settled property: the charge when both a close  company and interest in possession are involved
Identifier: IHTM04089
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04089
Published: 2016-03-20T09:16:29+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:54:18+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:52:38.558967+00:00

Where both a close company and an interest in possession (IIP) (IHTM16000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16000)) in settled property are involved the situations are catered for where

- a close company makes a transfer of value and part of the value transferred would be apportioned to a trustee of a settlement, see IHTM04090 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04090)

- an alteration is made in a close company’s share or loan capital (or in any rights attaching to its shares or debentures), see IHTM04091  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04091)

- a close company is entitled to an IIP in settled property, see IHTM04092 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04092)
