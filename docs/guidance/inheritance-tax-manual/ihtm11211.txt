Gifts to registered housing associations: summary
Identifier: IHTM11211

Under IHTA84/S24A (1) a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)) on or after 14 March 1989 became an exempt transfer to the extent that the value transferred is attributable to land in the United Kingdom given to a registered housing association (RHA). The exemption has no value limit.

RHAs have since been replaced in England and Wales by Registered Social Landlords (RSLs) (IHTM11212 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11212)), RHAs continue in Scotland and Northern Ireland.

You should also bear in mind the following points

- charity exemption (IHTM11101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11101)) under IHTA84/S23 is available for gifts to housing associations which are charities

- for the purposes of this exemption ‘land’ includes any buildings that are attached to that land

- the exemption is limited to land in the United Kingdom.

- subject to the limitation stated above the exemption is subject to the same general exclusions (IHTM11171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11171)) and specific restrictions (IHTM11161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11161)) for settled property that apply to charity exemption

- the instructions for quantifying exemption (IHTM11192 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11192)) for gifts to political parties also apply to this exemption
