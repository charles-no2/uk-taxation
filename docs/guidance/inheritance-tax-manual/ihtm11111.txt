General outline: requirements for exemption

For a transfer to a charity to be exempt under IHTA84/S23 (1) there are two requirements:

- the donee has to have the required charitable status (IHTM11112 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11112)), and

- the exemption must not be prevented by any of the exclusions and limitations (IHTM11171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11171)) which are designed to confine the exemption to gifts which are:

- genuinely charitable, and

- can only be used for charitable purposes
