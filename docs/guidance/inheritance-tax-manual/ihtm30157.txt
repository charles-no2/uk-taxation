Due date for payment: payment before due date
Identifier: IHTM30157
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30157
Published: 2016-04-09T09:57:50+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-12T15:23:11+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:59.738441+00:00

There is no statutory authority to allow a discount where tax is paid before its due date. Although interest is not charged for the period immediately after a chargeable transfer, it does not follow that a discount should be allowed if the account is delivered and the tax paid before interest begins to run.
