Special valuation matters: business relief claimed on the land

Any claim for business relief (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)) on a property should be investigated before a reference is made to the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)).

When considering whether 100% business relief is available, bear in mind that from 6 April 2026, there is a £2.5 million 100% relief allowance on the combined value of agricultural and business property (apportioned between all items of such property) and any value over that amount gets relief at 50% (see IHTM25500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25500)).

Business relief is due on the open market value of the property and you will need to consider the extent to which 100% relief is available (subject to the limit above) before deciding whether, or to what extent, to ask the VOA to consider the valuation returned by the taxpayer.
