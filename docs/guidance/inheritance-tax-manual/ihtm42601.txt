Foreign assets

From 6 April 2025, for inheritance tax purposes, domicile is replaced by long-term UK residence and you can find details of these rules at IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000#).

Whether foreign assets in a trust are excluded property depends upon the long-term UK residence status of the settlor, or in some cases, their domicile (see IHTM47050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47050)). For qualifying interest in possession settlements (IHTM16061 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16061)) foreign settled property is only excluded property at times on or after 6 April 2025 if the life tenant is also not a long-term UK resident (IHTM47051 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47051)).

Long-term UK residence/Domicile

If the taxpayer claims that the trust is exempt or partly exempt because of the long-term UK residence/domicile of the settlor, review the appropriate schedule in the account and refer to IHT Technical as appropriate. (IHTM10685 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10685))

The next page gives some details of the impact of not being long-term UK resident or having an overseas domicile. General long-term UK residence details are at the long-term UK residence section of this manual (IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000#)). General domicile details are at the domicile section of this manual. (IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000))

Avoidance

The use of foreign trusts by individuals who are long-term UK resident (from 6 April 2025) or were domiciled in the UK (before 6 April 2025) may be an indication of avoidance.
