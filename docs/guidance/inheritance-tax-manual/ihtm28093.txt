Liabilities: investigating liabilities: claims for financial relief under the Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006 or the Civil Partnership Act 2004
Identifier: IHTM28093
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28093
Published: 2016-04-09T09:53:13+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:12:47+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:07.349687+00:00

This instruction applies to Scottish estates only.

A claim by a former spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) for financial relief under the Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006 or Sch 28 Civil Partnership Act 2004 needs to be considered carefully to establish whether the deceased had a legally enforceable financial obligation to the former spouse or civil partner. You can find further consideration of the relevant sections of the Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006 in Gloag & Henderson 12th Edition from page 1023.

Any case that seems likely to be contentious should be referred to Technical.
