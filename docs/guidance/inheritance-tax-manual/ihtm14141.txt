Lifetime transfers: annual exemption: summary 
Identifier: IHTM14141
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14141
Published: 2016-04-09T09:16:47+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:04+00:00

A person’s lifetime transfers are exempt up to a total of £3,000 in an income tax year - 6 April to 5 April the following year.

The exemption applies to

- lifetime transfers by individuals (IHTM04053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04053))

- the lifetime termination of an interest in possession (IHTM16060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16060)) in settled property, and

- apportionments made to persons under IHTA84/S94 (transfers by close companies). (IHTM14851 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14851))

The exemption does not apply to the deemed potentially exempt transfer (PET) under FA 86/102(4) and FA86/103(5)

If a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024))) is more than the amount of the available exemption:

- it is an exempt transfer (IHTM04026 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04026)) up to the amount of the available exemption (IHTA84/S2 (2))

- the excess is the chargeable amount of the PET (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)) or immediately chargeable transfer (IHTM04027 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04027)).
