Settled property used in the life tenant's business:  Rate of relief
Identifier: IHTM25241
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25241
Published: 2016-04-09T09:45:08+01:00
Updated: 2023-03-10T12:13:42+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:25.385650+00:00

The rate of relief for IHTA84/S105 (1)(e) property is 50% for transfers on and after 10 March 1992.

For transfers before that date the rate of relief was 30%.
