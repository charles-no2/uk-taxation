Has an election been made?: introduction

Published: 2016-04-09T10:13:20+01:00

IHTA84/S142 (1) did not apply to a variation executed before 1 August 2002 unless there was a valid election under IHTA84/S142 (2). An election is not required for a disclaimer (IHTM35162 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35162)).
