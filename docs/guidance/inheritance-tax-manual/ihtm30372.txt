Special rules: voidable transfers

Published: 2016-04-09T10:00:01+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-15T13:20:59+00:00

Where a voidable chargeable transfer (IHTM14890 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14890)) is subsequently set aside by virtue of any enactment or rule of law, any tax paid on it is repayable.

Tax repayable under IHTA84/S150 carries interest (free of income tax) from the date on which the claim for repayment is made.
