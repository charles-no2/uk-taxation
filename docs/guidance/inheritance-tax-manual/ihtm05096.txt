IHT requirements and procedures (Scotland): review of form X-1 delivered after issue of confirmation ad non executa
Identifier: IHTM05096
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05096
Published: 2016-03-20T09:21:22+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:56:29+00:00

An application for confirmation ad non executa (IHTM05105 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05105)) will be accepted by the sheriff clerk without being seen by HMRC first. When we receive the ad non executa form X-1 from the sheriff clerk you should check:

- that the property included is truly ad non executa estate by checking that the same property (or the property in its previous form where it has been re-invested) is included in theIHT400 (IHTM10021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10021)) for the testator’s (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) estate, and

- where the original personal representative (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) (PR) has died, and had a beneficial interest in the testator’s estate, that their interest in the unadministered estate has been accounted for in connection with their (the original PR’s) death.
