The rules of intestacy are to be found in the Administration of Estates Act 1925 as amended from time to time.

Under these rules the personal representatives are required

- to sell the estate and convert it into money with power to postpone the sale and conversion (but personal chattels should not be sold except for special reasons)

- to pay the deceased’s debts and expenses and any legacies bequeathed by the Will (where there is a partial intestacy), and

- to distribute the residuary estate in accordance with the distribution rules (IHTM12111 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12111)) for an intestacy.
