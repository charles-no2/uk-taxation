Where there is a decrease in the total tax, you can easily include the adjustment in the calculation for the next instalment (IHTM30191 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30191)).

Remember to repay any interest that was overpaid on the tax that is now repayable (IHTM31616 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31616)). You should calculate this by looking at the interest charged on each instalment in turn.
