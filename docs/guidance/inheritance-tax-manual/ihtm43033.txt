Following the example from (IHTM43032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm43032)), if on Lucy’s death there had been no chargeable legacies the calculations would be as follows

Unused nil rate band calculation

M = £375,000

VT = £Nil

M is greater than VT by £375,000

Transferable nil rate band calculation

E =£375,000

NRBMD = £300,000 so

(375,000 ÷ 300,000) × 100 = 125.0000%

On David’s death the nil rate band available on his death would be limited to 100% by IHTA84/S8A(5).
