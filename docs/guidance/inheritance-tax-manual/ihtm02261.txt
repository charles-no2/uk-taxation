If you have a document in another language to be translated into English the HMRC Translation Service will be able to do this for you. You can find out what you need to do and how long you can expect a translation request to take on Sharepoint by searching 'Language Services'.

If you need assistance with customers who wish to contact us in Welsh, seek advice from Welsh Language Services, part of CDIO group, whose details can be found on the intranet.
