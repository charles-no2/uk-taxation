Life Policies: annuities payable to deceased/transferor by insurance companies: introduction

An annuity (IHTM16211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm16211)) is a yearly entitlement to a particular sum of money. The annuity may be payable at some other frequency, usually monthly, quarterly or half yearly. This entitlement may arise from a number of different sources, and it is important to determine the type of the annuity before deciding on the correct treatment for Inheritance Tax.

The instructions at IHTM20632 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20632)-IHTM20635 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20635) relate only to annuities that are

- linked to life policies

and

- which are not retirement annuities or pensions.

These are normally referred to as purchased life annuities (IHTM20632 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20632)).
