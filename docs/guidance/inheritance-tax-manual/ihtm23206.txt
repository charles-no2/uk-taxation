Special valuation matters: related property

Where the related property (IHTM09731 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm09731)) provisions under IHTA84/S161 ) apply to an interest in property which needs to be valued, when completing VOA 1 or VOA 2 you must indicate clearly in the appropriate part of the joint property box

- that the related property provisions apply,

and

- the share to be valued (for example if the property is owned jointly by the deceased and their spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) you should indicate that the share to be valued is the entirety)
