Once you have worked out the chargeable value of a failed potentially exempt transfer (PET) (IHTM14512 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14512)) or additional charge (IHTM14571 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14571)),

- add the previous lifetime cumulative transfers (PLCT) (IHTM14502 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14502)).

Then

- deduct the IHT nil-rate band, and multiply the remainder by the appropriate rate of tax (40% unless the transfer is before 18 March 1988 (IHTM30171 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30171))).

Apportion and deduct any tax attributable to previous lifetime transfers, leaving the tax due on the transfer itself.

Taper relief

Deduct any taper relief (IHTM14611 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14611)) due. COMPASS (IHTM31101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31101)) will calculate this automatically, but you must amend a manual calculation template yourself.

Adjusting additional charges

If you are calculating additional charges on an immediately chargeable transfer, deduct the tax paid (IHTM14576 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14576)) on the transfer at the time it was made. Show this as a 'relief against tax' in the appropriate box on your COMPASS (IHTM31189 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31189)) or manual template calculation.

Next action

You will now have the total tax charge for this lifetime transfer. You can follow the death estate instructions for the further aspects to consider.

- Revision to the original tax charge (IHTM31416 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31416))

- IOP cases (IHTM31418 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31418))

- Tax previously paid (IHTM31419 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31419)) (relating to this chargeable occasion only)

- Interest (IHTM31420 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31420))
