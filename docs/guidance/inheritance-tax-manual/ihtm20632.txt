Life Policies: annuities payable to deceased/transferor by insurance companies: purchased life annuities: types and potential claims
Identifier: IHTM20632
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20632
Published: 2016-04-09T09:34:33+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:08:23+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:29.673377+00:00

When dealing with a purchased life annuity you will need to consider the circumstances of each situation to decide on the appropriate course of action.

If the annuity:

Ceases on the deceased’s death follow the guidance at IHTM20633 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20633)

Continues after the deceased’s death for a guaranteed period follow the guidance at IHTM20634 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20634)

Was purchased in joint names follow the guidance at IHTM20635 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20635)

In some cases more than one instruction might be relevant.
