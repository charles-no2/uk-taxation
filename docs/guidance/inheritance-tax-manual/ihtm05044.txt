Completing the account: links between inventory form C1 and the IHT400 (Scotland)
Identifier: IHTM05044

Because every asset in the estate is listed for confirmation (IHTM05104 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05104)) purposes on the inventory (IHTM05025 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05025)) form C1, the personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) do not need to duplicate all this information in the IHT400. In particular:

- stocks and shares do not need to be individually listed on Schedule IHT411 (IHTM10033 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10033)),

- insurance policies paying out to the estate do not need to be listed on Schedule IHT410, and

- bank and building society accounts, national savings investments and premium bonds do not need to be listed on Schedule IHT406.
