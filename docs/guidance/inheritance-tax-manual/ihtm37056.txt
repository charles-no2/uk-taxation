Land valuation cases: action where an appeal is received

Updated: 2023-01-19T11:04:06+00:00

On receipt of a valid appeal HMRC will consider the grounds of appeal in line with HMRC’s Litigation and Settlement Strategy, the Appeals Reviews and Tribunal Guidance Manual (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/appeals-reviews-and-tribunals-guidance) and local guidance.

Should the dispute proceed to Litigation the disputed land value will be heard and decided by the Lands Chamber of the Upper Tribunal.
