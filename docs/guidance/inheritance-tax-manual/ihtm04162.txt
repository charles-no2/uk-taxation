Dispositions not intended to confer bounty: application of the relief

This page describes how a disposition may be excluded from the Inheritance Tax (IHT) charge under IHTA84/S10.

Step 1

There must be a disposition – see IHTM04023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04023).

Step 2

If there is a disposition what assets were disposed of? If the disposition was of unquoted shares or a reversionary interest (IHTM04282 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04282)) the provisions of IHTA84/S10 may not apply (IHTM04163 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04163)).

Step 3

Irrespective of the type of asset disposed of, was the disposition intended, or made in a transaction intended, to confer bounty (IHTM04165 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04165))?

If the transferor intended to make a gift or confer gratuitous benefit on another person, the provisions of IHTA84/S10 cannot apply.

Step 4

If the disposition was not intended to confer bounty, was the transaction made at arms-length between two people not connected with each other, or as might be expected to be made at arms-length between two people not connected with each other (IHTM04166 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04166))?

If yes, the provisions of IHTA84/S10 apply and the disposition is not a transfer of value (IHTM04024 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04024)).
