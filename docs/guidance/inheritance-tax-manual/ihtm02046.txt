Communications: Incoming telephone calls: requests for information where the caller cannot be verified
Identifier: IHTM02046
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02046
Published: 2016-03-20T09:12:21+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:53:14+00:00

In any case where the parties want to know any information we hold on the file you must return their call before you give it (IHTM02045 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02045)).

If the information given by the caller does not match that on the file you should treat the identity of the caller as not verified (IHTM02047 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02047)). You must follow the procedures at IHTM02047 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02047).
