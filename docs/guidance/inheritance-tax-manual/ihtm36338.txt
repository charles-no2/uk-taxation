Letters of offer: interest and penalties
Identifier: IHTM36338
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36338
Published: 2016-04-09T10:20:24+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:49+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:51.790980+00:00

The letter of offer should contain a reference to penalties. The letter should also include a reference to interest if the settlement included interest. But there should be no reference to interest if the settlement does not cover it.
