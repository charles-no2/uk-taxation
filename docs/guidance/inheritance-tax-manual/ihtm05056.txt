Submitting the account and obtaining a grant: what happens after the grant is obtained?

After the grant (IHTM05001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05001)) (or confirmation, in Scotland) is obtained the personal representative (PR) (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) can start administering the estate. Paying all the Inheritance Tax due (IHTM30151 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30151)) is part of this procedure but it involves a lot else besides. It may also involve:

- collecting any debts due to the deceased

- finding unknown creditors of the estate (for example, through adverts in the local newspaper).

- paying the deceased’s debts

- finalising the income tax and capital gains tax positions before the death and during the administration period

- drawing up estate accounts

- paying any pecuniary legacies (IHTM12082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12082))

- making sure that any specific legacies (IHTM12082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12082)) of assets are appropriated to legatees

- converting assets of the residuary estate into cash or assenting them to the residuary legatees (IHTM12082 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm12082))

- distributing the residue to the residuary legatees
