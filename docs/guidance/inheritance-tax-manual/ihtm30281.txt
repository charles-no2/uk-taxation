Lifetime cesser of a qualifying interest in possession: introduction

The cesser may be either a chargeable transfer when made (IHTM30283 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30283)), or with effect from 17 March 1987 a potentially exempt transfer (PET) (IHTM30282 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30282)). The two situations need to be considered separately.
