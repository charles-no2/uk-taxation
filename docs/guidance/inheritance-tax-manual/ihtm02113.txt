The post room will normally use window envelopes unless

- the correspondence is too bulky because of enclosures

- ‘By Recorded Delivery’/‘By Registered Post’ (IHTM02117 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02117)) is to be noted above the address

- the letter is ‘Private and Confidential’.
