The instalment conditions have to be considered separately for:

-

the tax immediately payable (IHTM30262 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30262)) on the making of the chargeable transfer and

-

the additional tax payable (IHTM30263 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30263)) on the transferor’s death within seven years of the transfer.
