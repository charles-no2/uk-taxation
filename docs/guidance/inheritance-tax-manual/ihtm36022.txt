Late accounts: accounts subject to a penalty
Identifier: IHTM36022
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36022
Published: 2016-04-09T10:15:57+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:08:03.051020+00:00

The late delivery (IHTM36033 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36033)) of the following accounts may result in a penalty under IHTA84/S245

-

IHT 100a, 100b, 100b(death), 100c, 100d, 100e and 100h

-

IHT400 (IHTM10021 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10021))

-

accounts for recapture charges (IHTM10840 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10840)) for heritage property and woodlands.

The penalty provisions need to be considered for all late accounts, including those delivered by persons domiciled outside the United Kingdom (IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000)) or not a long-term UK resident (IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000#)).

The legislation also applies to the late delivery of a corrective account (IHTM10011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10011)) (form C4). But you should not normally seek a penalty for the late delivery of the corrective account or Inventory under IHTA84/S245. You should instead consider a penalty for failing to correct an error in an account (IHTM36106 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36106)), IHTA84/S248 (1), if you discover that a corrective account or inventory was not submitted promptly after the discovery of a defect in the original account.
