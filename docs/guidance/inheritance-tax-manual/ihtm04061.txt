Lifetime transfers: transfers that cannot be potentially  exempt transfers
Identifier: IHTM04061
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04061
Published: 2016-03-20T09:15:56+00:00
Updated: 2018-04-10T17:11:03+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:52:30.910236+00:00

There are two transfers of value that are specifically prevented from being potentially exempt transfers (IHTM04057 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04057)). These are

- the deemed disposition on an alteration in the capital or share rights of close companies, (IHTM04069 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04069)) IHTA84/S98 (3), and

- a transfer to the extent to which the property transferred consists of woodlands subject to a deferred Estate Duty charge, (IHTM04062 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04062)) FA86/SCH19/PARA46.
