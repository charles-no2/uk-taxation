Compensation payments: ex gratia compensation payments made by the Criminal Injuries Compensation Authority
Identifier: IHTM10267
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10267
Published: 2016-03-20T09:28:32+00:00
Updated: 2023-06-29T13:38:54+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:15.187960+00:00

Any payments made by the Criminal Injuries Compensation Authority to the spouse or civil partner (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) and dependants of a deceased victim of a crime of violence are not included in the deceased’s estate. No payment is made under this scheme for the benefit of a deceased’s estate.
