Lifetime transfers: associated operations: the subjective test
Identifier: IHTM14824
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14824
Published: 2016-04-09T09:21:56+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:51+00:00

If you have considered the objective test (IHTM14823 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14823)) and determined that it does not apply, you must then consider the subjective test set out in IHTA84/S268(1)(b). To be relevant associated operations for the subjective test, there must be two or more operations (IHTM14826 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14826)), of which

- one is effected with reference to the other, or

- one is effected with a view to enabling the other to be effected, or

- one facilitates the effecting of the other.

If one of the operations is similarly linked to a further operation, all the operations are associated.

If none of the above conditions are met and the objective test (IHTM14823 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14823)) has also not been met, then the operations are not associated operations.
