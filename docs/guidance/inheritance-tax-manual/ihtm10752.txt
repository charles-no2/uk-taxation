Corrective account (C4S) Scotland: who should complete and sign the corrective account? 
Identifier: IHTM10752
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10752
Published: 2016-03-20T09:31:12+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:59:38+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:40.237008+00:00

All the accountable persons (IHTM10805 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10805)) who signed the IHT400 should sign the form C4(S. An agent acting on behalf of the accountable persons must not sign the form.
