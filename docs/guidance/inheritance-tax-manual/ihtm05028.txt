Is an account required?: record of the grant
Identifier: IHTM05028

Published: 2016-03-20T09:20:43+00:00
Updated: 2023-07-19T11:05:06+01:00

The probate registries do not tell us that a grant (IHTM05001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05001)) has been issued. This means that we do not record the date and place of grant on file.

You may be able to find the date and place of a grant on GOV.UK, for England and Wales cases.
