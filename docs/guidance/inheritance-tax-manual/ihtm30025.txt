Liability on death transfers: interests under  superannuation schemes
Identifier: IHTM30025
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30025
Published: 2016-04-09T09:56:17+01:00
Updated: 2018-03-05T11:18:24+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:04:40.799552+00:00

Where any tax is attributable to the value of a pension or annuity, in or under a fund as is specified in IHTA84/S151(1) and which satisfies the conditions in IHTA84/S151(2)(a) and (b), the personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012)) are expressly made liable by IHTA84/S210, which also excepts the trustees from liability.
