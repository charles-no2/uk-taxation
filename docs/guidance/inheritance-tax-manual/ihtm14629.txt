Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime reliefs: fall in value relief: portfolio of assets
Identifier: IHTM14629
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14629
Published: 2016-04-09T09:20:47+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:32+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:07.886509+00:00

When a number of assets are transferred on the same occasion, such as a portfolio of shares and cash when a interest in a trust comes to an end, allow relief on the individual assets which have fallen in value.

Do not net off the gains and losses for the whole portfolio (as you would for loss on sale of shares relief (IHTM34001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34001))).

Where there is more than one sale disposing of the underlying assets, see the instructions at IHTM14630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14630).

If the taxpayer queries this approach, refer to Technical.
