The Countrywide (formerly Premium Life) Assurance Company devised two schemes which were designed to avoid Capital Transfer Tax (CTT) and Inheritance Tax (IHT). They are known as

- the Asset Protection Plan

- the CTT or IHT Mitigation Plan.

These schemes differ from those referred to elsewhere in IHTM20000 onwards, so they require different treatment.
