Special valuation matters: conditional exemption claimed on  the land

If the taxpayer claims conditional exemption (IHTM11260 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11260)) on any land on the basis that it is of national interest, etc. you should refer it to Heritage Section as soon as possible.
