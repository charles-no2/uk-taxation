Other income due to the deceased - retrospective pay awards
Identifier: IHTM10125
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10125
Published: 2016-03-20T09:27:56+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:37+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:07.198006+00:00

Remuneration received as part of a retrospective pay award is only included as part of the estate for Inheritance Tax purposes if the date of the award was before the date of death.
