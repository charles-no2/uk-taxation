Valuing the partnership interest: Agricultural land  owned by deceased and occupied by a partnership
Identifier: IHTM25106
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25106
Published: 2016-04-09T09:44:09+01:00
Updated: 2016-09-29T09:42:34+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:02:13.563409+00:00

In the course of your investigation you may find that the deceased owned land which had been let to a partnership of which the deceased was a member. Unless it is clear from the information already supplied, ask the taxpayer to provide:

- details of the terms on which the partnership occupied the land

- supporting evidence such as copy leases or written statements setting out the terms of verbal agreements.

Before you refer to the Valuation Office Agency (IHTM23002 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm23002)), you have to determine whether or not the land should be valued subject to the tenancy.
