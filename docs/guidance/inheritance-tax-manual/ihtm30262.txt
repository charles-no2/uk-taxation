Lifetime transfers chargeable when made: tax immediately  payable
Identifier: IHTM30262
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30262
Published: 2016-04-09T09:59:12+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:15:19+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:05:14.232648+00:00

Instalments are available if the tax attributable to the qualifying property (IHTM30265 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm30265)) is borne by the person benefiting from the transfer.

Accordingly instalments are not available:

-

if the transfer is grossed up (IHTM14541 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14541))or

-

if it is grossed up in part, to the extent that it is grossed up.
