Other assets due to the deceased - Private health schemes
Identifier: IHTM10153
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10153
Published: 2016-03-20T09:28:01+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:38+00:00

Box 76 of the IHT400 will also include any payments due to the deceased under private medical insurance to cover hospital or other charges before death. Medical bills (IHTM28200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28200)) are usually deducted at box 82 of the IHT400.

You can generally accept the value offered without enquiry
