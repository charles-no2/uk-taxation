Qualifying the certificate: timber
Identifier: IHTM40132
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40132
Published: 2016-04-09T10:23:02+01:00
Updated: 2017-01-19T16:08:33+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:19.498716+00:00

You should specifically exclude timber on which tax has not been paid in the certificate.

Qualify the certificate by writing the following sentence above your signature and authorisation stamp:

‘save and except trees or underwood left out of account in accordance with IHTA84/S125’.
