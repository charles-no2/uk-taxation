Changes in shareholdings: rights disposed of without consideration
Identifier: IHTM34191
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34191
Published: 2016-04-09T10:12:03+01:00
Updated: 2017-11-07T16:24:19+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:07:19.085451+00:00

The disposal of rights without consideration is not a sale. Neither is it a capital receipt.

You should refer any case where rights are disposed of without consideration to Technical (IHTM01081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm01081)) who will consider whether the shares allotted to a donee form part of a new holding.
