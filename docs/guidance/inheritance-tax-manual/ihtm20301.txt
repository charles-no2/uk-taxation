Life Policies: joint life and joint name policies: introduction

It is quite common to encounter a life policy which has been issued to more than one policyholder. Usually the joint policyholders are a married couple or civil partners (IHTM11032 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11032)) but, provided there is sufficient insurable interest (IHTM20085 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20085)) it is possible for people who are neither married to, nor in a civil partnership with, each other to take out joint policies. Partners and co-directors of limited companies often do so, for example.

There are procedural instructions at IHTM20023 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20023).
