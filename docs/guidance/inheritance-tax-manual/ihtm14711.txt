Lifetime transfers: specific lifetime reliefs: gifts with reservation (GWRs): when double charges relief arises
Identifier: IHTM14711
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14711
Published: 2016-04-09T09:21:29+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:03:37+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:58:16.033604+00:00

Relief under the Inheritance Tax (Double Charges Relief) Regulations 1987 (SI1987/1130) Reg.5 will apply in instances where a failed potentially exempt transfer (PET) (IHTM14511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14511)), or an immediately chargeable transfer (IHTM04067 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04067)), is also a GWR (IHTM14301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14301)) and the asset transferred becomes chargeable a second time on the transferor’s death because either

- the reservation continued to the date of death so that the asset concerned is treated by FA86/S102(3) as property which the deceased was beneficially entitled to, or

- the asset ceased to be subject to a reservation before the death and became a deemed PET (IHTM04064 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04064)) on that date.
