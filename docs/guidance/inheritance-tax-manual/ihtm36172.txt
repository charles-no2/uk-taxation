Calculating the penalty: effect of criminal proceedings
Identifier: IHTM36172
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36172
Published: 2016-04-09T10:17:55+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:23:29+00:00

Any case in which criminal proceedings have been taken will usually be settled by SpecialCivil Investigations. However, it will be returned to you with instructions if,exceptionally, you are to negotiate a settlement. In general

- you will not be able to claim a civil financial penalty on the basis of fraud (IHTM36291 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36291)) in respect of an offence which has been before the criminal courts

- you will be given advice on whether to claim a penalty based on negligence (IHTM36301 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36301)) from a taxpayer who has been acquitted of fraud

- you should claim a penalty in the normal way in respect of any offence which did not form part of the criminal proceedings.
