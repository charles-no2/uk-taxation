Liabilities: investigating form IHT419: purpose of the form

Supplementary form IHT419 is enclosed with the IHT400 where the taxpayers are claiming a deduction from the deceased’s estate for particular types of debt. These are

- loans from friends and relatives (IHTM28321 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28321))

- money spent on behalf of the deceased by a close friend or relative (IHTM28330 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28330))

- other loans or overdrafts (IHTM28340 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28340))

- guarantee debts (IHTM28351 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28351)).

It should also be completed where FA86/S103 may apply to restrict the deduction (IHTM28361 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm28361)) for a debt. Broadly speaking this is where the deceased has both borrowed money from someone and has made a gift to that same person.
