Assets which form part of settled property in which the transferor had a qualifying interest in possession (IHTM25241 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25241)) may also qualify for relief under IHTA84/S105 (1)(d).
