Step 4 - grossing up: other property which bears its own tax
Identifier: IHTM26126
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm26126
Published: 2016-04-09T09:47:48+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:12:04+00:00

The following other property taxable on the death bears its own tax

- joint property (IHTM15011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15011)) passing by survivorship (IHTM15081 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15081))

- nominated property (IHTM15101 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm15101))

- property taxable as a gift with reservation (GWR) (IHTM14025) under FA86/S102 (3), and

- any other property which does not vest in the deceased’s personal representatives (IHTM05012 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05012))
