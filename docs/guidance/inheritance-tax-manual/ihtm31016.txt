You can prepare some settlement calculations on COMPASS (IHTM31241 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31241)), but the remainder must be calculated manually.

The later instructions provide advice on calculations relating to

- immediately chargeable transfers (IHTM31450 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31450))

- principal, proportionate and flat rate charges (IHTM31460 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31460)) in existing settlement cases, and

- recapture charges (IHTM31491 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31491)) when heritage property or timber is disposed of.
