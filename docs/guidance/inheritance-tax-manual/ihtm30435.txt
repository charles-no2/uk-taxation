Reconsideration by taxpayers: decrease  requested

Updated: 2016-02-11T17:15:27+00:00

Claims that the value of property should be decreased must be dealt with on their merits whether or not a statutory certificate of clearance (IHTM40010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm40010)) has been issued.

There are detailed instructions on the statutory loss on sale relief for land (IHTM33010 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm33010)) and for shares (IHTM34011 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34011)).
