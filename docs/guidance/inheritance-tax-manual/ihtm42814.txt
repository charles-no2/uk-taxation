Special trusts: excluded property
Identifier: IHTM42814
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42814
Published: 2016-04-09T10:25:48+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-07T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:09:47.950395+00:00

Foreign assets in trusts may qualify for exclusion from Inheritance Tax charges (IHTM42602 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42602)) depending on the long-term UK residence (IHTM47000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47000) and IHTM47053 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm47053)) or domicile (IHTM13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm13000)) of the settlor.
