The POA charge applies where the chargeable person is in possession of, or has the use of a chattel FA04/Sch15/Para 6(1), either alone or with other persons, and either the

- ‘disposal condition’ (IHTM44007 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44007)), or

- ‘contribution condition’ (IHTM44008 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44008))

is met. The charge is subject to certain exemptions (IHTM44040 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44040)) and exclusions (IHTM44030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm44030)).

Use

Very limited or occasional use of the chattel in question will not be subject to the POA charge. Examples 5 & 6 at IHTM14333 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm14333) give more detailed guidance on what you might regard as limited or occasional use.
