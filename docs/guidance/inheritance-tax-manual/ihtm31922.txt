Assessing: transferring money: transferring money to other HMRC offices
Identifier: IHTM31922
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31922
Published: 2016-04-09T10:07:45+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:20:00+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:06:43.360092+00:00

If you want to transfer money to another HMRC Office, you must use a form Sect 6 (IHTM31641 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm31641)).

Say ‘yes’ to the question ‘is money to be paid to HMRC.

You must quote the other office’s reference number when completing the form.
