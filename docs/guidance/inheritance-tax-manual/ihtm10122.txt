Other income due to the deceased - Civil service pay and pensions
Identifier: IHTM10122
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10122
Published: 2016-03-20T09:27:51+00:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T16:57:36+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:06.105284+00:00

Arrears of Civil Service pay or pension form part of the estate. If this is disputed you should refer the case to Technical. The exception will be when payments become due as the result of a retrospective post-death pay award.
