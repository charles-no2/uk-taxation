Example involving UK stocks and shares or insurance policies

In the estate of [name of deceased and Inheritance Tax reference number]

In consideration of HMRC agreeing to issue the letter with a unique code/form IHT421/C1 [as appropriate] without requiring full payment of the Inheritance Tax due upon delivery of the account I/we

Full name: ………………………………………

Address: ………………………………………

Full name: ………………………………………

Address: …………………………………………

undertake:

To use my/our best endeavours to pay the Inheritance Tax and interest due on delivery of the account within 4 weeks of the issue of the Grant of Probate. To this end I/we will

- submit the application for Probate as soon as I/we have received the receipted letter/IHT 421/C1 [as appropriate] and to notify HMRC as soon as the Grant/Letters of Administration have been issued.

- immediately following receipt of the Grant of Probate I/we will take the all the steps necessary to promptly realise sufficient assets in the estate to pay the Inheritance tax and any accrued interest due.

- pay to HMRC the outstanding tax and interest from the proceeds of sale/realisation of the assets as soon as received by me/us.

- advise HMRC immediately if the tax cannot be paid within the time specified.

Signed ………………………………… Dated ……………………..

Signed ………………………………… Dated ……………………..
