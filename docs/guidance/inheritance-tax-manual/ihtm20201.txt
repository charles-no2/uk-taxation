Life Policies: policy on the deceased’s life not connected with any other transaction (except a trust): introduction
Identifier: IHTM20201
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20201
Published: 2016-04-09T09:32:46+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:06:14+00:00

These instructions relate to unconnected policies and those connected with a trust only in the following situations;

- The deceased effected the policy on their own life for their own absolute benefit and retained it until their death (IHTM20211 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20211)). This should be shown in box 2 of IHT410 (IHTM20022 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20022)).

- The deceased effected the policy on their own life for their own absolute benefit and gave it to someone else or placed it in trust later (IHTM20221 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20221)). This should be shown on form IHT403 (IHTM20042 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20042)).

- The deceased effected the policy on their own life for the benefit of someone else or in trust from the start (IHTM20251 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20251)). This should also be shown on form IHT403 (IHTM20044 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm20044)).
