Sales: cancellation of shares

For deaths on or after 16 March 1992, relief is available if

- qualifying investments (IHTM34131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34131)) are held at the date of death

- they are cancelled without replacement within 12 months of the death, and

- the shares were still held by the appropriate person (IHTM34161 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm34161)) immediately before the cancellation.

If all the above conditions apply the investments are treated as having been sold immediately before cancellation for £1, IHTA84/S186A_._
