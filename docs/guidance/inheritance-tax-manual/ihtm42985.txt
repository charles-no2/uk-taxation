Employee benefit trusts: property leaving employee benefit trusts: approved Share Ownership Plans 
Identifier: IHTM42985
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42985
Published: 2016-04-09T10:27:43+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:26:14+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:11:41.636568+00:00

If the trusts of the employee benefit trust are those of a share incentive plan under ITEPA03/Sch2 (IHTM42941 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42941)), IHTA84/S72(4A) specifically waives the charge under IHTA84/S72(3)(b) on an appropriation of shares under the scheme.
