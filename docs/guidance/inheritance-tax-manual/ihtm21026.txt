Household goods and personal goods: types of asset: chattels of national interest
Identifier: IHTM21026
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm21026
Published: 2016-04-09T09:35:08+01:00
Updated: 2016-02-11T17:08:30+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T15:00:36.016061+00:00

Any case in which conditional exemption from Inheritance Tax (IHTM11260 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm11260)) is claimed on the basis that the chattels are of national interest or importance must be referred to Heritage as soon as possible.
