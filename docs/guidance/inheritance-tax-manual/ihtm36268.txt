Settling the penalty: administrative procedures

When you have issued the letter of acceptance (IHTM36267 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36267)) you will need to

- BU (IHTM32031) the file for the time when payment (of the whole or first instalment) of the penalty is due

- follow the procedures (IHTM36391 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36391)) for bringing the penalty to account

- cross through any note on the front of the file relating to penalty time limits (IHTM36165 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36165))

- consider issuing a letter (IHTM36271 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36271)) explaining how problems might be avoided in the future.

When the penalty is paid, you will need to close the penalty record on COMPASS (IHTM36376 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm36376))
