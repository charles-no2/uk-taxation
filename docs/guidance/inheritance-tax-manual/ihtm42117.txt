First quarter

Proportionate charges can arise at any time during the life of the trust except that under IHTA84/S65 (4), no proportionate charge arises if the exit event occurs in the first quarter following

-

the day the settlement commenced, or

-

the date of a ten-year anniversary.

However, because there is no IHT charge the trustees will not be able to claim Capital Gains Tax (CGT) holdover relief under TCGA92/S260. (CG66883 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg66883))

New will trusts - S144

IHTA84/S144 allows trustees to distribute from a will trust within two years of the death without a proportionate charge arising.(IHTM35181 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm35181))

Again, there is no chargeable event, and so holdover relief does not apply to the transfer for CGT purposes.
