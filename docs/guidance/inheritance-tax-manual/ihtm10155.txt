Other assets due to the deceased: copyrights, royalties, etc
Identifier: IHTM10155
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm10155
Published: 2016-03-20T09:28:05+00:00
Updated: 2017-12-19T14:21:24+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:55:09.013088+00:00

You should refer to Shares and Assets Valuation (SAV) any sums due by way of copyrights, royalties, performing rights and the like.

SAV also deal with business relief (BR) (IHTM25131 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm25131)) on copyright and royalties.
