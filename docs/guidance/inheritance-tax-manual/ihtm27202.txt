Foreign property: Double Taxation Conventions: proper law of settlement

Usually we do not need to consider which country’s law governs a settlement IHTM04273 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm04273)).

However the French and Italian pre-1975 double taxation conventions have exemptions from Inheritance Tax for settled property situated abroad, that do not apply if the ‘proper law’ is British.
