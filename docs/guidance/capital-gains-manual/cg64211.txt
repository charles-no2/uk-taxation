Private residence relief: outline of guidance

The guidance below explains the operation of private residence relief as follows

- identification of the dwelling house, see CG64230+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64230p)

- identification of an individual's only or main residence, see CG64420+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64420p)

- part of a dwelling house used as an individual's only or main residence, see CG64650+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64650p)

- the period for which entitlement to relief is considered, see CG64920+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64920p)

- identification of the garden or grounds of the residence, see CG64350+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64350p)

- determination of the permitted area of garden or grounds, see CG64800+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64800p)

- computation of relief, see CG64970+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64970p)

- exchanges of interests in residences, see CG65155 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65155)

- acquisition of or expenditure on the residence for the purpose of realising a gain, see CG65200+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65200p)

- division of the residence on separation or divorce or dissolution of a civil partnership, see CG65300+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65300p)

- private residence relief on disposal of settled property, see CG65400+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65400p)

- residence provided for a dependent relative, see CG65550+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65550p).
