TCGA92/SCH11/PARA17

Where Betterment Levy, see CG72600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg72600), has been charged in respect of the land which has been disposed of, certain additional expenditure may be allowable under TCGA92/S38 (1)(b).

The detailed guidance has now been archived so if you encounter such a case, please make a referral to the CG Technical Team for advice.
