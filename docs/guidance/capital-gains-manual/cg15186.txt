Enhancement expenditure: not reflected in the asset at disposal date
Identifier: CG15186
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15186
Published: 2016-03-12T10:09:35+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T11:58:52+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:37:58.102942+00:00

Expenditure which has proved futile or has wasted away before the disposal does not qualify as enhancement expenditure because it is not reflected in the state or nature of the asset at the date of disposal.
