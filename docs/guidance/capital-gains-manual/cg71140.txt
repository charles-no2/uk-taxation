A number of special rules apply to the disposal of a short lease, that is a lease with a remaining term of 50 years or less at the date of its disposal.

Those rules are dealt with in the following guidance:

- wasting of allowable expenditure: general, see CG71141 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71141)

- disposal of lease acquired subject to a sub-lease not at a rack rent, see CG71170 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71170)

- interaction with capital allowances, see CG71180 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71180)

- disposal of a lease which was a wasting asset at 6 April 1965, see CG71190 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71190)

- interaction with property income relief, see CG71200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71200)

- assignment of a short lease granted at under-value, see CG71210 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71210).
