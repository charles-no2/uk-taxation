TCGA92/S236T(5)

In CG67875 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67875) ‘third party’ means a person other than,

- C or a member of a group of which C is the principal company,

- a person who is, or has at any time in the preceding 12 months been, a participator in C or in a member of such a group, or

- a person connected with a person in the second point above.

In applying the guidance at CG67875 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67875), ‘close company’ and ‘participator’ have the same meaning as in IHTA84/S102. The meaning of ‘participator’ is extended in the same way as in the second paragraph of CG67839 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67839).
