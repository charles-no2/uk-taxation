Targeted rules to prevent income to capital converter schemes  by companies - no double taxation
Identifier: CG44122
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg44122
Published: 2016-03-12T11:05:31+00:00
Updated: 2021-04-22T11:28:23+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:32:04.907747+00:00

Where both TCGA92/S184G and existing anti-avoidance rules could potentially apply tothe same transaction, double taxation will not arise (for example through the restrictionof losses used against a capital gain and re-characterisation of all or part of theconsideration as an income profit). This is because TCGA92/S37(1) acts to disregard, incomputing the amount of the consideration for chargeable gains purposes, any amount takeninto account in computing the income of the person making the disposal.
