TCGA92/S251 (4)

CG53480 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53480) explains the anti-avoidance provisions of TCGA92/S251 (4) which apply to losses arising on disposals of debts by persons connected with the original lender. Although the word `debt' is not qualified, in the Revenue's view TCGA92/S251 (4) does not apply to debts on a security.
