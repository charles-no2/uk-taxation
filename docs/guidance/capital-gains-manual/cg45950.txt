Groups: business asset roll-over relief: the same person rule (3), takeovers

For the purposes of Section 175(2A) the group is defined by TCGA92/S170 including the extension to the group definition in Section 170(10), see CG45130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg45130). If the principal company of one group is taken over by another company, the original group and the enlarged group are regarded as the same group. So, roll-over relief may be claimed where a disposal is made by a company in the original group and the replacement asset is acquired by any company in the enlarged group.

Example

Company B, a member of a group headed by principal company A, makes a disposal of a trade asset in 2018. In 2019 company A is acquired by company X which already has subsidiary Y. In 2020 company Y acquires a trade asset. If the other conditions for relief are fulfilled, companies B and Y can claim roll-over relief.
