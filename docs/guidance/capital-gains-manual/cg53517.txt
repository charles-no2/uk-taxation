Debts: satisfied by issuing shares or securities: conversion of securities
Identifier: CG53517
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53517
Published: 2016-03-12T11:32:41+00:00
Updated: 2021-08-27T16:04:12+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:33:07.749134+00:00

TCGA92/S132 may apply if

- a debt that is a security

- is acquired by the debtor company and

- the company issues its own shares or other securities in satisfaction of the debt.

Guidance on TCGA92/S132 is at CG55000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg55000) onwards. When that section applies TCGA92/S127 operates so that the transaction is treated as involving no acquisition of the shares issued (see CG55000) so TCGA92/S251 (3) cannot apply.
