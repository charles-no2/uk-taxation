TCGA92/S226

Relief may also be available on the disposal of a residence which has been provided by an individual to their dependent relative. The residence must be provided rent-free and without any other consideration and must have been occupied by that dependent relative before 6 April 1988. For detailed instructions see CG65550+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65550p).
