TCGA92/S251 (3)

A creditor may accept property rather than cash in satisfaction of the debt. You have to consider a number of linked disposals/acquisitions.

- The disposal of the property by the debtor, see CG53511 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53511).

- The disposal of the debt by the creditor, see CG53512 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53512).

- The acquisition of the property by the creditor, see CG53513 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53513).
