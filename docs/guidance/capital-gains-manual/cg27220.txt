Partnerships: Calculating a fractional interest in a partnership asset

CG27150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg27150) explains that partners are treated as owning fractional interests in each of the assets of the partnership and that a disposal occurs when a partner's fractional interest in an asset is reduced. This may occur on the disposal of an asset by the partnership or on a change in the partners' sharing ratios.

A partner's fractional interest in a partnership asset should be determined by reference to:

- any specific agreement which sets out how capital assets are to be allocated, or if there is no such agreement,

- any agreement or other evidence which sets out how capital profits are to be shared, or if there is no such agreement or evidence,

- any agreement or other evidence which sets out how income profits are to be shared, or if there is no such agreement or evidence,

- Section 24 (1) Partnership Act 1890, which provides that assets should be treated as held by the partners in equal proportions, see BIM82055 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim82055).
