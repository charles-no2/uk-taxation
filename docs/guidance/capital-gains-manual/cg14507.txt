Consideration for disposal: meaning of consideration: rent charges
Identifier: CG14507

A rent charge or other such right to income or payments in the nature of income is itself a chargeable asset. A payment in respect of the voluntary or compulsory redemption of a feu duty, see BIM46845, or a rent charge, see BIM46830 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim46830), is consideration for the disposal of an asset and the recipient should be dealt with accordingly. As regards the payer, see CG15292 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15292).

Note: Some annuities and annual payments due under covenants which are not secured on any property are not chargeable assets. See CG11700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg11700).
