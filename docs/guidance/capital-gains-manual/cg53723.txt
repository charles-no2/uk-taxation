Qualifying corporate bonds: taxpayer receives shares/QCBs: charities

Published: 2016-03-12T11:33:43+00:00

The exemption for gifts to charities in TCGA 1992 section 257, see CG66620+, also applies to a gift of QCBs which carry a deferred gain. The deferred gain is not brought into charge on the disposal to the charity.
