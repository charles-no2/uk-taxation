Share exchange: TCGA92/S135: deferred consideration
Identifier: CG52560
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg52560
Published: 2016-03-12T11:26:41+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T08:53:30+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:42:39.662855+00:00

An agreement for the sale of shares in, or debentures of, a company may include the right to receive an unascertainable amount of future consideration depending, for instance, on the performance of the company. If the performance targets are met TCGA92/S135 would not by itself apply to the issue of any shares or debentures in satisfaction of the right to receive the unascertainable amount. However, TCGA92/S138A may operate so that TCGA92/S135 can apply. For guidance on deferred consideration arrangements involving shares or debentures to which TCGA92/S138A may apply see CG58000+.
