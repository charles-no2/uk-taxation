Wasting assets: chattels which are wasting assets

TCGA92/S45

CG76721 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76721) explains that disposals of chattels (tangible moveable property) which are wasting assets are exempt for the purposes of TCGA92 unless:

- Capital Allowances were or could have been claimed, see CG15400+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15400p), or

- TCGA92/S45(3B) applies, see CG76722 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76722).

The most common examples you will come across of assets which are wasting assets will involve plant or machinery of some description though some assets may naturally have a predictable life not exceeding 50 years.
