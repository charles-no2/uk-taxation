Debts: satisfied by issuing shares or securities

One of the most common situations where TCGA92/S251 (3) applies is when a loan is converted into shares. A person who has made a loan to a company may take shares issued by the company and meet the subscription price by reducing the amount of the loan.

However, TCGA92/S251 (3) does not override the ordinary rules of

- TCGA92/S126 to TCGA92/S128, which apply to share reorganisations (see CG53516A (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53516a)), or

- TCGA92/S132, which applies to the conversion of securities (see CG53517 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53517)), or

- TCGA92/S135, which applies to share exchanges (see CG53518 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53518)).
