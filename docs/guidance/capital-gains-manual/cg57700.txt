Unit trusts: different portfolios
Identifier: CG57700
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg57700
Published: 2016-03-12T11:44:22+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T10:27:47+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:44:00.230637+00:00

If a unit trust has a number of different portfolios, transfers by a unit holder between each portfolio should be treated as a chargeable disposal and an acquisition of new units. TCGA92/S102 prevents the share reorganisation provisions of TCGA92/S127-TCGA92/S131 from applying to the transfer.
