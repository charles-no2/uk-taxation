Reliefs: employee-ownership trusts: introduction

This guidance outlines the relief that is available when shares are transferred into an Employee-ownership Trust (EOT). The relief is available for disposals made on or after 6 April 2014 that meet the relevant requirements, but the detailed rules are modified in certain respects, see , for disposals made before 26 June 2014.

Guidance about the employment income aspects of EOTs can be found at EIM03050 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim03050).

For guidance on EOTs and Inheritance Tax, see IHTM42995 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42995).

If you need to know about

- settled property, see CG33220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg33220)

- gifts to employee trusts generally, see CG36000P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg36000p)

- share identification rules generally, see CG51550P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51550p)

- profit-sharing schemes and employee share-ownership plans generally, see CG56300P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56300p)

- Employee Share Ownership Trusts (ESOTs), see BIM44000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim44000) onwards and TSEM5500.
