If you are dealing with a disposal, or acquisition, of business assets, you may also have to consider whether any apportionment or valuation is required for a Capital Allowances computation. Guidance on this is available in the Capital Allowances Manual at CA11000+. Again, you should liaise with your counterpart in the other Office.
