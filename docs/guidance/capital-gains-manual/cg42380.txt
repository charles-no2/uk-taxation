Migration of companies: restriction of roll-over relief on ceasing UK residence

TCGA92/S185 (3)

If a company which ceases to be resident

- disposes of `old assets’ before it migrates,

- acquires `new assets’ after it migrates

TCGA92/S185 (3) prevents it from rolling over the gains under the provisions of TCGA92/S152, see CG60250+. The restriction does not apply if the new assets are assets of a permanent establishment which are exempted from the exit charge by TCGA92/S185 (4), see CG61350 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg61350).
