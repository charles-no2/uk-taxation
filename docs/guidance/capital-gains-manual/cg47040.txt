Targeted anti-loss buying rule - commencement
Identifier: CG47040
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg47040
Published: 2016-03-12T11:14:43+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T17:05:34+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:41:53.537673+00:00

The new provisions in TCGA92/S184A to F apply where a tax advantage arises on or after 5 December 2005. This can include situations where a loss arose prior to 5 December, but where the gain that it might otherwise have reduced arises on or after that date.

As explained at CG47034, TCGA92/S184A to F take effect whether or not the loss arises before, at the same time as, or after, the change of ownership, and whether or not there is a chargeable gain from which it may be deducted at that time.
