Roll-over relief: compulsory acquisition of land: depreciating assets

TCGA92/S248 (3)

Where the new land is a depreciating asset, such as a short lease, the guidance at CG60295 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg60295) should be followed with the following modifications.

- The words `new asset’ should be read as `new land’,

and

- the cessation of trade use of the new land is not a chargeable occasion in relation to the held-over gain

The claim for relief on the acquisition of land that is a depreciating asset can be replaced by a new claim on the subsequent acquisition of land that is not a depreciating asset. The guidance at CG60295  (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg60295)sets out the conditions that must be met and explains the effect of such a claim.
