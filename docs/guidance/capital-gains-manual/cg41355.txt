Section 42 of the Administration of Justice Act 1982 gives the courts discretion toinvest funds in court in one of the common investment funds. These are referred to inTCGA92/S61 and TCGA92/S100 as ‘court investment funds’. These common investment funds arecomparable to unit trusts. For further information on such funds see CG20703.
