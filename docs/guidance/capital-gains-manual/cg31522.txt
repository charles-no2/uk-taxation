If you are informed that the deed has not been considered by Trusts Technical, and it is not clear that the deed is valid, or that the conditions in CG31940 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31940) have been met, you should consult Trusts Technical. If you are uncertain of the legal (as opposed to CGT) effects of a disclaimer, or its validity, please consult Trusts Technical. The guidance at CG31400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31400) should be followed.

You should inform them whether you have opened an enquiry or not.
