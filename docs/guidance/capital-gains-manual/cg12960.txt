Capital sums derived from assets: s22(2) TCGA92: time of disposal

Updated: 2022-05-31T16:24:54+01:00

S22(2) TCGA92 provides that the time of disposal is the time when the capital sum was received if the capital sum was received in any of the circumstances described in s22(1)(a) - (d) TCGA92, see CG12948 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12948) - CG12955.

In cases where a capital sum falls within the general wording of s22(1) TCGA92, see CG12945 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12945), the time of disposal will be determined by reference to the rules in s28 TCGA 1992, see CG14250P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14250p), if it was received under the terms of a contract for the acquisition or disposal of an asset.
