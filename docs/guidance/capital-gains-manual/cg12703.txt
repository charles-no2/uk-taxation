Disposal of assets: deemed disposals
Identifier: CG12703
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12703
Published: 2016-03-12T10:00:09+00:00
Updated: 2026-08-13T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:36:51.976271+00:00

There are times when although no disposal has actually taken place a person is treated as having disposed of an asset. This is known as a deemed disposal and creates an occasion of charge which would not otherwise have existed. The following (non-exhaustive) list contains some examples of deemed disposals:

- a capital sum derived from an asset, see CG12960 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12960)

- the entire loss, destruction dissipation or extinction of an asset, see CG13120P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg13120p)

- a claim by the owner of an asset that it is of negligible value, see CG13120P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg13120p)

- the satisfaction of a debt, see CG53405 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53405)

- appropriations to and from trading stock, see CG47970 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg47970)

- value shifting, see CG58850P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg58850p), CG13260 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg13260)+

- a person becoming absolutely entitled to any settled property as against trustees, see CG37000C (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg37000c)

- the death of a person entitled to a life interest in possession in all or any part of settled property (if the property remains settled property), see CG36450P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg36450p)

- trustees ceasing to be resident in the UK or ceasing to be liable to UK tax by becoming dual resident, see CG38200SUBC (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg38200subc)

- company migration, see CG42300P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42300p)

- a non-resident person (other than a company) carrying on business through a UK branch or agency, see CG25500P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg25500p)

- a non-resident company carrying on a trade through a UK permanent establishment, see CG42100P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42100p)

- a company leaving a group and taking with it assets transferred to it by other group members in the previous 6 years, see CG45400P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg45400p)

This list may not be exhaustive. If you encounter a scenario not covered in this list, it is important to consider whether the statute and guidance in this manual provides an answer.
