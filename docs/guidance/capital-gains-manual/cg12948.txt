Capital sums derived from assets: s22(1)(a) TCGA92: compensation

Updated: 2022-05-31T16:15:29+01:00

S22(1)(a) TCGA92 deems a disposal when the owner of an asset receives a capital sum, see CG12980 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12980), by way of compensation.

In certain circumstances, and subject to a claim being made, s23 TCGA92 prevents the receipt of a capital sum within s22(1)(a) TCGA92 from being treated as an occasion of a disposal where the compensation is applied in restoring a damaged asset or replacing an asset which has been lost or destroyed, see CG15700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15700p)+.
