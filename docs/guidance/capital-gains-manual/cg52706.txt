Company reconstructions: shareholder: cancellation or extinction of shares
Identifier: CG52706
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg52706
Published: 2016-03-12T11:27:25+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T08:53:30+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:42:48.153422+00:00

There is no need for the original shares to be cancelled, or otherwise extinguished. In many cases the shares will have become worthless and may be cancelled or disposed of for a nominal amount. However, in the case of a demerger, see CG52725, and similar examples the shareholders will retain their original shares.
