TCGA92/S136

The guidance at CG52670 applies if

- TCGA92/S137 has prevented TCGA92/S136 from applying and

- the tax on the disposal is unpaid for six months and

- the new shares or debentures have been transferred to a spouse or civil partner of the transferee or to a member of the same group of companies.
