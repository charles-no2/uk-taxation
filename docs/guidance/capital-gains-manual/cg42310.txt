Migration of companies: changing a company's  residence: pre-15/3/88
Identifier: CG42310
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42310
Published: 2016-03-12T11:04:34+00:00
Updated: 2021-04-22T10:17:22+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:41.962861+00:00

Prior to 15 March 1988 a company was required to obtain Treasury consent to

- cease to be resident in the UK, or

- transfer a business to a non-resident.

See CTM34300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/company-taxation-manual/ctm34300) onwards for further details and transitional provisions.
