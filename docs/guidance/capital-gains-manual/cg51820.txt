Share reorganisations: consideration paid: general
Identifier: CG51820

Published: 2016-03-12T11:24:49+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T08:52:39+00:00

As a reorganisation is treated as not involving the acquisition of any new shares aspecial rule is required for dealing with any consideration actually paid for the new holding. TCGA92/S128 (1) provides any consideration which is given for the new holding is deemed to have been incurred on the original shares. This is most relevant to rights issues.
