A person who receives the interest disclaimed by another person receives that interest in general law as a legatee under the will. If the conditions in CG31490 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31490) are satisfied that person also receives it as a legatee for Capital Gains Tax purposes. The assets are therefore deemed to have been acquired by the person at market value at the date of death, see CG31140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31140). If those conditions are not satisfied see CG31540 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31540).
