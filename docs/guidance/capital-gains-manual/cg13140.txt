Quoted shares

The Shares and Assets Valuation (SAV) team are responsible for determining whether quoted shares were of negligible value at the relevant dates. Currently where SAV have agreed that quoted shares are of negligible value, this is published on at Negligible value claims and agreements (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/negligible-value-agreements) on GOV.UK.
