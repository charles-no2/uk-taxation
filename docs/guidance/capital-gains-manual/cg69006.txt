This chapter includes instructions on the goodwill or `book interest' of insurance agents employed by certain insurance companies, see CG69100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg69100)+.

Insurance companies are dealt with by specific people within certain teams. To find the appropriate person, contact the Insurance Technical Specialists in the Financial Services Team.
