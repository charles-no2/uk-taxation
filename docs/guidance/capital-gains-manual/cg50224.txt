Definitions: meaning of ‘relevant securities’

The definition of relevant security is in TCGA92/S108. Full details are in CG51650 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51650)-51651. Relevant securities have their own identification rules, see CG51653 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51653)+. These rules apply for all persons up to 5 April 1998. For disposals on or after 6 April 1998 the rules apply only for companies and other concerns within the charge to Corporation Tax; for disposals by others, eg individuals within the charge to Capital Gains Tax, the normal share identification rules apply, see CG51640 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51640).
