Section 24(2) of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act (TCGA) 1992 – negligible value claims

Under section 24(1A) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/24), the owner of an asset may make a negligible value claim to HMRC if either condition A or condition B is met, see section 24(1)(b) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/24) and section 24(1)(c) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/24).

There is no requirement to make a claim within a specified time of the asset having become of negligible value.

Section 24(1A) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/24) says that a negligible value claim may be made by the owner of an asset. This means the asset must still exist when the negligible value claim is made. If the asset has ceased to exist then the negligible value claim will not succeed. Instead, there will have been an actual disposal under section 24(1) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/24).

The asset that is the subject of the claim needs to be of negligible value at the date of the claim and at the date of the deemed disposal and reacquisition (if different), see CG13130 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg13130).

'Negligible' is not defined in TCGA 1992. HMRC take the view that it means 'worth next to nothing'.

Effect of a successful negligible value claim

A successful negligible value claim simply results in the claimant being treated as if the asset that is the subject of the claim had been sold and immediately reacquired for its negligible value.

If, as a result of a satisfactory negligible value claim, there’s a capital loss, it is necessary for the claimant to notify that capital loss to HMRC in order for it to be an allowable loss. This notice needs to be given in addition to a negligible value claim, although the two things may be sent to HMRC in the same return, letter or other document.

shares, see CG13140 to CG13150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg13120p)

-

goodwill, see CG68080 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg68080)
