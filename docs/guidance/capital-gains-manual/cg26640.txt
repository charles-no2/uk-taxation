Arrival in and departure from UK: temporary non-residence: trade conducted in UK through branch or agency - year of departure 2013-14 or later
Identifier: CG26640
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg26640
Published: 2016-03-12T10:27:38+00:00
Updated: 2019-06-26T12:17:27+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:30:57.087919+00:00

The provisions relating to temporary non-residents do not affect the tax treatment of gains or losses of a non-resident individual carrying on a trade in the UK through a branch or agency. Such gains or losses should continue to be dealt with under the provisions of TCGA92/S10* and TCGA92/S16(3)*. Also see CG25115 in relation to split years.

*These provisions were re-written for disposals from 6 April 2019 see CG10150.
