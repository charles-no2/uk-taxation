All statutory references in this page are to the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 unless otherwise specified.

The basic mechanics of section 162 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/162) incorporation relief in a straightforward case are illustrated in the example below. The example assumes Extra Statutory Concession (ESC) D32 applies.

A transferred his business with all its assets to X Ltd in consideration for an issue of 8,000 shares in X Ltd. Liabilities in the sum of £15,000 were transferred with the business.

The balance sheet of the business at the date of transfer was as follows:

This table shows a simple balance sheet. On the left: Capital and reserves, £70,000 plus Creditors, £15,000, totalling £85,000. On the right, Business assets listed as: Non chargeable, £49,000, plus chargeable (at cost) £23,000, plus cash 13,000, also totalling £85,000.

It was agreed in the course of the negotiations that the current market values of the assets of the business were:

- non-chargeable assets = £55,000

- chargeable assets = £50,000

and these values were adopted for the purpose of determining the consideration payable to A.

This table shows the gain calculation. Market value on transfer, £50,000, less cost, £23,000, gives Aggregate net gains, £27,000.

2. Proportion of aggregate net gains appropriate to the consideration in shares is the gain multiplied by the fraction of A divided by B:

£27,000 × (103,000 ÷ 103,000) = £27,000

This amount is to be deducted from the cost of the shares. The base cost of the shares to be used on the occasion of their disposal is £103,000 - £27,000 = £76,000.

3. The balance of aggregate net gains is nil, the gain is relieved in full.
