Contingent liabilities: the effect of TCGA92 S49: incidental costs
Identifier: CG14809
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14809
Published: 2016-03-12T10:07:52+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T11:58:52+00:00

The incidental costs of challenging a claim under a contingent liability are not allowable. TCGA92/S49 only applies to legal liabilities which are the subject of the contingency. These cannot include legal costs incurred at a later date.

TCGA92/S49

If a disposal is made subject to a contingent liability which does not fall within TCGA92/S49 (1) the decision in Randall v Plumb applies, see CG14801+. The disposal consideration must be reduced by the value of the contingent liability. No adjustment is made later whether or not the contingent liability is enforced. See CG14825+ for guidance on indemnities.
