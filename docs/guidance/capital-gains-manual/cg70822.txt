Leases: granting of a lease: no premium paid on grant of lease
Identifier: CG70822
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg70822
Published: 2016-03-12T12:14:19+00:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:47:12+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:48:07.643213+00:00

If no premium is paid on the grant of a lease, and provided that the grant of the lease was an arm’s length transaction, no chargeable gain will accrue.

However, if the landlord has incurred any expenditure allowable under TCGA92/S38 (1)(c) (incidental costs of disposal), see CG15250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15250), in granting the lease, a capital loss equal to the amount of that expenditure will accrue.
