Unit trusts: treated as shares
Identifier: CG57682
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg57682
Published: 2016-03-12T11:44:13+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T10:26:38+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:43:58.341770+00:00

TCGA92/S99 treats units in a unit trust as though they were shares in a company. The normal Capital Gains Tax rules which apply to shares apply to units in a unit trust subject to a special definition of market value, see CG57690.
