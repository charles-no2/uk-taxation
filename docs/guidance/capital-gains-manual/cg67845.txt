TCGA92/S236L(1)(b)(i)

This condition is met if, on 10 December 2013, IHTA84/S86 applied to the settled property. That legislation deals with trusts for the benefit of employees, about which guidance is given at IHTM42901 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm42901).
