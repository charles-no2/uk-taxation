Migration of companies before January 2020: reduction of recovery charge for unused losses
Identifier: CG42430
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42430
Published: 2016-03-12T11:05:00+00:00
Updated: 2021-04-22T11:06:53+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:47.150287+00:00

CG42400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42400) explains how exit charges under TCGA92/S185 (CG42370 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42370)) that arose before 1 January 2020 that are postponed under TCGA92/S187 (CG42390 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42390)) may be recovered on the happening of certain events.

The chargeable gain of the principal company may be reduced if the subsidiary company has unused allowable losses provided

- they have not been taken into account in computing the postponed gain, and

- an election is made, under TCGA92/S187 (5), by the two companies within two years of the event which gave rise to the charge.

A separate election is required in respect of each such event.

If the subsidiary company makes allowable losses after the relevant time, for example on assets of a UK permanent establishment, an election under TCGA92/S187 (5) may include these losses (provided they are unused).

The ‘relevant time’ is defined by TCGA92/S185 (1) as the time at which the chargeable company ceases to be resident in the UK, see CG42370 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42370).
