Leases: Disposal: Assignment or surrender of long lease
Identifier: CG71120
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71120
Published: 2016-03-12T12:15:25+00:00
Updated: 2018-12-14T01:47:12+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:46:19.328961+00:00

A gain arising on the disposal of a long lease, that is a lease with a remaining term of more than 50 years at the date of its disposal, is calculated in the normal way. The only special rules which might apply are those relating to the surrender of leases, see CG71230P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71230p) and CG71300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg71300).
