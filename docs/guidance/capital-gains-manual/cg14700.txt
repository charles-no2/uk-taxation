Section 19(5) of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act (TCGA) 1992

Section 19 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19) was not intended to apply to straightforward 'intra-group' transfers so the provisions of section 19 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19) are disapplied where the transaction is a disposal between fellow group members within the meaning of section 171 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/171), see CG45300P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg45300p).

However, groups of companies could exploit section 19(5) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19) in two ways:

- they could assemble assets in one group member by a series of 'intra-group' transfers and then sell them to a connected person or persons outside the group, see section 20(6) and (7) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/20).

- one group member could fragment assets by transfer to other group members before their disposal outside the group to a connected person or persons, see section 19(6) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19).

Section 20(6) and (7) TCGA 1992

To prevent the exploitation of the rules in CG14680 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14680) where the provisions apply to a disposal which is not an 'intra-group' transfer

- the first bullet of CG14680 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14680) does not apply to acquisitions from fellow group members and

- for the purposes of the second bullet of CG14680 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14680), any disposal by way of an intra-group transfer before the first transaction in the series of linked transactions is assumed to have been made after that transaction.

Section 19(6) TCGA 1992

It would be possible for a group of companies to avoid section 19(1) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19): one company could fragment assets and transfer them at no gain/no loss under section 171 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/171) to other companies in the group before their disposal outside the group to a connected person or persons. Section 19(6) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19) counters this kind of avoidance. We treat a disposal by a transferee company (A) as if it had been made by the original transferor company (B) for the purpose of determining whether section 19(1) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19) applies. If the application of section 19(1) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/19) results in an increase in the consideration, this has effect in relation to the disposal made outside the group by the transferee company (A).
