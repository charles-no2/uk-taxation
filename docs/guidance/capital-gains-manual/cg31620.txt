An instrument of variation may be used

- to vary the terms of a trust created by the will without varying the assets passing from the estate to the trustees

- to create a trust that was not in the will

- to delete a trust that was in the will

or

- to alter the assets passing to a trust created by the will.

For cases where the first point above applies the instrument of variation should be dealt with under CG37886 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg37886). For cases where the other points apply the guidance in paragraphs CG31630 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg31630)+ should be followed.
