Non-resident companies: non-resident group: TCGA92/S14*: UK resident

As shown in Example 3 in CG57401 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg57401) TCGA92/S14 applies even if the UK resident shareholder is not a company. Some taxpayers and tax advisers are uncertain about this because TCGA92/S171 includes the words `so far as relates to Corporation Tax on chargeable gains’. They suggest this means TCGA92/S14 can only apply if the TCGA92/S13* charge would be to Corporation Tax on chargeable gains. We do not take this restrictive view. This practice was published in Tax Bulletin, Issue 7, page 74.

* TCGA92/S13 was re-written for disposals from 6th of April 2019 see CG10150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg10150).
