Leases: introduction: grant of occupation rent-free
Identifier: CG70706
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg70706
Published: 2016-03-12T12:13:42+00:00
Updated: 2017-09-01T12:55:52+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:46:13.211927+00:00

If a person is simply allowed to occupy a property rent-free, without any formal agreement, this will not normally amount to a lease for CGT purposes. It should therefore not be treated as giving rise to a part-disposal.
