Co.purchases own shares: capital treatment: Employee share schemes
Identifier: CG58660
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg58660
Published: 2016-03-12T11:48:06+00:00
Updated: 2021-03-31T16:02:01+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:44:26.967657+00:00

This guidance deals with tax issues arising when company purchases its own shares back from employees. Where all of the conditions in s1033 CTA10 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/4/section/1033) are met, the sale by the employee of shares is treated as a capital transaction.

Where capital treatment is appropriate, the relevant disposal value for capital gains tax purposes where shares are sold via a single unconditional contract is the full amount receivable for all the planned instalment payments. If the full amount is not actually received, it may be possible to amend this at a future date by making a claim under s48(1) TCGA92 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/48) that the associated payment had become irrecoverable, see CG14933 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14933).

Where a charge to tax as employment income arises in relation to employee shares the employer needs to consider whether those shares are readily convertible assets (RCA), see EIM11900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim11900).
