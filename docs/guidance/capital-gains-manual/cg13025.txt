Incentive payments by financial institutions: introduction
Identifier: CG13025

Published: 2016-03-12T10:02:21+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T11:48:35+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:37:04.172360+00:00

Financial institutions such as building societies now commonly offer monetary inducements to customers to take out a new mortgage (a cashback). It is also becoming common for them to offer other inducements to members to vote favourably (or otherwise) for a proposed take-over. This guidance tells you when such payments may be chargeable to Capital Gains Tax.

CG13026 - CG13027 deal with cashbacks.

CG13028 - CG13029 deal with other inducements.
