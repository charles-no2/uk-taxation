Land: disposal of interest in land: form of the contract
Identifier: CG70281
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg70281
Published: 2016-03-12T12:13:11+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T17:28:29+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:35:22.546299+00:00

In order to be valid the contract must comply with the following rules,

- it must include all the terms of the agreement in one document, or

- if contracts are exchanged, each of the documents must be signed by, or on behalf of, each of the parties to the contract.
