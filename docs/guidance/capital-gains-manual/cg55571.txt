Financial options: over-the-counter options
Identifier: CG55571

Published: 2016-03-12T11:37:52+00:00

The commercial term for many of the options which fall within the definition of financial option is `over-the-counter’ or OTC option. These are options which are created by banks and other financial institutions to meet the specific requirements of their individual clients. There is no market in these options.
