If the disposal is only subject to one of these taxes do not use this table - see either the ATED-Related CGT Guidance at CG73600+ or the Non-Resident CGT Guidance at CG73700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg73700)+ for standard computational rules.

Has an election been made under Para 5 Sch 4ZZA or Para 2(1)(b) Sch 4ZZB, to use the retrospective basis of computation?

If yes: ATED-related CGT should be calculated under Para 6 Sch 4ZZA (see guidance at CG73638 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg73638)-73640) and NRCGT should be calculated under Para 14 Sch 4ZZB (see guidance at CG73881 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg73881)-73884).
