Capital sums derived from assets: s22(1)(b) TCGA92: insurance receipts
Identifier: CG12950
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12950
Published: 2016-03-12T10:01:46+00:00
Updated: 2022-05-31T16:16:59+01:00

S22(1)(b) TCGA92 deems a disposal when the owner of an asset receives a capital sum, see CG12980 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12980), under an insurance policy for the risk of:

- any kind of damage or injury to assets, or

- the loss or depreciation of assets.

In certain circumstances, and subject to a claim being made, s23 TCGA92 prevents the receipt of a capital sum within s22(1)(b) TCGA92 from being treated as an occasion of a disposal where the insurance receipt is applied in restoring a damaged asset or replacing an asset which has been lost or destroyed, see CG15700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15700p)+.
