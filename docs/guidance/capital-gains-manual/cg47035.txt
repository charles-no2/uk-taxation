Targeted anti-loss buying rule - limited exception to the rule for gain assets held before change of ownership
Identifier: CG47035
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg47035
Published: 2016-03-12T11:14:34+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T17:05:34+00:00

Between 5 December 2005 and 21 March 2007, TCGA92/S184A (2) provided limited circumstances in which the loss buying provisions in TCGA92/S184A to F did not prevent access to a loss where the loss is set against a gain which arises to a company on the disposal of an asset it either disposed of, or owned, prior to the change of ownership.

This exception was removed by FA07/S32 (2)
