A scheme of reconstruction is defined in Schedule 5AA TCGA 1992 as a scheme of merger, division or other restructuring meeting the relevant conditions of that Schedule.

A scheme of merger, division or other restructuring can involve the whole or part of the business or businesses of one or more companies being transferred to one or more companies. See CG52720.
