Land: land transferred to trustees
Identifier: CG70229
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg70229
Published: 2016-03-12T12:12:54+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T17:21:42+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:35:20.672275+00:00

A landowner may transfer land to one or more persons to hold that land in trust. The trustees will hold the legal interest in, or legal title to, the land on trust for the beneficiary or beneficiaries of the trust.
