Futures: gilt-edged securities and qualifying corporate bonds
Identifier: CG56090
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56090
Published: 2016-03-12T11:38:22+00:00
Updated: 2021-03-31T14:44:55+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:33:35.613088+00:00

Gains arising on the disposal of a futures contract to acquire or dispose of gilt-edged securities or qualifying corporate bonds are exempt, TCGA92/S115.
