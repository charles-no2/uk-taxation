Effects of residence/domicile: settlements
Identifier: CG10980
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg10980
Published: 2016-03-12T09:57:20+00:00
Updated: 2026-09-16T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:36:40.140553+00:00

For settlements there were no relaxations to the residence rules equivalent to the concessions that applied for individuals for the years up to and including 2012-13. Thus any gains realised by trustees of a settlement between the date they became resident and the preceding 6 April are within the charge to tax. Similarly any gains realised between the date trustees cease to be resident and the following 5 April remain within the charge to tax. In addition trustees may be liable to an exit charge under TCGA92/S80 when they cease to be resident in the UK, see CG38215, or under TCGA92/S83 when they become treaty non-resident, see CG13400 and CG38215.

Guidance on establishing the residence position of a settlement can be found in the Trusts, Estates and Settlement Manual see TSEM1461+.
