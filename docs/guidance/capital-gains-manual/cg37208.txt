Absolute entitlement: losses of trustees: transfer of losses is mandatory
Identifier: CG37208
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg37208
Published: 2016-03-12T10:50:45+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T16:28:29+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:39:45.782672+00:00

The application of TCGA92/S71 (2) is mandatory, even though it may be to the taxpayers’ disadvantage for the losses to be transferred to the beneficiary rather than retained by the trustees.
