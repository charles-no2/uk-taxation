General notes

A. Any unmatched capital payments from the years to 5/4/18 are within the scope of the disregards in s87D (subject to s87E) and s87N (subject to s87P).

B. Special rules in s87F may apply if the settlement ceases to exist in the year

C. Close family member defined in s87H

D. The residence or RB status of a close family member does not impact the treatment of a capital payment for the settlor

E. For split years any gains are treated as arising in the UK part of that year - s87(2A)

F. Amounts G, T, U and R see s87J/K

G. If two or more gifts are made in the same year all amounts are allocated on a pro rata basis - s871

Referenced notes

- Settlor has power of recovery from CFM beneficiary for any tax charged s87G(3). Equivalent provision in s87J(5) where recycling applies.

- If the individual to whom the capital payment is allocated becomes non resident then under s87N no account will be taken of any unmatched part of the capital payment unless person is within scope of the temporary no resident provisions in which case s87P will apply

- If the individual is only temporarily non-resident s87E will bring the payment back into account in the period of return

- Untaxed matched gains (U) would be chargeable for the year, unmatched capital payments (R) would be available for matching

- Untaxed matched gains (U) would be chargeable if remitted, unmatched capital payments (R) would be available for matching

- The position of the first UK resident recipient would be considered

- If the onward payment was to a close family member, the capital payment or gain is moved to the settlor if UK resident. If settlor NR CFM rule does not apply
