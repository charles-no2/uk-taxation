Tonnage Tax: shipping companies subject to tonnage tax

Published: 2016-03-12T11:03:55+00:00

FA2000/SCH22 introduced an alternative method for calculating the corporation tax profits of shipping companies (“Tonnage Tax”). If a company is within this regime, certain assets are outside the scope of the capital gains legislation. Detailed instructions can be found in the Tonnage Tax Manual at TTM08001 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/tonnage-tax-manual/ttm08001).
