Unit trusts: overseas unit trusts
Identifier: CG57730
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg57730
Published: 2016-03-12T11:44:41+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T10:26:38+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:44:03.975355+00:00

There are overseas counterparts of unit trusts, known in the USA and Canada as Mutual Funds. Shares in such concerns should be treated as units in a unit trust. Distributions of capital gains should be treated as part disposals.
