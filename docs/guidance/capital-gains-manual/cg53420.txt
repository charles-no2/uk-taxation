TCGA92/S251 (1)

TCGA92/S251 (1) refers to ... the debt on a security (as defined in Section 132). In fact TCGA92/S132 does not define the phrase ‘the debt on a security’. But it does define the word ‘security’, as including any ‘loan stock or similar security whether of the Government of the United Kingdom or of any other government, or of any public or local authority in the United Kingdom or elsewhere, or of any company, and whether secured or unsecured.’

This definition is of limited use because there is no statutory guidance on the meaning of loan stock or similar security. However, the final words make it clear that the debt does not have to be secured to be the debt on a security. Therefore ‘debt on a security’ is not the same as `secured debt.’
