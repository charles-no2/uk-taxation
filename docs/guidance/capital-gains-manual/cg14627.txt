Connected persons: share disposal following asset transfer from

Published: 2016-03-12T10:07:04+00:00

Where an asset is transferred to a company from a connected person and the shares in the company are then sold you may need to consider a special time apportionment formula. This applies when the shares have been owned since 6 April 1965. See CG15585 for instructions.
