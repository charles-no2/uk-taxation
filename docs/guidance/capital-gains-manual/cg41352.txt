Unit and investment trusts: unauthorised unit  trusts
Identifier: CG41352
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg41352
Published: 2016-03-12T11:03:09+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T16:52:30+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:30.329897+00:00

An exempt unauthorised unit trust is an unauthorised unit trust scheme in which all theissued units are held throughout a year of assessment by bodies which are wholly exempt(other than by reason of residence) from the charge to Capital Gains Tax (for example,charities and local authorities).
