Capital sums derived from assets: s22(1)(c) TCGA92: forfeiture or surrender of rights
Identifier: CG12952
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12952
Published: 2016-03-12T10:01:48+00:00
Updated: 2025-10-07T01:00:00+01:00

S22(1)(c) TCGA92 deems a disposal when the owner of an asset receives a capital sum, see CG12980 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12980), in return for:

- forfeiting or surrendering his rights over the asset, or

- refraining from exercising his rights over the asset.

Guidance on the circumstances in which a ‘right’ is an asset for CG purposes is given at CG12000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg12000).

Examples of capital sums within s22(1)(c) TCGA92 include (but are not limited to):

- payments received in consideration of giving up contractual rights, see CG13000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg13000);

- payments for entering into a covenant which restricts the recipient’s actions in relation to an asset, see CG68060 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg68060)

- release of an option for consideration ().

In certain circumstances, and subject to a claim being made, s23 TCGA92 prevents the receipt of a capital sum within s22(1)(c) TCGA92 from being treated as an occasion of a disposal where the receipt is applied in restoring a damaged asset or replacing an asset which has been lost or destroyed, see CG15700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15700p)+.
