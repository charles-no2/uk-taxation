Groups: rebasing: elections out of kink test: first disposal

The general rule is that the first disposal to which section 35 of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act (TCGA) 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/35) applies, and which is made by any company in the group, is the first relevant disposal for the group as a whole. This disposal establishes the time limit for the election by the principal company. There are special rules for disposals by outgoing companies and incoming companies.

If an outgoing company made the first disposal in the group to which section 35 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/35) applies, that disposal is disregarded in determining the group's first relevant disposal. This is the result of paragraph 8(5)(a) of Schedule 3 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/schedule/3).

The reference in section 35(6) TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/35) to the first relevant disposal includes the first disposal to which section 35 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/35) applies which is made by an incoming company. This is the result of paragraph 8(5)(b) of Schedule 3 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/schedule/3). In effect an incoming company is treated as though it were a member of the group at the time it made its first disposal to which section 35 TCGA 1992 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/35) applies.
