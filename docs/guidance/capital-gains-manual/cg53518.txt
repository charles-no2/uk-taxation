Debts: satisfied by issuing shares or securities: share exchanges
Identifier: CG53518

TCGA92/S135 may apply if

- a debt that is a debenture

- is acquired by a company other than the debtor and

- the company issues its own shares or debentures in exchange for the debenture.

Guidance on TCGA92/S135 is at CG52521 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg52521) onwards. In practice, an acquisition of debentures is most likely to fall within TCGA92/S135 if the acquiring company already owns shares in the target company, or is acquiring shares as well as debentures. When that section applies TCGA92/S127 operates so that the transaction is treated as involving no acquisition of the shares issued (see CG52579 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg52579)) so TCGA92/S251 (3) cannot apply.
