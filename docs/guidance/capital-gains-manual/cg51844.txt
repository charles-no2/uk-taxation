Share reorganisations: consideration paid: anti- avoidance: cost restricted
Identifier: CG51844
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51844
Published: 2016-03-12T11:25:12+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T08:52:39+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:42:26.791764+00:00

If the reorganisation is not a bargain made at arm’s length the consideration given is restricted to the lower of

- the increase in market value of the new holding after the reorganisation over the value of the holding before the reorganisation,

or

- the actual consideration given for the new shares.
