Targeted rules to prevent income to capital converter schemes by companies - grounds for the issue of a notice 
Identifier: CG44141
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg44141
Published: 2016-03-12T11:05:40+00:00
Updated: 2021-04-22T11:36:30+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:32:06.778957+00:00

HMRC will only issue a notice in a case under TCGA92/S184G(6) when it is considered on reasonable grounds that all the following apply:

- A gain arises to a participant in the arrangements,

- That gain effectively represents a sum that, in the absence of the arrangements, would have been charged to tax as income, either on the same company or a connected person,

- As part of the arrangements the gain would, but for this legislation, have been reduced by allowable losses,

- It was the main purpose, or one of the main purposes of the arrangements to achieve the conversion of the income to a capital sum.
