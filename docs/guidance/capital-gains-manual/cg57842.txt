Small capital distributions: overseas companies
Identifier: CG57842
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg57842
Published: 2016-03-12T11:45:37+00:00
Updated: 2026-05-22T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:44:10.207280+00:00

The value of shares in a company quoted on a stock exchange outside the UK can be checked by making a request to Shares and Assets Valuation. You should explain why you need the valuation.

A paying agent for an overseas company who asks about the application of section 122(2) of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 should be referred to Shares and Assets Valuation.
