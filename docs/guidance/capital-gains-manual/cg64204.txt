Private residence relief: availability of relief
Identifier: CG64204
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64204
Published: 2016-03-12T11:54:11+00:00
Updated: 2022-05-10T17:25:08+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:44:35.623402+00:00

The relief is only available on disposals made by

- individuals, s222(1) TCGA92;

- trustees of settled property, s225 TCGA92, (see CG65400+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65400)); and

-

personal representatives, s225A TCGA92, (see CG65460 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65460)).

- Under s226 TCGA92, relief may also be available on the disposal of a residence which has been provided by an individual to their dependent relative. The residence must be provided rent-free and without any other consideration and must have been occupied by that dependent relative before 6 April 1988. For detailed guidance see CG65550+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65550p).

Relief is not available

- on a disposal by a company; or

- on a disposal on or after 10 December 2003 if the Capital Gains Tax acquisition cost of the property is reduced by gift hold-over relief under s260 TCGA92 (see CG67030+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67030p)) in relation to an earlier disposal which took place on or after 10 December 2003 (see CG64933 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64933) (individuals) and CG65440 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65440) (trustees)).
