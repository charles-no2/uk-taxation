Unit and investment trusts: unauthorised unit  trusts
Identifier: CG41350
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg41350
Published: 2016-03-12T11:03:06+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T16:52:30+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:29.582431+00:00

If a unit trust scheme

- comes within the definition of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000

but

- is not authorised by an Order under section 243 of that Act

it is an unauthorised unit trust.
