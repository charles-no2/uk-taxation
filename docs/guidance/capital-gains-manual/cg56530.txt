Employee share schemes: trustees: composite trust deeds

Published: 2016-03-12T11:40:47+00:00

The trust deed may establish a separate trust for each participating employee, or may establish a composite trust for participating employees generally. You should apply the ordinary share identification rules, see CG50500+, to the trust, or trusts, as appropriate. See also CG33330 in relation to sub-fund settlements.
