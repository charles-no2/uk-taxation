Share Incentive Plan (SIP): trustees: rights issues
Identifier: CG56494
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56494
Published: 2016-03-12T11:40:26+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-26T09:56:05+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:43:37.852054+00:00

The trustees are exempt from Capital Gains Tax on any gain on the disposal of rights where they have sold those rights to fund the exercise of other rights to acquire shares. This only applies if similar rights were conferred on all ordinary shares in the company.
