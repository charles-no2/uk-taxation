TCGA92/S262 and TCGA92/S45

Chattels exemption

Disposals of antiques which are chattels (tangible moveable property) for £6,000 or less will be exempt under TCGA92/S262, see CG76573 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76573). If antiques are disposed of to the same person then they may form a set, see CG76631 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76631) onwards for more details.

Wasting asset exemption

Antiques by their nature have a predictable life exceeding 50 years. However antiques which are plant or machinery are treated as wasting assets regardless of their actual predictable life, TCGA92/S44(1)(c) see CG76721 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76721).

Antiques such as clocks and watches are always regarded as wasting assets, see CG76904 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76904).

Other antiques will be non-wasting unless they are used as plant in a business. For the meaning of plant see the Capital Allowances Manual CA21000 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-allowances-manual/ca21000) onwards.

Disposals of antiques which are chattels and are plant or machinery will be exempt under TCGA92/S45(1) unless:

- capital allowances have been or could have been claimed, see CG15400+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15400p),

or

- section 45(3B) applies, see CG76722 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76722).
