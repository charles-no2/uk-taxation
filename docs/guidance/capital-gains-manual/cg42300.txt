Migration of companies: company residence
Identifier: CG42300
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42300
Published: 2016-03-12T11:04:25+00:00
Updated: 2021-04-22T10:10:28+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:40.463214+00:00

The rules determining where a company is resident for taxation purposes depend on theperiod under consideration. Depending on what period you are dealing with you will need toconsider one or more of the following.

- Where the central management and control of the company is exercised.

- Where the company is incorporated.

- Whether the company is `treaty non-resident’, see CG42321 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg42321).
