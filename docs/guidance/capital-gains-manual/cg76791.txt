TCGA92/S46

Mr S purchases from a descendant the copyright over the memoirs of a writer 20 years after the end of the year in which the writer died. That copyright is, therefore, a wasting asset since it now has a predictable life of fifty years, see CG76723 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg76723). He pays the descendant £90,000 for it but does not expect it to have any residual value in fifty years time.

He later sells after twenty years for £80,000.

Subject to any incidental expenses, his Capital Gains computation will be:
