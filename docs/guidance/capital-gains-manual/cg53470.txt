Debts which are not debts on a security: introduction
Identifier: CG53470

Published: 2016-03-12T11:32:00+00:00
Updated: 2026-08-13T01:00:00+01:00

The general rules for the tax treatment of debts which are not debts on a security are set out at CG53471 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53471)-CG53480. Different rules apply if the original creditor was a trustee and the debt, when created, was settled property. See CG53485 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg53485).
