1.Acquisition in April 2014 for £4,000,000

Basic information:

Residential property acquired April 2014 for £4,000,000 and disposed of April 2018 for £6,000,000.

Estimated Indexation factor |  Year |

0.25 |  April 2014 to April 2018 |

Total number of days chargeable to ATED |  730 |

Total days April 2014 to April 2018 |  1,460 |

Stage 1

Disposal proceeds |  Amount |

- |  £6,000,000 |

Allowable deductions for CGT purposes |  £4,000,000 |

Gain on CGT rules |  £2,000,000 |

Stage 2

Days in period of ownership that are chargeable to ATED (CD) 730

Total days (TD) 1,460

CD/TD x gain on CGT rules ((730/1,460) x £2,000,000) = £1,000,000

ATED-related chargeable gain £1,000,000

Stage 3

Not required - gain under stage 2

Stage 4

Unadjusted gain from stage 1 above £2,000,000

Less

ATED related gain £1,000,000

Notional indexation (see below) £500,000 £1,500,000

£500,000

Non ATED-related gain £500,000

Notional indexation:

Maximum indexation (£4,000,000 x 0.25) = £1,000,000

Relevant fraction: ((TD - CD) / TD) x indexation

((1460 - 730) / 1460) x £1,000,000) = £500,000
