TCGA92/S17 & S18

Where a lease is granted between connected persons (TCGA92/S18), see CG14560 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14560) onwards, or is otherwise granted not at arm's length, see CG14540 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14540) onwards, the tenant is deemed to have acquired the lease at its market value and paid the appropriate premium.

The provisions of TCGA92/S17 will apply, see CG14530 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14530) onwards, and you should obtain the Valuation Office's opinion of the value of the deemed premium, see CG74000P (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg74000p).

TCGA92/S28 will determine the time at which TCGA92/S17 applies.

A premium which is deemed to arise in these circumstances is to be treated for capital gains purposes in precisely the same way as any other premium.
