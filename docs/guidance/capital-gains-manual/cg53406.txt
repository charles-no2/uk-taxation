TCGA92/S115

Gains or losses on gilt-edged securities are exempted from Capital Gains Tax by TCGA92/S115 (1). Gilt-edged securities (gilts) are UK Government debt. A list of gilt-edged securities is given in TCAG92/SCH9. For further instructions see CG54900 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg54900)+.
