Capital loss anti-avoidance rule: Interaction with negligible value claims
Identifier: CG40248
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg40248
Published: 2016-03-12T11:01:48+00:00
Updated: 2020-11-13T09:01:52+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:23.279780+00:00

Nothing in the FA 2006 amendments to TCGA92/S8 (TCGA92/S16A with effect from 6 December2006, see CG40241) prevents advantage being taken from the provisions in TCGA92/S24 where, for example, an asset has genuinely become of negligible value.
