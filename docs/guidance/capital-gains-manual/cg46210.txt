TCGA92/S184 (2)

The rule in TCGA92/S184 (2) ensures that the provisions restricting indexation remain effective where an asset passes at no gain/no loss from one company to another under

- TCGA92/S171 (1) (intra-group asset transfers, see CG45305)

- TCGA92/S140A (transfer of UK trade between companies resident in different EU member states, see CG45705).
