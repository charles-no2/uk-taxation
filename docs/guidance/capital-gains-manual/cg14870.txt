ITA2007/S752 (CTA2010/S815)

You need to consider whether the transaction comes within the scope of ITA2007/S752 for income tax (or CTA2010/S815 for corporation tax) if the asset sold is

- land,

or

- an interest in land,

or

- property deriving its value from land, for example shares in a property owning company.

There are detailed instructions in CG72800+.
