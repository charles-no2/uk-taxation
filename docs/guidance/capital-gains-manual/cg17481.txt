1 June 1982 Y acquires office premises for £100,000.

1 June 1985 premises improved by spending £25,000 on car parking and an entrance.

1 June 1988 part of premises sold for £45,000, the part retained is worth £180,000.

1 June 1990 rest of premises sold for £160,000.

FIRST DISPOSAL

The part-disposal formula, see CG12731, gives

- 45,000 divided by (180,000 plus 45,000)
