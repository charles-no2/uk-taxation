TCGA92/S13 (10)*

TCGA92/S13 (10) allows the gains of non-resident companies to be apportioned to non-resident trustees. A non-resident trust is not normally liable to UK Capital Gains Tax. But there are provisions for charging the gains of the trust on UK resident settlors and beneficiaries, see CG38200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg38200)+. These provisions also apply to a charge under gains falling within TCGA92/S13.

* TCGA92/S13 was re-written for disposals from 6th of April 2019 see CG10150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg10150).
