The disposal of particular assets may attract full or partial relief from Capital Gains Tax. These volumes deal with

- roll-over relief for the replacement of business assets, see CG60250C (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg60250c)

- roll-over relief for the replacement of land by local constituency associations of political parties on reorganisations of constituencies, see CG60260 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg60260)

- roll-over relief for the replacement of land acquired by an authority having powers of compulsory purchase, see CG61900+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg61900p)

- roll-over relief on a transfer of shares to an All Employee Share Ownership Trust, see CG61970+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg61970p)

- private residence relief on a disposal of a person's only or main residence, see CG64200+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64200p)

- relief on the transfer of a business to a company, see CG65700+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65700p)

- relief for losses on loans to traders or for payments under a guarantee, see CG65900C (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg65900c)

- double taxation relief on the disposal of assets where foreign tax is paid, see CG14380 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14380)

- hold-over relief on gifts, see CG66450C (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg66450c).

Particular types of organisation are exempt from Capital Gains Tax on the disposal of certain assets. The next part of Volume 7 deals with

- charities, see CG67500C (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67500c)

- trade unions, see CG67600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67600)

- superannuation funds, see CG67650+ (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg67650p)

Where reliefs are available in respect of discrete types of asset no confusion can arise about their interaction. However, particularly in relation to business assets, a number of different reliefs may be available. The order in which such reliefs should be allowed depends on the way in which they work.

- Roll-over relief (TCGA92/S152 - TCGA92/S158) reduces the consideration to be taken into account in computing the gain on disposal. This comes first.

- Relief on the transfer of a business to a company (TCGA92/S162) known as incorporation relief works by reducing the net chargeable gains.

- Relief for gifts of business assets (TCGA92/S165) also works by reducing the chargeable gains. Instructions on the interaction of this relief are at CG66450C (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg66450c).
