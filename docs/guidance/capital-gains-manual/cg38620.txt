Trustees' gains - offshore income gains
Identifier: CG38620
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg38620
Published: 2016-03-12T10:56:57+00:00
Updated: 2019-10-15T11:17:38+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:40:08.915239+00:00

Offshore income gains are taxed as income, SI 2009/3001 regulation 18. Therefore any offshore income gain arising to the trustees’ is not included in the trustees’ section 2(2)* amount. A loss on the disposal of an interest in a non-reporting fund is available to the trustees as a capital loss because losses are not deductible in calculating offshore income gains, SI 2009/3001 regulation 42. See the Offshore Funds Manual for guidance on offshore income gains.

*This section was re-written for disposals from 6 April 2019 to section 1(3) see CG10150 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg10150).
