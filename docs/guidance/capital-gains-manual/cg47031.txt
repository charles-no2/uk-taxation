Targeted anti-loss buying rule - effect of the legislation
Identifier: CG47031
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg47031
Published: 2016-03-12T11:14:23+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T17:05:34+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:41:49.845848+00:00

The effect of the legislation in relation to loss buying is that a loss which arises to a company on a disposal of a pre-change asset may not be set against any gains, except in certain very limited circumstances. See CG47035 for those limited circumstances.
