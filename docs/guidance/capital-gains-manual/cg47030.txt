Targeted anti-loss buying rule - interaction with deemed no gain/no loss disposals
Identifier: CG47030
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg47030
Published: 2016-03-12T11:14:22+00:00
Updated: 2016-01-25T17:05:34+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T14:41:49.494617+00:00

Nothing in the FA 2006 legislation ordinarily prevents a genuine loss on a real disposal of an asset from being used to frank gains by other companies within the same capital gains group following a claim TCGA92/S171A unless the provisions of TCGA92/S184A apply to the loss.
