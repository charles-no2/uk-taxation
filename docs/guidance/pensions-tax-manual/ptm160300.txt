The Registered Pension Schemes (Provision of Information) Regulations 2006 - SI 2006/567

Section 250 Finance Act 2004

The scheme administrator is required to retain records relating to the administration of the scheme, for example records of payment into and out of the scheme, for at least six tax years following the year to which the record relates. PTM160200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm160200) gives full details of the record keeping requirement.

The table below show the events where a scheme administrator has to provide information to another person.

Sometimes other persons are also obliged to provide information to the scheme administrator, this may be from

- scheme members - see PTM160400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm160400),

- employers - see PTM160500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm160500), and

- scheme managers of qualifying recognised overseas pension schemes (QROPS) or former QROPS - see PTM166700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm166700).
