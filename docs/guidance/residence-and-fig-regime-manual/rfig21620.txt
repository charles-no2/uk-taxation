Statutory Residence Test (SRT): Temporary non-residence: Chargeable event gains
Identifier: RFIG21620
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig21620
Published: 2025-04-04T17:52:11+01:00
Updated: 2026-03-10T00:00:00+00:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:18:45.163205+00:00

Where a chargeable event gain arises on a:

- life assurance

- life annuity

- capital redemption policy

when an individual is temporarily non-resident; they will be liable to tax on the gain in the period of their return.

For more details of the policies affected by this charge, see IPTM3300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/insurance-policyholder-taxation-manual/iptm3300). For detailed guidance on the interaction between chargeable event gains and temporary non-residence see IPTM3734 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/insurance-policyholder-taxation-manual/iptm3734).
