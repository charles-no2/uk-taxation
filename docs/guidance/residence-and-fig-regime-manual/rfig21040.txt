Statutory Residence Test (SRT): Split year treatment: Case 1 - Starting full-time work overseas
Identifier: RFIG21040
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig21040
Published: 2025-04-04T17:51:46+01:00
Updated: 2025-06-06T01:00:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:18:39.385658+00:00

An individual may meet the criteria for split year treatment for a tax year in which they begin full-time work overseas. They must:

- be UK resident for the tax year in question

- be UK resident for the previous tax year (whether or not this was also a split year)

- be non-UK resident in the following tax year, because they meet the third automatic overseas test (refer to RFIG20140 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig20140)) (even if they meet the first automatic overseas test in that following year)

- satisfy the overseas work criteria during a relevant period
