Statutory Residence Test (SRT): Days spent in the UK: Meaning of a day spent in the UK 
Identifier: RFIG20710
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig20710
Published: 2025-04-04T17:51:27+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-04T17:37:10+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:18:36.140310+00:00

An individual is considered to have spent a day in the UK if they are here at the end of the day (midnight). This is subject to:

- the deeming rule (see RFIG20720 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig20720)) which will count certain days even though they were not here at midnight

- transit days (see RFIG20730 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig20730))

- time spent in the UK due to exceptional circumstances - those days may not count towards the total day count for certain parts of the SRT

If an individual considers that they have been in the UK at the end of the day because of exceptional circumstances, and that this will have a bearing on their residence status see RFIG22220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22220) and RFIG22230 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig22230). These detail those parts of the SRT where exceptional circumstance days may, or may not be taken into account when counting the number of days spent in the UK.
