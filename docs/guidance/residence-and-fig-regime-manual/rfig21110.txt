Statutory Residence Test (SRT): Split year treatment: Case 2 - Deemed departure date 
Identifier: RFIG21110
Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig21110
Published: 2025-04-07T09:58:10+01:00

The deemed departure day is the later of:

- the day an individual joins their partner to live together overseas

- the day which is the first day of the overseas part of the year under Case1 for their partner.
