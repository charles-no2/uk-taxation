Statutory Residence Test (SRT): Split year treatment: Case 6 - Relevant period

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-and-fig-regime-manual/rfig21200
Published: 2025-04-04T17:51:52+01:00
Updated: 2025-04-04T17:38:00+01:00
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-09-18T16:18:41.926892+00:00

A relevant period is a period that:

- begins with the first day of the tax year

- ends with a day in that tax year on which the individual does more than 3 hours work overseas
