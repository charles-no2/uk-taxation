Where there is doubt about whether a payment is interest, or contains an element of interest, guidance must come from case law. SAIM2030 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2030) onwards has more on the meaning of ‘interest’.

SAIM2070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2070) onwards gives guidance on interest included in compensation.

ITTOIA05/S369 (1) provides for certain specific statutory inclusions in the tax charge on interest. Guidance is at SAIM2200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2200) onwards.

Exceptions from the charge on interest are set out in ITTOIA05/S369 (2) and explained at SAIM2300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2300) onwards.

SAIM2400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2400) onwards gives guidance on who is chargeable, and the basis on which income tax is charged.

SAIM2500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2500) onwards gives guidance on the tax rules where the right to receive interest is sold to another person.

SAIM2700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim2700) onwards gives guidance on the rules for disguised interest.
