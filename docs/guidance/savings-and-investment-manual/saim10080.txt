Interest relieved on a paid basis

Relief under ITA07/PT8/CH1 is given on a ‘paid’ basis so we are concerned only with the amount of interest actually paid, regardless of when it was or is due and payable.

Consequently, looking again at Example 2 at SAIM10070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim10070) it would not matter whether the payment pattern outlined arose from the terms of the loan or because the taxpayer defaulted in years 1 and 2.

Similarly in Example 1 at SAIM10070 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim10070) it would not matter whether the taxpayer had to pay £50,000 in year 1 under the terms of the loan agreement or whether he had paid it ‘voluntarily’.
