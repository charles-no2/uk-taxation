Special cases

The Accrued Income Scheme is adapted to deal with a number of special situations. These fall into the following categories.

Particular transactions treated as transfers, and excluded transfers

There are special rules for transfers that involve

- strips of gilt-edged securities (SAIM4250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4250))

- new securities issued with extra interest (securities issued in tranches). In this case, special rules on calculations apply - see below (SAIM4260 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4260))

- transactions by persons to and from themselves in different capacities - namely persons appropriating securities as trading stock, persons becoming trustees, and where securities cease to be held on charitable trusts (SAIM4270 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4270)).

In addition, certain transfers are completely excluded from the scheme. These are cases that involve stock lending and repos (SAIM4280 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4280)).

Special rules about some types of calculation

The rules on how accrued income amounts are calculated are adapted in cases that involve

- ‘interest in default’ (where interest is due but unpaid, including the case where there is also unrealised interest on the security) (SAIM4290 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4290))

- new securities issued with extra interest (one of the particular categories of transfer - see above) (SAIM4260 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4260))

- manufactured payments (SAIM4220 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4220))

- foreign currency securities (SAIM4310 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4310)).

Nominees and trustees

Special rules apply to transfers by or to nominees or trustees (SAIM4320 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4320)).

Unremittable proceeds

Special rules apply where proceeds from ‘foreign securities’ are unremittable (SAIM4340 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/savings-and-investment-manual/saim4340)).
