Paragraph 84 Schedule 7 Finance Act 2008

Background

Prior to Finance Act 2008, it was not necessary for an individual who was resident in the UK but who was not domiciled in the UK to make a claim to be taxed on the remittance basis in respect of gains accruing from the disposal of assets situated outside the UK. That is because TCGA92/s12 provided that the remittance basis applied automatically in respect of these gains. Whereas following the Finance Act changes the remittance basis only applies if an individual chooses to use it for that tax year.

Transitional

The transitional rule provides that capital gains for years prior to 2008-2009 continue to be taxable when remitted by applying TCGA92/s12, as amended by Finance Act 2008, as if the individual had made a claim under ITA07/s809B for the remittance basis of taxation to apply for the relevant year.

Provision is made to ensure that the change to the temporary non-residents rules in TCGA92/s10A do not apply to any part of the gain remitted to the UK in tax year 2007-2008 or any earlier year. See the Capital Gains Manual for more information.

Effect

This ensures that chargeable gains from tax years prior to 2008-2009 are taxable when remitted to the UK after 6 April 2008. Also, chargeable gains of earlier years are taxable even if the individual does not choose to use or claim the remittance basis under the rules at ITA07/Chapter A1 in 2008-2009 or later tax years.

For 2019-2020 and subsequent tax years foreign gains are treated as accruing when remitted by virtue of Schedule 1 TCGA.
