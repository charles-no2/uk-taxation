Remittance Basis: Exemptions: Business investment relief: Condition A - eligible stakeholder company (s809VD(3) ITA2007)
Identifier: RDRM34350

Prior to 6 April 2017 an eligible stakeholder company was a private limited company which;

- exists wholly for the purpose of making investments in eligible trading companies [see RDRM34345 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm34345)] ignoring minor or incidental purposes and

- must have held at least one investment in an eligible trading company or be preparing to do so within two years of the relevant event [see RDRM34320 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm34320)].

After 6 April 2017 an eligible stakeholder company must actually hold at least one investment in an eligible trading company, or be preparing to do so within 5 years of the relevant event. To take advantage of the 5 year extension the investment must be made no earlier than 6 April 2017. The first bullet point conditions (above) remain unchanged.
