Overview

Amounts can only be designated under the temporary repatriation facility (TRF) if they meet the definition of qualifying overseas capital – see RDRM72100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm72100). However, these amounts may not simply be money in an overseas bank account. They may have been used to purchase overseas assets (see RDRM74300 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm74300)), or they may have been brought to the UK, for example, as exempt property (see RDRM74400 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm74400)) or having been invested in a UK company and business investment relief has been claimed (see RDRM74700 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm74700)). Amounts may be held within a mixed fund (see RDRM75100 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm75100) onwards) or in a joint account (see RDRM74200 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm74200)).

Additionally, an individual may not have some of their foreign income and gains in their possession on 6 April 2025, either because those amounts are being held by someone else (see RDRM74500 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm74500)), or because they receive income during the TRF period that arose prior to 6 April 2025, such as earnings for a previous tax year (see RDRM74600 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm74600)).

Pre-2008 foreign income and gains

As with any other income and gains which arose in a period during which an individual was subject to the remittance basis, pre-6 April 2008 foreign income and gains can be designated under the TRF.

Pre-2012 foreign currency gains

Since 6 April 2012, gains on foreign currency bank accounts have been exempt from Capital Gains Tax (CGT). Pre-6 April 2012 foreign currency gains, which would have been subject to CGT on remittance, but which have not yet been remitted to the UK, can be designated under the TRF.
