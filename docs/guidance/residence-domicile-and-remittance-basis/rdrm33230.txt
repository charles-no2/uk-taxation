Condition C is concerned with the remittance of an individual’s foreign income or gains by, or through, a gift recipient.

A ’gift recipient’ is a person, other than a relevant person to whom the individual makes a gift of money or other property that either:

- is income or chargeable gains of the individual

- derives (wholly or in part, and directly or indirectly) from income or chargeable gains of the individual’ ( see section 809N(2) ITA 2007)

So, if an individual remittance basis user makes a gift (RDRM33250 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm33250)) of:

- money, that is itself his or her untaxed foreign income or gains

- other property, including intangible property, that was bought using untaxed foreign income and gains, for example a motor car purchased out of their foreign chargeable gains, or shares in an offshore company purchased from their untaxed foreign income and gains

- money or other property that is in any way derived from untaxed foreign income or gains, for example proceeds from sale of shares in an offshore company originally purchased using relevant foreign earnings, or a work of art originally purchased offshore using untaxed foreign chargeable gains

to another person who is not a relevant person, that other person is a gift recipient.
