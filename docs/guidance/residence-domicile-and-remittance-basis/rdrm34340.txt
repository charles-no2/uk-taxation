Condition A

To meet the requirements of a qualifying investment, the investment must be made in a target company (s809VC(2) ITA2007).

A target company is a company that is either an;

- eligible trading company, [see RDRM34345 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm34345)]

- eligible stakeholder company, [see RDRM34350 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm34350)]

- eligible holding company [see RDRM34355 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm34355)]

- eligible hybrid company [see RDRM31358].
